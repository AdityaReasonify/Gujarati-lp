# No-Hallucination Policy — READ FIRST

Everything in the plan is derived from **this chapter's text**. Three rules, no exceptions.

## 1. Examples in these files are never data

Every named poem, poet, character or line quoted anywhere in this pack — in a genre profile, in
`alankar_chhand.md`, in this policy — is **illustrative**. `નરસિંહ`, `મીરાં`, `અખો` appear
here to show a shape, not to be copied into your output. Derive 100% from YOUR chapter.

## 2. The verbatim is sacred

`original_chunk` is copied, never composed. If you cannot extract a passage cleanly, say so and
stop — do not reconstruct it from memory. A poem you half-remember is not the poem the child is
holding. See `gujarati_verbatim.md`.

## 3. An empty field beats an invented one

- **અલંકાર** — if the કડી has none, `figures_of_speech: []`. Naming a device that is not in the
  lines teaches the child something false about the text.
- **પ્રાસ** — if the lines do not rhyme, say so in the `note`. Do not assert a pattern.
- **કવિ / લેખક facts** — only what the chapter itself states. The કવિ-પરિચય / લેખક-પરિચય box
  contributes exactly what is printed in it; a birth year, a movement, an award that is not
  printed in the chapter does not go in the plan, however confident you feel.
- **Page numbers, textbook edition, source paths** — record the gap rather than guessing
  (Agent 11 fails soft and never blocks).
- **સ્વાધ્યાય answers** — answer from the chapter. Where an exercise asks the child for a personal
  opinion or their own words (a "તમારા વિચારો લખો"-type block — use the exact printed heading from
  Agent 1's `exercise_inventory`, never an assumed one), give a model answer clearly marked as one
  possible response, not as *the* answer.

## The failure this prevents

A plausible, fluent, confidently-wrong Gujarati plan is worse than an incomplete one: the teacher
cannot see the error, and the child learns it. When you are unsure, record the uncertainty in
`validation_report.md` and surface it. **Surfacing a gap is a success of this pipeline, not a
failure of it.**
