# સ્વરૂપ Diagnosis (Step 1) — read four signals, then decide

Diagnose from **your** chapter, off the rendered page. Never from a sample, never from the title
alone, and never from the genre column in the corpus inventories
(`reference/corpus/std-6_inventory.md` … `reference/corpus/std-10_inventory.md`) — those are
**priors**, recorded to tell you what to expect, not to save you the diagnosis.

> **Roster status (VERIFY-3, closed).** The roster below is fixed from **all five** measured
> inventories — `reference/corpus/std-6_inventory.md` … `std-10_inventory.md`, every unit read off a
> rendered page, none of it title-level guessing any more. Seventeen profiles exist on disk and
> `profiles/genres/_genre_index.md` is the routing authority; every slug named here is a file in that
> directory, checked in both directions by `check_pack.py`. Priors in the inventories remain evidence,
> never a verdict: if the rendered page disagrees with a prior, the page wins and the prior is
> corrected.

## The book usually names the સ્વરૂપ itself — read that first

Std 6–8 chapters open with a teacher-addressed **blue intro box** that states the form outright:
"આ એક સુંદર લોકગીત છે", "આ એક ચાતુર્યકથા છે", "આ એક સરસ હાસ્યનિબંધ છે", "આ એક ગઝલ છે",
"આ કથાકાવ્ય છે". Std 9–10 carry the same claim in the unlabelled **કૃતિ-પરિચય** paragraph under the
poet's bio ("કવિએ આ નાનકડા ઊર્મિકાવ્યમાં…", "…મંદાક્રાન્તા છંદમાં"), and sometimes in the author slot
itself, which prints `- લોકગીત` or `- સંકલિત` where a poet's name would go.

This is the strongest single signal on the page and it is evidence for all four signals below — but
it is **evidence, not a verdict**: the label is often broad (કાવ્ય, વાર્તા) where the routing needs a
sub-form, and it is teacher apparatus, so it is **never a reading scene and never a topic**.

## The four signals

**A — Structure.** Is the text in પંક્તિઓ with પ્રાસ and લય, or in paragraphs?
If verse: **how long is one self-contained unit** — two lines (દુહો), three (હાઈકુ), a શેર, fourteen
lines (સૉનેટ), a કડી under a ટેક (ગીત / પદ / લોકગીત), a કડી chain that tells a story (કથાકાવ્ય), or
no fixed length at all (અછાંદસ)? Unit length decides before theme does.
If prose: does it move by ઘટના, by સ્મૃતિ, by તથ્ય, by સંવાદ, or by તર્ક?

**B — Unit / chapter theme.** What is this chapter *for* in the reader — ભક્તિ, પ્રાર્થના, પ્રકૃતિ,
દેશભક્તિ-શૌર્ય, શ્રમ-આત્મનિર્ભરતા, સંપ-સહકાર, સ્વચ્છતા-નાગરિકતા, હાસ્ય, ચરિત્ર, લોકજીવન-સંસ્કૃતિ,
પર્યાવરણ, વિજ્ઞાન-કુતૂહલ, પ્રામાણિકતા-મૂલ્ય? Signal B breaks ties, it does not lead: a sung poem in
the સ્વચ્છતા slot is still a ગીત and is taught as one — its theme picks the lens, not the સ્વરૂપ.

**C — સ્વાધ્યાય.** The exercises reveal what the book thinks it taught.
"કાવ્યપંક્તિના સૌથી નજીકના અર્થ સામે ખરું (✓) કરો." and "નીચેનો ભાવ દર્શાવતી કાવ્યપંક્તિ કાવ્યમાંથી
શોધીને લખો." say verse. "સરખા પ્રાસવાળા શબ્દો લખો." says કાવ્ય-કલા. "આ કાવ્યનું વર્ગમાં સમૂહગાન કરો."
and "આ કૂચગીતનું અભિનય સાથે ગાન કરો." say a **sung** form. "તમારા વિસ્તારમાં બોલાતા ઉપરના જેવા તળપદા
શબ્દોની યાદી બનાવો." says લોકગીત or લોકકથા. "નીચેની ઘટનાઓને પાઠના ક્રમમાં ગોઠવીને ફરીથી લખો.",
"પાઠને આધારે કહો કે આ વાક્યો કોણ બોલી શકે ?" and the std-7 signature counterfactual "…હોત તો શું
થાત ?" say વાર્તા. "આ એકમને વર્ગમાં સંવાદ સ્વરૂપે રજૂ કરો." and પ્રવૃત્તિ ordering નાટ્યીકરણ say
સંવાદ or એકાંકી. "રણછોડ પગી વિશે પાંચ-છ વાક્યો લખો." beside real dates on the page says ચરિત્ર.

Two calibrations, both measured: (i) **grammar blocks are not a genre signal.** રૂઢિપ્રયોગ, કહેવત,
શબ્દસમૂહ માટે એક શબ્દ, સંધિ-સમાસ and the chapter-final green ભાષા-બોધ box appear beside every
સ્વરૂપ, and std 9–10 print standalone **વ્યાકરણ એકમ** units with no literature in them at all.
(ii) **Signal C weakens at std 9–10.** Std 6–8 re-word their exercise headings per chapter, so the
headings are chapter-specific and highly diagnostic; std 10 prints a fixed four-tier ladder
(✓-MCQ → એક-એક વાક્યમાં ઉત્તર → બે-ત્રણ વાક્યમાં ઉત્તર → સવિસ્તર ઉત્તર) for every chapter alike.
There, read the **ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ** block instead — it is where the book comments on ધ્રુવપંક્તિ,
repetition, 'જી રે' લોકપરંપરા, સજીવારોપણ — and the **વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ** bullets (પદોનું ગાન કરો,
લોકગીતોની સ્પર્ધા).

**D — Purpose.** What does the text *do* — sing, pray, remember a life, tell a story, describe a
custom or a process, argue a case, make you laugh, hand down a શિખામણ through a દૃષ્ટાંત?

**Five near-misses the corpus actually contains.** Route on the tell, not the feel, and route to a
file — every slug below is a profile in `profiles/genres/`.

**Not a genre — never diagnose these, never cut them as topics.** The revision checkpoints
(આગળ વધતાં પહેલાં, પૂર્ણ કરતાં પહેલાં), the std 9–10 વ્યાકરણ એકમ units, the શબ્દસીડી board game, the
blue intro box, the chapter-final green grammar box, the fun boxes (humour, ઉખાણું, "ગાઈએ" —
see the mixed-chapter exception below), ચર્ચા-વિચારણા, પ્રવૃત્તિ and શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા. Record them in
`exercise_inventory` where they are exercises, in `extraction_notes[]` where they are apparatus, and
let Agent 10 handle everything that asks the child a question.

If signals conflict, set `genre_confidence: "low"`, record the conflict in `01_meta.json`, and
**surface it before drafting.** Conflicts this corpus actually produces: an intro box saying "કાવ્ય"
over a verse whose unit length says કથાકાવ્ય; a વાર્તા whose whole payload is a process
(જલચક્ર, વીજળીનું પ્રવાસ) and whose exercises test the process, not the plot; a ચરિત્ર told as a
frame story by a third person; a chapter whose intro box names one form and whose apparatus
tests another (std-8 ch 9 શતરંગી ભારત, std-8 ch 14 નિયમો કોના માટે ?). Low
confidence plus a precise question is the correct output — a confident wrong સ્વરૂપ costs the whole
downstream chapter.

## Mixed chapters — common in the GSEB દ્વિતીય-ભાષા readers

Measured instances: std 6 ch 4 is a લોકગીત with a second, complete poem appended in a yellow
"ગાઈએ" box; std 6's પૂરકવાચન unit is an anthology of four unrelated pieces (two poems, a travel
essay, a riddle set); std 8 ch 6 is a ચરિત્રલેખ carrying medieval પ્રભાતિયું verse inside the prose;
std 9 ch 22 and std 10 ch 18 "લઘુકાવ્યો" each print દુહા, મુક્તક and હાઈકુ by several poets under
one head with per-section credits; std 9 ch 23 પ્રેરક પ્રસંગો holds three પ્રસંગ under an umbrella
head; std 7 ch 4 is a વાર્તા whose second layer is straight માહિતી. When that happens:

1. `genre: "mixed"`, parts listed **dominant first**; the dominant part owns `guiding_question`.
2. **Load every matching profile**, dominant first.
3. **Cut each part under its own explanation unit** — the appended poem is cut કડી-wise even though
   the dominant part is cut ઘટના-wise. Do not flatten to one unit.
4. Apply each part's lens and avoid-list **to that part only**.
5. Agents 7 and 13 check that no part lost its essence to the dominant સ્વરૂપ's template.

**The two boxes that look alike and are not.** A poem printed in an appended reading box (std 6
ch 4's "ગાઈએ") is **reading matter** — it is a part of a mixed chapter and gets topics. A poem
printed *inside a numbered exercise* (std 8 ch 1's block 10, "કવિતા વાંચી… ✓ નિશાની કરો") is
**exercise matter** — it is one `exercise_inventory` item, it is never a topic, and Agent 10 answers
it. Decide by what the block asks the child to do, never by the presence of verse.

## What must be recorded in `01_meta.json`

`genre`, `genre_signals` (what each of A–D showed, and quote the intro box / કૃતિ-પરિચય line
verbatim if it named the form), `genre_confidence`, `teaching_lens`, `guiding_question` (derived
from THIS chapter, never copied from the profile), `explanation_unit`, `active_genre_profiles`,
plus the structural inventory (how many કડી / દુહા / શેર / પદ / ઘટના, and every ટેક occurrence with
whether its words changed) and the **સ્વાધ્યાય inventory** — `exercise_inventory[{group, items,
verbatim_heading}]`, every block found with its heading exactly as printed, so Agent 10 can answer
all of them and Agent 4 can confirm none was cut as a topic.
