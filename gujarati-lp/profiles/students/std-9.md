# Std 9 — learner profile and tutor tiers (ગુજરાતી, દ્વિતીય ભાષા)

Decides **who the std-9 reader is, which teaching method the plan must follow, and how the same
chapter is authored at three understanding levels** — સહાય / ધોરણ / પ્રગત.

Three facts frame everything below.

- **Tier changes authored prose only.** The phase-2 contract, the 32 root keys, the 31 topic keys,
  the id grammar, the objectives registry and its character-for-character mirror, and every
  verbatim rule are identical in all three variants. A tier that changes a key, an id, or an
  `original_chunk` is a bug, not a differentiation.
- **The ceiling never moves.** Bloom is never capped for સહાય. Every tier reaches analyze and
  evaluate; only the on-ramp differs. A સહાય plan that teaches less is a defect — it is the same
  learning in smaller, more concrete steps.
- **Std 9 is the hard switch.** The NEP/NCF Middle→Secondary boundary falls at 8→9. Std 6–8 was
  fluency and consolidation with unnamed craft; std 9 is named analysis, exam-mirroring સ્વાધ્યાય,
  and the heaviest single-year grammar increase in the whole span.

Sources: `PEDAGOGY.md` §1 (Std 9), §2 (tier model), §3 (the three std-9 prompt blocks), §4
(pipeline implications) — the pedagogy report is the authority for the method and the prompts;
`reference/corpus/std-9_inventory.md` is the measured authority for what the book actually
contains. Where the report describes the book and the inventory contradicts it, **the inventory
wins** and the difference is recorded in §1.4 below.

## 1. Who this learner is

### 1.1 Cognitive stage

Consolidating formal operations, roughly age 14. Hypothetico-deductive reasoning now runs on
**unfamiliar** content, not only on the familiar; systematic comparison of two things is available;
an argument can be examined as an argument. Attention block 15–20 minutes (chat equivalent:
longer explanation segments than std 8, one action per segment). Genuinely independent work is
about **30%** feasible.

The load-bearing caveat: **a large minority of the class still operates one tier below.** That is
exactly what the સહાય tier exists for — not a reduced syllabus, a concrete on-ramp to the same
canon.

### 1.2 L2 proficiency expectation

Gujarati is the **second language**, and the std-9 reader has typically had it since the primary
years in an English- or Hindi-medium school. The realistic CEFR track is **A2/A2+ at entry,
heading to B1 by the end of std 10 — not B2.** Assume a three-level spread inside every classroom;
at age ~15 only about 40–45% of L2 learners reach independent-user level anywhere.

Two consequences the authoring agents must hold:

- **Heritage-oral learners run listening/speaking about one level above reading/writing.** A child
  who converses fluently in Gujarati at home may still decode તળપદા and તત્સમ print slowly. Track
  the skills separately; never infer reading ability from talk.
- **The glossing bar sits lower than a first-language pack's.** More everyday Gujarati words
  genuinely stop an L2 child (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`). At std 9 this compounds: the book's own
  શબ્દ-સમજૂતી blocks are large and the dialect load is heavy (§1.4).

### 1.3 What changed vs std 8

| Dimension | Std 8 ("name it after you've met it") | Std 9 ("the analysis switch") |
|---|---|---|
| **Abstraction** | Interpretation, counterfactuals, moral in own words; single-line irony | Unstated themes **with evidence citing**; abstract/cultural essays; abstract lyric poetry; irony as a sustained genre; abstract-proposition debate becomes fair |
| **Text length** | Social/biographical prose, bhakti verse, સૉનેટ exposure; two-clause complex sentences | Full essays, આત્મકથાંશ, multi-page prose; multi-clause sentences, passives, nominalisation |
| **Craft vocabulary** | Structure-counting only (હાઈકુ 5-7-5, દુહા માત્રા feel); **no** device labels | The big jump: the named canon + genre (સાહિત્યપ્રકાર) labels + **7 અલંકાર** |
| **Grammar load** | Explicit-after-encounter rules, contrastive L1 notes | Heaviest single-year increase in the span; the full std-9 vyakaran canon |
| **Assessment shape** | Activity-first સ્વાધ્યાય | Exam-mirroring સ્વાધ્યાય; chapter-front apparatus taught as study equipment |
| **Book architecture** | Semester volumes with પુનરાવર્તન blocks | **Annual single volume, no revision blocks** — the pipeline must schedule its own spaced review |

The std-9 named canon (the ceiling — do not exceed it): સમાનાર્થી, વિરુદ્ધાર્થી, કોશક્રમ, જોડણી, લિંગ,
વચન, અનુગ/નામયોગી, સંધિ, વિશેષણ, ક્રિયાવિશેષણ, સંયોજક, વિરામચિહ્નો, સમાસ (5 types with વિગ્રહ),
શબ્દસમૂહ માટે એક શબ્દ, રૂઢિપ્રયોગ, કહેવત, અલંકાર (7: વર્ણાનુપ્રાસ, પ્રાસસાંકળી, ઉપમા, રૂપક, ઉત્પ્રેક્ષા,
વ્યતિરેક, અતિશયોક્તિ), કૃદંત (6 suffix signatures), નિપાત (4 types).

**Out of scope at every tier:** છંદ (std-10 only), voice transformation કર્તરિ→કર્મણિ→ભાવે→પ્રેરક
(std-10 signature), બહુવ્રીહિ/દ્વિગુ (out of scope entirely).

### 1.4 What the std-9 book actually contains (measured)

  i.e. **23 teaching chapters**, 4 વ્યાકરણ એકમો interleaved, 4 પૂરક વાચન units. **No પુનરાવર્તન

  text → the text → **શબ્દ-સમજૂતી** block with sub-heads (સમાનાર્થી/શબ્દાર્થ, વિરુદ્ધાર્થી, તળપદા શબ્દો,

  શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા is **teacher-addressed — not a child-facing exercise block**; Agent 10 must not
  answer it as one.
- **Measured સ્વાધ્યાય ladder is three rungs, not five.** Across all 23 literature chapters the
  printed banners are: the MCQ banner `પ્રશ્નની નીચે આપેલા વિકલ્પોમાંથી સાચો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરી ખરાની (✓) નિશાની કરો :`
  (23/23) → `નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના બે-ત્રણ વાક્યોમાં ઉત્તર લખો :` (23/23, with a `વાક્યમાં` singular variant) →
  a long-answer banner that is **`છ-સાત વાક્યોમાં` / `પાંચ-છ વાક્યોમાં` / `સવિસ્તાર`**, plus an
  occasional `કારણ આપો :`. **`એક વાક્યમાં` and `મુદ્દાસર` banners were not observed in std 9.** The
  report's four/five-step exam ladder is the *board answer grammar* and stays in the tutor prompts;
  the *authored* exercise deliverable follows the printed banners, inventoried per chapter by Agent
  1 (never assumed — brief §3.4). Note also that the book prints **સવિસ્તાર** where the report
  writes **સવિસ્તર**; `prompt_verbatim` copies the page.
- **Genre spread (priors, diagnose don't assume):** ઊર્મિકાવ્ય-ગીત dominates the verse (6 of the
  23 chapters, 7 with P1), plus છપ્પા (અખો), ગઝલ, સોનેટ, અછાંદસ/મુક્ત છંદ, લોકગીત and the mixed
  લઘુકાવ્યો page (દુહો, મુક્તક, હાઈકુ); prose runs હાસ્યનિબંધ, ચિંતનાત્મક નિબંધ, લલિત નિબંધ,
  નવલકથા-અંશ, લઘુકથા, પ્રસંગકથા, સંસ્મરણ/અનુભવ-કથન (અનૂદિત), પ્રવાસ-અનુભવકથન, પ્રેરક પ્રસંગ,
  હળવું એકાંકી, and in the પૂરક section ચરિત્રનિબંધ, પૌરાણિક કથા and a સંવાદ-લેખ. The book names its own
  પ્રકાર — one MCQ even tests it (`નવલકથાખંડ`), which is the analysis switch showing up in the
  assessment itself.
- **Dialect and archaic load is the std-9 difficulty signal.** મધ્યકાલીન ગુજરાતી in the છપ્પા (લેઈ,
  પહાણ, સનાન), sustained ગીર/સોરઠી બોલી in dialogue (`સે` for છે, હાલ્યો, ઓલીકોર), Parsi and Marwadi
  speech transcribed as printed, Saurashtra-flavoured તળપદું in a translated memoir (મે'નત, વરહ,
  એરુ). None of it is ever corrected — brief §3.1. It is glossed, and the gloss dial in §3 is the
  main thing tier changes at this standard.
- **Named devices appear in the book's own ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ bullets:** વર્ણાનુપ્રાસ, ઉપમા, યમક,
  વ્યતિરેક, સજીવારોપણ, વ્યંગ/વક્રોક્તિ. Teach a device where the chapter's apparatus names it and the
  line carries it; `[]` remains a correct `figures_of_speech` answer, and a device is never named
  for the field's sake.

## 2. Method mix for this std

The report's recommended mix for "the analysis switch":

- **Named grammar canon begins**, on the encounter → name → apply sequence: meet the form in a line
  of *this* chapter, attempt it, then define it, then hunt more instances. The canon in §1.3 is the
  ceiling.
- **Genre naming and analysis.** Every chapter carries a પ્રકાર tag; "આ કૃતિનો સાહિત્યપ્રકાર જણાવો"
  is now a fair question. (The pack already carries this as root `genre` — the tag is taught, not
  invented.)
- **Exam-mirroring સ્વાધ્યાય escalation:** objective → એક વાક્યમાં → બે-ત્રણ વાક્યમાં → મુદ્દાસર →
  સવિસ્તર as the *answer-length grammar the student is drilled in*, reconciled with the printed
  banners per §1.4. The chapter-front apparatus (author bio, synopsis, તળપદા glossary) is taught as
  study equipment.
- **Unstated-theme extraction with evidence citing** ("કઈ પંક્તિઓ બતાવે છે…?"); irony readable as a
  sustained genre.
- **Error analysis of the student's own writing;** full explicit grammar including exceptions and
  register.
- **લેખન canon:** અનુચ્છેદ (~120 words), વાર્તાલેખન from points, formal પત્ર/અરજી, જાહેરાતલેખન;
  ગદ્યાર્થગ્રહણ/પદ્યાર્થગ્રહણ (unseen comprehension) practice begins.
- **Spaced review is the pipeline's job at std 9** — the annual volume drops પુનરાવર્તન blocks, so
  recycling of earlier chapters' vocabulary and devices has to be scheduled deliberately.

### Top-5 DO / DON'T (report §1, Std 9)

| # | DO | DON'T |
|---|----|----|
| 1 | Teach every named device from a line in the chapter first, then define, then hunt more instances (encounter → name → apply). | Don't teach devices from generic invented examples, and don't let the model supply "traditional" examples from memory. |
| 2 | Drill the four-step answer-length ladder (1 વાક્ય / 2-3 વાક્ય / મુદ્દાસર / સવિસ્તર) explicitly — it is the exam grammar. | Don't stay on activity-style tasks alone; સ્વાધ્યાય format shifts to exam-mirroring here. |
| 3 | Require text evidence for every interpretive claim ("કઈ પંક્તિ પરથી કહો છો?"). | Don't accept or model theme claims without quoted lines. |
| 4 | Run error logs and self-assessment against criteria (~30% independent time); analyse the student's own sentences for વાક્યશુદ્ધિ. | Don't exceed the Std 9 canon — છંદ, બહુવ્રીહિ/દ્વિગુ, and voice transformation are NOT Std 9 scope. |
| 5 | Use the chapter apparatus (author, પ્રકાર, synopsis, તળપદા) as the standard lesson opener. | Don't drop the concrete anchor entirely — ~half the class still needs it (સહાય/ધોરણ variants keep it). |

Two pack rules that DO/DON'T #1 and #3 turn into hard gates already in force: no invented અલંકાર
and no invented poet detail (`reference/no_hallucination_policy.md`), and `figures_of_speech.lines`
must quote the topic's own `original_chunk` exactly (Agent 13 blocks otherwise).

### Cultural anchoring for this standard

Every `real_life_example` (and any example the tutor improvises) is anchored in Gujarat's own
world — the bank is `reference/gujarat_cultural_anchors.md`, already injected with every call
(§5.2). Std 9 opens the age-ladder's **civic reach**: સ્થળાંતર, ઉદ્યોગ, પર્યાવરણ and the state's
lifelines are fair anchors now — **provided the abstraction arrives through something seen**, a
single concrete picture, never an editorial. The std-9 form of that rule is exact: **one face, or
one day, standing for the whole process.** A cousin leaving is સ્થળાંતર; a canal filling for the
first time is a water scheme; the salt on the household થાળી is one અગરિયા's whole summer. The
student who can now say *which line shows it* (§2 DO #3) should likewise be able to point at the
part of the anchor that carries the feeling.

**Choosing one.** Name the scene's ભાવ, then take the row.

| Scene's ભાવ | The std-9 anchor that carries it |
|---|---|
| દૂરની વસ્તુ ઘર સુધી પહોંચી | નહેરમાં પહેલી વાર પાણી આવ્યું તે દિવસ, ને શેઢે ઊભું આખું ગામ |
| ખોટ, ને છતાં જવું પડે | કામ માટે સુરત ગયેલો પિતરાઈ; બસ ઊપડી ત્યાં સુધી સ્ટૅન્ડ પર ઊભાં રહેલાં બા |
| રોજની સાદી વસ્તુ પાછળની અઘરી મહેનત | તડકે ચમકતા મીઠાના ઢગ, ને સાંજે ઘરની થાળીમાં એ જ મીઠું |
| ચાલતું ઘર | ઘંટડી સંભળાય ને ધણ સાથે ઋતુ પ્રમાણે નીકળી પડતું માલધારી કુટુંબ |
| પરંપરા સાંભળનાર હોય ત્યાં સુધી જીવે | ડાયરાની મોડી રાત — હોંકારો ન દે તો વાત આગળ ન ચાલે |
| બે પેઢી વચ્ચેની બોલી | દાદીની કહેવત, ને પૌત્રના મોઢે તેનું અંગ્રેજી ભાષાંતર |

The standing rules do not move: exactly ONE example per scene, 55–90 words, inside the child's own
experience; the anchor illustrates the text's FEELING and never adds a claim about the text, the
poet, or the chapter. Rotate domains across a chapter, and across the book show the whole state
and all its communities — never one slice, never a costume, never
"Gujarati = businessman".

**The std-9 failure mode: the editorial.** The civic reach is granted this year, and the picture
promptly evaporates into an issue statement. One sentence out of the middle of an example:

- ✅ `બસ ઊપડી ત્યાં સુધી બા સ્ટૅન્ડ પર ઊભાં રહ્યાં; ઘરમાં હવે એક ખાટલો વધારે છે.`
- ❌ `રોજગારી માટે ગામડાંમાંથી શહેરો તરફ થતું સ્થળાંતર આજની એક મોટી સમસ્યા છે.`

The ❌ half fails for a nameable reason: there is nothing in it to point at. It hands the student a
conclusion to receive, when the whole std-9 habit being built is that every interpretive claim is
backed by something you can put a finger on (§2 DO #3). An anchor with no picture inside it trains
the opposite habit.

Tier gradient: **સહાય** stays with the bank's most familiar, most concrete anchors;
**ધોરણ** the everyday spread with the civic picture where the text asks for it;
**પ્રગત** may carry the bank's less common anchors (અગરિયાના મીઠાના પાટા, ઋતુ પ્રમાણે ફરતું
માલધારીનું ધણ) provided they need no glossary.

## 3. The three tiers

Three tiers, named in Gujarati, shared by every generator and the tutor runtime.

| Tier | Meaning | Expected population |
|---|---|---|
| **સહાય** (Support) | below-grade understanding | ~15–25% |
| **ધોરણ** (Core) | at-grade | ~60–80% |
| **પ્રગત** (Advanced) | above-grade | ~5–15% |

**Assignment rule — pre-test cut scores.** A short unit pre-test decides the tier:

| Pre-test score | Tier | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| **≥80–85%** | પ્રગત | compacting: skip mastered blocks, go to extension |
| **~50–80%** | ધોરણ | the grade-level ladder as written |
| **<50%, or missing prerequisites** | સહાય | the same canon on a concrete on-ramp |

Re-sort **per unit, or every 4–8 weeks**. Tiers are flexible readiness states, not labels, and
**tier ≠ intelligence**: the WIDA warning applies — a language tier must never cap cognitive
demand.

**FIXED across tiers:** the chapter, the learning outcome (અધ્યયન નિષ્પત્તિ), the big idea, the
assessment target, and access to higher-order thinking. **Every tier reaches analyze/evaluate — the
on-ramp differs, never the ceiling.**

**Authoring order: write the પ્રગત variant first, then scaffold down.** This is the mechanism that
prevents સહાય from silently becoming "less learning" instead of "more support".

**VARIES:** everything in the table below (Tomlinson's Equalizer dials — concrete↔abstract,
simple↔complex, structured↔open, dependent↔independent, small↔great inference leap), instantiated
here for std 9.

| Dimension | સહાય (Support) | ધોરણ (Core) | પ્રગત (Advanced) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vocabulary load** | 2–3 new words per lesson on the full 8-step routine (definition, 2 examples, 2 non-examples, use, spaced review) + picture/L1 support; **also gloss the everyday polysemous words the text assumes** | 2–3 new words per lesson in depth (8–10/week), academic (Tier-2) words prioritized; તળપદા glossed | Same core words + morphology and word-family extension (-નાર, -વાળું, -પણું/-તા, અ-/બિન-/ગેર-, compounds); rare/તત્સમ words as stretch, decomposed as સંધિ/સમાસ |
| **Sentence length of explanations** | ≤12 words per sentence, simple + compound only, one idea per sentence; say–show–do redundancy | Grade cap: multi-clause acceptable, reported speech and passives allowed; say once, check once | May run one notch above the grade cap; denser academic register is acceptable at std 9 |
| **Gloss density** | Every low-frequency, તળપદા, તત્સમ and archaic word gets an inline appositive gloss **plus** an English equivalent ("વ્યથા — એટલે દુઃખ, sorrow"); cumulative bilingual glossary, re-quizzed | તળપદા/તત્સમ/new words glossed inline in Gujarati; the student attempts inference first on roughly half; English gloss on request | Minimal: the student infers, you confirm; તત્સમ words get bonus structural analysis; end-glossary only |
| **Abstraction level** | Concrete end of every dial: story-level material, familiar contexts, worked templates; abstraction always pre-chewed into a chart or numbered steps | Concrete anchor first, then the grade-level abstraction extracted with guidance | May open **from** the abstraction ("આ નિબંધનો કેન્દ્રીય પ્રશ્ન શું છે?") and demand grounding in lines; far transfer and cross-text links |
| **Example concreteness** | 3–4 worked examples from familiar Gujarat contexts (મેળો, રસોડું, શેરી ક્રિકેટ, ઉત્તરાયણ) **before** any practice; identification steps shown ("પગલું 1: બે પદ છૂટાં પાડો…") | 1–2 worked examples → example-problem pair → independent problem | 1 model example, then self-derivation; non-examples and boundary cases ("ઉપમા છે કે ઉત્પ્રેક્ષા? માર્કર શોધો — 'જાણે' છે?") |
| **Question Bloom range & form** | **Full Bloom range, reached through structured concrete on-ramps.** Forms: point/show → this-or-that → yes/no → simple wh-; anything harder is decomposed into a chain; answers may be recast into full form. Evidence-citing taught as a mechanical step | Wh- → why/how → prediction; the std-9 ladder of §2 applies as written; interpretive claims require cited evidence | Why/how → embedded/modal → open evaluation and device-effect ("આ રૂપક કાઢી નાખો તો પંક્તિ શું ગુમાવે?"); minimal framing, far inference leaps; abstract-proposition debate is fair |
| **Pacing** | 3–5 min chunks (chat: 1–2 short paragraphs), then an action; one micro-step per turn; comprehension check every turn; **2–3** new interacting elements max; extended I-do/We-do | 10/2 rhythm: explain a grade-length chunk, then the learner does something; **≤4** new interacting elements; I-do → We-do → You-do; 15–20 min segments | Probe first and skip what is known (compacting); longer uninterrupted segments; may enter at You-do; ~40% self-paced independent work with a rubric |
| **Media / support** | Visual + audio support by default (pictures, audio-assisted repeated reading); sentence frames with 1 slot | Visuals for key concepts; frames with 2–3 slots, retired as proficiency rises | Text-first; media only where the form demands it (hearing a ગઝલ's rhythm); no frames |
| **Translanguaging** | Generous: concept and grammar explanation in English allowed, production always in Gujarati; explicit L1 contrast notes | English for grammar explanations and quick equivalents; discussion drifts back to Gujarati | Gujarati throughout; English only if the student asks |
| **Feedback style** | Flag → name with the proper term **glossed** ("આ 'જોડણી'ની ભૂલ છે — spelling") → correct form → one retry on the same category; one category per task; the tutor keeps the error log **for** the student and reads it back | Flag → name the category with the proper term (જોડણી, વિભક્તિ/અનુગ, કાળ, વાક્યરચના, અલંકાર-ઓળખ) → correct form → retry; then **the student** logs the error | Self-correction first; precise terminology; discuss the error's *pattern* across the student's log; push register — recast good answers into board-topper phrasing and analyse the difference |

Two std-9 constants across all three tiers:

- **Scope discipline.** The named canon of §1.3 is the same for સહાય and પ્રગત alike. પ્રગત does not
  get છંદ early; સહાય does not get a shortened canon.
- **The 2-attempt rule.** No answer before an attempt; after two failed attempts, explain directly,
  then one retry. What differs is the size of an "attempt" — a frame-filled sentence at સહાય, a
  full draft at પ્રગત.

## 4. The three tutor system prompts

Paste one block as the system prompt for the tutor session. The chapter text, the apparatus
(લેખક/કવિ-પરિચય, શબ્દ-સમજૂતી, ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ) and the સ્વાધ્યાય must be pasted **verbatim** into the
context — these prompts assume it. Because prompt-level difficulty control decays within about
**9 turns**, re-inject the block (or at minimum its REGISTER / QUESTIONING / PACING fields) with
every generation call, and pin difficulty **per exercise block, not per session**. The blocks are
the report's, **plus one CULTURAL ANCHORING line added to each by this pack** (see §2's cultural
anchoring subsection) — the only addition.

### Std 9 × સહાય

```
ROLE: You are a structured, encouraging Gujarati (second language) tutor for a GSEB Std 9 student below grade level (સહાય tier). Std 9 introduces named grammar and exam formats; your student needs the SAME canon in smaller, concrete steps — never a reduced syllabus.

SOURCE FIDELITY: Teach only from the provided chapter text, apparatus (author bio, synopsis, તળપદા glossary), and સ્વાધ્યાય. Quote lines verbatim. Never state poet facts, meanings, or અલંકાર labels from memory — only what the provided material contains.

SCRIPT: Gujarati in Gujarati script only (U+0A80–U+0AFF); no Devanagari, no romanized Gujarati; require Gujarati-script answers.

REGISTER: Clear simple Gujarati; English freely for explaining grammar concepts and contrasts; production in Gujarati. Sentences ≤12 words in explanations.

EXPLANATION: 2–3 sentences then action. Always open with the chapter apparatus, simplified: who wrote it, what happens (3-line synopsis in easy Gujarati), 3 તળપદા words pre-taught.

GLOSSING: Dense: every hard/તળપદા/તત્સમ word glossed inline Gujarati + English; keep a cumulative glossary and re-quiz.

EXAMPLES: For every named grammar item (સમાસ, કૃદંત, નિપાત, અલંકાર…): 3 worked examples from the chapter with the identification steps SHOWN ("પગલું 1: બે પદ છૂટાં પાડો…"), then a template the student fills.

CULTURAL ANCHORING: Anchor every example in Gujarat's own world per reference/gujarat_cultural_anchors.md, using only its most familiar, most concrete anchors (ઉત્તરાયણનું ધાબું, નવરાત્રિનું શેરી-કૂંડાળું, દિવાળીની કોડિયાંની હાર, બસ-ડેપોનું પાટિયું) — one seen day, with something in the picture the student can point at. The anchor shows the text's feeling only; it never adds facts about the text, the poet, or the chapter.

QUESTIONING: Exam ladder scaffolded: MCQ and જોડકાં first; એક વાક્યમાં ઉત્તર with a sentence frame; બે-ત્રણ વાક્યમાં with a bullet skeleton you provide; સવિસ્તર attempted ONLY as guided assembly (you give the મુદ્દા, student expands each into a sentence). Evidence-citing taught as a mechanical step: "જવાબ પછી લખો: 'આ પંક્તિ પરથી: …'" All Bloom levels reached — through structure, never skipped.

FEEDBACK: Flag → name with the proper term now, glossed ("આ 'જોડણી'ની ભૂલ છે — spelling") → correct form → one retry on the same category. One category per task; keep an error log FOR the student and read it back at session start.

PACING & ANSWERS: 2-attempt rule then direct explanation; 2–3 new elements max per segment; every session ends with 2 MCQs + 1 એક-વાક્ય item as exit check (board-format habit, low stakes).
```

### Std 9 × ધોરણ

```
ROLE: You are a Gujarati (second language) tutor for a GSEB Std 9 student at grade level (ધોરણ tier). This is the analysis year: named devices, genre labels, exam answer formats.

SOURCE FIDELITY: Only the provided chapter material and apparatus; every question stem quotes the exact line. Never supply verses, poet facts, meanings, or device labels from memory; treat unprovided literary claims as forbidden. Grammar examples come from the chapter.

SCRIPT: Gujarati script only (U+0A80–U+0AFF); no Devanagari; no romanization. Require Gujarati-script answers.

REGISTER: Academic-but-warm Gujarati; English for grammar explanations where it speeds understanding. Multi-clause sentences acceptable.

EXPLANATION: 3–4 sentences then action. Standard lesson opener = chapter apparatus: author, સાહિત્યપ્રકાર (genre tag), synopsis, તળપદા glossary — treat these as study equipment the student must internalize.

GLOSSING: Inline Gujarati glosses for તળપદા/તત્સમ words; the student should attempt inference first on roughly half of them.

EXAMPLES: Encounter → name → apply, for every named item: find the ઉપમા in the quoted line, define ઉપમા, then hunt one more in the chapter. Scope discipline: Std 9 canon only (સંધિ, સમાસ-5, કૃદંત-6, નિપાત-4, વિરામચિહ્નો, અલંકાર-7, રૂઢિપ્રયોગ, કહેવત…). NO છંદ, NO voice transformation, NO બહુવ્રીહિ/દ્વિગુ.

CULTURAL ANCHORING: Anchor examples in Gujarat's own world per reference/gujarat_cultural_anchors.md — the everyday spread first, and civic Gujarat where the text asks for it, always as one face or one day standing for the whole process — never a statistic (નહેરમાં પહેલી વાર પાણી આવ્યાનો દિવસ; કામ માટે સુરત ગયેલો પિતરાઈ, ને બસ ઊપડી ત્યાં સુધી ઊભાં રહેલાં બા) — drawn from across the state's regions and communities. Rotate domains; the anchor illustrates the feeling and never adds facts about the text, the poet, or the chapter.

QUESTIONING: Run the four-step exam ladder explicitly and tell the student which step they're on: MCQ/objective → એક વાક્યમાં → બે-ત્રણ વાક્યમાં → મુદ્દાસર/સવિસ્તર. Interpretive questions require cited evidence: "તમારો દાવો + 'કઈ પંક્તિ પરથી?'" Unstated-theme questions are now fair; irony may be examined ("લેખક ખરેખર વખાણ કરે છે કે કટાક્ષ?").

WRITING: અનુચ્છેદ (~120 words), formal પત્ર/અરજી, વાર્તાલેખન from points — one target feature declared per task; correct only that feature plus content.

FEEDBACK: Flag → name the category with the proper term (જોડણી, વિભક્તિ/અનુગ, કાળ, વાક્યરચના, અલંકાર-ઓળખ) → correct form → retry. Then have the student log the error themselves.

PACING & ANSWERS: 2-attempt rule; segments 15–20 min equivalent; ~30% of tasks independent with rubric-based self-assessment before your review.
```

### Std 9 × પ્રગત

```
ROLE: You are a Gujarati (second language) tutor for a GSEB Std 9 student above grade level (પ્રગત tier) — treat them as an apprentice literary reader.

SOURCE FIDELITY: Provided chapter material only; verbatim quotation; zero literary claims from memory (no poets, no meanings, no device labels beyond what the material supports). If the student asks something outside the provided text, say it needs checking.

SCRIPT: Gujarati script only (U+0A80–U+0AFF); no Devanagari; no romanization.

REGISTER: Full academic Gujarati; English only if requested. Model the register of સવિસ્તર answers in your own prose.

EXPLANATION: Probe-first: diagnostic question, skip the mastered, go deep on the rest. You may open from the abstraction ("આ નિબંધનો કેન્દ્રીય પ્રશ્ન શું છે?") and ask the student to ground it in lines.

GLOSSING: Student infers all glosses first; you confirm; extend તત્સમ words into their સંધિ/સમાસ structure as a bonus analysis ("'સપ્તર્ષિ' — કઈ સંધિ?").

EXAMPLES: One model analysis, then self-derivation; non-examples and boundary cases ("આ પંક્તિમાં ઉપમા છે કે ઉત્પ્રેક્ષા? માર્કર શોધો — 'જાણે' છે?").

CULTURAL ANCHORING: Anchor examples in Gujarat's own world per reference/gujarat_cultural_anchors.md; this student can handle the bank's less common and civic anchors (તડકે ચમકતા અગરિયાના મીઠાના પાટા, ઋતુ પ્રમાણે ફરતું માલધારીનું ધણ, ડાયરાનો મોડી રાતનો હોંકારો) so long as they need no glossary and arrive through something the student has seen. The anchor illustrates the feeling only — never a fact about the text, the poet, or the chapter.

QUESTIONING: Start at analysis: unstated theme with multi-line evidence; device-effect ("આ રૂપક કાઢી નાખો તો પંક્તિ શું ગુમાવે?"); genre reasoning ("આને રેખાચિત્ર કેમ કહેવાય, વાર્તા કેમ નહિ?"); cross-question the text ("લેખકની દલીલમાં નબળી કડી ક્યાં છે?"). Abstract-proposition debate is now fair. Exam ladder runs top-down: the student drafts સવિસ્તર answers unaided, then compresses them to મુદ્દાસર (summary discipline).

WRITING: Full-length અનુચ્છેદ/પત્ર/વાર્તા with self-assessment against criteria BEFORE your feedback; error log maintained by the student; occasional "examiner mode": the student writes marking points for their own answer.

FEEDBACK: Self-correction first; precise terminology; discuss the error's pattern across their log, not just the instance. Push register: recast good answers into board-topper phrasing and analyze the difference.

PACING & ANSWERS: Fast; long independent blocks (~40%); 2-attempt rule, but attempts are full drafts. Extension: connect this chapter to a previously provided chapter (only if both texts are in context) for comparison practice.
```

## 5. Pipeline wiring

### 5.1 Where the tier enters

- **Optional input at Phase 0.** The orchestrator takes `tier ∈ {સહાય, ધોરણ, પ્રગત}` alongside the
  chapter. **Default is ધોરણ** — a run with no tier is a ધોરણ run, and the validation report header
  says so explicitly rather than leaving it blank.
- **The tier is a run parameter, not a JSON field.** It is never written into any emitted
  deliverable: the root object has exactly the 32 keys of brief §2.1 and a topic has exactly 31.
  Record the tier in the run log and in `validation_report.md`'s header line, next to
  `સ્વરૂપ: <genre> (confidence)`.
- **One run authors one tier.** The pack fixes `output9/chNN/` as the output directory (brief
  §3.7); do not invent a per-tier sub-directory. If all three variants of a chapter are wanted,
  that is three runs — and per §3, author **પ્રગત first**, then scaffold down.

### 5.2 Agent 12 re-injects the descriptor every call

Prompt-level difficulty control **drifts within about 9 turns**. Therefore Agent 12 (and Agent 16,
and Agent 10 when it drafts model answers) must re-inject the std-9 + tier descriptor block — the
row set from §3 plus the scope ceiling from §1.3 — **in every generation call**, not once per
session. Difficulty is pinned **per authored block** (per topic, per exercise item), never once per
chapter. Alongside it, per report §4.8, pass 1–2 required items from the curated Gujarat examples
bank (ઉત્તરાયણ, નવરાત્રિ, મેળો, શેરી ક્રિકેટ, ST બસ, ખેતર-કૂવો) — **સહાય examples draw exclusively
from that bank.**

### 5.3 Which fields tighten per tier

Every band below is the contract band from brief §2.2. **The tier selects a sub-range inside the
band — it never widens or leaves it.** An out-of-band value is a QC failure at Agent 13 regardless
of tier.

| Field (contract band) | સહાય | ધોરણ | પ્રગત |
|---|---|---|---|
| `explanation` (55–90 words) | **low end, 55–70**; sentences ≤12 words; one idea per sentence; the named device explained in steps | 65–85; multi-clause allowed | 75–90; academic register; may open from the abstraction |
| `real_life_example` (55–90 words, exactly ONE Indian anchor) | low end; anchor drawn from the Gujarat examples bank; may end on a this-or-that question | mid band; anchor inside the child's world | may use an unfamiliar context; ends on an evaluative question |
| `key_terms` (3–6, `"શબ્દ — અર્થ"`) | **5–6**, and the bar for "worth glossing" sits lowest — everyday polysemous words count | 4–5, તળપદા/તત્સમ first | **3–4**, only what context cannot yield; તત્સમ entries may carry સંધિ/સમાસ structure |
| `concept_bullets` / `important_points` (3–4, keyword-first) | 4 lines, fuller glosses after the keyword | 3–4 | 3 lines, denser |
| `recall_questions` (2–3 per topic, Bloom-laddered) | **3 questions; forms are point/choice/yes-no/simple wh-, and a hard question is decomposed into a chain — but the ladder still reaches analyze** (via a chart or a given skeleton). At least one prompt carries the mechanical evidence step "આ પંક્તિ પરથી: …" | 2–3, remember → understand → analyze; interpretive answers cite a line | 2–3 starting at understand; at least one analyze/evaluate with multi-line evidence or device-effect |
| `brief_summary` / `summary` / `detailed_summary` (1 / 2–3 / 4–6 sentences, strictly increasing) | counts unchanged; sentences short and concrete | as written | counts unchanged; register denser |
| module `difficult_words` (5–10, fresh example sentence) | **8–10** | 6–8 | 5–6, plus word-family extension in the meaning field |
| `estimated_exchanges` (string int) | higher — more, smaller turns (e.g. `"6"`) | e.g. `"4"` | lower — longer student turns (e.g. `"3"`) |
| `figures_of_speech`, `rhyme_scheme` | **identical at every tier.** Same devices, same exact quotes; only the explanatory `note` gets simpler or denser | | |

Fields that do **not** move with tier: `topic.difficulty` (it describes the text, not the reader),
`word_count`, `objective_text` (owned by Agent 2, mirrored character-for-character), media count
(one image per reading scene, ≤1 `2d_tool` per chapter — tier-keyed picture/audio/frame support is
a **tutor-runtime** affordance, not a plan-shape change), and every id.

### 5.4 What never varies by tier

- The **phase-2 contract**: 32 root keys, 31 topic keys, the closed `topic_type` enum, the concept
  layer, `strand_to_objective_map`, the objectives registry and its inline mirror.
- **Ids**: `M{m}.S{s}.T{t}.C{c}` with chapter-continuous `c`, `.RQ{n}` (never `.SR{n}`),
  `MEDIA_ID_RE`. Frozen at Agent 4, renumbered only by Agent 14.
- **Verbatim**: `original_chunk` is the transcribed page — complete Gujarati script, nothing
  dropped, paraphrased, transliterated or "corrected". Archaic and બોલી forms (લેઈ, પહાણ, સે,
  મે'નત) stay; punctuation is the printed `.`, never `।`; no nukta. A tier may change how a form is
  *explained*, never what is printed.
- **Exercise coverage**: every inventoried સ્વાધ્યાય block answered, `prompt_verbatim` exact,
  everything skill-tagged and mapped. સ્વાધ્યાય is never a topic at any tier.
- **The no-hallucination floor** and the Phase-8 LP2 gate: zero `validation_errors`, at every tier.
- **Ids and DB values remain provisional** — `chapter_id` is the provisional form
  `gseb_eng_gujarati9_ch{N}` and the medium slot is **unverified** (brief §2.3, VERIFY-1). A wrong
  medium uploads clean and mis-files the plan. Nothing in this file resolves that; the board
  profile and `naming_conventions.md` do, when verification lands.

### 5.5 QC hooks

Agent 13 already blocks on band violations; add the tier as a **reported** (§E–G, non-blocking)
line: declared tier, measured median `explanation` length, `key_terms` count distribution, and the
recall Bloom mix — so a drifted run is visible rather than silently "in band". A સહાય run whose
recall questions never reach analyze is the failure this line is meant to catch, and it routes to
Agent 12.
