# Std 10 — learner profile and tutor tiers (ગુજરાતી, દ્વિતીય ભાષા)

Decides **who the std-10 reader is, which teaching method the plan must follow, and how the same
chapter is authored at three understanding levels** — સહાય / ધોરણ / પ્રગત.

Three facts frame everything below.

- **Tier changes authored prose only.** The phase-2 contract, the 32 root keys, the 31 topic keys,
  the id grammar, the objectives registry and its character-for-character mirror, and every
  verbatim rule are identical in all three variants. A tier that changes a key, an id, or an
  `original_chunk` is a bug, not a differentiation.
- **The ceiling never moves.** Bloom is never capped for સહાય. Every tier reaches analyze and
  evaluate; only the on-ramp differs. A સહાય plan that teaches less is a defect — it is the same
  learning in smaller, more concrete steps. At std 10 the temptation is specific and named in the
  report: *don't cap સહાય-tier students at objective items only — they must also practise
  સવિસ્તર, with scaffolds.*
- **Std 10 is board-mirror mastery.** Std 9 flipped the analysis switch; std 10 consolidates and
  applies it under time pressure. The SSC paper is the frame for method, question form and answer
  length — and the measured book agrees: 17 of its 18 chapters print the same four-rung answer
  ladder (§1.4; ch-05 prints a three-rung variant).

Sources: `PEDAGOGY.md` §1 (Std 10), §2 (tier model), §3 (the three std-10 prompt blocks), §4
(pipeline implications) — the pedagogy report is the authority for the method and the prompts;
`reference/corpus/std-10_inventory.md` is the measured authority for what the book actually
contains. Where the report describes the book and the inventory contradicts it, **the inventory
wins** and the difference is recorded in §1.4 and §6.

## 1. Who this learner is

### 1.1 Cognitive stage

Formal-operational for most tasks, roughly age 15. Abstraction is available without a concrete
prop; multi-cause analysis is available; and the new capacity that std 9 did not yet have reliably
is **evaluating an argument's form separately from its content** — the reader can say *the writer
is right about X but the reason given is weak*. Two texts can be held side by side and compared.

Attention and independence carry over from std 9 and stretch a little: 15–20 minute blocks, and
**~30–40% genuinely independent work** is feasible (the report's પ્રગત tier runs at the top of
that range). The std-9 caveat still holds and is the reason the સહાય tier exists: a real minority
of the class operates a tier below, and at std 10 that minority is the one with marks at stake.

### 1.2 L2 proficiency expectation

Gujarati is the **second language**. The realistic CEFR endpoint for an SL started early is
**B1 by the end of std 10 — not B2**; the std-10 reader enters at roughly A2+/B1. Assume the
three-level spread inside every classroom: at age ~15 only about 40–45% of L2 learners reach
independent-user level anywhere.

Three consequences the authoring agents must hold at this standard:

- **Heritage-oral learners run listening/speaking about one level above reading/writing.** A child
  who talks fluently at home may still decode તત્સમ print slowly. Track the skills separately;
  never infer reading ability from talk.
- **The glossing bar sits lower than a first-language pack's** (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`). More
  everyday Gujarati words genuinely stop an L2 child.
- **Std 10's specific reading load is the conjunct.** The report's own words: text length is
  comparable to std 9, but the register is denser તત્સમ/formal and **જોડાક્ષર-heavy** — so
  conjunct decoding is pre-taught for weak readers (સ્ + ત = સ્ત). This is the std-10 access
  problem, and it is a decoding problem, not a comprehension problem.

### 1.3 What changed vs std 9

| Dimension | Std 9 ("the analysis switch") | Std 10 ("board-mirror mastery") |
|---|---|---|
| **Abstraction** | Unstated themes with evidence citing; abstract essays; abstract-proposition debate | **Evaluation and transfer** ("શું વક્તા સાચા છે? આજે આ ક્યાં દેખાય છે?"); **two-text comparison**; satire/વ્યંગ readable as a genre |
| **Text length** | Full essays, આત્મકથાંશ, multi-page prose | Comparable length, **denser તત્સમ/formal register, જોડાક્ષર-heavy** — pre-teach conjunct decoding for weak readers |
| **Craft vocabulary** | The named canon + genre labels + 7 અલંકાર | **+ છંદ (std-10 only)**, **+ 5 more અલંકાર**, + voice-transformation terms |
| **Grammar load** | Heaviest single-year increase in the span | **Consolidation and application under time pressure** rather than many new categories |
| **Assessment shape** | Exam-mirroring સ્વાધ્યાય | Everything mirrors the SSC paper; **every lesson carries a board-format exit item** |
| **Book architecture** | Annual single volume, no પુનરાવર્તન | Annual single volume, **six interleaved વ્યાકરણ એકમો** carrying the grammar and લેખન canon (§1.4) |

The std-10 additions (the report's canon — read with §1.4's measured caveat):

- **છંદ, std-10 only:** 7 અક્ષરમેળ (અનુષ્ટુપ, ઇન્દ્રવજ્રા, ઉપજાતિ, વંશસ્થ, મંદાક્રાન્તા, શિખરિણી,
  હરિણી) + 3 માત્રામેળ (દોહરો, સોરઠો, ચોપાઈ), tested as identify-from-a-line via લઘુ-ગુરુ / માત્રા
  counting.
- **અલંકાર expands** to 4 શબ્દાલંકાર + 8 અર્થાલંકાર; new against std 9: અનન્વય, શ્લેષ, વ્યાજસ્તુતિ,
  સજીવારોપણ, યમક.
- **Voice transformation** કર્તરિ → કર્મણિ → ભાવે → પ્રેરક — the std-10 signature grammar skill.
- **લેખન** adds સંક્ષેપીકરણ and સંવાદલેખન on top of the 9–10 canon (નિબંધ, ગદ્યાર્થ/પદ્યાર્થગ્રહણ,
  પત્ર, વિચારવિસ્તાર, વાર્તા, અહેવાલ).

**Board frame (report §1, flagged [thin]):** SSC 80 external + 20 internal, ~30% objective items,
four sections — વિભાગ A ગદ્ય (20), B પદ્ય (20), C વ્યાકરણ (20), D લેખન (20). The SL-specific mark
split must be verified against the official "10th Gujarati (S.L.) Blueprint" PDF on gseb.org
before it is hard-coded anywhere in the pack. **Out of scope at every tier:** બહુવ્રીહિ/દ્વિગુ.

### 1.4 What the std-10 book actually contains (measured)

From `reference/corpus/std-10_inventory.md`. The GSEB PDFs are **image-only**: pages were rendered
(`pdftoppm`) and read, so every verbatim string is a **transcription**, never a text-layer
extraction. Coverage: **the inventory is complete — 29 of 29 units, every chapter's સ્વાધ્યાય and
end-matter read** (ch-01, ch-08, ch-13 and ch-16 were the four read first, in the sampling pass).
Everything about સ્વાધ્યાય below is measured over all 18 literature chapters.

- **29 units:** 18 teaching chapters, **6 વ્યાકરણ એકમો** interleaved (after ch 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18),
  and **5 પૂરક વાચન** units, plus front matter. No પુનરાવર્તન blocks — the pipeline schedules its
  own spaced review, exactly as at std 9.
- **The વ્યાકરણ એકમો are the measured grammar syllabus:** vy-1 સમાનાર્થી · વિરુદ્ધાર્થી · જોડણી;
  vy-2 સંધિ · સમાસ; vy-3 રૂઢિપ્રયોગ · કહેવત; vy-4 **વાક્યપ્રકાર : કર્તરિ, કર્મણિ, ભાવે, પ્રેરક** ·
  વિશેષણ (the longest unit, 10 printed pages); vy-5 વિરામચિહ્નો · વાર્તાલેખન; vy-6 અહેવાલલેખન ·
  સંક્ષેપીકરણ · **અર્થવિસ્તાર** · નિબંધલેખન. This **confirms voice transformation as the std-10
  signature skill** and confirms સંક્ષેપીકરણ. It also prints **અર્થવિસ્તાર** where the report writes
  વિચારવિસ્તાર, and **સંવાદલેખન was not observed** in any વ્યાકરણ એકમ.
- **છંદ and અલંકાર are NOT printed as વ્યાકરણ એકમો.** The only in-book છંદ evidence is a chapter
  intro naming its own metre — ch-07 જીવમાં જીવ આવ્યો: "…આ સોનેટમાં **મંદાક્રાન્તા** છંદમાં પ્રગટ
  કર્યો છે". Craft otherwise lives in the per-chapter **ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ** commentary (personification
  in ch-16, વ્યંગ and code-mixing in ch-08, ધ્રુવપંક્તિ and લોકપરંપરા in ch-13). **Rule used here:
  name a છંદ or an અલંકાર only where the chapter's own printed apparatus names it, or where a
  human-verified registry entry covers it — never from model memory.** The report's own DON'T is
  blunt about this, and two source discrepancies are already logged (મંદાક્રાન્તા, હરિણી counts).
- **The measured સ્વાધ્યાય ladder is four rungs, and it holds in 17 of the 18 chapters** (ch-05
  prints a 3-tier ladder with no બે-ત્રણ વાક્ય rung):
  1. `નીચેના પ્રશ્નો સાથે આપેલા વિકલ્પોમાંથી સાચો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરી ખરાની (✓) નિશાની કરો.` — 2 MCQs
     (3 in ch-05 and ch-16; chs 12 and 14 print "નીચેના પ્રશ્નોમાં આપેલા…")
  2. `નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના એક-એક વાક્યમાં ઉત્તર લખો :` — 2–3 items
  3. `નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના બે-ત્રણ વાક્યમાં ઉત્તર લખો.` — 1–3 items (absent in ch-05)
  4. `નીચેના પ્રશ્નનો સવિસ્તર ઉત્તર લખો.` — 1–3 items

  Wording micro-varies and must be matched fuzzily: લખો/આપો, વાક્યમાં/વાક્યોમાં, **સવિસ્તર/સવિસ્તાર**,
  વિદ્યાર્થી પ્રવૃત્તિ/વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ. `prompt_verbatim` copies the page, whichever form it prints.
  **`મુદ્દાસર`, `જોડકાં` and `ખાલી જગ્યા` banners were not observed** anywhere in the book — they
  are board / question-bank item types, not printed textbook banners. Note the contrast with std 9,
  where `એક વાક્યમાં` was *absent*: std 10 restores it, so the printed ladder here is the closest
  the whole span gets to the board's answer grammar.
- **Fixed end-matter sequence per chapter:** શબ્દ-સમજૂતી → સમાનાર્થી શબ્દો/શબ્દાર્થ →
  (chapter-dependent) તળપદા શબ્દો / વિરુદ્ધાર્થી શબ્દો / રૂઢિપ્રયોગો / કહેવત / શબ્દસમૂહ માટે એક શબ્દ →
  **સ્વાધ્યાય** → **વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ** → **ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ** → **શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા**.
  **શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા is teacher-addressed — not a child-facing exercise block; Agent 10 must not
  answer it as one.**
- **The chapter opener is unlabeled.** Chapters begin with a one-paragraph author bio plus a
  one-paragraph કૃતિ-પરિચય before the text proper, separated only by a horizontal rule — there is
  no printed `લેખક-પરિચય` heading as at std 9. The author tag under the title carries the era line
  (`(જન્મ : તા. …; અવસાન : …)`), and its format drifts (ch-10 stacks two parentheses; પૂ-4 prints no
  parentheses at all; ch-01 has "(જન્મ : ઈ.સ. સોળમી સદી)"; ch-05 and ch-13 print a genre label —
  સંકલિત, લોકગીત — in the author slot). The tutor prompts' "chapter apparatus opener" means *this*
  block plus the end-matter શબ્દ-સમજૂતી, and only what is printed there.
- **Genre spread (priors — diagnose, don't assume):** verse in ch 1, 4, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 18, પૂ-5;
  prose in ch 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 10, 14, 16, 17, પૂ-1…પૂ-4; drama in ch 12. Forms named by the book
  itself: પદ-ભજન (મીરાં), પ્રાર્થનાકાવ્ય/ગીત, **સૉનેટ**, ઊર્મિકાવ્ય, અછાંદસ, **લોકગીત**, **ગઝલ**,
  લઘુકાવ્યો (દુહા · મુક્તક · હાઈકુ in one chapter), ટૂંકીવાર્તા, હાસ્ય-નવલકથાખંડ, **હાસ્યનિબંધ
  (વ્યંગાત્મક)**, ચરિત્ર/રેખાચિત્ર, પ્રસંગકથા, લોકકથા, અનુવાદિત વાર્તા, લઘુકથા, નાટક-એકાંકી. The
  report's "satire as genre" and "reflective/civilizational prose" both land: ch-08 is the satire,
  ch-05 શ્વેતક્રાંતિના પ્રણેતાઓ is the civilizational chapter the report names by title.
- **Archaic, dialect and છાપ load.** મીરાં's મધ્યકાલીન diction with the છાપ line "બાઈ મીરાં કે
  પ્રભુ ગિરિધરના ગુણ…"; લોકગીત dialect (વજોગણ, સૈયર, હેર્યાં) with mid-line "જી રે" inserts and the
  dash-refrain "– સમી સાંજની" set to the right of each line; apostrophe-elision "લો'તાં" in ch-04;
  traditional દુહો diction in ch-18. None of it is ever corrected (brief §3.1) — it is glossed, and
  **the gloss dial in §3 is the main thing tier changes at this standard.**
- **Printed Roman script is real content at std 10, and it is a trap for the purity check.** The
  book prints English inline: "Wall to wall કારપેટ નખાવી દીધી." (ch-11), "Purring" (પૂ-2), and
  English lexemes in Gujarati script (ટેસ્ટી, ફૅન્ટાસ્ટિક in ch-08). It is transcribed exactly.
  Agent 13's "no Roman outside bracketed terms" check applies to **authored** prose, not to
  `original_chunk`; record each such string in `extraction_notes[]` so the QC line reads as
  intentional rather than as a defect.
- **Rendering defect seeds** (they degrade the verbatim the tutor is then pinned to): every title
  bar carries a QR code with a Latin code string beneath it (M1M9A7, S6Q5W1, …) that pollutes the
  title zone; the QR box intrudes into ch-05's first paragraph and breaks reading order; ch-07's
  sonnet is set with wide per-અક્ષર letter-spacing that produces spurious spaces; ch-18 prints no
  chapter-number box and puts poet bios mid-chapter; the manifest misspells ફિલિપ ક્લાર્ક as
  "ઝિલિપ ક્લાર્ક" (manifest typo — the page is the authority).

## 2. Method mix for this std

The report's recommended mix for "board-mirror mastery":

- **Everything mirrors the SSC paper.** The A/B/C/D section skeleton is the practice-set skeleton;
  the four descriptive escalation levels are the answer format; every lesson carries a board-format
  exit item. (Mark split is [thin] until the official SL blueprint is read — §1.3.)
- **Objective-item fluency:** MCQ pools, જોડકાં (poet–કૃતિ matching), ખાલી જગ્યા, true/false, plus
  timed practice. These are ~30% of the paper — bank them. Per §1.4 only the ✓-MCQ rung is printed
  in the book's chapters; the rest are board formats the tutor drills from a provided registry,
  never from memory.
- **છંદ enters, std-10 only**, taught as an explicit લઘુ-ગુરુ / માત્રા counting **method** on a line
  quoted from a taught poem — never as a label recalled and asserted.
- **અલંકાર expands** (+ અનન્વય, શ્લેષ, વ્યાજસ્તુતિ, સજીવારોપણ, યમક) and **voice transformation**
  (કર્તરિ→કર્મણિ→ભાવે→પ્રેરક) becomes the signature grammar skill — measured in vy-4.
- **લેખન adds સંક્ષેપીકરણ and સંવાદલેખન** on top of the 9–10 canon; the book's own લેખન units add
  અહેવાલલેખન, અર્થવિસ્તાર, નિબંધલેખન, વાર્તાલેખન (§1.4).
- **Evaluation and transfer questions** ("શું વક્તા સાચા છે? આજે આ ક્યાં દેખાય છે?"), comparison of
  two poems, and satire/વ્યંગ plus reflective prose read as genres.
- **Spaced review remains the pipeline's job** — the annual volume has no પુનરાવર્તન blocks.

### Top-5 DO / DON'T (report §1, Std 10)

| # | DO | DON'T |
|---|----|----|
| 1 | Mirror the A/B/C/D section grammar in practice sets; keep the four descriptive escalation levels as the answer format. | Don't invent novel exam formats; the section grammar is standardized across question banks (Gala/Vikas/Apekshit). |
| 2 | Teach છંદ via explicit લઘુ-ગુરુ counting method on quoted lines from taught poems. | Don't trust model-recalled છંદ/અલંકાર labels or syllable counts — always verify against the registry (two source discrepancies already found: મંદાક્રાન્તા, હરિણી counts). |
| 3 | Run timed સંક્ષેપીકરણ, અહેવાલ, નિબંધ drills with rubric-based self-assessment (~30-40% independent time). | Don't cap સહાય-tier students at objective items only — they must also practice સવિસ્તર with scaffolds. |
| 4 | Ask evaluation/transfer questions and two-poem comparisons. | Don't ask them below પ્રગત tier without a structured comparison chart. |
| 5 | Draw all idiom/proverb/MCQ items "from the textbook" (board convention) — harvest per-chapter inventories. | Don't source રૂઢિપ્રયોગ/કહેવત items from generic lists. |

Four of these are already hard gates in the pack, and they are the same gates:
DON'T #2 is `reference/no_hallucination_policy.md` (no invented અલંકાર, no invented poet detail;
`[]` is a correct answer) plus Agent 13's rule that every `figures_of_speech.lines` string must be
found verbatim inside that topic's own `original_chunk`. DO #5 is Agent 1's per-chapter
`exercise_inventory` (brief §3.4): the રૂઢિપ્રયોગ and કહેવત items come from the chapter's own
શબ્દ-સમજૂતી apparatus, inventoried from the rendered page, never translated from a list. DO #1's
"two-text comparison" is only available when both texts are in context — a plan authored for one
chapter cannot reach across to another chapter it was not given.

### Cultural anchoring for this standard

Every `real_life_example` (and any example the tutor improvises) is anchored in Gujarat's own
world — the bank is `reference/gujarat_cultural_anchors.md`, already injected with every call
(§5.2). Std 10 sits at the **civic/abstract** end of the bank's age-ladder: સ્થળાંતર, ઉદ્યોગ,
પર્યાવરણ, નર્મદા યોજના are all fair — **but the abstraction must still arrive through something
seen**, one concrete picture, never an editorial or a tourism-brochure superlative.

Std 9 already granted the civic reach, so "concrete, and civic" is not what separates this
standard. **What separates std 10 is that the anchor has to hold two truths at once.** The year's
own questions are evaluation, transfer, form-vs-content and two-text comparison (§2): ask
`આજે આ ટકે છે? ક્યાં તૂટે છે?` of a one-sided picture and there is nothing to push against. The
same material moves up the rung rather than being replaced — સ્થળાંતર at std 9 is one cousin's
face; at std 10 it is the cousin's phone call, પગાર on one end and an empty ઓસરી on the other.

**Choosing one.** Name the tension the text turns on, then take the row.

| The tension the text turns on | The std-10 anchor that holds both halves |
|---|---|
| હસાવતાં હસાવતાં કહેવાતી ચોટ (વ્યંગ) | ભવાઈનો ગામ-ચોક — વેશ હસાવે છે, ને હસનારને જ કંઈક કહી જાય છે |
| બન્ને બાજુથી સાચું હોવું | પટોળાની અવળી બાજુ પણ સવળી જેવી જ ભાત |
| એક ઘરનું ટીપું, ને આખું સરોવર | રોજ સવારે ડેરીએ જતું એક ઘરનું દૂધ, ને તેમાંથી ઊભી થયેલી મંડળી |
| ઘર છૂટ્યું, ને ઘર ચાલ્યું | સુરત ગયેલા કાકાનો ફોન — ત્યાં પગાર છે, અહીં ઓસરી ખાલી છે |
| જૂનું ટકે છે, પણ એ જ રહીને નહિ | ભજન-મંડળીનાં મંજીરાં આજેય સવારે વાગે છે — શબ્દો એ જ, ગાનારા જુદા |
| નાનું સ્વરૂપ, ને એમાં માતી મોટી વાત | ડાયરે લલકારાતો બે પંક્તિનો દુહો — વાત પૂરી, ને યાદ પણ રહી જાય |

The standing rules do not move: exactly ONE example per scene, 55–90 words, inside the child's own
experience; the anchor illustrates the text's FEELING and never adds a claim about the text, the
poet, or the chapter. Rotate domains across a chapter, and across the book show the
whole state and all its communities — never one slice, never a costume, never
"Gujarati = businessman".

**The std-10 failure mode: the anchor that cannot be argued with.** At this standard it usually
arrives as a brochure line. One sentence out of the middle of an example:

- ✅ `સાળ પરથી ઊતરેલું પટોળું અવળી બાજુથી જુઓ તોય એ જ ભાત દેખાય છે.`
- ❌ `ગુજરાતનું ગૌરવવંતું પટોળું આજે વિશ્વભરમાં પ્રખ્યાત છે.`

The ❌ half fails for a nameable reason: a superlative off a hoarding offers nothing to agree or
disagree with, so the transfer question this standard exists to train (`આજે આ ક્યાં લાગુ પડે?`)
has no purchase. At std 6 an anchor only had to be felt; at std 10 it must also be usable as
evidence in an argument the student is about to make.

Tier gradient: **સહાય** stays with the bank's most familiar, most concrete anchors even at this
standard — but hangs one transfer question off the familiar picture; **ધોરણ** the everyday spread
plus the civic picture where the text carries it; **પ્રગત** may carry the bank's least common
anchors (રોગાનની એક તારથી દોરાતી
વેલ; ચાંદનીમાં કચ્છના સફેદ રણની મીઠાની ચાદર, spent only where the text's feeling is itself that
large) provided they need no glossary.

## 3. The three tiers

Three tiers, named in Gujarati, shared by every generator and the tutor runtime.

| Tier | Meaning | Expected population |
|---|---|---|
| **સહાય** (Support) | below-grade understanding | ~15–25% |
| **ધોરણ** (Core) | at-grade | ~60–80% |
| **પ્રગત** (Advanced) | above-grade | ~5–15% |

**Assignment rule — pre-test cut scores.** A short unit pre-test decides the tier:

| Pre-test score | Tier | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| **≥80–85%** | પ્રગત | compacting: skip mastered blocks, go to extension |
| **~50–80%** | ધોરણ | the grade-level ladder as written |
| **<50%, or missing prerequisites** | સહાય | the same canon on a concrete on-ramp |

Re-sort **per unit, or every 4–8 weeks**. Tiers are flexible readiness states, not labels, and
**tier ≠ intelligence**: the WIDA warning applies — a language tier must never cap cognitive
demand.

**FIXED across tiers:** the chapter, the learning outcome (અધ્યયન નિષ્પત્તિ), the big idea, the
assessment target, and access to higher-order thinking. **Every tier reaches analyze/evaluate — the
on-ramp differs, never the ceiling.** At std 10 that includes the board's own top rung: **સવિસ્તર
is practised at every tier**, as guided assembly at સહાય and unaided at પ્રગત.

**Authoring order: write the પ્રગત variant first, then scaffold down.** This is the mechanism that
prevents સહાય from silently becoming "less learning" instead of "more support" — and at std 10 it
is what stops a સહાય plan from collapsing into an MCQ bank.

**VARIES:** everything in the table below (Tomlinson's Equalizer dials — concrete↔abstract,
simple↔complex, structured↔open, dependent↔independent, small↔great inference leap), instantiated
here for std 10.

| Dimension | સહાય (Support) | ધોરણ (Core) | પ્રગત (Advanced) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vocabulary load** | 2–3 new words per lesson on the full 8-step routine (definition, 2 examples, 2 non-examples, use, spaced review) + picture/L1 support; **also gloss the everyday polysemous words the text assumes**; જોડાક્ષર-heavy words pre-taught by breaking the conjunct apart visually (સ્ + ત = સ્ત) | 2–3 new words per lesson in depth (8–10/week), academic (Tier-2) words prioritized; તળપદા and તત્સમ glossed; extend into શબ્દસમૂહ માટે એક શબ્દ where natural | Same core words + morphology and word-family extension (-નાર, -વાળું, -પણું/-તા, અ-/બિન-/ગેર-, compounds); તત્સમ words decomposed as સંધિ/સમાસ વિગ્રહ "as analysis sport" |
| **Sentence length of explanations** | ≤12 words per sentence, simple + compound only, one idea per sentence; say–show–do redundancy | Grade cap: multi-clause, passives and nominalisation acceptable; say once, check once | May run one notch above the grade cap; full literary-academic register |
| **Gloss density** | Every low-frequency, તળપદા, તત્સમ and archaic word gets an inline appositive gloss **plus** an English equivalent; cumulative bilingual glossary, re-quizzed | Inline Gujarati glosses for તળપદા/તત્સમ; the student attempts inference first on roughly half; English gloss on request | **None by default — the student glosses, you verify;** તત્સમ words get bonus decomposition; end-glossary only |
| **Abstraction level** | Concrete end of every dial: worked templates, numbered identification steps, abstraction pre-chewed into a chart | Concrete anchor first, then the grade-level abstraction extracted with guidance | May open **from** the abstraction and demand grounding in lines; far transfer and cross-text links |
| **Example concreteness** | 3–4 worked examples per item type from the chapter, **all steps shown, twice**, then template-fill, then one independent item | 1–2 worked examples → parallel problem → independent problem | 1 model, then self-derivation; non-examples and edge cases ("આ પંક્તિમાં યમક છે કે વર્ણાનુપ્રાસ? બંને કેમ નહિ?") |
| **Question Bloom range & form** | **Full Bloom range, reached through structured concrete on-ramps.** Board ladder from the bottom: objective items until secure, એક-એક વાક્યમાં with frames, **સવિસ્તર as guided assembly** (tutor supplies the મુદ્દા, student expands each into a sentence); evidence-citation as a mechanical step | Full board ladder each week: objective → એક વાક્યમાં → બે-ત્રણ વાક્યમાં → મુદ્દાસર → સવિસ્તર; **evaluation and transfer are fair**; two-poem comparison when both texts are in context | Analysis-first: device-effect, form-vs-content, two-poem comparison, evaluation and transfer, satire mechanics; ladder runs **top-down** — full સવિસ્તર unaided, then self-compressed to મુદ્દાસર |
| **Pacing** | 3–5 min chunks, then an action; comprehension check every turn; **2–3** new interacting elements max; short timed drills (5-minute MCQ sets) to build exam stamina gradually | 10/2 rhythm; **≤4** new interacting elements; 15–20 min blocks with one long writing block per session; **~30–40% independent timed work** | Probe first and skip what is known (compacting); longer uninterrupted segments; may enter at You-do; **~40% independent**, full-draft attempts |
| **Media / support** | Visual + audio support by default (pictures, audio-assisted repeated reading); sentence frames with 1 slot | Visuals for key concepts; frames with 2–3 slots, retired as proficiency rises | Text-first; media only where the form demands it (hearing a ગઝલ's or a લોકગીત's rhythm); no frames |
| **Translanguaging** | Generous: method and grammar explanation in English allowed, production always in Gujarati; explicit L1 contrast notes | English for method explanations; discussion drifts back to Gujarati | Gujarati throughout; English only if the student asks |
| **Feedback style** | Flag → name with the exam term **glossed** ("આ 'સંધિ'ની ભૂલ છે") → correct form → one retry on the same category; one category per task; the tutor keeps a **"marks leaked" log framed positively** ("આ 2 માર્ક પાછા જીત્યા") | Flag → exam-term category → correct form → retry; then marks-framing ("સવિસ્તરમાં પુરાવા-પંક્તિ ન ટાંકો તો માર્ક જાય"); **the student** maintains the error log | Self-correction first; discuss error *patterns* from the log; push register and precision ("સારો જવાબ — હવે એ જ વાત પરીક્ષક-સ્તરની ભાષામાં"); content critique with evidence is rewarded |

Three std-10 constants across all three tiers:

- **Scope discipline.** The canon of §1.3 is the same for સહાય and પ્રગત alike. પ્રગત does not get
  anything beyond it; સહાય does not get a shortened one. બહુવ્રીહિ/દ્વિગુ stay out at every tier.
- **The 2-attempt rule.** No answer before an attempt; after two failed attempts, explain directly,
  then one retry. What differs is the size of an "attempt" — a frame-filled sentence at સહાય, a
  full draft at પ્રગત. At સહાય the direct explanation comes **with the marking points visible**.
- **Every section every week.** A/B/C/D are all visited at every tier, and the student is told which
  section a task serves. Tier changes the scaffold on a section, never which sections exist.

## 4. The three tutor system prompts

Paste one block as the system prompt for the tutor session. The chapter text, its apparatus (author
bio + કૃતિ-પરિચય, શબ્દ-સમજૂતી and its sub-blocks, ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ) and the સ્વાધ્યાય must be pasted
**verbatim** into the context — these prompts assume it, and at std 10 they additionally assume a
**human-verified registry** for any poet–કૃતિ attribution, છંદ or અલંકાર label the session uses.
Because prompt-level difficulty control decays within about **9 turns**, re-inject the block (or at
minimum its REGISTER / QUESTIONING / PACING fields) with every generation call, and pin difficulty
**per exercise block, not per session**. The blocks are the report's, **plus one CULTURAL
ANCHORING line added to each by this pack** (see §2's cultural anchoring subsection) — the only
addition.

### Std 10 × સહાય

```
ROLE: You are a calm, systematic Gujarati (second language) tutor for a GSEB Std 10 student below grade level (સહાય tier). The SSC board exam frames everything: your student must score, so build reliable marks first (objective items, formula answers), while still practicing every section including સવિસ્તર — scaffolded, never skipped.

SOURCE FIDELITY: Only the provided chapter text, apparatus, and સ્વાધ્યાય/question-bank items. Quote lines verbatim. NEVER supply poet names, કૃતિ attributions, meanings, છંદ or અલંકાર labels from memory — Gujarati literary facts from model memory are unreliable; only the provided registry counts.

SCRIPT: Gujarati script only (U+0A80–U+0AFF); no Devanagari, no romanized Gujarati; board answers must be in correct script — enforce it in every exchange.

REGISTER: Simple, steady Gujarati; English for explaining methods; ≤12-word sentences. Encouraging, exam-practical tone.

EXPLANATION: 2–3 sentences then action. For દરેક method (છંદ counting, અલંકાર identification, voice transformation): show ALL steps worked on a chapter line, twice, before the student tries with a checklist in hand.

GLOSSING: Dense inline glossing (Gujarati + English) of તત્સમ/તળપદા words; pre-teach જોડાક્ષર-heavy words by breaking the conjunct apart visually (સ્ + ત = સ્ત).

EXAMPLES: 3–4 worked examples per item type from the chapter; then template-fill; then one independent item.

CULTURAL ANCHORING: Anchor every example in Gujarat's own world per reference/gujarat_cultural_anchors.md, using only its most familiar, most concrete anchors even at Std 10 (ધોમધખતા ઉનાળે બસ-સ્ટૅન્ડ પાસેના લીમડાનો છાંયડો, વાવણી પહેલાં આકાશ સામે જોતો ખેડૂત, નાતાલની શેરીનો સ્ટાર ને મધરાતની પ્રાર્થના, ચોમાસાનું પહેલું ઝાપટું). Familiar picture first, then hang one board-style transfer question off it ("આજે આ ક્યાં દેખાય છે?"). The anchor shows the text's feeling only; it never adds facts about the text, the poet, or the chapter.

QUESTIONING: Board ladder from the bottom: MCQ, જોડકાં, ખાલી જગ્યા until secure (these are ~30% of the paper — bank them); એક વાક્યમાં with frames; સવિસ્તર as guided assembly (you give મુદ્દા, student expands). Evidence-citation as a mechanical habit. All sections A/B/C/D visited every week; tell the student which section each task serves.

FEEDBACK: Flag → name with the exam term glossed ("આ 'સંધિ'ની ભૂલ છે") → correct → retry; one category per task; maintain a "marks leaked" log framed positively ("આ 2 માર્ક પાછા જીત્યા").

PACING & ANSWERS: 2-attempt rule then direct model answer WITH the marking points visible; short timed drills (5-minute MCQ sets) to build exam stamina gradually.
```

### Std 10 × ધોરણ

```
ROLE: You are a Gujarati (second language) tutor for a GSEB Std 10 student at grade level (ધોરણ tier), preparing for the SSC paper (80 external + 20 internal; sections: A ગદ્ય, B પદ્ય, C વ્યાકરણ, D લેખન).

SOURCE FIDELITY: Only the provided chapter material and question-bank items; quote lines verbatim in stems. Never state poet-કૃતિ attributions, meanings, છંદ or અલંકાર labels from memory — use only what is provided; if absent, say it must be checked.

SCRIPT: Gujarati script only (U+0A80–U+0AFF); no Devanagari; no romanization. Exam-correct script and જોડણી are themselves scoring skills — correct them consistently.

REGISTER: Academic Gujarati; English for method explanations. Model સવિસ્તર-answer register in your own prose.

EXPLANATION: 3–4 sentences then action; 15–20 min blocks with one long writing block per session. Lesson opener: chapter apparatus + which board sections this chapter feeds.

GLOSSING: Inline glosses for તત્સમ/તળપદા; student infers first on half; extend into શબ્દસમૂહ માટે એક શબ્દ practice where natural.

EXAMPLES: Worked example → parallel problem for every method: છંદ via explicit લઘુ-ગુરુ/માત્રા counting on the quoted line; અલંકાર via marker-hunting (e.g., 'જાણે' → ઉત્પ્રેક્ષા); voice transformation (કર્તરિ→કર્મણિ→ભાવે→પ્રેરક) as a 3-step recipe on chapter sentences.

CULTURAL ANCHORING: Anchor examples in Gujarat's own world per reference/gujarat_cultural_anchors.md — the everyday spread, and the civic reach where the text carries it, always through one seen picture that has two sides to it (સુરત ગયેલા કાકાનો ફોન — ત્યાં પગાર, અહીં ખાલી ઓસરી; રોજનું એક ઘરનું દૂધ, ને તેમાંથી ઊભી થયેલી મંડળી), drawn from across the state's regions and communities. Rotate domains; the anchor illustrates the feeling and never adds facts about the text, the poet, or the chapter.

QUESTIONING: Run full board ladder each week: objective set → એક વાક્યમાં → 2-3 વાક્યમાં → મુદ્દાસર → સવિસ્તર; plus જોડકાં for poet-કૃતિ (from provided registry only). Evaluation and transfer are fair: "લેખકની વાત આજે ક્યાં લાગુ પડે?" Comparison of two poems when both texts are in context.

WRITING: Rotate section-D forms: નિબંધ, અહેવાલ, પત્ર, વિચારવિસ્તાર, સંક્ષેપીકરણ, સંવાદલેખન — one target feature declared per task; timed occasionally; self-assess against a rubric before your feedback.

FEEDBACK: Flag → exam-term category → correct form → retry; then marks-framing ("સવિસ્તરમાં પુરાવા-પંક્તિ ન ટાંકો તો માર્ક જાય"). Student maintains their own error log.

PACING & ANSWERS: 2-attempt rule; ~30–40% independent timed work; end sessions with a mixed 5-item board-format exit set.
```

### Std 10 × પ્રગત

```
ROLE: You are a Gujarati (second language) tutor for a GSEB Std 10 student above grade level (પ્રગત tier) — target: top-band SSC performance plus genuine literary capability (B1+ user).

SOURCE FIDELITY: Provided material only; verbatim quotation; absolutely no literary facts from memory — no poet attributions, no છંદ/અલંકાર labels, no "traditional meanings" beyond the provided registry. Flag anything unverifiable.

SCRIPT: Gujarati script only (U+0A80–U+0AFF); no Devanagari; no romanization.

REGISTER: Full literary-academic Gujarati; English only on request.

EXPLANATION: Probe-first compacting: skip the secure, spend time on discrimination (ઉપમા vs ઉત્પ્રેક્ષા vs અનન્વય; શ્લેષ double-meanings; મંદાક્રાન્તા vs શિખરિણી counting) and on writing quality. You may open from the abstraction and demand grounding in lines.

GLOSSING: None by default — the student glosses; you verify. તત્સમ words get bonus decomposition (સંધિ/સમાસ વિગ્રહ as analysis sport).

EXAMPLES: One model, then self-derivation, non-examples, and edge cases ("આ પંક્તિમાં યમક છે કે વર્ણાનુપ્રાસ? બંને કેમ નહિ?").

CULTURAL ANCHORING: Anchor examples in Gujarat's own world per reference/gujarat_cultural_anchors.md; this student can handle the bank's least common and most civic anchors (પટોળાની અવળી બાજુ પણ સવળી જેવી ભાત, રોગાનની એક તારથી દોરાતી વેલ, સ્થળાંતર-ઉદ્યોગ-પર્યાવરણ through one concrete picture) so long as they need no glossary, arrive through something seen, and leave the student something to argue with. The anchor illustrates the feeling only — never a fact about the text, the poet, or the chapter.

QUESTIONING: Analysis-first: device-effect ("આ સજીવારોપણ કાઢી નાખો — પંક્તિ શું ગુમાવે?"), form-vs-content ("સૉનેટ સ્વરૂપ આ ભાવને કેવી રીતે બંધ બેસે છે?"), two-poem comparison, evaluation and transfer ("વક્તાની દલીલ આજે ટકે છે? ક્યાં તૂટે છે?"), satire mechanics ("કટાક્ષ ક્યાં છે અને કોના પર?"). Exam ladder top-down: full સવિસ્તર unaided → self-compress to મુદ્દાસર → self-generate the MCQs an examiner would set (examiner mode).

WRITING: Full section-D range under time; સંક્ષેપીકરણ to exact length; the student writes the marking scheme for their own answer, then you compare against the rubric.

FEEDBACK: Self-correction first; discuss error patterns from their log; push register and precision ("સારો જવાબ — હવે એ જ વાત પરીક્ષક-સ્તરની ભાષામાં"). Content critique is welcome and expected — disagreeing with the text, with evidence, is rewarded.

PACING & ANSWERS: Fast, ~40% independent; 2-attempt rule with full-draft attempts; every session ends by connecting the chapter to the wider board map (which sections, which item types, what typically leaks marks).
```

Three notes on using these blocks against the measured std-10 book:

- **`જોડકાં` and `ખાલી જગ્યા` are board item types, not printed banners** anywhere in the measured
  book (§1.4). The tutor may drill them; Agent 10 answers only what the page prints, with
  `prompt_verbatim` copied exactly.
- **`સવિસ્તર` vs `સવિસ્તાર`.** The prompts use the report's spelling. The book prints both, sometimes
  in adjacent chapters. Never normalize a printed banner to match the prompt.
- **The "apparatus opener"** at std 10 is an *unlabeled* bio + કૃતિ-પરિચય paragraph pair plus the
  end-matter શબ્દ-સમજૂતી (§1.4). If a chapter's bio prints only a century ("ઈ.સ. સોળમી સદી") or a
  genre label in place of an author (સંકલિત, લોકગીત), that is what the opener says — nothing is
  filled in from memory.

## 5. Pipeline wiring

### 5.1 Where the tier enters

- **Optional input at Phase 0.** The orchestrator takes `tier ∈ {સહાય, ધોરણ, પ્રગત}` alongside the
  chapter. **Default is ધોરણ** — a run with no tier is a ધોરણ run, and the validation report header
  says so explicitly rather than leaving it blank.
- **The tier is a run parameter, not a JSON field.** It is never written into any emitted
  deliverable: the root object has exactly the 32 keys of brief §2.1 and a topic has exactly 31.
  Record the tier in the run log and in `validation_report.md`'s header line, next to
  `સ્વરૂપ: <genre> (confidence)`.
- **One run authors one tier.** The pack fixes `output10/chNN/` as the output directory (brief
  §3.7); do not invent a per-tier sub-directory. If all three variants of a chapter are wanted,
  that is three runs — and per §3, author **પ્રગત first**, then scaffold down.

### 5.2 Agent 12 re-injects the descriptor every call

Prompt-level difficulty control **drifts within about 9 turns**. Therefore Agent 12 (and Agent 16,
and Agent 10 when it drafts model answers) must re-inject the std-10 + tier descriptor block — the
row set from §3 plus the scope ceiling from §1.3 — **in every generation call**, not once per
session. Difficulty is pinned **per authored block** (per topic, per exercise item), never once per
chapter.

Two std-10-specific riders on the re-injection:

- Per report §4.8, pass 1–2 required items from the curated Gujarat examples bank (ઉત્તરાયણ,
  નવરાત્રિ, મેળો, શેરી ક્રિકેટ, ST બસ, ખેતર-કૂવો) with every call — **સહાય examples draw
  exclusively from that bank.**
- Re-inject the **no-memory clause** with every call that could touch a છંદ name, an અલંકાર name or
  a poet–કૃતિ attribution. This is the field where std-10 output degrades first, and the report is
  explicit that Gujarati literary facts recalled by a model are unreliable (best benchmark score
  58%). A label the chapter's own apparatus does not print, and the registry does not carry, is
  simply not written.

### 5.3 Which fields tighten per tier

Every band below is the contract band from brief §2.2. **The tier selects a sub-range inside the
band — it never widens or leaves it.** An out-of-band value is a QC failure at Agent 13 regardless
of tier.

| Field (contract band) | સહાય | ધોરણ | પ્રગત |
|---|---|---|---|
| `explanation` (55–90 words) | **low end, 55–70**; sentences ≤12 words; one idea per sentence; any method (છંદ counting, voice transformation) explained in numbered steps | 65–85; multi-clause, passives allowed; academic register | 75–90; full literary-academic register; may open from the abstraction |
| `real_life_example` (55–90 words, exactly ONE Indian anchor) | low end; anchor drawn from the Gujarat examples bank; may end on a this-or-that question | mid band; anchor inside the child's world; may end on a transfer question ("આજે આ ક્યાં લાગુ પડે?") | may use an unfamiliar context; ends on an evaluative question |
| `key_terms` (3–6, `"શબ્દ — અર્થ"`) | **5–6**, and the bar for "worth glossing" sits lowest — everyday polysemous words count; જોડાક્ષર-heavy તત્સમ words prioritized | 4–5, તળપદા/તત્સમ first | **3–4**, only what context cannot yield; તત્સમ entries may carry સંધિ/સમાસ structure |
| `concept_bullets` / `important_points` (3–4, keyword-first) | 4 lines, fuller glosses after the keyword | 3–4 | 3 lines, denser |
| `recall_questions` (2–3 per topic, Bloom-laddered) | **3 questions; forms are objective / frame-supported / decomposed chains — but the ladder still reaches analyze and evaluate** via a chart or a given મુદ્દા skeleton. At least one prompt carries the mechanical evidence step "આ પંક્તિ પરથી: …" | 2–3, remember → understand → analyze, with at least one evaluate/transfer at this standard; interpretive answers cite a line | 2–3 starting at understand; at least one evaluate with multi-line evidence, device-effect or form-vs-content |
| `brief_summary` / `summary` / `detailed_summary` (1 / 2–3 / 4–6 sentences, strictly increasing) | counts unchanged; sentences short and concrete | as written | counts unchanged; register denser |
| module `difficult_words` (5–10, fresh example sentence) | **8–10** | 6–8 | 5–6, plus word-family or સંધિ/સમાસ extension in the meaning field |
| `estimated_exchanges` (string int) | higher — more, smaller turns (e.g. `"6"`) | e.g. `"4"` | lower — longer student turns (e.g. `"3"`) |
| `figures_of_speech`, `rhyme_scheme` | **identical at every tier.** Same devices, same exact quotes, same છંદ/pattern claim; only the explanatory `note` gets simpler or denser. `[]` and `null` stay correct answers at all three tiers | | |

**The std-10 rule on `rhyme_scheme.pattern` and `figures_of_speech.device`:** name a છંદ or an
અલંકાર only where the chapter's own printed apparatus names it (as ch-07's intro names
મંદાક્રાન્તા) or a verified registry entry covers it. Otherwise describe the pattern without
labelling it. A device named for the field's sake is a hard fail at Agent 13, and tier does not
soften it.

Fields that do **not** move with tier: `topic.difficulty` (it describes the text, not the reader),
`word_count`, `objective_text` (owned by Agent 2, mirrored character-for-character), media count
(one image per reading scene, ≤1 `2d_tool` per chapter — tier-keyed picture/audio/frame support is
a **tutor-runtime** affordance, not a plan-shape change), and every id.

Agent 10's exercise deliverable is likewise tier-invariant in **what** it answers: every printed
block, `prompt_verbatim` exact, all four measured rungs (§1.4), everything skill-tagged and mapped.
Tier may shape the `explanation` and the `teacher_note` beside a model answer; it never changes the
answer, and it never drops the સવિસ્તર rung.

### 5.4 What never varies by tier

- The **phase-2 contract**: 32 root keys, 31 topic keys, the closed `topic_type` enum, the concept
  layer, `strand_to_objective_map`, the objectives registry and its inline mirror.
- **Ids**: `M{m}.S{s}.T{t}.C{c}` with chapter-continuous `c`, `.RQ{n}` (never `.SR{n}`),
  `MEDIA_ID_RE`. Frozen at Agent 4, renumbered only by Agent 14.
- **Verbatim**: `original_chunk` is the transcribed page — complete Gujarati script, nothing
  dropped, paraphrased, transliterated or "corrected". મધ્યકાલીન and બોલી forms stay (વજોગણ, હેર્યાં,
  લો'તાં, the "જી રે" inserts, the છાપ line "બાઈ મીરાં કે…"); the dash-refrain "– સમી સાંજની" is not
  merged into the verse line; printed English strings ("Wall to wall કારપેટ", "Purring") are kept
  exactly and noted in `extraction_notes[]`; punctuation is the printed `.`, never `।`; no nukta.
  A tier may change how a form is *explained*, never what is printed.
- **Exercise coverage**: every inventoried સ્વાધ્યાય block answered, `prompt_verbatim` exact,
  everything skill-tagged and mapped. સ્વાધ્યાય is never a topic at any tier, and
  **શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા is never answered as an exercise** — it is teacher-addressed.
- **The no-hallucination floor** and the Phase-8 LP2 gate: zero `validation_errors`, at every tier.
- **Ids and DB values remain provisional** — `chapter_id` is the provisional form
  `gseb_eng_gujarati10_ch{N}` and the medium slot is **unverified** (brief §2.3, VERIFY-1). A wrong
  medium uploads clean and mis-files the plan. Nothing in this file resolves that; the board
  profile and `naming_conventions.md` do, when verification lands.

### 5.5 QC hooks

Agent 13 already blocks on band violations; add the tier as a **reported** (§E–G, non-blocking)
line: declared tier, measured median `explanation` length, `key_terms` count distribution, and the
recall Bloom mix — so a drifted run is visible rather than silently "in band". Two failures this
line is meant to catch at std 10, both routing to Agent 12:

- a સહાય run whose recall questions never reach analyze or evaluate (the ceiling was capped), and
- a run at any tier whose `figures_of_speech` or `rhyme_scheme` carries a છંદ/અલંકાર label that the
  chapter apparatus does not print and the registry does not cover.

Per report §4.7, **auto-generated objective items are not auto-published** — MCQ, જોડકાં and
anything involving છંદ syllable counts go to human review before they reach a student.
