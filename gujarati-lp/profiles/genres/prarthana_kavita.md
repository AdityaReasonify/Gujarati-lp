## Diagnosis signals

**Verse addressed in the second person to પ્રભુ — or to a life-ideal — asking for something.** The
poem is one sustained petition, not a picture that produces a feeling. The decisive evidence is
grammatical: the spine of the poem is a verb of asking in the આજ્ઞાર્થ/વિધ્યર્થ — std-10 ch 4 asks
with **થાજો** ("મારું જીવન અંજલિ થાજો !") — and a vocative that keeps returning — std-8 ch 1's
**"પ્રભુ હે…"**, printed after its first occurrence as an ellipsis shorthand the page expects the
singer to complete.

Two printed shapes, both belonging here:

- **સંબોધન-પ્રાર્થના** — the addressee is named and re-named, and the ટેક *is* the address
  (std-8 ch 1: "પ્રભુ હે…").
- **અભીપ્સા / ધ્રુવપંક્તિ-પ્રાર્થના** — the petition is stated as a wish about the speaker's own
  life and the addressee stands behind it rather than in front of it (std-10 ch 4:
  "મારું જીવન અંજલિ થાજો !"). Still a petition, still second-person in stance.

**What is asked for is the second signal, and it is the one that teaches.** A GSEB પ્રાર્થના at
these standards does not ask to be spared. It asks to be *spent*: std-10 ch 4 asks that the life
become an offering and that the hands be the ones "દીન-દુઃખિયાનાં આંસુ લો'તાં". The petition runs
towards strength and usefulness, away from rescue — and that direction, not the piety, is the
subject.

**The intro box usually names the form outright** ("આ એક ભાવપૂર્ણ પ્રાર્થનાકાવ્ય/પ્રાર્થનાગીત છે")
and it is the single strongest signal. The author slot names a **modern, named poet** — 'સુન્દરમ્',
કરસનદાસ માણેક — never "લોકગીત", never "સંકલિત", and there is **no છાપ** inside the verse.

**Little to look at.** Where a picture does appear it is a condition of the asking, not scenery:
std-8 ch 1's "વણદીવે અંધારે", "વણજહાજે દરિયાને" — the dark without a lamp, the sea without a ship.
An agent carrying the ઊર્મિકાવ્ય lens into this chapter will go hunting imagery that is not there;
that is precisely why this profile exists separately.

**સ્વાધ્યાય tell.** The exercises ask about prayer as a subject, not only about the poem: std-8 ch 1
opens with **વાતચીત.** — seven oral prompts on prayer — closes its પ્રવૃત્તિ with
"પ્રાર્થનાગીત મેળવીને ગાઓ…" and "કાવ્યભાવ વર્ગ સમક્ષ રજૂ કરો", and ends the chapter with a
ચર્ચા-વિચારણા box whose single bullet is **"પ્રાર્થનાનું મહત્ત્વ"**. A prose-meaning → find-the-line
block ("આપેલ ભાવને અનુરૂપ કાવ્યપંક્તિઓ લખો.") and a rhyme hunt ("સરખા પ્રાસવાળા શબ્દો લખો.") sit
beside them: the form is sung, so પ્રાસ is tested.

## Lens

**યાચના + સંબોધન + આત્મબળ** — what is asked, who is being asked, and the strength the speaker asks
to be given rather than the trouble he asks to be spared.

## Guiding question

*કવિ પ્રભુ પાસે શું માગે છે — અને એ માગણી કવિ પોતાના વિશે શું કહી જાય છે?*

A shape, not a text. Each chapter derives its own — what a life becomes when it is offered; what a
lamp is for when the dark has none. The derived question goes in `01_meta.json`, never copied from
this file (`reference/teaching_lens_map.md`).

## Explanation unit — **one કડી of the petition**

One કડી normally carries one complete asking-move, and that move must stay whole. Two short કડી that
finish a single petition together may join; one કડી carrying two distinct petitions may split.
**Decide from where the asking closes, not from the line count.**

**Do not cut between the asking and what is asked for.** A topic holding only the condition — the
dark, the shipless sea — with the petition landing in the next topic teaches nothing, and reads as
though the poet described a hardship and stopped.

**The ટેક of a પ્રાર્થનાગીત is normally unchanged: teach it once, reference it after.** Both measured
units repeat their refrain verbatim ("પ્રભુ હે…", "મારું જીવન અંજલિ થાજો !"). It is taught in the
topic where it first appears; every later કડી topic naming it carries that topic in `depends_on`, not
a second teaching of the same line. Where the refrain's **words change**, the changed-words rule of
`urmikavya_geet.md` applies unchanged — a topic per distinct wording, teaching what changed.

**The printed refrain shorthand is verbatim.** std-8 ch 1 prints later occurrences as "પ્રભુ હે…"
with an ellipsis standing for the whole refrain. That ellipsis is reproduced exactly in
`original_chunk`; its expansion belongs in the `explanation`
(`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`, Agent 5 owns the defect).

સ્વાધ્યાય blocks are never topics, and neither is the poem *inside* a સ્વાધ્યાય block: std-8 ch 1's
item 10 embeds a second prayer poem, "મંદિર તારું વિશ્વ રૂપાળું" - જયંતીલાલ આચાર્ય, with three MCQs
on it. It is exercise-internal reading matter — Agent 10's, with its own attribution, never a
reading scene.

## પ્રાર્થના કેવી રીતે ચાલે છે — અને તેથી કેમ ભણાવવી

A petition in this form runs in four moves, and the explanation follows the same order:

1. **સંબોધન** — the listener is named ("પ્રભુ હે…"), or held implicitly by the ધ્રુવપંક્તિ.
2. **માગણી** — the asking verb and its object: what, exactly, is being asked for
   (અંજલિ થાજો — that the life become an offering).
3. **કારણ કે શરત** — the ground of the asking, usually a lack or a condition rather than a scene
   (વણદીવે અંધારે — where there is dark and no lamp).
4. **ટેક પાછી આવે** — the address returns and the poem is a conversation again rather than a
   conclusion.

So the explanation names the asking first, the ground second, and lets the refrain close it — the
mirror image of `urmikavya_geet.md`, where the picture must come before the feeling. An explanation
of a પ્રાર્થના that opens on scenery has read the wrong form.

## Emphasise

- **Name what is asked, in the poet's own verb.** Every topic's explanation says what this કડી asks
  for, quoting the asking word from the કડી (થાજો, and whatever this chapter's verbs are). If a
  child cannot answer "શું માગ્યું?" after the topic, the topic failed.
- **The direction of the asking.** Not "save me" but "make me of use": the life offered, the hands
  that wipe "દીન-દુઃખિયાનાં આંસુ". Ask what the poet is willing to spend. This is the form's whole
  argument and the one thing a summary will lose.
- **The repeated address.** "પ્રભુ હે…" returns like a ટેક and functions as one, but it is a form of
  address — it keeps the poem spoken *to* someone. Ask why the poet keeps naming the listener.
- **Grammar doing the work of imagery.** The optative and imperative endings; the વણ- privative
  compounds (વણદીવે, વણજહાજે) that build a condition instead of a picture. Say this out loud in the
  chapter — a child trained on પ્રકૃતિ-ગીતો will look for scenery and find none, and that absence is
  worth one sentence rather than a substitute picture.
- **આત્મબળ inside devotion, never against it.** The speaker never doubts the listener; he asks to be
  changed rather than protected. Teach a relationship being defined — never "ઈશ્વરની જરૂર નથી".
- **It is a ગીત, so the ear is content.** પ્રાસ, લય, પુનરાવર્તન — GSEB tests them here (std-8 ch 1's
  "સરખા પ્રાસવાળા શબ્દો લખો.") and the class is expected to sing the poem. Name the rhyme the
  exercise will ask for, at point of use.
- **The poetic lexicon is small and hard.** અમ, ઉર, આભ, અંજલિ — short, તત્સમ-tinged, high-density.
  Gloss at first use; the difficulty here is vocabulary, not syntax.

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these per topic; Agent 13 blocks on them; the structural ones are
checked at Agent 4.

- **Turning the prayer into a moral.** No sentence of `explanation`, `real_life_example`, `summary`,
  `brief_summary`, `detailed_summary`, any `concept_bullets`/`important_points` line, or any
  `recall_questions[].answer` is an exhortation of the shape "આપણે … જોઈએ" / "…કરવું જોઈએ" /
  "…રાખવી જોઈએ" unless those exact words are quoted from this topic's `original_chunk`. A પ્રાર્થના
  is a request, not a કહેવત, and an appended બોધ destroys the form.
- **Cutting a petition in half — structural, checked at Agent 4.** Every topic cut from the poem's
  body carries, inside its `original_chunk`, either an asking-verb of this poem or the ટેક line. A
  topic containing only the condition clause is a blocking structural fail owned by A2.
- **The first sentence must name the asking.** The first sentence of `explanation` names what this
  કડી asks for, using a word that occurs in this topic's `original_chunk`. A first sentence that
  opens on a described scene, on the ભાવ, or on the બોધ is a fail. (This gate is deliberately the
  inverse of the `urmikavya_geet.md` picture-first gate — do not carry that one in here.)
- **No supplied imagery.** Every concrete noun in any scene described by `explanation` or
  `real_life_example` occurs in this chapter's `original_chunk` or its printed શબ્દાર્થ/શબ્દ-સમજૂતી
  box. Inventing scenery to make an abstract poem look like a પ્રકૃતિ-ગીત is a hard fail.
- **Flattening the address.** The vocative string is reproduced character-for-character in
  `original_chunk`, and the topic that teaches the ટેક names it verbatim inside its `explanation`.
  Paraphrasing "પ્રભુ હે" into "ભગવાનને" and dropping it is a fail.
- **Reading the asking as doubt.** No `explanation`, `real_life_example` or `recall_questions[]
  .answer` states that the poet does not need ઈશ્વર, doubts ઈશ્વર, or that પ્રાર્થના is unnecessary.
  The poem asks *more* of the listener, not less.
- **No theology, no comparative religion.** No `explanation`, `real_life_example` or recall answer
  names a religion, sect, scripture or tradition, or compares two of them, unless the printed text
  of this chapter names it (`agents/08_sensitivity_safety.md`).
- **Invented craft.** Every `figures_of_speech[].lines` string appears character-for-character inside
  this topic's `original_chunk` (mechanical check at Agent 13). `[]` is a correct and complete
  answer; a named અલંકાર that is not on the page is a hard fail.
- **Skipping the craft.** Every topic carries either a non-empty `figures_of_speech[]` or an
  `explanation` sentence naming this કડી's પ્રાસ, લય or પુનરાવર્તન — the સ્વાધ્યાય asks for the rhyme
  and the class is asked to sing the poem.
- **ટેક handling — structural, checked at Agent 4.** If all `[[ટેક]]` markers in
  `00_chapter_normalized.md` are identical, exactly one topic teaches the ટેક and every later topic
  naming it lists that topic in `depends_on`. If the markers differ in wording, the number of topics
  carrying a `[[ટેક]]` marker equals the number of distinct wordings.
- **Verbatim of the refrain shorthand.** `original_chunk` reproduces the printed shorthand exactly
  ("પ્રભુ હે…"). Expanding it into the full refrain inside `original_chunk` is a verbatim defect
  owned by Agent 5; the expansion belongs in `explanation`.
- **Never modernise the poet.** વણદીવે, વણજહાજે, અમ, ઉર, આભ, and the apostrophe elision લો'તાં stay
  exactly as printed, apostrophe included, and are explained as લય and કાવ્ય-રૂપ — never corrected
  toward માનક ગુજરાતી (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **No deity in any image.** No `media[].generation_prompt` or `negative_prompt` describes ઈશ્વર, a
  deity figure, or an idol as a depicted subject.
- **No numbers in display text.** `"બીજી કડીમાં"`, never `"કડી 2માં"` — numbers live in ids and
  provenance fields only.

## Priors

**Media** — the hardest form in the pack to illustrate, because the poem refuses scenes. One image
per કડી, and it shows the **concrete thing the petition names**, never the petition's meaning: a
hand wiping a tear, a dark room with an unlit દીવો, an empty sea horizon with no boat, a
figure standing with folded hands and no temple behind it. **Do not draw ઈશ્વર.** Where a કડી names
nothing depictable, a single human figure in the posture the poem implies beats an invented tableau —
and where even that would be invention, fewer frames is the correct answer, recorded as such. The
ટેક topic gets its own frame only if the address names something new; otherwise the earlier frame is
referenced. Indian setting, soft digital watercolour, 16:9, narrator bar carrying one exact Gujarati
line. No Gujarati frame pool exists, so every scene is `image_url: ""` plus an authored,
self-contained `generation_prompt` (`agents/09_media_planning.md`); `reuse_report.reused` is 0.
≤1 `2d_tool` per chapter, and this form needs none.

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — **measured, std-8 ch 1**: there is **no printed "સ્વાધ્યાય" banner** in this reader —
numbered pink headings begin directly under the yellow શબ્દાર્થ box. In order: `વાતચીત.` (7 oral
prompts on prayer) · `પ્રશ્નોના જવાબ લખો.` (5) · `આપેલ ભાવને અનુરૂપ કાવ્યપંક્તિઓ લખો.` (3 prose
meanings → find the line) · `સરખા પ્રાસવાળા શબ્દો લખો.` · a વાક્ય-બનાવો block · a
રેખાંકિત-શબ્દ-બદલો block · a દ્વિરુક્ત/રવાનુકારી cloze (ગણગણાટ, ઝગમગાટ, ખડખડાટ…) · word-order

**Sensitivity** — this form always needs the paragraph. The poem addresses ઈશ્વર directly and a

record it, and never cut it as a topic.

## Recall priors

Per topic, two to three, Bloom-laddered, each with a real model answer:

- one **remember** — what does this કડી ask for, and whom does the poet address;
- one **understand** — why the poet asks for *that* rather than for rescue, or what the returning
  address does to the poem;
- one **analyze** (std 6–8) or **evaluate** (std 9–10) — what the poem would lose if the address were
  removed, or whether the child would ask for the same thing and why.

The third question is where the form is actually assessed, and at std 10 it is also where the SSC
સવિસ્તર answer is prepared — keep its model answer long enough to be one.
