## Diagnosis signals

Centred sung verse with a **ટેક / ધ્રુવપંક્તિ** that returns, often printed in abbreviated form —
મોરલી carries the refrain cue `વૃંદાવન.` set to the right of the lines rather than written out
again. The author slot holds a medieval ભક્ત poet, and the era line is a century rather than a
date: `મીરાંબાઈ (જન્મ : ઈ.સ. સોળમી સદી)`. The closing line carries a **છાપ** — the poet signing
inside the verse: `બાઈ મીરાં કે પ્રભુ ગિરિધરના ગુણ, દર્શન થકી દુઃખ ભાંગે છે.` (મોરલી), `ભણે
નરસૈયો એનું દર્શન કરતાં કુળ એકોતેર તાર્યાં રે!` (નરસિંહ, quoted at std-8 ch-6). The source
attribution names a પદ-collection — `('મીરાંનાં શ્રેષ્ઠ પદ'માંથી)`. Medieval and તળપદા forms sit
in the verse and are glossed in the શબ્દ-સમજૂતી or in-text (મોરલી: `થૈ થઈ`, `મારગ માર્ગ`). Theme:
ભક્તિ, પ્રેમ, વિરહ, વાત્સલ્ય — a relationship, addressed. The સ્વાધ્યાય asks what the speaker
feels, asks about the returning line, and the વિદ્યાર્થી પ્રવૃત્તિ prescribes **ગાન** (મોરલી:
"પદોનું ગાન કરો", "વાદ્યોનો પરિચય મેળવો").

**The decisive tell: a medieval ભક્ત poet AND a છાપ inside the verse.** Sung + a returning line +
a devotional address are shared by half the verse corpus. Drop either of the first two and the unit
is almost always ગીત, લોકગીત, or પ્રાર્થનાગીત — route it there.

## Lens

**ભાવ + સંબંધ + લોકભાષાની મીઠાશ** — એક સંબંધ, ગાઈને કહેલો.

## Guiding question

*ભક્ત અને ભગવાન વચ્ચેનો આ સંબંધ કેવો છે, અને કવિ કયા શબ્દોથી એને જીવતો કરે છે ?*

(A shape, not a template. મોરલી's own question is nearer to *મોરલી વાગે છે ત્યારે સાંભળનારનું મન
કેમ થંભી જાય છે ?* — each chapter derives its own from its own text.)

## Explanation unit — **one પદ, whole**

A પદ is one continuous utterance meant to be sung from its first line to its છાપ. Splitting it
breaks the voice: half of મીરાં's hearing of the flute is not a teaching unit, it is an
interruption.

- **One પદ = one topic**, even when it runs longer than a કડી of any other poem in the book.
- The **ટેક is quoted inside that topic** and named as the line that returns. It is never a topic
  of its own, never a concept of its own, and never a separate media scene.
- The **છાપ line stays** — in the verbatim, and quoted once in the explanation as the poet putting
  her own name into the song. It is verse, not an attribution label; the printed "— કવિનું નામ"
  attribution is the separate thing, and it belongs inside the last topic's `original_chunk`
  (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- A chapter that prints two or more separate પદો gives one topic to each, in printed order.
- Where the chapter is prose that *quotes* પદ lines (std-8 ch-6), the prose genre owns the cut; the
  quoted lines are glossed under this profile's language rules and are never re-lineated.

### How the ટેક is printed, and therefore how it is read

GSEB books abbreviate returning lines rather than reprinting them, and the convention varies by
chapter: મોરલી sets the cue `વૃંદાવન.` in the right margin beside the lines; std-9 ch-20 prints
`- શેરી વળાવી` at the right margin to mean "repeat the opening refrain couplet"; std-6 ch-1 ends
each કડી with `- એક જ.`. The shorthand is **print convention, not text**. Agent 5 expands it
consciously and records the expansion in `extraction_notes[]`; a margin cue must never be merged
into the verse line it sits beside, and must never be silently dropped. The topic's explanation
then names what the returning line does to the song — why the poem keeps coming back to it.

## Emphasise

- **ભાવ above all.** The પદ *is* the feeling; everything else serves it. મોરલી is a flute heard,
  and a mind that stops — not a fact about વૃંદાવન.
- **The speaker's voice.** Who is speaking, and to whom? મીરાં speaks as herself, signs as herself
  (`બાઈ મીરાં કે`), and an L2 child can hear that first-person nearness at once.
- **The relationship.** ભક્તિ in these પદો is not distance and awe — it is nearness: a woman and
  her ગિરિધર, a mother and a child. The theology, where it exists at all, is a household scene.
- **The sound.** The archaic and તળપદા forms are chosen, not accidental — `થૈ` for `થઈ`, `મારગ`
  for `માર્ગ`, the doubled `થનક થનક` in "વૃંદા તે વનની કુંજગલીમાં, વહાલો થનક થનક થૈ નાચે છે."
  They sound softer and rounder than their માનક ગુજરાતી equivalents. Name that once, with the line
  quoted, and move.
- **The ટેક** — what returns, and what returning does.
- **Repetition as craft.** મોરલી's own ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ block points at the repeated `વાગે છે`, at
  `વૃંદા તે વન`, and at the rhythm of verbs closing on `છે`. Where the book names the device, the
  explanation may follow it — but only by quoting the words it lives in.

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these as a per-topic testable sentence; Agent 13 blocks on `hard`
severity; the structural ones are checked at Agent 4 against the `[[પદ]]` / `[[ટેક]]` marker counts.

- **Flattening the પદ into a morality lesson.** *If the explanation's conclusion could be printed on
  a classroom poster, it is wrong.* Machine form: the final sentence of `explanation`, of
  `detailed_summary`, and of every `recall_questions[].answer` must name a person, object, act or
  image that occurs in this topic's `original_chunk` — not a class of people and not a rule of
  conduct. A closing clause built on સૌએ / હંમેશાં / જીવનમાં / આપણે બધાએ … જોઈએ fails the check.
  This is the single most common failure with this સ્વરૂપ.
- **Splitting the પદ.** Marker check: `count([[પદ]]) == count(topics carrying a પદ)`, and no two
  topics may carry `original_chunk` slices of the same `[[પદ]]` block. Fails at Agent 4.
- **Making the ટેક its own topic or its own concept.** No `topic_id` and no `concept_id` may hold
  only the ટેક; the ટેક string must occur inside the પદ topic's `original_chunk` **and** be quoted
  in that topic's `explanation`.
- **Losing the છાપ.** The છાપ line as printed must occur, character for character, in the chapter's
  concatenated `original_chunk`. Dropping it, moving it out of the verse, or converting it into an
  attribution line is a hard fail.
- **Correcting the poet.** Every quotation in `explanation`, `key_terms`, `concept_bullets`,
  `figures_of_speech[].lines`, `recall_questions[].answer` and `publication_text` must match
  `original_chunk` character for character — `થૈ` stays `થૈ`, `મારગ` stays `મારગ`, `બાઈ મીરાં કે`
  stays `કે`. No modernisation to માનક ગુજરાતી anywhere, in the verbatim or in a quotation.
- **Turning it into theology or comparative religion.** No `explanation`, `real_life_example`,
  `concepts[].content`, `recall_questions[].answer` or exercise answer may state what a religion
  teaches, assert or deny what any deity is or did, rank traditions, or compare this devotion with
  another's. Teach the poem. (The `08_sensitivity.json` area label is `ધર્મ`.)
- **Iconographic media.** No media node's `generation_prompt` may describe an idol, a halo, a
  devotional calendar/poster framing, or a worshipper-before-a-shrine tableau; each prompt must name
  at least one concrete element drawn from this topic's `original_chunk` and at least one human
  relationship or action.
- **Explaining the feeling to death.** At most **one** sentence of `explanation` may name the
  emotion abstractly; the rest works through the poem's own words and pictures.
- **Adding biography the page does not carry.** No claim about the poet's life beyond the printed
  કવિ-પરિચય and era line — and મોરલી's era line is a century, not a date. `[]` and silence beat
  invention (`reference/no_hallucination_policy.md`).

## Priors

**Media** — one image for the પદ, showing the **scene and the relationship**: for મોરલી, the
કુંજગલી at evening, the flute being played and being *heard*, the listener stopped mid-step —
warm, domestic, Gujarat-Indian, at human scale. Not a devotional portrait, not a shrine, not a
moral tableau. `negative_prompt` carries the baseline of `reference/field_shape_rules.md`
(including `Devanagari script labels`) plus `devotional poster art, idol photograph, halo, temple
calendar art`. The narrator bar carries one exact line of Gujarati in Gujarati script.

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — measured at std-10 ch-1: a four-tier ladder — `નીચેના પ્રશ્નો સાથે આપેલા
વિકલ્પોમાંથી સાચો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરી ખરાની (✓) નિશાની કરો.` (2 MCQs) → `નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના એક-એક
વાક્યમાં ઉત્તર લખો :` (2) → `નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના બે-ત્રણ વાક્યમાં ઉત્તર લખો.` (2) → `નીચેના
પ્રશ્નનો સવિસ્તર ઉત્તર લખો.` (1) — preceded by the word apparatus (શબ્દ-સમજૂતી / શબ્દસમૂહ માટે
એક શબ્દ) and followed by **વિદ્યાર્થી પ્રવૃત્તિ** (ચિત્ર દોરો, પદોનું ગાન કરો, વાદ્યોનો પરિચય
મેળવો), **ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ** (prose commentary on the repetitions) and **શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા**
(teacher-addressed — never an exercise, never a topic, never answered as one). Banner wording
micro-varies across the reader (લખો/આપો, વાક્યમાં/વાક્યોમાં, સવિસ્તર/સવિસ્તાર): Agent 1
inventories the exact printed headings per chapter and Agent 10 answers every block from that
inventory — never from this list. Where a topic prepares the ગાન task, name the **લય** and the
**ટેક** as the things the child carries to it.

**Language** — heavy glossing of the medieval and તળપદા forms at point of first use, with the
poet's form kept in the text and the માનક ગુજરાતી equivalent given beside it in `"શબ્દ — અર્થ"`
form. મોરલી glosses `થૈ` and `મારગ` on the page itself: printed glosses are **evidence that the
word stops a reader, not a source to copy** (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`, printed-glossary trap).
The L2 bar sits lower than a first-language pack's — more ordinary Gujarati words genuinely stop a
std 6–10 second-language reader, and ભક્તિ vocabulary (ગિરિધર, દર્શન, કુંજગલી) needs concrete,
not dictionary, glosses. Script discipline per `reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`: માત્રા, અનુસ્વાર,
ચંદ્રબિંદુ and જોડાક્ષર exactly as printed; ળ/લ and શ/ષ/સ are load-bearing; full stop `.` as
printed — the Devanagari danda is never introduced into a Gujarati text.

**Sensitivity** — a devotional text held by children of many faiths. Present it as the chapter
does: literature with a living tradition behind it, taught for its ભાવ and its craft. Never invite
the class to affirm or doubt the devotion, never treat one tradition as the default, and never let
a comparison in. `08_sensitivity.json` guides *how* it is taught; it never censors, and the text is
never softened or cut.

## Recall priors

Three per પદ topic, laddered:

- one **remember** — who is speaking, and what is the line that keeps returning;
- one **understand** — what the speaker is feeling at this moment of the song, in the poem's own
  words;
- one **analyze** — why the poet returns to that line, or why she puts her own name into the last
  line (the છાપ), or what the repeated word does to the sound.

At std 9–10 the third may be **evaluate** where the text carries it. Every answer is a real model
answer, and every answer obeys the poster gate above: it ends inside the poem, not on a rule.
