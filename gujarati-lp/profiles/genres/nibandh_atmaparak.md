## Diagnosis signals

Prose in which **the subject is the writer's own experience and the writer's own voice**. પ્રથમ
પુરુષ throughout — હું, મેં, અમે, મારું — and the "હું" is not a device for telling someone else's
story: it is the thing being looked at. There is no obligation to have a plot, an opponent or a
turning point, and normally there is none. What moves the piece is a **sequence of પ્રસંગ** — a day,
a childhood scrape, an attempt that fails, a thing that is gone — and the writing is honest about
the failures.

**The book names the form in its own intro box**, and that label is the strongest single signal,
though it is evidence and never a verdict:

- "…વિદ્યાર્થીઓને **આત્મકથનાત્મક નિબંધસ્વરૂપ**નો પરિચય કરાવવો." (std-6 ch 15) — the box says the
  form outright, and adds "પાલુબેન અહીં પોતાની દિનચર્યા પોતાના શબ્દોમાં રજૂ કરે છે".
- "અહીં રોજબરોજના જીવનનો એક **રમૂજી પ્રસંગ** સરસ રીતે મુકાયો છે." (std-7 ch 6)
- "આ એક સરસ **હાસ્યનિબંધ** છે, જેમાં વ્યાયામને વિષય તરીકે લઈને નિર્દોષ… હાસ્ય નિપજાવવામાં આવ્યું છે"
  (std-8 ch 3)
- "આ એકમ એક **લલિતનિબંધ** છે… બાનું વ્યક્તિચિત્ર પણ ઊપસ્યું છે" (std-8 ch 5)
- "લેખકની **આત્મકથા** 'સ્મરણયાત્રા'માંથી આ પાઠ લેવામાં આવ્યો છે" (std-8 ch 11) ·
  "…જેમાં લેખકની કિશોરાવસ્થાની એક ઘટના રજૂ થઈ છે" (std-8 ch 15)

  નર્મ-મર્મ ગદ્યમાં નિરૂપણ છે" (std-9 ch 2)

- "ફોટોગ્રાફી નિમિત્તે દેશ-વિદેશમાં ફરતા **લેખકના કેટલાક અનુભવો**… પુસ્તકમાંથી અહીં મૂકવામાં આવ્યા છે"
  (std-9 ch 17) · "મૂળ મરાઠીમાં લખાયેલી **અનુભવ કથા**ના… અનુવાદમાંથી આ અંશ" (std-9 ch 21)

**The person test, and its one honest exception.** Ask who the "હું" is. If it is the writer, this
profile. If it is an invented character, it is fiction however funny (std-10 ch 3 પ્રયાણ narrates in
first person, but the "હું" is અંબારામ, a character in a હાસ્યનવલકથા → `varta`). The exception is
std-7 ch 6 જો કરી જાંબુએ, which is **third person** and is held here anyway — see the હાસ્ય section
below for exactly what that costs.

**Signal C — the સ્વાધ્યાય tells.** Two ladders, both measured.

- **std 6–8: a transfer-composition in the same form.** The book asks the child to produce the genre,
  not to describe it — "'એક સાઈકલ'ની આત્મકથા પર લખો." and "લારીમાં વસ્તુ વેચતા ફેરિયાની દિનચર્યા
  લખો." (std-6 ch 15 blks 18 and 10), "તમારા જીવનમાં બનેલ રમૂજી પ્રસંગ વર્ગમાં કહો." (std-7 ch 6
  blk 9), and the closing ચર્ચા-વિચારણા bullets "મારા બાળપણનું તોફાન" (std-8 ch 11), "મારું સાહસ
  (હાસ્ય)" and "હાસ્ય - ઉત્તમ ઔષધ" (std-8 ch 3 — the only chapter measured with three bullets).
  A block that asks the child to write **as** somebody or something is this genre's fingerprint.
- **std 9–10: a ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ block that teaches the writer's manner.** std-9 ch 2's runs on
  'પૂળો મૂકવો' and then lists six comic વાક્યપ્રયોગો from the text and sends the child to other
  હાસ્યલેખકો; std-9 ch 19's names the essay's લાઘવ ("ઝાડમાં લાકડું જોનારા લોકો વધ્યા") and catalogues
  the તળપદા words that paint the ગ્રામસંસ્કૃતિ; std-9 ch 15's is entirely about the **translator's**
  craft. In this form that block, not the question ladder, is the richest craft evidence in the book.
- **A quoted-line paraphrase block** recurs across both ladders: "નીચેના જેવો અર્થ ધરાવતાં વાક્યો
  પાઠમાંથી શોધો." (std-6 ch 15 blk 6), "વાક્યની નજીકનો અર્થ ધરાવતું વાક્ય એકમમાંથી શોધીને લખો."
  (std-8 ch 15 blk 8, ch 11 blk 5), and std-8 ch 15 blk 4, which prints six of the chapter's own
  spoken lines against three meanings each. The sentence *as the writer said it* is the object of
  study — that is the genre showing through the exercise.

**What is present but not diagnostic.** std-6 ch 15 does print an ordering block, but it orders a
**દિનચર્યા**, not a plot ("પાલુબેનની એક દિવસની ઘટનાઓને યોગ્ય ક્રમમાં ગોઠવો."), and std-8 ch 15 and
std-7 ch 6 print counterfactuals ("જો… હોત તો શું થયું હોત ?"). Neither block makes the chapter a
વાર્તા; both are answered by Agent 10 like any other.

## Lens

**સ્વર + પ્રસંગ + પોતાની જાત પરની નજર**

Whose voice this is, which single પ્રસંગ we are inside, and what the writer is willing to say about
themselves while telling it.

## Guiding question

*લેખક પોતાની જ વાત કરતાં કરતાં પોતાના વિશે શું કબૂલી લે છે?*

The wording above is a **shape**. Each chapter derives its own from its own text and Agent 1 writes
it into `01_meta.json` — for std-6 ch 15 it is about what a day costs પાલુબેન and what changed when
'સેવા'ની બહેનો came; for std-8 ch 3 about how a man who cannot do a દંડબેઠક talks about વ્યાયામ; for
std-9 ch 19 about what the writer can no longer hear from his own પડસાળ. A guiding question that
would fit any first-person chapter in the book has not been derived.

## Explanation unit — **one પ્રસંગ, અથવા વિચારનો એક વળાંક**

One episode, or one movement of the reflection. **The boundary is where the attempt fails or the
mood turns** — not the paragraph break, not the page, not the printed name of a person.

- **Cut at the writer's own turn.** std-6 ch 15's monologue runs by the clock but breaks by પ્રસંગ:
  the pre-dawn ઘરકામ, the જમાપુર market buying, the spreading of the પાથરણું and the બોણી, the
  ઘરાક-ભાવતાલ, the દબાણ raids of the old days ("આખરે નુકસાન તો અમારું જ થતું."), the 'સેવા' turn
  ("ડગલે પગલે થતાં અપમાન બંધ થયાં."), the night cooking, the rolling up of the પાથરણું. "Afternoon"
  is not a topic; a raid is.
- **Never cut a comic turn from its setup.** In std-7 ch 6 each courier is one topic and that topic
  holds both halves — the jamun eaten *and* the ઠળિયા put back. Split them and the chapter becomes a
  list of thefts instead of one joke told five times.
- **A printed internal sub-head IS a cut.** std-9 ch 17 prints centre-bold section titles inside the
  chapter; each begins a topic and the sub-head sits in that topic's `original_chunk` as printed.
  Agent 1 marks them in `00_chapter_normalized.md` with `[[ઘટના: …]]` above the block.
- **A litany inside one movement is one topic.** std-9 ch 19 names ટીટોડી, વનલાવરી, ઢેંક,
  કાળિયોકોશી, પતરંગા, કલકલિયો, શક્કરખોર, નાયણ in a single elegiac sweep — that is one topic about a
  vanished ગ્રામસૃષ્ટિ, not eleven topics of one bird each. Where a creature gets its own scene and
  its own loss, the feeling has moved and a new topic starts. The test is the movement of ભાવ, never
  the count of nouns.
- **A remembered episode keeps its opening.** The સ્મૃતિ hook ("વર્ષો પહેલાં…", "તે દિવસે…") belongs
  to the topic it opens, per cutting rule 2 of `reference/explanation_unit_map.md` — a hook that sits
  before a topic goes to the NEXT topic, never alone.
- **Quoted dialogue creates no boundaries.** std-8 ch 3 and std-8 ch 15 run long dialogue inside
  `''…''` with no speaker labels. Cut on the પ્રસંગ; several turns normally sit in one topic, and a
  turn that carries two episodes is split at the episode, not at the quote mark.
- **The closing gesture is its own topic.** These essays end on an image or a ચોટ, not on a lesson —
  the પાથરણું rolled up and "આવજો ત્યારે પૃથાબેન !", the last line of a હાસ્યનિબંધ. Mark it
  `topic_category: "resolution"`; the attribution and any source or translator credit printed after
  the text go inside that same LAST topic's `original_chunk` (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **Where one પ્રસંગ genuinely does two separable things**, that is what `concepts[]` is for — two
  concepts in one topic, never a third.
- Printed illustrations (std-6 ch 15's market picture, std-7 ch 6's cart) are page matter, not
  reading scenes: they never open a topic of their own.
- Cutting rules 1–5 of `reference/explanation_unit_map.md` apply unchanged, including the one that
  governs everything below: **સ્વાધ્યાય is never a topic.** The ચર્ચા-વિચારણા box, the
  ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ notes, વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ and શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા are apparatus, recorded in
  `exercise_inventory` or `extraction_notes[]` and never cut as reading scenes.

## હાસ્ય — the humour sub-form, and therefore how it is taught

The comedy of these chapters lives in two places and nowhere else: **the writer at their own
expense**, and **timing**. std-8 ch 3 is a slight man solemnly recounting his કુસ્તીના દાવ and
દંડબેઠક; std-9 ch 2 is a narrator with an આંજણી buried alive under everyone's free advice; std-7
ch 6 is one temptation defeating five couriers in a row. The engine of the third is
**repetition-with-escalation**, and its own record says so.

Four things follow.

1. **The explanation names where the laugh lands, in one clause, and moves on.** "અહીં હસવું આવે છે
   કારણ કે…" spread over three sentences kills the chapter. Say it once, at the place it happens.
2. **It never apologises for the joke and never softens it.** No "આ તો મજાક છે", no "ખરેખર તો આવું ન
   કરાય", no moral appended to a comic turn. The chapter is funny on purpose.
3. **Irony is named once and never inverted.** Both std-8 ch 3 and std-9 ch 2 are measured as
   L2-hard precisely here: an L2 child can read a કટાક્ષ sentence as advice. Name it as કટાક્ષ or
   વ્યંગ at the sentence where it occurs — and do not "correct" the sentence into what it literally
   should have said.
4. **The register carries the joke, so the gloss must not flatten it.** std-7 ch 6's own difficulty
   note is explicit: "humour depends on register and repetition — L2 glossing must keep the joke."
   Gloss અહાલેક, માંહ્યલીકોર, પટ કરતાંક, હત્તારીની ! at point of use and keep the printed form as the
   headword (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`).

**The third-person caveat.** std-7 ch 6 is narrated in the third person about અમથાલાલ. It is held
here for its **unit** (one comic turn per topic) and its **gate** (never explain the joke), not for a
voice it does not have. No field may attribute a self-deprecating "હું" or a writer's confession to a
chapter whose `original_chunk` carries none — a hard check, listed below.

## સંસ્મરણ / આત્મકથા — real people, real places

Everything named in these chapters is real: કાકા કાલેલકરનાં સતારા-શાહપુર-બેલગામ and the Marathi
sentences printed among the Gujarati (std-8 ch 11 blk 4 hunts them, so they are kept exactly as
printed); પન્નાલાલ પટેલનો મોડાસા-તલોદ ઝાંપો, બેલગાડી, વીશી (std-8 ch 15); સુધા મૂર્તિનાં કુતમ્મા and
ઐયપ્પા (std-9 ch 15); અશ્વિન મહેતાનાં સિડની and ન્યૂયોર્ક (std-9 ch 17); પ્રકાશ આમટેનું હેમલકસા with
બબલી, કાળુ and મુન્નો named in the book's own table (std-9 ch 21); પાલુબેનનાં જમાપુર માર્કેટ and
દાણાપીઠ (std-6 ch 15).

- **No invented interiority.** A real named person's thought, feeling or motive is written only if
  the chapter states it. "બાને દુઃખ થયું હશે" is invention unless the page says so.
- **No fabricated biography.** The chapter's printed લેખક-પરિચય and intro box are the only sources of
  life-facts, and `reference/no_hallucination_policy.md` governs: empty beats invented.
- **Credits are text.** ('સ્મરણયાત્રા'માંથી), ('પ્રકાશની પગદંડી'માંથી), `અનુવાદક : સોનલ મોદી` —
  transcribed as printed inside the last topic's `original_chunk`, including the printed
  inconsistency std-9 ch 21 carries between પગદંડીઓ (intro) and પગદંડી (end credit). Record the
  inconsistency in `extraction_notes[]`; never resolve it.
- **A translated memoir is still this genre.** std-9 ch 15 and ch 21 are translations, and std-9
  ch 15's own ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ block teaches the translator's choices. Where the book teaches the
  translation as craft, the topics may too — but the writer of the experience stays the "હું".

## Emphasise

- **The voice, quoted.** Open the explanation on the speaker, not on the subject matter: પાલુબેન
  saying "ના રે ના !" and "છોકરાંને ભણાવીશ. તો જ ઉજળા દિવસો આવશે.", the essayist admitting what he
  cannot do. One short quoted phrase inside the explanation is worth a paragraph of description.
- **The admission.** This form's gift to a std 6–10 L2 child is the discovery that a writer is
  allowed to say the attempt failed, the fear was real, the body did not obey. Name it wherever the
  chapter offers it — it is the single most useful thing this genre teaches a child who must write
  about themselves in an exam.
- **The turn.** Where the luck, the mood or the treatment changes: "'સેવા'ની બહેનોએ સાથ આપ્યો",
  and the sentence that measures the change, "ડગલે પગલે થતાં અપમાન બંધ થયાં."
- **Where the laugh lands** — one clause, per the હાસ્ય section.
- **The concrete object.** This સ્વરૂપ is made of specifics and dies when generalised: the પાથરણું,
  the ઠળિયા in a courier's fist, the બેલગાડી at the ઝાંપો, the મંગાળો, the પડસાળ and the નેળિયું,
  the ગોધૂલિ. Let the child hold the object before any abstraction is offered.
- **The spoken register as craft, not error.** "હારુ વઢવા કોણ નવરું હોય ?", દા'ડો, કો'કવાર, મે'નત,
  વરહ, એરુ, અલ્યા, વેંત જેવડો — these are the writing, not mistakes in it. Gloss them; never
  standardise them (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **The essay's own sentence-craft, once, where it works.** std-9 ch 19's લાઘવ, std-9 ch 17's
  unfinished sentence "…અને છતાં…", std-9 ch 15's વર્ણાનુપ્રાસ pairs નાનકડા-રૂપકડા and શીળું સ્મિત.
  Name a device only where it is actually present (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`).
- **One anchor in the child's own world**, Indian and concrete — the ફેરિયો in their own street, a
  કસરત they gave up on, a grandmother's વાડો, a day they were caught out
  (`reference/teaching_voice_gu.md`).

## Avoid (hard gate)

left unaddressed. Each check names the field it is checked against.

1. **Flattening the voice into information.** **Check:** for every topic whose `original_chunk`
   contains a first-person form (હું, મેં, અમે, મારું, મને), the topic's `explanation` names the
   speaker or writer as the grammatical subject of at least its first sentence (પાલુબેન, લેખક,

2. **Converting the essay into a story with a moral.** **Check:** no topic carries

7. **Correcting the spoken register.** **Check:** every colloquial or તળપદું form printed in the

## Priors

**Media** — one image per પ્રસંગ, and two compositions work. The **writer or speaker inside the
moment**: પાલુબેન spreading the પાથરણું at the દાણાપીઠ pitch as the market wakes, a thin man in an
અખાડો attempting a દાવ, a boy and a porter arguing beside a bullock cart at a station gate. And the
**everyday object made literal**: a fistful of ઠળિયા where the jamun should be, an empty પડસાળ where
the birds used to sit. For a હાસ્ય scene, draw the *moment*, never a caricature of the person and
never the punchline as a cartoon gag — the one-sentence depiction test applies, and a picture that
cannot be described as a single moment someone could photograph is a summary and must be re-authored.
For સંસ્મરણ chapters about a real living or historical person, image the **scene**, not an invented
portrait likeness the book does not print; keep the same fixed appearance for a recurring figure
across the chapter's images. Printed photographs and illustrations in the book are page matter, not
scenes. Setting Indian, Gujarat-flavoured where the text is; `generation_prompt` self-contained per
`reference/field_shape_rules.md`; the narrator bar carries one line of Gujarati in Gujarati script,
named exactly. No Gujarati frame pool exists, so every scene is authored: `image_url: ""`,
`reuse_report.reused: 0`, `rejected: []`. This form rarely earns the single permitted `2d_tool`, and
it is still at most one per chapter.

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — *measured, std 6–10 (all five inventories complete).* **std 6–8**: વાતચીત (8–10 oral prompts,
opening on the child's own experience of a ફેરિયો, કસરત, મોસાળ, દાદા-દાદી), "પ્રશ્નોના જવાબ લખો" with
counterfactuals, an event or દિનચર્યા ordering block, a word-bank cloze, dialogue completion followed
by dialogue continuation (std-6 ch 15 blks 4–5), the paraphrase→find-the-sentence block, અ/બ matching,
idiom-judgement ✓ blocks, grammar batteries (સર્વનામ types in std-7 ch 6's green box, present→past
conversion and antonym repair in std-8 ch 3, anusvāra minimal pairs in std-8 ch 5, noun classification
in std-8 ch 15), a paired reading-fluency table (std-8 ch 5 blk 10, ch 15 blk 5), role-play or
અભિનય, the standing L2 block "નીચેનાં વાક્યોનો તમારી પ્રથમ ભાષામાં અનુવાદ કરો.", the
transfer-composition, and a closing ચર્ચા-વિચારણા box in std 8. **std 9–10**: a 3–4 tier ladder —
MCQ with ✓ (one MCQ often tests the સાહિત્યપ્રકાર itself), sometimes કારણ આપો, then બે-ત્રણ વાક્ય,
then પાંચ-છ વાક્ય or સવિસ્તાર — followed by વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ, ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ and શિક્ષકની
ભૂમિકા. std-9 ch 2 prints **no** વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ at all: absence is measured, not a gap.
How topics prepare them: the topic holding a comic turn prepares the quoted-line ભાવાર્થ block; the
topic that glosses the speaker's register prepares the રૂઢિપ્રયોગ and idiom-judgement blocks; the
topic on the essay's own sentence-craft prepares ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ; the closing-gesture topic prepares
the transfer-composition. Name the skill each topic prepares
(`reference/teaching_block_format.md`). **No સ્વાધ્યાય block is ever a topic.**

**Language** — the load here is **spoken register and idiom**, not તત્સમ vocabulary, and that is what
separates this profile's glossing policy from every other prose profile in the pack. Three layers sit
on the page at once. (a) **તળપદાં and બોલચાલનાં રૂપો**, printed with their elisions — દા'ડો,
કો'કવાર, હારુ, મે'નત, વરહ, ડિલ, એરુ, ચિંત્યા, ભણી, પરણ, લેત — kept exactly and glossed, never
standardised. (b) **રૂઢિપ્રયોગ density, the highest measured in the corpus**: std-8 ch 15 prints ten
idioms *and* the corpus's only કહેવત pre-block (ચોરનું મોં કાળું; નસીબ અવળા હોય તો ભોંયમાંથી ભાલા
વાગે), std-8 ch 11 nine, std-8 ch 5 eight, std-8 ch 3 seven beside a શબ્દસમૂહ block. Each idiom is
both a gloss and a craft point — it is *how the writer sounds*. (c) **Period and regional detail**
(બેલગાડી, ટપાલના ટાંગા, વીશી, મંગાળો, ભાગોળ, પડસાળ, નેળિયું, મોભાર, ડીબાં) plus, in std-8 ch 11,
Marathi sentences embedded verbatim in the Gujarati. The printed શબ્દાર્થ box is evidence of
difficulty, never the source of the gloss (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`), and the L2 bar sits lower than
a first-language pack's: an L2 child stops on ભાગોળ and પડસાળ, not only on ગોધૂલિ.
**Extraction trap, specific to this form:** the first-person "મેં" renders with a Devanagari-look
glyph in std-8 ch 3, std-9 ch 2 and std-9 ch 17 — precisely the word a first-person essay repeats
most. The script-purity check must not "fix" it by transliteration; re-read the rendered page and
record the defect in `extraction_notes[]` (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).

**Sensitivity** — this form needs the paragraph. std-6 ch 15 puts a working woman's poverty, the
municipality and police દબાણ raids ("માલ ખટારામાં") and a real named organisation on the page; std-9
ch 21 puts an આદિવાસી region and its people (હેમલકસા) on the page; std-9 ch 15 puts an unschooled
village woman's household on it; std-8 ch 11 and ch 15 carry period village and railway life with its
hierarchies. Agent 8 **flags and guides, never censors**: it says how to teach the passage, not
whether. Keep the chapter's own dignity register — labour is described as work, not as misfortune —
use communities' and people's own printed names, recount the conflict with authority factually
without turning it into a political argument, and do not convert a described custom into a debate
about it. **The humour carries its own sensitivity rule:** where a chapter's comedy touches a
person's hunger, body, poverty, community or accent — the couriers of std-7 ch 6, the Parsi and
Marwadi speech quoted in std-9 ch 2 — the text is kept verbatim and the teaching does **not extend
the joke**, add a fresh one, or invite the class to imitate the accent. Area labels in Gujarati per
the pack convention (ધર્મ, સમુદાય, ક્ષેત્ર, વિકલાંગતા, સંઘર્ષ, જાતિ-ભૂમિકા, સુરક્ષા).

## Recall priors

Two to three per topic, Bloom-laddered, each with a real model answer, `bloom_level` lowercase:

- one **remember** — what happened in this પ્રસંગ, or what the writer tried: what પાલુબેન does before
  દા'ડો ઊગે, what each courier put back in the packet, what the ઉસ્તાદ said.
- one **understand** or **analyze** — why it did not work, what one concrete detail tells us, or
  where the laugh lands and who it is aimed at. Answerable from **this** topic, not from the whole
  chapter, and phrased so that a plot-style answer ("કારણ કે તે ખરાબ પાત્ર હતું") cannot satisfy it.
- one **apply** or **evaluate** — the child's own પ્રસંગ, or their judgement of the writer's. This
  form invites the child to answer back, which is exactly what the book asks: "તમારા જીવનમાં બનેલ
  રમૂજી પ્રસંગ વર્ગમાં કહો.", "મારા બાળપણનું તોફાન", and std-6 ch 15's own routine block "નીચે આપેલી
  ક્રિયાઓ સામે તમારો સમય ઉમેરી વાક્ય લખો." The model answer is a real answer, in a child's voice,
  and it says which part of the chapter it reasons from.

Recall answers are bound by the same hard gates as `explanation`: no invented interiority or
biography, no fact from outside the chapter, no appended બોધ, no explained or softened joke, no
pity register, and the printed spoken forms quoted as printed.
