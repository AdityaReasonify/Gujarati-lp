# લોકગીત

> Provenance: written from GSEB pages actually read — std-6 ch 4 આવ્યો મેહુલો (વર્ષા-લોકગીત, the
> textbook's own label), std-8 ch 7 કંકોતરી (લગ્નગીત), std-9 ch 20 હરિ ! આવોને
> (ભક્તિ-આતિથ્ય લોકગીત). std-10 ch 13 ક્યાં રે વાગી: first page rendered and its full સ્વાધ્યાય
> read, verse body beyond p. 81 not transcribed — treated as label-and-apparatus evidence.
> See `reference/corpus/std-{6,8,9,10}_inventory.md`.

A song **nobody signed**. It is not a poem that happens to sound folk; it is a text the books
themselves present as having no author — std-6 ch 4's intro says it in as many words:
"લોકગીતના કોઈ કવિ હોતા નથી. લોકગીત લોકો દ્વારા સર્જાય છે.", and std-9 ch 20's
શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા gives the definition the pack uses:
"લોકો દ્વારા રચાયેલું, લોકો માટે અને લોકો થકી ગવાતું ગીત એટલે લોકગીત."

## Diagnosis signals

**The author slot prints a genre label where a name would go.** `- લોકગીત` (std-6 ch 4, std-8 ch 7),
`સંકલિત` (std-9 ch 20), or the word લોકગીત in the chapter table's author column (std-10 ch 13). There
is no કવિ-પરિચય paragraph, no birth-death line, no source-collection credit of the
`('એકાંત'માંથી)` kind. This single signal routes more chapters here than any other.

**The blue intro box names the form and prescribes singing.** "આ એક સુંદર લોકગીત છે… કાવ્યનું
સમૂહગાન કરાવવું." (std-6 ch 4); "આ એક લગ્નગીત છે. લગ્નગીત એ લોકગીતનો જ એક પ્રકાર છે… લગ્નગીતોમાં
તળપદા શબ્દો સાદા લોકઢાળમાં ગોઠવાઈને ધાર્યો પ્રભાવ પાડતા હોય છે." (std-8 ch 7); "લોકગીતમાં સમગ્ર
પ્રજાસમૂહની ભાવનાઓનું નિરૂપણ થતું હોય છે." (std-9 ch 20). A **સમૂહગાન** instruction — the class sings
it *together*, not one reciter — is the performance tell of this form.

**An occasion, not a mood.** Every measured instance is tied to something people do: the first rain
over the fields (વર્ષા-લોકગીત), sending out the wedding invitation (લગ્નગીત), receiving a guest
into the house (આતિથ્ય), the evening a flute is heard (std-10 ch 13). Ask *when would somebody
actually sing this?* — a લોકગીત always has an answer.

**તળપદા forms carrying the song.** ઓતર, દખ્ખણ, મેહુલો, ડેડકડી, ધોરી, ખેડુ, અસતરી, છલી વળ્યાં,
વરસિયા (std-6 ch 4 — the book glosses each of them itself and corrects none); રોપિયો, હોંશે, બેનીબા
(std-8 ch 7); દૈશ, નદીયું (std-9 ch 20); વજોગણ, સૈયર, હેર્યાં, કણ, કોઠી (std-10 ch 13). Filler
syllables belong to the singing and are printed: "જી રે" inserted mid-line (std-10 ch 13),
"રે !" and "હરિ !" holding the beat.

**A ટેક that returns unchanged**, usually printed once in full and thereafter as shorthand the
singer is expected to complete — `આવ્યો...` after each couplet (std-6 ch 4), the full refrain couplet
"કંકુ છાંટી કંકોતરી મોકલો, / કેસર છાંટી કંકોતરી મોકલો," between rounds with a trailing
`... કંકોતરી...` (std-8 ch 7), `- શેરી વળાવી` in the right margin after every couplet-pair
(std-9 ch 20), `– સમી સાંજની` set with a leading dash right of each line (std-10 ch 13).

**સ્વાધ્યાય tell.** Two blocks are near-diagnostic. (1) A **તળપદા-શબ્દ hunt** run in reverse —
std-6 ch 4 item 2 gives the standard word and asks which word the poem used instead
("ઉદાહરણ મુજબ આપેલા શબ્દને માટે કવિતામાં વપરાયેલો શબ્દ લખો." — ઉત્તર → ઓતર), then item 3 asks the
child for the તળપદા words of their own area. (2) A **fill-the-song-from-a-family-elder** task —
std-8 ch 7 item 8: "નીચે આપેલા લગ્નગીતને તમારાં માતા-પિતા અથવા દાદા-દાદીની મદદ લઈને પૂર્ણ કરો."
No other form asks the child to go find the rest of the text at home, because no other form is
transmitted that way. std-10 ch 13's વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ does the same outward move
(લોકગીતો ગાવાની સ્પર્ધા; મેઘાણીની 'રઢિયાળી રાત').

**Near misses — route on the tell, not on the feel.**

| **લોકગીત** | ઊર્મિકાવ્ય-ગીત | the author slot names a living or modern poet **and the ટેક develops** across the poem; "લોકઢાળમાં રચાયેલી" in a કૃતિ-પરિચય means composed *in* the folk manner (std-9 ch 12 તો જાણું, સુરેશ દલાલ, lokgeet idiom and a variant refrain; std-10 પૂરક 5 દીવડો) → `urmikavya_geet.md` |
| **લોકગીત** (devotional) | પદ / ભજન | a **છાપ inside the verse** ("બાઈ મીરાં કે…", "ભણે નરસૈંયો"), a named ભક્ત, medieval forms (std-10 ch 1 મોરલી) → `pad_bhajan.md`. Devotion alone does not move a chapter: std-9 ch 20 હરિ ! આવોને is devotional, anonymous and folk — it stays here |

## Lens

**કંઠ + ધ્રુવપંક્તિ + લોકજીવન**

The voice that sings it, the line it keeps coming back to, and the life it comes out of. All three
are collective: not a poet's feeling but a village's, not a reciter's line but the group's line
(`reference/teaching_lens_map.md`).

## Guiding question

*આ ગીત કયા પ્રસંગે, કોણ, અને કેમ ગાય છે?*

A shape, not a text. Each chapter derives its own — who sweeps a street before whose arrival; whose
uncle gets the invitation first; what the fields do when the first rain lands. The derived question
goes in `01_meta.json`, never copied from this file.

## Explanation unit — **one કડી, or one refrain-round**

Two grains, and the printed layout tells you which one this song has
(`reference/explanation_unit_map.md`).

- **કડી-wise** where the song moves picture to picture. std-6 ch 4 opens on a triplet
  ("ઓતર ગાજ્યા ને દખ્ખણ વરસિયા રે ! / મેહુલે માંડ્યાં મંડાણ. / આવ્યો ધરતીનો ધણી મેહુલો રે !") and
  then gives four couplets, each closing on the shorthand `આવ્યો...` — the opening triplet is one
  topic, each couplet is one topic.
- **રાઉન્ડ-wise where the song cycles a list.** std-8 ch 7 sends the same કંકોતરી to કાકા, then
  મામા, then માસી, with the identical refrain couplet between rounds: the **round** is the unit, and
  the topic holds the round *plus* the refrain that frames it. std-9 ch 20 runs seven
  couplet-pairs down one hospitality list — ઉતારા-ઓરડા, દાતણ-દાડમી, નાવણ-કૂંડિયું, ભોજન-લાપસી,
  મુખવાસ-એલચી, રમત-સોગઠાં, પોઢણ-ઢોલિયા — each pair a round.

Never split a round across two topics, and never separate a ટેક from the round it frames. Two
adjacent rounds may join into one topic when they genuinely do one thing (receiving a guest at the
door: ઉતારા then દાતણ); wanting to join three means the cut has stopped following the song.

Keep the printed line breaks and the printed refrain shorthand exactly as set inside
`original_chunk` (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`). The shorthand IS the printed text; expanding
`- શેરી વળાવી` or `આવ્યો...` into full refrain lines inside `original_chunk` is a verbatim defect
owned by Agent 5. Expand it consciously in the `[[ટેક]]` marker scaffolding of
`00_chapter_normalized.md` and in the `explanation` — that is where the child is told what the
singers actually sing back.

## The ટેક here is ground, not argument — and therefore how it is taught

This is the whole reason this profile is separate from `urmikavya_geet.md`, and the rule **inverts**.

In an ઊર્મિકાવ્ય-ગીત the teaching often lives in a ટેક whose words change: std-9 ch 3 returns
`જ્યાં જ્યાં વસે એક ગુજરાતી` as `જ્યાં જ્યાં બોલાતી ગુજરાતી`, and the change is the argument. That
profile therefore makes a changed-words ટેક a topic at each occurrence, and the explanation's job is
to say **what changed**.

A લોકગીત cannot work that way. A group of people singing together has to know exactly where to come
back in — so the returning line is fixed by the singing itself, and it is printed as shorthand
precisely because everyone already knows it. **The ટેક carries no new claim. It is the ground the
song stands on and returns to.** In all four measured chapters the refrain returns word for word.

Therefore:

1. **Teach the ટેક once, in the topic where it is first printed in full** — what it means, who sings
   it (usually the whole group, while one voice carries the round), and why the song keeps coming
   home to it. That topic's explanation is allowed a sentence on the returning itself: a song for a
   crowd needs a line the crowd owns.
2. **Every later topic references it, never re-teaches it.** The later topic lists the ટેક topic in
   `depends_on`, quotes at most the shorthand as printed, and spends its words on what THIS round
   adds.
3. **The explanation must not hunt for what the ટેક changed.** There is nothing to find. An
   explanation or a recall question asking "ટેકના શબ્દો ક્યાં બદલાયા?" of an unchanged refrain
   invents a change the page does not print, and is a hard fail below.
4. **The list is the argument instead.** Where the song cycles, teach why the list runs in the order
   it runs: the invitation goes out in kinship order (કાકા → મામા → માસી); the guest is received in
   the order of a real visit (a room, then દાતણ, then a bath, then લાપસી, then એલચી, then સોગઠાં,
   then a ઢોલિયો to sleep on). Reading that order aloud is reading the household.

If a chapter routed here *does* print two different ટેક wordings, that is a diagnosis signal, not a
licence: record it in `structure_inventory`, re-check the author slot, and consider
`urmikavya_geet.md` before treating the change as folk variation.

## The appended song — std-6 ch 4's measured trap

std-6 ch 4 prints a **second, complete poem** in a yellow "ગાઈએ" box at the end of the chapter —
"મેહુલો", by ત્રિભુવન વ્યાસ ("આવ રે મેહુલા આવ, મેહુલા અષાઢના રે…"), an authored poem for singing,
sitting under the same rain subject as the લોકગીત. It is neither exercises nor part of the
લોકગીત.

Handle it under the mixed-chapter protocol (`reference/genre_diagnosis.md`,
`profiles/genres/_genre_index.md`): the લોકગીત is dominant and sets `genre`, the lens and the
`guiding_question`; the appended poem is recorded in `extraction_notes[]`; where the book asks
nothing of it, it becomes neither a topic nor an exercise item; where a topic is cut for it, it is
cut and taught under `urmikavya_geet.md` — it has a named poet — and never merged into the
લોકગીત's units. The green chapter-final grammar box ("વિશેષણ વિશે જાણીએ") is apparatus, never a
topic.

## Emphasise

- **The occasion, concretely.** Who is doing what while this is sung — a courtyard being swept and
  strewn with ફૂલ, a કંકોતરી being dabbed with કંકુ and કેસર, ધોરી taking the yoke, ખેડુ picking up
  the બેવડી રાશ. The song lives in an action; the explanation starts there.
- **The group voice.** આપણે, not "the poet". Say who sings and who sings back. A લોકગીત has no
  કવિ to attribute a feeling to, and that absence is worth one plain sentence in the first topic.
- **The તળપદો શબ્દ as the thing itself**, glossed at point of use and left standing: ડેડકડી is what
  the song calls a દેડકી, and std-6 ch 4's own item 13 says why — "તેનાથી માધુર્ય પેદા થયું છે."
  That is a craft observation the child can make, not a correction.
- **What this round adds to the list**, when the song cycles. Each round is one more preparation,
  one more relative, one more thing the rain sets moving.
- **The ટેક's returning, once.** Why the line comes back at all — and then let it be.
- **Sound as content.** પુનરાવર્તન, વર્ણાનુપ્રાસ ("શેરી સજજ કરું", "નદીયુંના નીર" — std-9 ch 20's own
  ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ names these), the filler "જી રે" that lets a line breathe, the ellipsis that hands
  the line back to the crowd. Name one or two per topic, quote them, say what they do
  (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`).
- **The cultural furniture, plainly** — માણેકથંભ, મોસાળું, પીઠી, લાપસી, ઢોલિયો, સોગઠાં. Explain the
  object; do not turn it into a lesson about tradition.

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these per topic; Agent 13 blocks on them; the structural ones are
checked at Agent 4.

- **Hunting for a change in an unchanged ટેક — structural, checked at Agent 4.** If every `[[ટેક]]`
  marker in `00_chapter_normalized.md` carries identical wording, then (a) exactly ONE topic carries
  a `[[ટેક]]` marker, (b) every later topic whose `original_chunk` contains the refrain or its
  printed shorthand lists that topic in `depends_on`, and (c) no `explanation`, `concept_bullets`
  line, `important_points` line, `recall_questions[].prompt` or `recall_questions[].answer` contains
  બદલા / બદલાય / બદલાયા / બદલાયું / ફેરફાર / નવા શબ્દો in a sentence that also names ટેક or
  ધ્રુવપંક્તિ. Where `structure_inventory` records two or more distinct ટેક wordings, (a) and (c)
  are lifted and the chapter's routing is re-checked first.
- **Naming an author.** No `explanation`, `real_life_example`, `brief_summary`, `summary`,
  `detailed_summary`, `publication_text`, `figures_of_speech[].note` or `recall_questions[].answer`
  contains a personal name in the role of the song's composer, and none contains the word કવિએ or
  કવિની attached to such a name. Any proper name in these fields must occur in this chapter's
  `original_chunk` or in `01_meta.json`. The books print `- લોકગીત` / `સંકલિત`; an attributed poet
  here is invention (`reference/no_hallucination_policy.md`).
- **Standardising the dialect.** Every string left of the em-dash in `key_terms` occurs
  character-for-character in this topic's `original_chunk` — ઓતર — ઉત્તર દિશા is correct,
  ઉત્તર — ઓતર is not. No `explanation` quotes a line of this topic with a તળપદો form replaced by
  its માનક equivalent: every quoted run of three or more words matches `original_chunk` exactly. The
  standard word belongs on the right of the gloss, never in place of the printed one
  (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`, `reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **Deleting the singing syllables.** `original_chunk` reproduces જી રે, રે !, હરિ ! and the printed
  refrain shorthand exactly as set, including the leading dash and its right-margin position as a
  line of its own where the page sets it that way (`– સમી સાંજની`, `- શેરી વળાવી`). No field calls
  these words extra, meaningless, filler or unnecessary; they are named as what holds the ઢાળ.
- **Expanded refrain inside the verbatim.** No `original_chunk` contains a refrain line that the
  printed page renders only as shorthand. Expansion lives in `explanation` and in the `[[ટેક]]`
  marker line, never in `original_chunk` (Agent 5 owns this defect).
- **Tacked-on બોધ.** No `explanation`, `summary` family field, `concept_bullets`/`important_points`
  line or recall answer ends on an exhortation of the shape "આપણે … જોઈએ" / "…રાખવું જોઈએ" /
  "…કરવો જોઈએ" unless those exact words occur in this topic's `original_chunk`. A hospitality song
  is not a lesson in manners; a rain song is not a lesson in gratitude. **Poster test:** if a
  topic's closing sentence could be printed on a classroom poster with the song deleted, it is
  wrong.
- **Theology or comparative religion.** For a devotional લોકગીત (std-9 ch 20), no field explains
  who હરિ is doctrinally, compares traditions, or asserts a belief. The song's own content is a
  household preparing for a guest it loves; teach that (`reference/global_content_rules.md`).
- **Over-explaining the occasion into a custom lecture.** No `explanation` states a rule about how
  weddings, guests or festivals *should* be conducted, and none names dowry, expense norms or family
  obligation as something the child ought to do. std-8 ch 7's own ચર્ચા-વિચારણા box raises
  "લગ્ન સાદાઈથી કરવાં જોઈએ કે નહીં" — that debate belongs to Agent 10's exercise answer, not to a
  teaching topic.
- **Invented craft.** Every `figures_of_speech[].lines` string appears character-for-character inside
  this topic's `original_chunk` (mechanical check at Agent 13). `[]` is a correct and complete
  answer; a named device that is not on the page is a hard fail.
- **Literal reading of a figurative line.** No topic states as fact something the song states as
  image — the ધરતી has no ધણી, the ડેડકડી grants no આશિષ. Keep it a delight, not a confusion.
- **Merging the appended poem.** No topic's `original_chunk` contains a line from an appended
  "ગાઈએ"-box poem or from any authored poem printed alongside the લોકગીત, and no `key_terms`,
  `figures_of_speech` or `rhyme_scheme` entry is drawn from it (std-6 ch 4).
- **No numbers in display text.** `"બીજી કડીમાં"`, never `"કડી 2માં"` — numbers live in ids and
  provenance fields only.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per કડી or per round, showing **that unit's occasion**: the first rain
striking a ખેતર with the ધોરી under the yoke and the ખેડુ with his રાશ; a courtyard swept and
strewn with ફૂલ before a guest arrives; a family dabbing કંકુ and કેસર onto a કંકોતરી under a તોરણ.
Show **people singing together** where the ટેક is the subject — a group at a મંડપ, women at a
courtyard, children in a doorway — never a single poet at a desk, because this form has none. A
devotional લોકગીત's image is the household and its preparations, **not** an iconographic portrait
of the deity (`agents/09_media_planning.md`, `agents/08_sensitivity_safety.md`). The ટેક topic's
image is the framing scene; later round topics show their own item from the list and never repeat
it. Indian setting, soft digital watercolour, 16:9, narrator bar carrying one exact Gujarati line.
≤1 `2d_tool` per chapter, and a લોકગીત rarely needs one.

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — per the inventories, and never assumed: std-6 ch 4 carries eighteen numbered blocks
with **no printed સ્વાધ્યાય banner** (the intro box uses the word anyway — record the absence, do
not invent the banner) — વાતચીત, standard-word→તળપદો-word substitution, a local-dialect list task,
true/false against the poem, four short answers, letter-play word building, a comprehension passage
with MCQs, agreement and conjunction drills, વિરુદ્ધાર્થી and synonym substitution, the
diminutive-coinage item (ડેડકડી → ગાવલડી), a picture-description task, visarga reading,
વિશેષણ collocation and cloze, and a first-language translation block. std-8 ch 7 adds
find-the-line-for-this-ભાવ, strike-the-wrong-word, a word-bank cloze, question-formation,
complete-the-લગ્નગીત-with-a-family-elder, a mock કંકોતરી fill, active→દ્વારા-passive,
redundancy deletion, kinship-term rewriting and translation, with પ્રવૃત્તિ numbered `14.` here and
un-numbered elsewhere. std-9 ch 20 and std-10 ch 13 run the late-book four-tier ladder
(✓-MCQ → એક-એક વાક્ય → બે-ત્રણ વાક્ય → સવિસ્તર/સવિસ્તાર) followed by વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ,
ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ and શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા. Where a topic prepares the તળપદા-શબ્દ item, gloss that exact
word at point of use; where a topic prepares the ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ note on પુનરાવર્તન or
વર્ણાનુપ્રાસ, name that sound in its explanation first. Exercises are never topics — Agent 10 owns
every one of them (`reference/exercise_alignment.md`).

**Language** — તળપદી ગુજરાતી is the register, and it is the content. Gloss at point of first use in
`"શબ્દ — અર્થ"` form and **never correct toward માનક ગુજરાતી** — the hard gate above is the
machine-checkable half of a rule the books themselves keep: std-6 ch 4 prints its own શબ્દાર્થ box
for ઓતર, દખ્ખણ, મંડાણ, મેહુલો, છલી વળ્યાં, હિલોળ, ડેડકડી, ધોરી, આશિષ, ખેડુ, બેવડી, રાશ, અસતરી, and
corrects none of them; std-9 ch 20 files દૈશ under a printed **તળપદા શબ્દો** sub-head. The printed
box is evidence of what stopped the editor, not the limit of what stops an L2 child — the bar for
glossing sits **lower** here, and the occasion vocabulary (માણેકથંભ, મોસાળું, પીઠી, લાપસી, સોગઠાં,
કોઠી) stops L2 readers as hard as the dialect does (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`,
`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`). Folk plurals and elisions — નદીયું, ઊંચક્યો'તો, પો'રો — are
લય, explained, never rewritten.

**Sensitivity** — this form needs the paragraph. Devotional લોકગીત (std-9 ch 20) is taught as
literature with a living singing tradition — the songs still sung at ગરબા, at a મંડપ, at a
પ્રભાતિયું hour — and never as doctrine or comparison. Wedding songs carry kinship structures,
gendered roles and family-expense talk (std-8 ch 7's own debate box; std-6 ch 4's
"અસતરીએ લીધાં નાનાં બાળ"): name what the song shows, in the song's own time and place, without
prescribing it to the child and without editorialising against it. Flag and guide, never censor
(`agents/08_sensitivity_safety.md`); standard area labels ધર્મ, સમુદાય, ક્ષેત્ર, જાતિ-ભૂમિકા.

## Recall priors

Per topic, two to three, Bloom-laddered, each with a real model answer:

- one **remember** — what this કડી or round names: who is sung to, what is prepared, what the rain
  set moving, which તળપદો શબ્દ the song uses for a known thing;
- one **understand** — what this round adds to the song's list, or what the ટેક line means and why
  the singers come back to it (asked **once**, in the ટેક's own topic);
- one **apply** or **analyze** — on the occasion in the child's own world (કયા પ્રસંગે તમારા ઘરમાં
  ગીત ગવાય છે?), on the order of the list (મહેમાનનું સ્વાગત આ જ ક્રમે કેમ થાય છે?), or on a sound the
  song repeats.

**Never** an "ટેકમાં શું બદલાયું?" question where the ટેક does not change — that is the changed-ટેક
ladder of `urmikavya_geet.md` and it does not belong to this form. Where a chapter genuinely prints
two ટેક wordings, re-check the routing before writing that question.
