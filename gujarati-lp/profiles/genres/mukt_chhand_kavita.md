## Diagnosis signals

Verse — printed as lines with deliberate ragged right edges and a lot of white space — with **no
છંદ, no પ્રાસ and no repeating unit**. Line lengths vary freely: in std-9 ch 10 one line runs the
width of the column and the poem then hangs a single short line, `બીજું એને કાંઈ ભાવતું નથી,`, on
its own. There is no ટેક returning at intervals, no કડી of equal length, no couplet, no છાપ.

Blank lines group the text loosely — std-9 ch 10 prints four verse-paragraphs of **3 / 4 / 2 / 2**
lines plus that hanging line. Those groups are **not કડી**: they are uneven, they are not sung, and
counting them answers a different question every time. Treat a printed gap as **evidence** for a
boundary, never as a countable unit. (`reference/explanation_unit_map.md` states the same thing in
one line: there is no કડી to count.)

**The intro box does not name the form here, so read the other three signals.** std-9 ch 10's box
describes only what the poem does — "આ કાવ્ય જગતની પ્રવર્તમાન સ્થિતિનો સચોટ ચિતાર વ્યક્ત કરે છે…
કાવ્યને અંતે કવિ આક્રોશપૂર્વક કહે છે કે મારે કવિ નથી થવું, હું જંતુનાશક દવા થાઉં તો બસ" — and it is
the **કવિ-પરિચય** that carries the form word: `'પ્રબલગતિ' મુખ્યત્વે ગદ્યકાવ્યોનો સંગ્રહ`. A
કૃતિ-પરિચય or કવિ-પરિચય saying ગદ્યકાવ્ય / અછાંદસ / મુક્ત છંદ routes here. The author slot names a
**modern poet with an era line** (`પ્રિયકાન્ત મણિયાર (જન્મ : 24-1-1927, અવસાન : 25-6-1976)`;
`પન્ના નાયક`), never `- લોકગીત`, never a medieval ભક્ત.

**સ્વાધ્યાય tell — what is missing decides.** std-9 ch 10 asks MCQ (one of them a શબ્દાર્થ item on
`માણસનું ફૂટવું`, one a સરખામણી with ઊધઈ), `નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના બે-ત્રણ વાક્યમાં ઉત્તર લખો :` (2 Qs) and
`નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના સવિસ્તારા વાક્યમાં ઉત્તર લખો :` (2 Qs — the banner really is printed
"સવિસ્તારા વાક્યમાં"; transcribe the misprint). Its **ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ** block names વિરોધી ચિત્રો,
the ઊધઈ રૂપક, the પુનરાવર્તન `વાર વાર વેચે છે` / `કિલો કિલો વેચે છે` / `થોડી થોડી રેડે છે`, and વ્યંગ.
Its વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ asks for a ચર્ચાસભા, a નોંધ and a મુલાકાત — **not a ગાન, not an અભિનય-ગાન,
and no પ્રાસ-finding item anywhere.** A poem unit whose exercises never ask the class to sing it and
never ask for a rhyme is very rarely a ગીત.

**Tested FIRST in the verse branch, before પદ / દુહો / ગીત.** Every other verse profile's cutting
rule presupposes a repeating unit — one દુહો, one પદ, one કડી — and a unit-first test run on
અછાંદસ matches nothing, then falls through to whichever thematic profile is nearest, which silently
invents કડી boundaries that are not on the page. Ask the negative question first:
*શું અહીં કોઈ પુનરાવર્તિત એકમ છે ખરો?* Only if the answer is yes does the length test in
`reference/genre_diagnosis.md` mean anything.

## Lens

**ચિત્ર + મૌન + પંક્તિ-ભંગ** — the image, the silence the white space makes, and the break that puts
it there (`reference/teaching_lens_map.md`).

મૌન is the term that carries this form's weight and it is the one an L2 child has never met as a
craft word: the gap after `બીજું એને કાંઈ ભાવતું નથી,` is doing work, and the explanation has to
say what work. Name whichever of the three this વિચાર-એકમ actually carries; never all three by habit.

## Guiding question

*પંક્તિ અહીં જ કેમ તૂટે છે?*

A shape, not a text. Each chapter derives its own from its own poem — what the poet puts on a line
by itself and why; where the sentence is made to wait; what the poem refuses to say out loud. The
derived question goes in `01_meta.json`, never copied from this file.

## Explanation unit — **one વિચાર-એકમ**

A **વિચાર-એકમ** is a stretch of the poem held together by one movement of thought, and it ends where
the image or the address changes. Because the form prints no unit boundary, the cut is a **reading
judgement** and must be defended from the text. Three usable boundaries:

- **a turn in the address** — the speaker stops describing others and starts speaking for himself
  (std-9 ch 10 turns from `એ લોકો` to `મારે કવિ થવું જ નથી.`);
- **a completed બિંબ** — an image is introduced, extended and closed before the next begins (the
  કાપડના તાકા and the ઉઘાડો માણસ are one such pair; the ઊધઈ is another);
- **a syntactic close** — a full stop after several lines that have run on. GSEB Gujarati prints
  the full stop `.`, and the Devanagari daṇḍa is never introduced
  (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).

Rules:

- **Record the boundary reasoning in the topic's own terms.** For every cut, the `explanation` must
  be able to say *what changes here*. If it cannot, the cut is arbitrary and belongs elsewhere.
  The reasoning is also written into `extraction_notes[]` as a judgement, never presented as printed
  structure.
- **Do not use the printed gaps as કડી.** Use them as evidence among others. Four uneven
  verse-paragraphs are not four topics by arithmetic.
- **A repeated frame is one movement, not one topic per repetition.** std-9 ch 10's
  `એ લોકો પહેલાં… પછી જ્યારે… ત્યારે…` climbs કાપડ → ધાન્ય → ઔષધ. Cutting per rung destroys the only
  thing the climb does, which is accelerate the accusation. One વિચાર-એકમ; the rungs go in
  `concepts[]`.
- **A hanging line belongs with the movement it completes**, not as a topic of its own —
  `બીજું એને કાંઈ ભાવતું નથી,` closes the ઊધઈ image and is taught inside it.
- **Where an opening line returns at the end it is a frame, not a ટેક.** The closing occurrence
  belongs with the final વિચાર-એકમ.

**Markers.** An અછાંદસ chapter's `00_chapter_normalized.md` carries **no `[[કડી n]]` and no
`[[ટેક]]` markers at all**. Agent 1 marks each authored boundary as
`[[વિચાર-એકમ: <opening words>]]`, one line above the block, following §3.3's open colon-form (the
same shape as `[[ઘટના: …]]`), so Agent 4's marker-count equality check keeps its teeth: the number of
`[[વિચાર-એકમ]]` markers equals the number of topics carrying them. See Open questions in the porting
notes — this marker name is an extension of §3.3's listed set and needs confirmation in the brief.

## Emphasise

- **The પંક્તિ-ભંગ as the craft.** This is the form's one deliberate device and the child has never
  been taught it. Where a short line stands alone, say what standing alone does to it. Where a
  sentence spills across three lines, say what the delay buys.
- **બિંબ over અલંકાર.** અછાંદસ often carries no named અલંકાર at all, and `figures_of_speech: []` is
  the correct, honest answer when nothing is there (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`). Where the page
  itself names one — std-9 ch 10's ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ block names the ઊધઈ **રૂપક** outright — quote the
  line and say what it does. What the form always carries is images, and those are teachable
  without a label.
- **Who is speaking, to whom, about whom.** These poems are addressed or accusing. std-9 ch 10 has
  an unnamed `એ લોકો` and an `હું` who arrives only at the end. Identify all three positions early;
  the whole reading depends on it.
- **પુનરાવર્તન as structure, not decoration.** `વાર વાર`, `કિલો કિલો`, `થોડી થોડી` — the repetition
  is the poem's spine, and the inventory flags that an L2 child must be **taught** the
  repetition-structure because there is no narrative scaffold to hold on to.
- **Everyday words doing heavy work.** `સંઘરવું`, `વેચે છે`, `ભાવતું નથી` are plain; the meaning is
  not. Resist finding the difficulty in the words instead of in the thought — while still glossing
  the genuinely closed vocabulary (`તાકા`, `થાન`, `વાર`, `ઔષધ`).
- **The absence of પ્રાસ is a choice.** Say so once, plainly, and say what it buys — speech that
  sounds like a person talking rather than a poem reciting.
- **The વ્યંગ, held as વ્યંગ.** `મારે કવિ થવું જ નથી.` means the opposite of what it says. Teach the
  bitterness as the poem's, and let the child hear it rather than be told a conclusion.

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these per topic; Agent 13 blocks on them; the structural ones are
checked at Agent 4.

- **Naming a છંદ.** No `explanation`, `real_life_example`, `summary`, `brief_summary`,
  `detailed_summary`, `concept_bullets`/`important_points` line, `figures_of_speech[].note` or
  `recall_questions[].answer` contains a છંદ name (દુહો, સોરઠો, ચોપાઈ, ઝૂલણા, હરિગીત, સવૈયા,
  મંદાક્રાન્તા, શિખરિણી, અનુષ્ટુપ, વસંતતિલકા …) unless that word occurs in this chapter's printed
  text or its printed શબ્દ-સમજૂતી box.
- **Inventing કડી.** The token `કડી` appears in **no** display field of a chapter whose root `genre`
  is અછાંદસ, and `00_chapter_normalized.md` contains zero `[[કડી` occurrences. Numbering
  "પહેલી કડી / બીજી કડી" for a poem that has none is the failure this profile exists to prevent.
- **Imposing a ટેક.** `00_chapter_normalized.md` contains zero `[[ટેક]]` markers, and the tokens
  `ટેક` and `ધ્રુવપંક્તિ` appear in no teaching field unless they occur in this chapter's printed
  text. An anaphora is not a ટેક (checked at Agent 4 against the near-miss row above).
- **"Fixing" the line breaks.** Every topic's `original_chunk` reproduces the printed lines exactly:
  the newline count equals the printed line count for that stretch, no two printed lines are joined
  into one, no printed line is split, trailing commas stay (`બીજું એને કાંઈ ભાવતું નથી,`), and the
  printed gaps between verse-paragraphs are preserved as printed. Owned by Agent 5, checked
  mechanically at Agent 13 (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **Filling `rhyme_scheme` with anything but the truth.** For this form
  `{"pattern": "અછાંદસ", "rhyming_words": [], "note": …}` — the note says there is no repeating
  pattern and, where true, what stands in its place (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`). A non-empty
  `rhyming_words` on an અછાંદસ poem is a hard fail; `overall_rhyme_scheme` follows the same rule.
- **Invented અલંકાર.** Every `figures_of_speech[].lines` string appears character-for-character
  inside this topic's `original_chunk` (mechanical check at Agent 13). `[]` is a correct and
  complete answer.
- **Prose-summarising and calling it explanation.** Every topic's `explanation` contains at least one
  word that occurs in that topic's own `original_chunk`, **and** either a non-empty
  `figures_of_speech[]` or one sentence naming this વિચાર-એકમ's પંક્તિ-ભંગ, અવકાશ/મૌન, પુનરાવર્તન or
  બિંબ. The lines and their breaks are the poem; a paraphrase deletes it.
- **Cutting one line per topic** because the lines are short. The number of topics is at most half
  the number of printed verse lines, and no topic's `original_chunk` is a single printed line unless
  `extraction_notes[]` records the defence for that cut.
- **Attaching a બોધ.** No sentence of any teaching field is an exhortation of the shape
  "આપણે … જોઈએ" / "…રાખવું જોઈએ" / "…કરવો જોઈએ", and the nouns બોધ / શિખામણ / ઉપદેશ do not appear in
  teaching fields unless the printed text uses them. An આક્રોશ is not advice; a poem about hoarding
  is not a lesson in generosity.
- **Naming the poem's target.** Where the poem's accused party is unnamed — `એ લોકો` — no teaching
  field, prompt, or recall answer supplies a real-world referent (a caste, community, religion,
  region, party, or named trade) that the printed page does not name. See Sensitivity below; this is
  a `severity: "hard"` item for Agent 8 as well as Agent 7.
- **No numbers in display text.** Numbers live in ids and provenance fields only.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per વિચાર-એકમ, built from the poem's **own બિંબ** rather than from an invented
scene: the stacked કાપડના તાકા beside a man with nothing on, sacks of ધાન્ય behind a shutter, a
medicine bottle held back. Where the poem is an address, the picture may hold the two positions;
where it widens, the picture widens. **Never literalise a metaphor into an object** — the ઊધઈ is a
રૂપક for a person and must not be drawn as a termite eating currency notes. Never illustrate the
moral, never put the whole poem in one frame. Indian setting, soft digital watercolour, 16:9,
narrator bar carrying one exact Gujarati line. ≤1 `2d_tool` per chapter; this form rarely needs one.

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — expect, per the std-9 measurement: MCQ including a શબ્દાર્થ item and a
સરખામણી/રૂપક item; `નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના બે-ત્રણ વાક્યમાં ઉત્તર લખો :`; a સવિસ્તાર block; શબ્દ-સમજૂતી
under સમાનાર્થી/શબ્દાર્થ, વિરુદ્ધાર્થી and શબ્દસમૂહ માટે એક શબ્દ; a **ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ** block that
quotes lines and names પુનરાવર્તન, રૂપક, વિરોધી ચિત્રો and વ્યંગ; and વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ of the
discussion / visit / written-note kind. Where a topic prepares the ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ item, its
explanation should already have shown that repetition or that image doing work; where a topic
prepares the સરખામણી MCQ, it should have quoted the line the comparison sits in. Banner headings are
inventoried verbatim per chapter by Agent 1 and never assumed from this list. Exercises are never
topics — Agent 10 owns every one of them.

**Language** — plain contemporary બોલચાલની ગુજરાતી, and `reference/teaching_lens_map.md` records a
mixture of આગત અંગ્રેજી words as expected in this form (unverified for std 10 — see the provisional
box). The difficulty is conceptual, but the L2 gloss bar still sits **lower** than the printed
શબ્દ-સમજૂતી box suggests (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`): std-9 ch 10's economic vocabulary — તાકા,
થાન, વાર, સંઘરવું, ઔષધ — is glossed at point of first use in `"શબ્દ — અર્થ"` form, while everyday
verbs carrying the weight are explained as thought, not as vocabulary. Gujarati script throughout,
Roman only in brackets on first technical use (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).

**Sensitivity** — this form carries social anger as its engine. std-9 ch 10's શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા itself
lists માનવતાનો અભાવ, સ્વાર્થવૃત્તિ, ગરીબોની દુર્દશા, આવકની અસમાનતા, શોષણ and સંવેદનશૂન્યતા as the
poem's causes. Flag and guide, never censor (`agents/08_sensitivity_safety.md`): teach the આક્રોશ as
**the poet's**, grounded in the lines that produce it, and keep it there. The unnamed `એ લોકો` stays
unnamed — the hard gate above is the mechanism. Do not turn the topic into a lecture on economics or

## Recall priors

Per topic, two to three, Bloom-laddered, each with a real model answer (`bloom_level` lowercase in
`recall_questions`):

- one **remember** — what this વિચાર-એકમ names: the objects, the people, the actions;
- one **understand** — what the બિંબ means, who is speaking here, or what the repetition does;
- one **analyze** or **create** — why the break falls where it does
  ("કવિએ આ પંક્તિ એકલી કેમ મૂકી છે?"), what the વ્યંગ turns on, or an invitation to write two lines
  of the child's own in the same shape.

The third question is where this form is actually tested: with no છંદ and no પ્રાસ to ask about, the
only craft question available is about the break, the gap and the repetition — which is exactly what
the ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ block asks, and exactly what the explanation must have earned.
