## Diagnosis signals

**Count the lines. Fourteen, and the counting is over.** No other form in this corpus is fixed at a
line count, and it is the cheapest decisive test in the whole verse tree. std-9 ch 8 prints fourteen
lines with no stanza break anywhere; std-10 ch 7 prints fourteen lines grouped as seven couplets.
Both are સૉનેટ, and the difference in the printing is not a difference in the form.

**One continuous argument, and a turn near the end.** The fourteen lines are one sentence-movement,
not fourteen sayings and not seven. std-9 ch 8 opens `અમે તો આકાશો ભણી પીઠ કરી'તી ભીંત ચણી,` and
runs on across line ends (enjambment is normal here) until the last two lines turn it:
`ભલું કે મિટ્ટીમાં હજી વન તણાં આદિમ મૂલો, / હજી વાડા-છોડે નથી ઊઘડતાં પ્લાસ્ટિક ફૂલો.` std-10 ch 7
walks a storm through heat, dust and thirst, and turns when the first drops land —
`ઝીણાં ફોરાં ઝરમર ઝર્યાં, જીવમાં જીવ આવ્યો.`

**The book defines the form itself, and this is the strongest single signal.** std-9 ch 8's
કૃતિ-પરિચય is a definition: *"સોનેટ કાવ્ય સ્વરૂપમાં કવિ પોતાની અનુભૂતિ ચૌદ પંક્તિની મર્યાદામાં
વ્યક્ત કરતો હોય છે અને અંતિમ બે પંક્તિઓમાં સમગ્ર કાવ્યભાવ વ્યક્ત કરતી અસરકારક ચોટ સિદ્ધ કરતો હોય
છે."* std-10 ch 7's names it twice and adds the metre: *"તે ભાવને કવિએ આ સોનેટમાં મંદાક્રાન્તા
છંદમાં પ્રગટ કર્યો છે."* Where the box names the form, `genre_confidence` is high on that sentence
alone — but the box is teacher apparatus and is never a reading scene and never a topic.

**No ટેક, no છાપ, no રદીફ.** Nothing returns at fixed places to be sung, no medieval signature sits
inside the verse, and no single word closes line after line. std-10 ch 7 repeats its title line at
the start *and* at the close — `જીવમાં જીવ આવ્યો` — and that is a **circular closure**, not a
ધ્રુવપંક્તિ: it returns exactly once, at the end, to answer the opening. Reading it as a ટેક drags
the whole refrain machinery of `urmikavya_geet.md` into a poem that has none.

**A modern, dated, named poet.** `ઉશનસ્`, `હરિકૃષ્ણ પાઠક` — with an era line and a source collection
after the verse. Never `- લોકગીત`, never `- સંકલિત`, never a ભક્ત with a century for a date.

**સ્વાધ્યાય tell.** Both chapters test the form-name outright: std-9 ch 8's MCQ block asks the
સાહિત્યપ્રકાર with સોનેટ against ગઝલ, ઊર્મિકાવ્ય and પદ as the wrong options — the book itself treats
these four as the confusable set. std-10 ch 7 sets `પંક્તિ સમજાવો` on a quoted couplet and, in the
સવિસ્તર tier, **શીર્ષકની યથાર્થતા ચર્ચો** — a title-fitness question, which in this form is a
question about the ચોટ, because the title usually names it. Its શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા goes further and
asks for *"સોનેટનો પ્રાથમિક પરિચય"* and *"ગુજરાતી સાહિત્યમાં સોનેટની શરૂઆત કોણે કરી"* — teacher
apparatus, evidence that naming the form is this standard's declared work.

## Lens

**બંધ + વળાંક + ભાવની ઘનતા** — the fixed frame, the turn inside it, and the pressure the frame puts
on the feeling.

Fixed by `reference/teaching_lens_map.md`; state it in exactly these terms. The first slot is the
fourteen-line limit as a *shape the poet accepted*, the second is where the poem turns, the third is
what the limit does to the language — a સૉનેટ says a great deal in very little, which is why its
diction is dense. The L2 language target riding with this lens is fixed there too: **તત્સમ
શબ્દભંડોળ** and **લાંબા વાક્યને પંક્તિઓમાં પકડવું** — following one long sentence as it crosses
several printed lines is a real reading skill and this form teaches it better than any other.

## Guiding question

*ચૌદ પંક્તિમાં ભાવ ક્યાં વળાંક લે છે?*

A shape, not a text. std-9 ch 8's own question is nearer *જે હજી બચ્યું છે એની ગણતરી કવિ કેમ કરે
છે?*; std-10 ch 7's is nearer *પહેલા વરસાદનાં બે ટીપાં આખા ગામમાં શું બદલી નાખે છે?*. The derived
question goes in `01_meta.json` and is never copied from this file.

## Explanation unit — **one ભાવ-ખંડ (movement)**

One movement of the single argument: the stretch of lines that holds one picture or one step of the
feeling, ending where the poem itself turns. **Not the couplet, not the line-pair, not the printed
line.**

- **A fourteen-line poem is two to four topics.** Three is the common shape (setting → pressure →
  turn); two is right where the poem simply builds and then flips; four is the ceiling. **One topic
  for the whole poem is a legitimate answer** where the fourteen lines are one unbroken move — say so
  in `extraction_notes[]` and cut it once. Five or more topics means the couplet error has crept in.
- **The closing ચોટ is never split, and never merged away.** The last two lines carry the poem's
  whole ભાવ by the book's own definition. They live inside one topic, together, and that topic's
  explanation names the turn. Splitting them across two topics, or burying them mid-topic under
  eight earlier lines, are the two ways this form is most often lost.
- **Cut where the syntax closes, not where the line ends.** Enjambment is normal here — std-9 ch 8
  runs sentences straight across line ends — so a cut that lands on a printed line end mid-sentence
  is wrong. Read the sentence, then cut.
- **Where the page prints groups, they are evidence, not the unit.** std-10 ch 7's seven couplets are
  a typesetting of one continuous મંદાક્રાન્તા movement; the poem turns at the rain, not at every
  white gap. Where a poem prints a real ચતુષ્ક / ષટ્ક break, that break is a strong candidate for a
  movement boundary — check it against the sense before accepting it.
- **The attribution and the source line belong to the LAST topic.** The printed `- કવિનું નામ` under
  the title and the right-aligned `('અશ્વત્થ'માંથી)` / `('સૂરજ કદાચ ઊગે'માંથી)` after the verse go
  inside the last topic's `original_chunk` (§3.1 of the brief, `reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **`depends_on` runs forward, and sparingly.** Unlike a ગઝલ, a સૉનેટ *is* one argument, so the
  ચોટ topic legitimately lists the topic that set up what it overturns. One link, not a chain.

## How a સૉનેટ is built, and therefore how it is taught

A સૉનેટ is a poem that agreed to a size before it began. Fourteen lines, and no more — so nothing in
it is spare. That is the fact to teach first, and it is teachable to a std-9 child in one sentence:
*કવિએ પોતાને ચૌદ પંક્તિની હદ આપી છે, એટલે અહીં એક પણ શબ્દ નકામો નથી.*

**Then the turn.** Somewhere the poem stops adding and starts answering — and in both measured
chapters the answer arrives in the last two lines. std-9 ch 8 spends twelve lines on what has been
walled out (`આકાશો ભણી પીઠ કરી'તી ભીંત ચણી`) and turns on what has not yet been lost: the old roots
still in the soil, and no plastic flowers opening on the hedges yet. std-10 ch 7 spends its lines on
a dust-storm and a gasping village and turns the instant the drizzle lands. The explanation for the
ચોટ topic therefore has a fixed shape: **what was true till now → what these two lines do to it →
what the poem leaves the reader holding.**

**And the pressure.** Because the frame is small, the diction is dense: તત્સમ words (કપોત, મિષ, તૃણ,
આદિમ, નભતા) standing beside તળપદા ones (ભણી, પરણ, ફોરાં, લંગાર, આઘે-ઓરે), and coined sound-words
doing a line's whole work — `ફુક્ક`, `ટહૌકા`, `ખદખદી`, `ફરફર`. An L2 child meets more unfamiliar
words per line here than anywhere else in the reader, which is why the glossing bar in this form sits
lowest of all (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`).

**Test for a finished cut:** read the topics' `original_chunk` values in order. If they reconstruct
the printed poem exactly, line for line, with the last two lines together in the last topic, the cut
is right. If any sentence is broken across two topics, it is not.

## છંદ — name it only where the book does

`reference/teaching_lens_map.md`'s craft-naming gate is hard: **છંદ may be named at std 10 only.**
std-10 ch 7's own કૃતિ-પરિચય prints `મંદાક્રાન્તા`, so the word is used there — quoted from the book,
glossed in the same sentence as *the poem's fixed walking-rhythm*, and never turned into prosody.
std-9 ch 8 names no metre, so **no metre is named in that chapter**: naming પૃથ્વી or શિખરિણી because
a sonnet usually carries one is invention, and `reference/no_hallucination_policy.md` calls it what it
is. No authored field ever counts માત્રા or ગણ for the child, and `overall_rhyme_scheme` says plainly
that the poem's binding is the છંદ and the argument, not an end-rhyme scheme, wherever that is what
the page shows.

## Emphasise

- **The fourteen-line frame, once, in the child's terms.** A size the poet chose. Said once per
  chapter, in the first topic, and never repeated as a lecture.
- **The turn, named in the topic that carries it** — and only there. `topic_category: "climax"` marks
  it in the cut.
- **The long sentence walking across the lines.** Point at where a sentence begins and where it
  actually ends. This is the L2 win this form hands over, and it is invisible unless it is pointed at.
- **The dense word doing a whole line's work.** `પ્લાસ્ટિક ફૂલો` against `આદિમ મૂલો`; `ફોરાં` against
  a whole page of heat. Quote the word, say what it carries.
- **Sound as content.** std-10 ch 7's own ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ block lists the repetition-series
  (હાંફી હાંફી…, પરણ પરણે…, ફૂટ્યું ફૂટ્યું…, ગાણે-ગાણે…), the રવાનુકારી forms `ફરફર` and `ખદખદી`,
  and the દ્વિરુક્ત `અડખે-પડખે`, `ઝરમર`. std-9 ch 8's lists the `હજી / હજુ સારું છે કે…` repetition
  and the child-sound `ફુક્ક`. The book names these; the explanation should have got there first.
- **The title, and whether it earns itself.** Both chapters take their titles from a line of the ચોટ
  or the refrain-like return, and std-10 ch 7 sets શીર્ષકની યથાર્થતા as a full-length question. One
  sentence in the last topic is enough.
- **The અલંકાર that is actually working** — સજીવારોપણ where the village gasps, વિરોધાભાસ where
  plastic flowers stand against forest roots. One or two per chapter, quoted, never a catalogue.

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these per topic as a testable sentence; Agent 13 blocks on them; the
structural ones are checked at Agent 4, before ids freeze.

- **Couplet-wise or line-pair-wise cutting — structural, checked at Agent 4.** A સૉનેટ chapter's
  verse topics number **at most four**, and the count of topics carrying a `[[ભાવ-ખંડ …]]` marker
  equals the count of those markers in `00_chapter_normalized.md`. Seven topics for seven printed
  couplets is the ગઝલ template applied to a poem that is not one, and it is a blocking fail owned
  by A2.
- **A split ચોટ — structural, checked at Agent 4.** The poem's last two printed lines appear in the
  **same** topic's `original_chunk`, in order, and that topic is the last verse topic of the chapter.
- **A cut through a sentence.** No topic's `original_chunk` begins mid-sentence unless the printed
  poem itself begins that movement mid-sentence and the previous topic ends at that exact point;
  the concatenation of all verse topics' `original_chunk` values reproduces the printed poem
  character for character (mechanical check at Agent 13).
- **An invented ટેક.** No authored field calls any line of a સૉનેટ a ટેક or a ધ્રુવપંક્તિ, and
  `00_chapter_normalized.md` carries no `[[ટેક]]` marker in a સૉનેટ chapter. A returning title line
  (std-10 ch 7) is described as the poem coming back to where it started — never as a refrain.
- **An invented કડી.** No `explanation`, `summary` or recall answer refers to "પહેલી કડી" /
  "બીજી કડી" in a poem the page prints without stanza breaks. Say *પહેલી પંક્તિઓમાં*, or name what
  is in them.
- **A named છંદ the page does not print.** No authored field names a છંદ unless that છંદ's name
  appears in this chapter's own printed text, and no chapter below std 10 names one at all
  (`reference/teaching_lens_map.md`). No માત્રા or ગણ counting anywhere.
- **The ચોટ turned into a બોધ.** No sentence of `explanation`, `real_life_example`, `summary`,
  `brief_summary`, `detailed_summary`, any `concept_bullets` / `important_points` line, or any
  `recall_questions[].answer` converts the closing turn into an instruction of the shape
  "આપણે … જોઈએ" unless those exact words are quoted from this topic's `original_chunk`. std-9 ch 8's
  irony is the standing temptation: a poem that is glad the plastic flowers have not arrived yet must
  not become a tree-planting appeal. If the conclusion could be printed on a classroom poster, it is
  wrong.
- **Invented craft.** Every `figures_of_speech[].lines` string appears character-for-character inside
  this topic's `original_chunk` (checked at Agent 13). `[]` is a correct and complete answer; a device
  named because the field exists is a hard fail (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`).
- **The form's parts as અલંકાર.** No `figures_of_speech[].device` value is સૉનેટ, ચોટ, વળાંક,
  ભાવપલટો, અષ્ટક, ષટ્ક or છંદ. They are parts of the form; they belong in the explanation and in
  `rhyme_scheme.note`.
- **Never modernise the poet.** `થૈ`, `લૈને`, `ટહૌકી`, `કરી'તી`, `મિટ્ટી`, `ફુક્ક`, `પરણ`, `ભણી`
  stay exactly as printed and are explained as કાવ્ય-રૂપ and લય, never corrected toward માનક ગુજરાતી.
  std-10 ch 7's own ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ block spells out that `થૈ`, `લૈને`, `ટહૌકી` are રૂપો of થઈ, લઈને,
  ટહુકી — quote the book, keep the line (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **Letter-spacing is not text.** std-10 ch 7 is set with wide space between અક્ષરો — the worst
  extraction hazard in that book. `original_chunk` carries the words as they read on the rendered
  page, never the typesetting gaps; the em-dash inside `ક્યાંથી આવ્યાં - મનભર મળ્યાં લોક કાચી ઘડીમાં !`
  **is** content and stays. Any doubt is re-read off the render, never guessed
  (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **No numbers in display text.** `"છેલ્લી બે પંક્તિમાં"`, never `"પંક્તિ 13-14માં"`. Numbers live in
  ids and provenance fields only.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per ભાવ-ખંડ, showing **that movement's own concrete thing**: the wall built
with its back to the sky and the birds outside it; the old roots still in the soil; the dust-storm
over a hot village; the first drizzle on leaves and a village lifting its head. Indian setting, soft
digital watercolour, vibrant textbook illustration style, 16:9, narrator bar carrying one exact
Gujarati line from that movement. Three refusals specific to this form: **never a diagram of the
form** (no fourteen numbered lines, no octave/sestet chart — the shape is taught in words, not
pictured); **never an image of the abstraction** the ચોટ names, only of the thing it names it with;
and **never one frame standing for the whole poem** where the chapter has more than one movement.
≤1 `2d_tool` per chapter and a સૉનેટ does not need one. `image_url: ""` plus an authored,
self-contained `generation_prompt` for every scene — there is no Gujarati frame pool
(`agents/09_media_planning.md`).

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — measured at both standards, and they differ. **std 9** (ch 8): MCQ with the (✓) mark,
one item of which tests the **સાહિત્યપ્રકાર** itself; then બે-ત્રણ વાક્યોમાં ઉત્તર; then a single
છ-સાત વાક્યોમાં ઉત્તર — followed by વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ (a letter to a bird friend, putting out
માળા-કૂંડાં, "ધારો કે તમે પંખી છો"), **ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ** (the `હજી` repetition, the sound `ફુક્ક`,
the ઝીણી ચીં ચીં / ટહૌકા / મલમલી પીંછાં cluster) and **શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા**. **std 10** (ch 7): the fixed
four-tier ladder — ✓-વિકલ્પ → એક-એક વાક્યમાં → બે-ત્રણ વાક્યમાં (one of them `પંક્તિ સમજાવો` on a
quoted couplet) → સવિસ્તર (શીર્ષકની યથાર્થતા; the new life after the first rain) — plus
વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ, ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ and શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા. Three items this form must prepare
deliberately: the **સાહિત્યપ્રકાર** item (so the form is named in the chapter, at std 9–10, in the
order encounter → name → apply), the **પંક્તિ સમજાવો** item (the topic holding that couplet explains
it in its own explanation), and the **શીર્ષકની યથાર્થતા** item (one sentence in the ચોટ topic).
ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ and શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા are teacher-addressed commentary — evidence for A12's craft
fields, never exercise blocks for A10, and never topics. Exercises are never topics; Agent 10 answers
every printed block; the inventory comes from `01_meta.json`, never from this list.

**Language** — the densest verse in the pack for an L2 reader. તત્સમ diction (કપોત, મિષ, તૃણ, આદિમ,
શિર, નભતા, પરિકથા) sits beside તળપદા forms (ભણી = તરફ, પરણ = પાન, ફોરાં, લંગાર, ગાણું, આઘે, ઓરે,
ઊઘલ્યો) and coined or contracted ones (કરી'તી, મિટ્ટી, ફુક્ક, ટહૌકા). Gloss at point of first use in
`"શબ્દ — અર્થ"` form, 3–6 per topic; the printed **શબ્દ-સમજૂતી** box with its સમાનાર્થી / વિરુદ્ધાર્થી
/ તળપદા / રૂઢિપ્રયોગ sub-heads is evidence of what stopped the editor, not the limit of what stops an
L2 child — the bar sits **lower** here than anywhere else in the roster
(`reference/shabd_gloss.md`). Where a રૂઢિપ્રયોગ is the poem's own title — `જીવમાં જીવ આવવો`
(ભય જતાં સ્થિરતા અનુભવવી) — gloss it as an idiom the first time and let the poem show it working.
Where the syntax inverts or a verb is ellipted, say what the ordinary order would be **and leave the
line as printed** (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).

**Sensitivity** — light, and specific. Both measured chapters carry a public feeling — one an
ecological loss, one a village's relief at rain — and the guidance is the same as for
`urmikavya_geet.md`: **the land, the work and the people, never the state.** No authored field names
a government, party, scheme or campaign that the printed text does not name, and no chapter turns
into an appeal (`agents/08_sensitivity_safety.md`). Where the poem's feeling is loss, it is taught as
loss and not smoothed into optimism; where it is relief, it is not inflated into celebration.

## Recall priors

Per topic, two to three, Bloom-laddered, each with a real model answer:

- one **remember** — what this movement names: the objects, the sounds, the colours, the words the
  poet chose;
- one **understand** — what this movement shows, or what one dense word carries here;
- one **analyze** or **evaluate** — in the ચોટ topic this is always the turn (*છેલ્લી બે પંક્તિ
  આખા કાવ્યની વાત કઈ રીતે ફેરવી નાખે છે?*); elsewhere it is on a word's choice, on the sentence that
  runs across the lines, or on the child's own surroundings.

**One whole-poem question, at most, in the entire chapter**, and its home is the ચોટ topic's third
question — the title's fitness or the turn. A second whole-poem question means the movements were
never taught as movements.
