# કથાકાવ્ય / કથાગીત

> Provenance: written from the two GSEB units the corpus actually measured for this form —
> **std-6 ch 10 સાથી મારે બાર** (રમણલાલ સોની, printed pp. 63–67) and **std-7 ch 11 અંધેરી નગરી**
> (દલપતરામ, printed pp. 71–76). For both, the verse pages *and every સ્વાધ્યાય page* were read off
> the rendered PDF, so every exercise claim below is measured, not assumed
> (`reference/corpus/std-6_inventory.md`, `reference/corpus/std-7_inventory.md`).
>
> **These two are the whole measured evidence base.** No કથાકાવ્ય was recorded at std 8, 9 or 10.
> One unmeasured watch item: std-8 પૂરકવાચન 1 ભુલભુલામણી (યોગેન્દ્ર વી. ભટ્ટ) is printed as couplet
> stanzas with no ટેક and was recorded at medium-high confidence from a single page — it may be a
> બાળકાવ્ય and it may be a short કથાકાવ્ય, and it is **provisional (VERIFY-3)** until its page is
> read properly. Genre priors are evidence, never a verdict: Agent 1 re-diagnoses from the rendered
> page every time.

The form is verse that **tells a story**. વાર્તા's lens is the right one here — but on a unit વાર્તા
does not have, and over a text whose પ્રાસ, લય and printed line breaks are as load-bearing as the
plot. Two grains at once: cut like a વાર્તા, transcribe and hear like a કાવ્ય.

## Diagnosis signals

**Rhymed કડી with named characters, a chain of ઘટના and a result at the end. No ટેક.**

- **The book names the form itself, in its own blue intro box or its own સ્વાધ્યાય** — the strongest
  single signal this profile has, and both chapters carry it in print:
  - std-7 ch 11: "આ એક કથાકાવ્ય છે. એ રમૂજી અને રસપ્રદ તો છે જ, પણ સાથે એમાં સરસ શીખ પણ આપવામાં આવી
    છે... **એમાં આવતા ગુજરાતી ભાષાના જૂના શબ્દો તરફ વિદ્યાર્થીઓનું ખાસ ધ્યાન દોરવું.**"
  - std-6 ch 10 uses **both** labels and never settles on one: the intro box says
    "આ કથાકાવ્યની બાળકો પાસે નાટ્યાત્મક રજૂઆત કરાવવી.", સ્વાધ્યાય blk 2 says
    "આ એક કથાગીત છે. તેને વાર્તા સ્વરૂપે લખો." and blk 13 "આ કથાગીતનું ગાન વર્ગમાં કરો." Both labels
    route here; `01_meta.json` records the label as printed and does not pick a winner.
- **Speakers without speaker tags.** Who is talking is carried inside the line by કહે / બોલ્યો, and
  the speech itself sits inside single quotes within the verse — std-6 ch 10's
  'નથી કદી હું એકલો, સાથી મારે બાર !' A child reading alone misses the switch, and when it is missed
  the poem goes flat.
- **Events in sequence, with a વળાંક.** std-6 ch 10: ધૂળો વાણિયો leaves ઈડર for કોટડે at dusk → he
  loses the વાટ → four ચોર surround him → he bluffs that he is never alone → he fights with the
  કોથળો of કાટલાં → the closing tally reveals who the બાર સાથી are. std-7 ch 11: ગુરુ-ચેલો reach the
  city → the ગુરુ leaves, the શિષ્ય stays for cheap ખાજાં → a ભીંત falls on the ચોર → the blame runs
  વણિક → કડિયો → ગારો કરનાર → પખાલી → મુલ્લા → 'જાડા નરને જોઈ' the fattened શિષ્ય is seized → the
  ગુરુ returns with "આ અવસર શૂળીએ ચઢે, વેગે મળે વિમાન" → the રાજા climbs the શૂળી himself.
- **પ્રાસ closing every કડી**, and the poem is sung as well as told: નામ/ગામ, વાટ/ઉચાટ, વિચાર/બાર
  (std-6 ch 10); રાજા/ખાજાં, એકે/વિવેકે (std-7 ch 11). Both chapters end their સ્વાધ્યાય on a
  singing or acting task.

**Signal C — the સ્વાધ્યાય tells (measured in both chapters).** Four block types route here almost
by themselves, because each of them only makes sense if a poem has a plot:

- a **verse→prose retelling** block — "આ એક કથાગીત છે. તેને વાર્તા સ્વરૂપે લખો." with the printed
  stem "તો ચાલો, શરૂ કરીએ... ઈડર નામનું એક ગામ હતું..." (std-6 ch 10 blk 2);
  "વિદ્યાર્થી પાસે અંધેરી નગરી કથાકાવ્યને ગદ્યસ્વરૂપે બોલાવવું." (std-7 ch 11 blk 16);
- an **event-ordering on verse lines** block — "નીચેની પંક્તિઓ ઘટનાક્રમમાં ગોઠવીને ફરીથી લખો."
  (std-7 ch 11 blk 6);
- a **speaker-attribution** block — "કાવ્યમાં આ કોણ બોલ્યું ? તે લખો." (std-7 ch 11 blk 5);
- a **dramatise-it** task — "શિક્ષકની મદદથી આ કાવ્યને નાટક સ્વરૂપે રજૂ કરો." (std-6 ch 10 blk 12),
  "આ કથાકાવ્યને નાટક સ્વરૂપે ભજવો." (std-7 ch 11 blk 14).

Beside them sits a **પ્રાસ block** that treats the rhyme as the chapter's own material — the
rhyme-family grid નામ ગામ ઠામ કામ રામ / વાટ…ઘાટ / દાવ…સાવ / ચાર…પાર (std-6 ch 10 blk 6) and the
rhyme hunt inside the poem "નીચેના શબ્દો સાથે પ્રાસવાળા શબ્દો શોધીને લખો." (std-7 ch 11 blk 3).
A form that gets both a retelling block and a rhyme block is telling you what it is.

**The decisive test.** Ask whether removing one કડી breaks the sequence. In an ઊર્મિકાવ્ય the કડી
could often be reordered and little would be lost; here the ચોર cannot be beaten before they attack,
and the રાજા cannot mount the શૂળી before the ગુરુ invents the reason to want it.

## Lens

**ઘટના + પાત્ર + વળાંક**

The same three terms as `varta.md`, applied to verse — because what a કથાકાવ્ય is doing is what a
વાર્તા is doing. **લય and પ્રાસ are not a fourth term and not a replacement for the middle one**:
they are carried in every topic by one sentence, inside the explanation, after the event is clear.
(`reference/teaching_lens_map.md` writes the middle term as લય; this profile's reading, from the two
measured chapters, is that પાત્ર — who is speaking — is the term that decides how the topic is
built, and that લય is craft the explanation names once. The two files are reconciled in one commit,
never left disagreeing.)

## Guiding question

*આ કડીઓમાં શું બન્યું, અને એ કોણ કહે છે?*

A shape, not a text. Each chapter derives its own from its own poem — what a lone traveller counts
when four men close in; why the whole city agrees that everything costs one ટકો. The derived
question goes in `01_meta.json` and is never copied from this file.

## Explanation unit — **one વાર્તા-પગલું (narrative step)**

One plot beat per topic, **and its boundaries always fall where a printed કડી ends.** A step may
span several કડી; a કડી is never split; and a topic never ends in the middle of an episode.

- **Cut where the event moves or the speaker changes** — not where the કડી happens to end. In
  std-6 ch 10 the attack of the ચોર and ધૂળો's bluffing answer are two steps even though they sit
  close together; in std-7 ch 11 the arrival of ગુરુ-ચેલો and the ગુરુ's departure are one step,
  because the departure is what sets the trap.
- **Never the કડી alone.** A chapter cut one-topic-per-કડી has been cut as an ઊર્મિકાવ્ય and has
  lost the story: અંધેરી નગરી would become about seventeen topics, none of which contains an event
  with a beginning and an end.
- **Never mid-episode.** A topic may not end between a question and its answer, between a threat and
  the blow, or between a trick and its result.
- **Repetition with escalation is a chain of steps, not one step** — but only where the page gives
  each link its own turn of speech. std-7 ch 11's blame chain (વણિક → કડિયો → ગારો કરનાર → પખાલી →
  મુલ્લા) moves the danger to a new person each time, so each accusation that has its own speech is
  a step; where two accusations share one કડી they stay one topic. Never split a કડી to make the
  pattern look tidy.
- **The opening scene-setting કડી is its own topic.** "પુરી એક અંધેરી ને ગંડુ રાજા, / ટકે શેર ભાજી
  ટકે શેર ખાજાં." establishes the whole satire and nothing else; `topic_category: "introduction"`.
- **The closing reveal or tally is its own topic even when short.** std-6 ch 10's count — two hands,
  two eyes, two legs, four કાટલાં and the કોથળો, plus હિંમત and વિશ્વાસ — is the વળાંક and the
  પરિણામ together, and it is what the title has been promising since the first કડી.
- **A pre-step hook belongs to the NEXT topic** (`reference/explanation_unit_map.md` cutting rule 2),
  and the attribution line "- રમણલાલ સોની" / "- દલપતરામ" belongs inside the LAST topic's
  `original_chunk` (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **સ્વાધ્યાય is never a topic**, including the retelling, the dramatisation and the singing task;
  the chapter-final riddle box of std-6 ch 10 and the green grammar box "કાળ અને સહાયકારક રૂપો" of
  std-7 ch 11 are teaching matter and reading matter, not exercises and not topics.

**Marking, so the cut can be checked.** Agent 1 writes **both** marker kinds into
`00_chapter_normalized.md` (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md` §markers): `[[કડી n]]` above every
printed stanza, and `[[ઘટના: …]]` above every narrative step. That gives Agent 4 two counts to
compare instead of a judgement to argue about.

## રમૂજ-બોધ કથાકાવ્ય — the satire sub-form

std-7 ch 11 is the book's own label for it: a nineteenth-century comic tale in verse that is funny
first and pointed second. Three things move for this sub-form, and nothing else does:

- **The comedy is the teaching, not a decoration.** A ગંડુ રાજા who prices ભાજી and ખાજાં the same,
  a blame chain that reaches a પખાલી, a king who queues to be executed — the child is meant to laugh
  and then see why the laugh is uncomfortable. An explanation that reports the plot without letting
  the joke land has missed the chapter.
- **The બોધ is printed, so it is quoted.** Where the poem states its own lesson, teach that as the
  poet's move and quote his words; never append one of your own (hard gate 5).
- **The language is the chapter's second subject.** The intro box orders it outright, and the
  ~35-entry શબ્દાર્થ box on p. 73 is the biggest in the std-7 reader. See **Language** below.

## Emphasise

- **Who is speaking, named in every topic.** These poems have no speaker tags. Point at the clause
  that carries the switch (કહે, બોલ્યો, the single quotes) and say it plainly to the class:
  *અહીં કોણ બોલે છે?* The speaker-attribution સ્વાધ્યાય block is scored on exactly this.
- **What happens next, in the poem's own order.** The `explanation` is one step of narrative, and it
  should be followable by a std 6–7 child without the કડી in front of them — and then send them back
  to the કડી.
- **The turn, named once.** std-6 ch 10's is the bluff 'નથી કદી હું એકલો, સાથી મારે બાર !';
  std-7 ch 11's is the ગુરુ's invention that the શૂળી is now a doorway to a વિમાન. One topic carries
  it, marks `topic_category: "climax"`, and says so.
- **Why the character chooses.** ધૂળો does not fight first; he talks first, and the કાટલાં come
  after. The ગુરુ does not argue with the રાજા's justice; he makes it desirable. A કથાકાવ્ય teaches
  through motive exactly as a વાર્તા does.
- **The પ્રાસ, heard before it is named.** Say the pair aloud together — નામ–ગામ, વાટ–ઉચાટ,
  રાજા–ખાજાં — and then, in one sentence, say what the rhyme does to the reading voice. One
  sentence, after the event, never before it.
- **The joke, where there is one.** Both chapters are comic: std-6 ch 10 is a bluff that turns out
  to be true, std-7 ch 11 is a city where the punishment fits nobody. A topic that explains them
  without letting the child enjoy the trick has missed the poem.
- **The old words as the poem's own.** તહાં, આંહીં, નીસર્યા, તણો, છાંડો, દિશ, જાવું'તું, વારતા,
  અલ્યા — glossed at the point of use, in the poem's own form, never replaced.

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these as a per-topic testable sentence; Agent 13 blocks on any hard item
left unaddressed; the structural ones (1, 2, 3) are checked at Agent 4. Each check names the field it
is checked against.

1. **Splitting a કડી.** **Check:** every topic's `original_chunk` begins at the first line of a
   printed `[[કડી n]]` block and ends at the last line of one; every `[[કડી n]]` marker in
   `00_chapter_normalized.md` is carried by exactly one topic; the sum of કડી across topics equals
   the chapter's કડી count. Severity: hard (Agent 4).
2. **Cutting કડી-wise instead of step-wise.** **Check:** the number of instructional topics equals
   the number of `[[ઘટના: …]]` markers, and is strictly less than the number of `[[કડી n]]` markers
   for any chapter of more than six કડી. Where the two counts are equal, `extraction_notes[]` states
   in one sentence why every કડી is a complete step on its own. Severity: hard (Agent 4).
3. **Ending mid-episode.** **Check:** single-quote marks balance inside each topic's
   `original_chunk` — a topic that opens a speaker's speech and does not close it has cut mid-turn.
   Severity: hard (Agent 4).
4. **Prose-summarising the poem away.** **Check:** every topic's `explanation` quotes at least one
   phrase that appears character-for-character in that topic's `original_chunk`, **and** adds motive,
   craft or consequence to what `modified_chunk` already narrates — if `explanation` could be
   replaced by `modified_chunk` with no loss of information, the topic fails. The verse→prose
   retelling is Agent 10's block and its model answer never becomes a topic's `explanation`,
   `summary` or `detailed_summary`. Severity: hard.
5. **A tacked-on બોધ.** **Check:** no `explanation`, `summary`, `detailed_summary`,
   `concept_bullets`, `important_points` or recall `answer` contains a sentence of the shape
   "આ કાવ્ય આપણને શીખવે છે…" / "આપણે પણ … જોઈએ" / "બોધ એ છે કે…" unless those exact words are quoted
   from this chapter's own `original_chunk`. Where the poem prints its own શીખ, quote it and name it
   as the poet's move. Severity: hard.
6. **Craft-naming displacing the story.** **Check:** the first sentence of `explanation` names a
   character or an action that occurs in this topic's `original_chunk`; a first sentence that opens
   on પ્રાસ, લય, છંદ or અલંકાર terminology is a fail. At most one craft sentence per topic.
   Severity: hard.
7. **Invented craft.** **Check:** every `figures_of_speech[].lines` string appears
   character-for-character inside this topic's `original_chunk`, and every `rhyme_scheme`
   `rhyming_words[]` pair is two words printed in this chapter. `[]` is a correct and complete
   answer; a named device that is not on the page is a hard fail
   (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`). Severity: hard.
8. **Treating a couplet as self-contained advice.** **Check:** no topic whose `original_chunk` is a
   single couplet presents it as a general rule about life; its `explanation` names the event that
   couplet advances. This is the `duha_chhappa` failure and it is the one this profile exists to
   prevent alongside the ઊર્મિકાવ્ય failure. Severity: hard.
9. **Spoiling the turn early.** **Check:** no topic preceding the topic that carries
   `topic_category: "climax"` names the outcome — the outcome's result words and proper nouns (the
   tally of બાર સાથી; the રાજા on the શૂળી) do not appear in an earlier topic's `explanation`,
   `summary` or recall `answer`. Severity: hard.
10. **Inventing a speaker.** **Check:** every character named as speaking in an `explanation`,
    recall `answer` or exercise `answer` is named in this topic's `original_chunk` or in an earlier
    topic's. Severity: hard.
11. **Modernising the poet.** **Check:** every archaic, elided or dialect form present in
    `original_chunk` — તહાં, આંહીં, જ્યાંહીં, વસીજે, છાંડો, તણો, નીસર્યા, દિશ, ભૂપ, સધ, રાતેમાતે,
    જાવું'તું, અલ્યા, કાંક, વારતા, પાય, સબોસબ — appears in the same form wherever the teaching
    quotes it, and is glossed rather than replaced by its માનક ગુજરાતી equivalent. The printed
    apostrophe in જાવું'તું is content. Severity: hard
    (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
12. **Silently converting the old measures.** **Check:** ટકો, શેર, ગાઉ, ખાજું and their like are
    glossed with the chapter's own શબ્દાર્થ wording (ટકો = ત્રણ પૈસા; શેર = મણનો ચાળીસમો ભાગ) and no
    field states a rupee, kilogram or kilometre value the chapter does not print. Severity: hard
    (`reference/no_hallucination_policy.md`).
13. **Reading the satire as being about a community.** **Check:** no `explanation`,
    `real_life_example`, `summary`, recall `answer` or exercise `answer` presents the blame chain's
    occupational or religious figures (કડિયો, પખાલી, વણિક, મુલ્લા) as the target of the poem, or
    attaches a trait to the group they belong to; the target named is the city's arbitrary justice.
    Severity: hard. See **Sensitivity**.
14. **Numbers in display text.** `"બીજી કડીમાં"`, never `"કડી 2માં"`; numbers live in ids and
    provenance fields only (`reference/naming_conventions.md`). Severity: hard.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per narrative step, showing **that step's moment**, not the ballad in
general: the four ચોર closing in on a lone traveller at dusk on an unlit વાટ; the raised કોથળો of
કાટલાં; a market where every stall shows the same price; the ગુરુ whispering at the foot of the
શૂળી while a crowd volunteers. The depiction test is one sentence — if the picture cannot be
described as a single moment someone could photograph, it is a summary and must be re-authored.
Characters keep the same likeness across the chapter (same clothes, same age, same features), which
matters more here than in any other verse form because the child is following people, not pictures.
Setting is Indian, Gujarat-flavoured where the text is (an ઈડર-to-કોટડે road at dusk, a
nineteenth-century town market). **Never illustrate the moral, and never the whole ballad in one
frame.** For std-7 ch 11, show the contest of volunteers and the king's foot on the ladder — **no
body on the શૂળી, no depicted execution**; the comedy is in the queue, not the device.
`generation_prompt` is self-contained and the narrator bar carries one exact line of Gujarati in
Gujarati script (`reference/field_shape_rules.md`). No Gujarati frame pool exists, so every scene is
authored: `image_url: ""`, `reuse_report.reused: 0`. At most one `2d_tool` per chapter — and an
event-order strip is the one such tool this form can justify, only where the chapter's own સ્વાધ્યાય
prints an ordering block (std-7 ch 11 blk 6).

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — *measured in both chapters.* Expect: વાતચીત (oral, 6–8 prompts); the verse→prose
retelling; comprehension "નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના ઉત્તર લખો." / "પ્રશ્નોના જવાબ લખો."; true/false against
the poem (std-6 ch 10 blk 3); paraphrase→find-the-printed-line ("નીચેનાં વાક્યો જેવો અર્થ ધરાવતી
પંક્તિ-પંક્તિઓ કવિતામાંથી શોધો.", 8 items) and its std-7 mirror
"આપેલી પંક્તિઓનો ભાવાર્થ તમારા શબ્દોમાં લખો."; speaker attribution; event ordering; the પ્રાસ block;
verse→prose sentence conversion (std-6 ch 10 blk 7); a punctuation battery carried over from the
previous chapter (std-6 ch 10 blks 8–9); tense and auxiliary drills fed by the green grammar box
(std-7 ch 11 blks 11, 15, 17); tongue-twisters in pairs; a story-writing prompt from given words; the
standing L2 block "નીચેનાં વાક્યોનો તમારી પ્રથમ ભાષામાં અનુવાદ કરો."; and the closing
નાટ્ય-રજૂઆત / ગાન tasks. **How topics prepare them:** cutting step-wise is itself what prepares the
ordering block; the topic carrying a speech prepares the attribution block; the one craft sentence
prepares the પ્રાસ block; glossing the જૂના શબ્દો at point of use prepares the ભાવાર્થ block. Name
the skill each topic prepares (`reference/teaching_block_format.md`). Agent 10 answers every printed
block and **no સ્વાધ્યાય block is ever a topic** (`reference/exercise_alignment.md`).

**Language** — narrative verse in speakable Gujarati, and the register is where the L2 difficulty
sits, not the syntax. std-6 ch 10 carries colloquial and poetic forms — જાવું'તું (elided હતું, the
apostrophe printed), અલ્યા (vocative), કાંક (= કંઈક), વારતા (poetic for વાર્તા), પાય, સબોસબ,
ખાળે — plus its own શબ્દાર્થ box (વાટ, ઉચાટ, વિકરાળ, કાટલું, ભયાનક જંગ) and a રૂઢિપ્રયોગ pre-block
(ટાયલી કરવી, હાથ બતાવ્યો, દિલમાં ઉચાટ થવો). std-7 ch 11 is the reader's archaic-language peak:
nineteenth-century Dalpatram Gujarati with obsolete coin and measure (ટકો, શેર, ગાઉ), inverted
syntax, and forms like તહાં, આંહીં, વસીજે, છાંડો, તણો, નીસર્યા, દિશ, સધ. Gloss at point of first
use in `"શબ્દ — અર્થ"` form, 3–6 per topic; the printed શબ્દાર્થ box is **evidence of what stopped
the editor, not the limit of what stops an L2 child**, and the bar for glossing sits lower here
(`reference/shabd_gloss.md`). The idioms and the archaic forms are the first candidates for
`key_terms`. Poetic licence is explained as લય and બોલી, never corrected
(`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`); a two-column verse page is read down, never across (std-6 ch 10,
p. 64).

**Sensitivity** — this form needs the paragraph, and mostly because of std-7 ch 11. The poem's
engine is an execution device (શૂળી), and its blame chain sweeps up named occupational and religious
figures — વણિક, કડિયો, ગારો કરનાર, પખાલી, મુલ્લા — before ending on a king who kills himself with
his own rule. Teach it as period satire whose target is **arbitrary justice**, never a community and
never a trade; the મુલ્લા is one more innocent link in a chain, and saying so plainly is the
teaching. Keep the dark-comic register the poem itself keeps: the child may laugh, and the
explanation does not turn the શૂળી into a gruesome picture or into a lesson about punishment.
std-6 ch 10 carries a night-time robbery and a fight: it is a ballad adventure and the કાટલાં are
part of the joke, so neither glorify the blow nor moralise about it, and never attach the ધૂળો's
trade to a caste. Agent 8 **flags and guides, never censors** — it says how to teach the passage,
not whether. Area labels in Gujarati per the pack convention (ધર્મ, સમુદાય, ક્ષેત્ર, વિકલાંગતા,
સંઘર્ષ, જાતિ-ભૂમિકા, સુરક્ષા).

## Recall priors

Two to three per topic, Bloom-laddered, each with a real model answer, `bloom_level` lowercase:

- one **remember** — શું બન્યું / આ કોણ બોલ્યું: an event or a speaker this topic's own
  `original_chunk` states;
- one **understand** — પાત્રે એમ કેમ કર્યું: motive, answerable from this topic and not from the
  whole chapter; or what a જૂનો શબ્દ means in this line;
- one **apply** or **analyze** — on the પ્રાસ pair and what the rhyme does to the line, on where
  this step sits in the chain (શું પહેલાં બન્યું, શું પછી), or on the child's own experience
  ("તમે એ જગ્યાએ હોત તો શું કહ્યું હોત ?"). On the topic that carries the વળાંક, this question is
  on the turn itself.

Recall answers are bound by the same hard gates as `explanation`: no appended બોધ, no invented
speaker, no modernised word, no outcome named before the climax topic. Keep every stem inside the
child's own world (`reference/teaching_voice_gu.md`, std 6–10 L2 band).
