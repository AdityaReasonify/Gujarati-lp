# ઊર્મિકાવ્ય / ગીત

> Provenance: written from GSEB pages actually read — std-6 ch 1 એક જ ડાળનાં પંખી, std-6 ch 7
> ચોખ્ખાઈના સરદાર, std-7 ch 1 સુંદર સુંદર, std-7 ch 5 આપણે ભરોસે, std-7 ch 9 અલ્લક દલ્લક,
> std-7 પૂરકવાચન 2 સમજણ તે આપણા બેની, std-7 પૂરકવાચન 4 કાલે, and the two poems inside std-6
> પૂરકવાચન 1 (કબૂતરોનું ઘૂ ઘૂ ઘૂ, રંગ રંગ વાદળિયાં); std-9 ch 3 જ્યાં જ્યાં વસે એક ગુજરાતી for the
> changed-words ટેક. From the now-complete std-10 inventory: std-10 ch 9 હાથ મેળવીએ (a short
> ઊર્મિકાવ્ય in સંવાદ-શૈલી), std-10 ch 11 દીવાનખાનામાં's near-miss row and std-10 પૂરક 5 દીવડો (a
> named poet writing in લોકઢાળ, so it stays here). std-10 ch 7 જીવમાં જીવ આવ્યો is a **સૉનેટ** and
> routes to `sonnet.md`. See `reference/corpus/std-{6,7,9,10}_inventory.md`.

This is also **the verse fallback**: a poem that is not a દુહો, not a પદ/ભજન, not a ગઝલ, not a
લોકગીત and not અછાંદસ lands here.

## Diagnosis signals

A sung unit. **ટેક / ધ્રુવપંક્તિ plus કડી of roughly equal length**, the ટેક usually printed after
the first occurrence as an ellipsis shorthand that the page expects the singer to complete —
`- એક જ.` (std-6 ch 1), `- હો ભેરુ.` (std-7 ch 5), `... ચોખ્ખાઈના સરદાર અમે સહુ...` (std-6 ch 7),
`કે રંગ...` (std-6 P1, રંગ રંગ વાદળિયાં). Some songs carry the refrain as a full line inside every
કડી instead (std-7 ch 1: `વિભુ હશે તો કેવા સુંદર, એવું થાતું મુજ મનમાં`), and a few build a ring by
repeating the opening કડી verbatim at the end (std-7 ch 9).

The author slot names a **living or modern poet** — ધર્મેન્દ્ર માસ્તર 'મધુરમ્', પ્રહ્લાદ પારેખ,
બાલમુકુન્દ દવે, ચંદ્રકાન્ત શેઠ, સુરેશ દલાલ, નિરંજન ભગત — not "લોકગીત", not a medieval ભક્ત, and there
is no છાપ inside the verse.

The blue intro box is the strongest single signal and it usually names the form itself:
"આ પ્રકૃતિના સૌંદર્યનું ગીત છે... આ કાવ્યનું પઠન અને ગાન અનેક વાર કરાવવું" (std-7 ch 1);
"આ રાધા-કૃષ્ણનું ઊર્મિગીત છે" (std-7 ch 9); "આ ગીત આત્મબળ અને આત્મનિર્ભરતાની પ્રેરણા આપે છે"
(std-7 ch 5); "અહીં 'કૂચગીત' દ્વારા આ સંદેશ આપવામાં આવ્યો છે" (std-6 ch 7);
"કવિએ આ નાનકડા ઊર્મિકાવ્યમાં…" (std-10 ch 9). Where the box prescribes **ગાન / સમૂહગાન**, the text
is a ગીત.

Subject matter is wide and does **not** decide the routing: પ્રકૃતિ (સુંદર સુંદર), સંપ/બાળગીત
(એક જ ડાળનાં પંખી), શ્રમ અને આત્મબળ (આપણે ભરોસે), રાસ-લીલા (અલ્લક દલ્લક), સ્વચ્છતા-કૂચગીત
(ચોખ્ખાઈના સરદાર), સહિયારાપણું (સમજણ તે આપણા બેની), આશા (કાલે), અસ્મિતા (જ્યાં જ્યાં વસે એક
ગુજરાતી), વ્યંગ (કબૂતરોનું ઘૂ ઘૂ ઘૂ).

**સ્વાધ્યાય tell.** std 6–7 poem units ask for the line that carries a given meaning
("કવિતામાંથી ઉદાહરણ મુજબ પંક્તિ શોધીને લખો"), nearest-meaning MCQ on a કાવ્યપંક્તિ, word-pairs and
sound-word matching, તળપદા-શબ્દ hunts, and they close with a singing or acting task
("આ કાવ્યનું વર્ગમાં સમૂહગાન કરો", "આ કૂચગીતનું અભિનય સાથે ગાન કરો"). std 9–10 poem units replace
these with MCQ + two-three-line and five-six-line ઉત્તર, plus a **ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ** block that quotes
a line and names its craft (પ્રાસ lists, પુનરાવર્તન, ઉપમા). પૂરકવાચન poems carry **no સ્વાધ્યાય at
all** — they are reading scenes only.

**Near misses — route on the tell, not on the feel.**

## Lens

**ચિત્ર + ભાવ + અલંકાર** — a feeling built out of pictures.

In a sung ગીત the craft often reaches the child through the ear before the eye, so લય and પ્રાસ
occupy the same third slot as અલંકાર (`reference/teaching_lens_map.md` states the lens with લય
there). Name whichever of the three this કડી actually carries; never all three by habit.

## Guiding question

*કવિ કયાં ચિત્રો બતાવીને આ ભાવ જગાડે છે?*

A shape, not a text. Each chapter derives its own from its own poem — what makes a branch everyone's
branch; whose hand the boat's oar is in; what the moon does to the રાસ; what the child will be
tomorrow. The derived question goes in `01_meta.json`, never copied from this file.

## Explanation unit — **one કડી**

Two to five lines typically. Where one કડી does two clearly different things it may split; where two
very short કડી do one thing they may join. Decide from the content, never from length alone.
Keep the printed line breaks and the printed refrain-shorthand exactly as set inside
`original_chunk` (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`) — the expansion of `- એક જ.` into the full ટેક
belongs in the `explanation`, not in the verbatim.

**A ટેક whose words CHANGE is a topic at each occurrence.** This is where the teaching of this form
lives. When a refrain returns altered — std-9 ch 3 opens
`જ્યાં જ્યાં વસે એક ગુજરાતી / ત્યાં ત્યાં સદાકાળ ગુજરાત`, then returns as
`જ્યાં જ્યાં બોલાતી ગુજરાતી / ત્યાં ત્યાં ગુર્જરીની મહોલાત`, then is woven into the closing જયઘોષ —
the poem is arguing, and each return is a new claim about the same thing. Teach **what changed**.
Similarly std-7 P2, where the shared thing's gender re-shapes the refrain
(`…તે આપણા બેનો` → `…તે આપણા બેનું` → `…તે આપણા બેની`) — the change is the lesson.

**Where the ટેક returns identical, teach it once and reference it after.** std-7 ch 1's
`વિભુ હશે તો કેવા સુંદર…` closes all four કડી unchanged: it is taught in the first topic, and the
later topics carry it in `depends_on`, not as new teaching. std-7 ch 9's opening કડી is reprinted
verbatim as the closing કડી — nine printed કડી-units, eight distinct topics, and the ring itself is
worth one sentence in the last topic's explanation.

A poem printed with no કડી breaks at all — std-9 ch 16 ગોકુળમાં આવો તો runs about sixteen continuous
lines in call-and-response pairs; std-10 ch 9 હાથ મેળવીએ is short free-flowing lines — is cut on its
**turns of address or image**, and the cut is recorded in `extraction_notes[]` as a judgement, not
presented as printed structure.

## કૂચગીત — the marching sub-form

std-6 ch 7 ચોખ્ખાઈના સરદાર is the book's own label: a collective, exhortative song in the first
person plural, addressed outward, six કડી in two printed columns around one refrain
`ચોખ્ખાઈના સરદાર અમે સહુ / ચોખ્ખાઈના સરદાર.`, ending in a class task
"આ કૂચગીતનું અભિનય સાથે ગાન કરો".

It cuts and is taught exactly like any other ગીત — one કડી per topic — but **the slogan gate below
bites hardest here**, because the poem's own surface is close to a slogan. The distinction the
explanation must hold: *the poem earns its call through pictures, so the explanation must too*.
The કડી gives the child થાળી-વાટકો, માખી-મચ્છર, an ઉકરડો, a placard — teach the picture, and let the
call arrive the way the poem lets it arrive. An explanation that opens on "આપણે સ્વચ્છતા રાખવી
જોઈએ" has skipped the poem and is a hard fail.

The child's own body is the real-life anchor here (the શેરી that is swept, the school prayer line,
the ઉત્તરાયણ terrace next morning) — never a government campaign, never a state.

Two-column verse is an extraction hazard: columns are read down, never across
(`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`). Same for std-6 P1 રંગ રંગ વાદળિયાં.

## Emphasise

- **The picture before the feeling.** The child sees the birds on one branch, then feels the સંપ.
  An explanation that starts at the feeling has skipped the poem.
- **અલંકાર that is doing work** — સજીવારોપણ letting a મેહુલો own the earth, વર્ણાનુપ્રાસ putting the
  sound of the રાસ into the line (ઝલ્લક ઝલ્લક, ઢમ્મક ઢમ્મક), રૂપક turning a દીકરી into ઘરનું અજવાળું.
  Name it, quote it, say what it does. One or two per topic, never a catalogue.
- **The ટેક's movement** across the poem — the whole argument of a changed-words refrain.
- **પ્રાસ and લય** — what the rhyme does to the reading voice, why the કડી wants to be sung. The
  std 9–10 ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ block does exactly this and the explanation should get there first.
- **Sound-words as content, not decoration.** In std-7 ch 9 the echo-forms *are* the poem; in
  std-6 P1 the bird-cries carry the satire.
- **Scale, and the child's smallness before it** — સાગર, આભ વિશાળ, અનંત. This is the emotional engine
  of the form and it is directly available to a std 6–10 child standing on a terrace at dusk.

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these per topic; Agent 13 blocks on them; the structural ones are
checked at Agent 4.

- **Slogans.** No sentence of `explanation`, `real_life_example`, `summary`, `brief_summary`,
  `detailed_summary`, any `concept_bullets`/`important_points` line, or any
  `recall_questions[].answer` is an exhortation of the shape "આપણે … જોઈએ" / "…રાખવી જોઈએ" /
  "…કરવો જોઈએ" unless those exact words are quoted from this topic's `original_chunk`. This is the
  failure this profile exists to prevent, and it fails the કૂચગીત first.
- **Feeling before picture.** The first sentence of `explanation` must name something in THIS કડી
  that can be seen, heard, touched or counted, using a word that occurs in this topic's
  `original_chunk`. A first sentence that states the ભાવ or the બોધ instead is a fail.
- **Over-scientifying.** No word naming a scientific mechanism (બાષ્પીભવન, જલચક્ર, ઘનીભવન,
  પ્રકાશસંશ્લેષણ, ગુરુત્વાકર્ષણ …) may appear in `explanation` unless it appears in this chapter's
  own text or its printed શબ્દાર્થ box. A poem about the first drop of rain is not a water-cycle
  lesson.
- **Skipping the craft.** Every poem topic carries either a non-empty `figures_of_speech[]` or an
  `explanation` sentence naming the કડી's પ્રાસ, લય, પુનરાવર્તન or ધ્વનિ. A topic with `[]` and no
  craft sentence is half-taught.
- **Invented craft.** Every `figures_of_speech[].lines` string must appear character-for-character
  inside this topic's `original_chunk` (mechanical check at Agent 13). `[]` is a correct and
  complete answer; a named device that is not on the page is a hard fail.
- **Literal reading of a figurative line.** No topic states as fact something the poem states as
  image — the ધરતી has no ધણી, the મુખડું does not literally emit light. Make it a delight, not a
  confusion.
- **Political framing.** No `explanation`, `real_life_example` or recall answer names a government,
  party, scheme, campaign, army, or border unless the printed text names it. The land, the work and
  the people — not the state. (std-6 ch 7 and std-9 ch 3 are the two that tempt this.)
- **ટેક handling, structural — checked at Agent 4.** If the `[[ટેક]]` markers in
  `00_chapter_normalized.md` differ in wording, the number of topics carrying a `[[ટેક]]` marker
  equals the number of distinct ટેક wordings; if all `[[ટેક]]` markers are identical, exactly one
  topic carries the ટેક and every later કડી topic naming it lists that topic in `depends_on`.
- **Verbatim of the refrain-shorthand.** `original_chunk` reproduces the printed shorthand exactly
  (`- એક જ.`, `- હો ભેરુ.`, `કે રંગ...`). Expanding it into full refrain lines inside
  `original_chunk` is a verbatim defect owned by Agent 5; the expansion belongs in `explanation`.
- **Never modernise the poet.** તળપદા and poetic forms — હાલીએ, ભેરુ, છઈએ, મોજારે, કો', ઘોડલા, થાતો,
  ચાંદલિયો, આલને, ચરિતર, મલ્લક — stay as printed and get explained as લય and બોલી, never corrected
  toward માનક ગુજરાતી.
- **No numbers in display text.** `"બીજી કડીમાં"`, never `"કડી 2માં"` — numbers live in ids and
  provenance fields only.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per કડી, showing **that કડી's picture**: the branch and the birds, the oar and
the wave, the moon over the રાસ, the swept શેરી. Indian setting, soft digital watercolour, 16:9,
narrator bar carrying one exact Gujarati line. A ટેક topic's image shows what the ટેક's **new** words
name (a Gujarati speaking in a far city for ત્યાં ત્યાં સદાકાળ ગુજરાત; the shared road for
રસ્તો તે આપણા બેનો) instead of repeating the previous picture. Never illustrate the moral, never
illustrate the whole poem in one frame. ≤1 `2d_tool` per chapter, and a ગીત rarely needs one.

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — expect, per the inventories: find-the-line from a prose meaning; nearest-meaning MCQ
on a કાવ્યપંક્તિ; true/false against the poem; શબ્દજોડકાં and sound-word matching; તળપદા-શબ્દ meaning
hunts; પ્રાસ-word finding (std-8/9/10); a first-language translation block at std 6–7; and a closing
સમૂહગાન / અભિનય-ગાન task. Where a topic prepares the પ્રાસ item, name that rhyme in its explanation;
where a topic prepares a તળપદો-શબ્દ item, gloss that word at point of use. Exercises are never
topics — Agent 10 owns every one of them. પૂરકવાચન poems have no સ્વાધ્યાય at all, and their absence
is recorded, not filled in.

**Language** — modern Gujarati, lightly તત્સમ-tinged (વિભુ, ઉષા, નિશા, સમીર, મિરાત, મહોલાત) sitting
beside તળપદા and coined song-forms (ડેડકડી, ઘોડલા, ચાંદલિયો, મધુરપ, કરવૈયો). Gloss at point of first
use, in `"શબ્દ — અર્થ"` form; the printed શબ્દાર્થ box is evidence of what stopped the editor, not the
limit of what stops an L2 child — the bar for glossing sits **lower** here
(`reference/shabd_gloss.md`). Poetic licence is explained as લય, never corrected
(`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).

**Sensitivity** — several songs in this form carry devotional or communal material as their engine:
રાધા-કૃષ્ણ રાસલીલા (std-7 ch 9), a self-reliance argument that dismisses reliance on ખુદા and રામ by
name (std-7 ch 5 કડી 1), અસ્મિતા and its જયઘોષ (std-9 ch 3). Flag and guide, never censor
(`agents/08_sensitivity_safety.md`): teach them as literature with a living singing tradition —
ગરબા, રાસ, પ્રભાતિયું — keep શ્રમ-મહિમા as શ્રમ-મહિમા rather than theology, and let pride in a
language stay pride in a language.

## Recall priors

Per topic, two to three, Bloom-laddered, each with a real model answer:
