## Diagnosis signals

**Couplets, printed apart, that do not continue one another.** The page shows two-line units with
white space between them, under one title, with no ટેક line, no four-to-six-line કડી, and no
stanza that finishes a sentence the previous stanza began. std-9 ch-14 prints seven such couplets
across three pages; the opening one is `લે, આ મને ગમ્યું તે મારું / પણ જો તને ગમે તો તારું !`

**A word or a rhyme comes back at the line-ends and nothing else does.** Two shapes occur in the
measured corpus and both are ગઝલ:

- **With a રદીફ** — the same word or phrase closes શેર after શેર, and the rhyming word *in front of*
  it changes each time. std-8 ch-10 runs `દરિયો` to the end of every શેર, including the last:
  `તમે જાળ નાખ્યા કરો રોજ 'આદિલ', પરંતુ કદીયે ન પકડાય દરિયો.`
- **With no રદીફ at all** — the rhyme family alone does the binding. std-9 ch-14 has no returning
  word; it has a returning *sound*: મારું, તારું, સહિયારું, હારું, પ્યારું, ખારું. A ગઝલ is not
  disqualified by the absence of a રદીફ, and the કાફિયા is then the only thread — teach it as the
  thread.

**The book's intro box usually names the form outright** and it is the strongest single signal:
"આ એક ગઝલ છે" (std-8 ch-10); "આ રચના ગઝલ સ્વરૂપની છે… મારા-તારાથી પર સૌનું એવી ભાવના આ ગઝલના
કેન્દ્રમાં છે" (std-9 ch-14). Where the box names it, `genre_confidence` is high on that sentence
alone.

**A તખલ્લુસ / છાપ in the last શેર — when it is there.** std-8 ch-10's મક્તા carries `'આદિલ'` inside
the verse, in single quotes, as the poet addresses himself. This is a powerful confirmer but it is
**not required**: std-9 ch-14's closing શેર carries no signature at all. Never route *away* from
ગઝલ because no તખલ્લુસ is present, and never invent one that the page does not print.

**The source attribution names a ગઝલ collection.** std-9 ch-14 prints `('ગઝલ સંહિતા'માંથી)` after
the verse, and its કવિ-પરિચય says the poet's ગઝલો fill 'ગઝલ સંહિતા' ભાગ-1 થી 5. The author slot
holds a **modern named poet with a date** — `રાજેન્દ્ર શુક્લ (જન્મ : 12-10-1942)`, `- આદિલ મન્સૂરી` —
never "લોકગીત", never "સંકલિત", never a medieval ભક્ત.

**સ્વાધ્યાય tell.** Up to std 9 the books never print the words શેર, રદીફ, કાફિયા, મત્લા or મક્તા —
**their word is પ્રાસ**, and the exercises go straight at it. std-10 ch-15's ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ block is
the exception and teaches શેર, રદીફ and કાફિયા by name. std-8 ch-10 sets a rhyme-hunt with six blanks,
"કાવ્યમાં વપરાયેલા પ્રાસવાળા શબ્દો શોધીને લખો." — that item *is* the કાફિયા taught under another
name. std-9 ch-14's ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ block lists the whole rhyme family
(મારું-તારું-સહિયારું-હારું-ખ્યારું-ખારું-લલકારું) and the દ્વિરુક્ત forms (લાવ-લાવ, ફરી-ફરીને,
પ્યારું-પ્યારું) as the source of લય-ગતિ. Both chapters also carry a line-meaning item —
"આ કાવ્યપંક્તિનો અર્થ સમજાવો અથવા આ કાવ્યપંક્તિનું ગદ્યમાં રૂપાંતર કરો" (std-8) — which only makes
sense because a single couplet is complete on its own.

## Lens

**શેર + રદીફ-કાફિયા + મિજાજ**

Fixed by `reference/teaching_lens_map.md`; state it in exactly these terms. The first slot is the
unit's self-sufficiency, the second is the binding craft, the third is the poem's temper — playful
in મારું તારું !, unbeatable-sea in હલેસે હલેસે. The L2 language target that rides with this lens is
also fixed there: **રદીફની પુનરાવૃત્તિથી વાક્યભાત** (the sentence-pattern the repetition builds) and
the **આગત (ફારસી-અરબી)** layer the form brings with it.

## Guiding question

*દરેક શેર પોતાની રીતે કઈ એક વાત પૂરી કરે છે?*

A shape, not a text. std-9 ch-14's own question is nearer *જે વસ્તુ 'મારી' મટીને 'આપણી' થાય છે
ત્યારે એમાં શું ઉમેરાય છે?*; std-8 ch-10's is nearer *જે પકડાય નહિ એને કવિ કેમ વારે વારે પકડવા મથે
છે?*. The derived question goes in `01_meta.json` and is never copied from this file.

## Explanation unit — **one શેર**

Two printed lines, one complete turn. **Topic count equals શેર count** — std-9 ch-14's seven
couplets are seven topics.

- **Never merge two શેર into one topic**, however short they are and however similar they sound.
  This is the same structural gate `duha_chhappa.md` carries, checked the same way at Agent 4, and
  it is the failure this profile exists first to prevent.
- **Never split one શેર across two topics.** Both printed lines live in one `original_chunk`,
  line break preserved (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **The મત્લા (opening શેર) is its own topic**, and it is where the રદીફ and the કાફિયા are
  introduced — both its lines carry the rhyme, which is exactly why the poem starts there.
- **The મક્તા (closing શેર) is its own topic.** Where it carries a તખલ્લુસ, that word is verse text
  and stays inside `original_chunk` — `'આદિલ'` is part of the line, not a byline.
- **There is no ટેક.** None of the refrain machinery in `urmikavya_geet.md` applies here: no ટેક
  topic, no `[[ટેક]]` marker, no "identical refrain taught once" rule. The રદીફ is a line-ending,
  not a stanza.
- **`depends_on` is empty for every શેર topic except one.** A topic may list the મત્લા topic when it
  names the returning રદીફ/કાફિયા introduced there. Nothing else. A chain of `depends_on` down the
  poem is the "developing argument" error wearing a different hat.

**Where the page prints no gaps between couplets**, cut at the rhyme-return: a new topic begins at
each line that closes on the કાફિયા (plus the રદીફ where there is one). Record the cut in
`extraction_notes[]` as a judgement, never as printed structure.

**The attribution line is not the તખલ્લુસ.** The printed `- આદિલ મન્સૂરી` under the title and the
right-aligned source line `('ગઝલ સંહિતા'માંથી)` after the verse both belong inside the **last
topic's** `original_chunk` per §3.1 of the brief. The તખલ્લુસ is a different thing in a different
place — inside the મક્તા's second line — and the two are never conflated, merged, or swapped.

## How a ગઝલ is built, and therefore how it is taught

A શેર is a two-line machine. **Line one sets something up — a picture, a claim, an address. Line two
turns it, and the turn lands on the rhyme.** In `લે, આ મને ગમ્યું તે મારું / પણ જો તને ગમે તો તારું !`
the first line states an ordinary child's ownership; the second gives it away, and the giving-away
*is* the rhyme word. The explanation follows that shape: what this શેર shows → where it turns →
what word it lands on.

Because the રદીફ is the same word every time, **the weight falls on the કાફિયા**. The returning
word is the frame; the changing word is the news. In હલેસે હલેસે every શેર ends `દરિયો`, so what a
particular શેર says is carried by the word standing just before it. A teaching that names only the
રદીફ has named the frame and skipped the picture.

**And here is the thing this form is easiest to get wrong.** The શેર are bound by sound, not by
argument. They do **not** develop, they do not build to a conclusion, and the poem does not have a
plot. Seven શેર are seven complete sayings that happen to rhyme — a ring of independent turns around
one મિજાજ. An explanation that opens "આગળના શેરમાં કવિએ… અને હવે અહીં…" has imported a story that
the page does not contain, and it is a hard fail below. The only legitimate cross-શેર sentence in
this whole form is the one that names the return: *આ શેરને છેડે પણ પેલો જ શબ્દ પાછો આવે છે.*

**Test for a finished topic:** read its `explanation` alone, with every other topic hidden. If it
still makes complete sense, the શેર has been taught. If it leans on a neighbour, it has not.

## રદીફ, કાફિયા, and the two rhyme fields

The form's craft lives in fields that already exist in the contract, so use them properly rather
than describing rhyme inside prose only.

- **Module-level `overall_rhyme_scheme`** records the ગઝલ's own pattern in one string —
  `"મત્લામાં બંને પંક્તિ પ્રાસમાં (AA), પછી દરેક શેરની બીજી પંક્તિ એ જ પ્રાસ લે (BA, CA, DA…)"` —
  and names the રદીફ where there is one (`રદીફ : દરિયો`), or says plainly that there is none and
  the કાફિયા binds alone (std-9 ch-14).
- **Per-topic `rhyme_scheme`** = `{pattern, rhyming_words[], note}`. `rhyming_words[]` holds **this
  શેર's** કાફિયા, quoted exactly as printed, with the રદીફ beside it where the poem has one. The
  `note` says in one child-sized sentence what that particular word choice does.
- **`figures_of_speech[]` is for અલંકાર only.** રદીફ, કાફિયા, મત્લા, મક્તા and તખલ્લુસ are parts of
  the form, not devices — they never appear as a `device` value (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`).
  પ્રાસ, પુનરાવર્તન and દ્વિરુક્ત forms belong in `rhyme_scheme.note` or in the explanation
  sentence about લય. Real અલંકાર still get named where the line carries them, quoted verbatim, `[]`
  otherwise.

**Naming the form-words to the child.** Through std 9 GSEB's own apparatus says **પ્રાસ**, not
કાફિયા; at std 10 it says both, and ch-15's ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ defines શેર, રદીફ and કાફિયા itself. Use the
form-words — they are worth having, and the L2 target names them — but the **first** use of each of
શેર / રદીફ / કાફિયા / મક્તા / તખલ્લુસ anywhere in the chapter's authored prose glosses it in the
same sentence, in Gujarati, at point of use (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`): *શેર એટલે ગઝલની બે
પંક્તિનો સ્વતંત્ર એકમ*, *રદીફ એટલે દરેક શેરને છેડે પાછો આવતો એક જ શબ્દ*. Where a સ્વાધ્યાય item says
પ્રાસ, the topic preparing it says પ્રાસ too.

## Emphasise

- **The શેર standing on its own two feet.** Every explanation is a complete small reading of a
  complete small poem. This is the form's whole point and the child's whole win.
- **The returning word, named in every topic.** Quote it. Say it is back. Say what is standing in
  front of it this time and what that changes — `દરિયો` after a net, `દરિયો` after a shore.
- **The picture in line one and the turn in line two** — in that order, and both from words that are
  actually on the page.
- **Metaphor held as metaphor.** A sea nobody can net; a thing that stops being મારું and becomes
  સહિયારું; salt in what was sweet. Say what the picture *does*, never re-state it as a fact about
  the world.
- **મિજાજ — the poem's temper.** Playful, defiant, tender, resigned. std-9 ch-14 is a child's
  bargain turned generous; std-8 ch-10 is human effort against something unbeatable and cheerful
  about losing. Name the temper once, in the words of this poem.
- **Repetition as લય.** દ્વિરુક્ત and echo forms — લાવ-લાવ, ફરી-ફરીને, પ્યારું-પ્યારું — are content,
  not decoration; std-9's own ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ block says so.
- **A one-word picture.** `થૂ` does an entire gesture in one syllable (std-9 ch-14, noted by the
  book itself). Small words carry weight in this form; point at them.
- **The આગત layer as ordinary Gujarati.** ગઝલ, શેર, દોસ્ત, દિલાવર are ફારસી-અરબી in origin and
  everyday in use. Gloss them like any other word; never make their origin a topic of its own.

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these per topic as a testable sentence; Agent 13 blocks on them; the
structural ones are checked at Agent 4.

- **Merged or split શેર — structural, checked at Agent 4.** The number of topics carrying a
  `[[શેર …]]` marker equals the number of `[[શેર …]]` markers in `00_chapter_normalized.md`; no
  topic's `original_chunk` contains more than one શેર; every શેર topic's `original_chunk` contains
  both printed lines of that શેર. Any inequality is a blocking fail owned by A2.
- **No running story across શેર.** No sentence of `explanation`, `summary`, `brief_summary`,
  `detailed_summary`, `real_life_example`, any `concept_bullets` / `important_points` line, or any
  `recall_questions[].answer` asserts a temporal, causal or narrative link between this શેર's
  content and another શેર's content — no "આગળના શેરમાં", "પછીના શેરમાં", "ત્યાર પછી કવિ",
  "એ પછી શું થાય છે", "છેવટે કવિ સાબિત કરે છે". The **only** permitted cross-શેર reference is to the
  returning રદીફ/કાફિયા, to the poem's title, or (in the મક્તા topic only) to the fact that this is
  the closing શેર.
- **Every શેર topic names the return.** The topic's `explanation` contains the રદીફ string
  character-for-character as printed in this topic's `original_chunk`; where the poem has no રદીફ,
  it contains this શેર's કાફિયા word, likewise character-for-character. A topic with neither is
  half-taught (mechanical substring check at Agent 13).
- **Mood-vocabulary is never read literally.** સાકી, જામ, પ્યાલો, મયખાનું, શરાબ, મદિરા, ઇશ્ક,
  મહેબૂબ, વિરહ, દર્દ, જખમ, કત્લ — if any such word occurs in this topic's `original_chunk`, the
  topic's `explanation` must name it as કલ્પન / પ્રતીક / રૂપક in the same sentence that uses it, and
  no field may state it as a fact about anyone's life. And no such word may be **introduced** into
  any authored field if it does not occur in the chapter's own printed text. *(None of the three
  measured chapters contains one of these words; the gate binds forward.)*
- **Never romanticise the form for a school reader.** No `explanation`, `real_life_example`,
  `publication_text`, summary or recall answer presents drinking, intoxication, longing for a
  beloved, self-harm or a wish to die as beautiful, admirable, brave or desirable; and no
  `real_life_example` places a std 6–10 child in an adult romantic or intoxication scene. The anchor
  is the child's own world (`reference/teaching_voice_gu.md`), one anchor only.
- **No invented ટેક.** No authored field calls any line of a ગઝલ a ટેક or a ધ્રુવપંક્તિ, and
  `00_chapter_normalized.md` carries no `[[ટેક]]` marker in a ગઝલ chapter. The રદીફ is a
  line-ending; saying otherwise teaches the wrong form.
- **No form-part as an અલંકાર.** No `figures_of_speech[].device` value is રદીફ, કાફિયા, મત્લા,
  મક્તા, તખલ્લુસ, છાપ, પ્રાસ or ગઝલ. Devices that ARE named must be real: every
  `figures_of_speech[].lines` string appears character-for-character inside this topic's
  `original_chunk` (checked at Agent 13). `[]` is a correct and complete answer.
- **The તખલ્લુસ stays in the verse.** The signature word inside the મક્તા is not moved into an
  attribution field, not deleted, not expanded into the poet's full name inside `original_chunk`,
  and not described as a printing error. Conversely the printed attribution line and the
  `('…'માંથી)` source line are not treated as part of the last શેર's argument.
- **Un-glossed form-words.** The first occurrence of શેર, રદીફ, કાફિયા, મત્લા, મક્તા or તખલ્લુસ in
  the chapter's authored prose is glossed in Gujarati in the same sentence. Later uses are free.
- **Exercise-embedded poems are never chapter text.** std-8 ch-10's સ્વાધ્યાય quotes poems by
  રજની મહેતા, સુરેશ દલાલ and સુન્દરમ્. No such line is cut as a topic, quoted into any
  `original_chunk`, or used in `figures_of_speech[].lines`. Agent 10 owns them
  (`reference/exercise_alignment.md`).
- **Never modernise the poet.** તળપદા and ellipted forms — શીદ, લગ, થૂ, કદીયે — stay exactly as
  printed and are explained as બોલી and લય, never corrected toward માનક ગુજરાતી
  (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **No numbers in display text.** `"બીજા શેરમાં"`, never `"શેર 2માં"`. Numbers live in ids and
  provenance fields only.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per શેર, showing **that શેર's own concrete thing**: the net lying on the water
with the sea beyond it; two children and the one thing standing between them; salt on a tongue.
Indian setting, soft digital watercolour, vibrant textbook illustration style, 16:9, narrator bar
carrying one exact Gujarati line from that શેર. Three refusals specific to this form: **never one
frame for the whole ગઝલ** (there is no whole to draw); **never a frame that continues the previous
frame** — no returning character walking from picture to picture, because that is the running story
the gate forbids, drawn instead of written; and **never a caption-image of the રદીફ** repeated seven
times. Where the mood-lexicon appears, image the safe concrete object or the situation, never a
drinking scene, never a romantic pairing. ≤1 `2d_tool` per chapter and a ગઝલ rarely needs one;
`image_url: ""` with an authored self-contained `generation_prompt` for every scene — there is no
Gujarati frame pool (`agents/09_media_planning.md`).

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — the two standards differ and both are measured. **std 8** prints *no* સ્વાધ્યાય
banner: the exercises begin unlabelled after the yellow શબ્દાર્થ box, item 1 is always **વાતચીત.**
(twelve oral prompts on દરિયો in ch-10), a **શબ્દસમૂહ માટે એક શબ્દ** pre-block sits above them, the
**last** numbered item is always a first-language translation, and a teacher-addressed
**ચર્ચા-વિચારણા** box closes the chapter. **std 9** prints MCQ with the (√) mark, then
બે-ત્રણ વાક્યોમાં ઉત્તર, then સવિસ્તાર ઉત્તર, followed by વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ,
**ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ** and **શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા**. The two items this form must prepare deliberately:
the **પ્રાસ-word hunt** (std-8 ch-10 item 8, six blanks) — each શેર topic supplies one blank, so
name that rhyme in that topic's explanation; and the **line-meaning / ગદ્યમાં રૂપાંતર** item, which
is simply the શેર-is-complete principle set as homework. Exercises are never topics; Agent 10
answers every printed block; the inventory comes from `01_meta.json`, never from this list.

**Language** — modern standard Gujarati carrying two extra layers: the form's own **આગત
(ફારસી-અરબી)** vocabulary (ગઝલ, શેર, દોસ્ત, દિલાવર) and ellipted **તળપદા** forms (શીદ = શા માટે,
લગ = સુધી, થૂ). Gloss at point of first use in `"શબ્દ — અર્થ"` form, 3–6 per topic; the printed
શબ્દાર્થ / શબ્દ-સમજૂતી box is evidence of what stopped the editor, not the limit of what stops an
L2 child — the bar sits **lower** here (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`). Poetic licence and dialect are
explained, never corrected (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`). Where the ગઝલ's syntax inverts or
ellipses a verb, say what the ordinary order would be *and leave the line as printed*.

**Sensitivity** — the ગઝલ arrived in Gujarati from Persian and Urdu, and its poets in this reader
include આદિલ મન્સૂરી. Two guides, never a censorship (`agents/08_sensitivity_safety.md`): teach the
form's ancestry as **literary history and a shared Gujarati inheritance** — no authored field turns
the form, its vocabulary, or a poet's name into a statement about community or religion, and no
comparative-religion turn is taken. And where the inherited mood-lexicon appears, the guidance is
the gate above: read it as image, gloss it plainly, do not dwell on it, do not moralise about it,
and do not build a real-life example on it.

## Recall priors

Per topic, two to three, Bloom-laddered, each with a real model answer:

- one **remember** — what this શેર names, or which word comes back at its line-end;
- one **understand** — what this શેર says **on its own**. The model answer must be complete without
  any other શેર, and must not mention one; this is the form-specific check and it is readable by
  eye;
- one **apply**, **analyze** or **evaluate** — on why *this* કાફિયા word and not another, on what
  the picture does, or on the child's own surroundings.

**One whole-poem question, at most, in the entire chapter**, and its home is the મક્તા topic's third
question (`આખી ગઝલમાં એક જ શબ્દ વારે વારે પાછો આવે છે — એથી વાંચતાં શું થાય છે?`). Any second
whole-poem question is the running-story error entering through the recall ladder, and Agent 7
flags it there.
