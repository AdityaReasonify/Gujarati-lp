## Diagnosis signals

Short, closed verse units printed one after another with numbers or a rule between them, **each one
finished where it stops**. Nothing before a unit is needed to read it and nothing after it completes
it — the tell that separates this સ્વરૂપ from every other verse form in the readers.

- **દુહો** — two printed lines, four ચરણ, the તુક at the line ends: `એકલ દેતા દાન જે, એકલ ઝૂઝત જંગ, /
  એકલ જગનિંદા સહે, એ વીરોના રંગ` (std-9 ch-22). The author slot may be **empty** — that દુહો names
  no poet at all; only the source is printed, `('સાયુજ્ય'માંથી)`.
- **છપ્પો** — a self-contained argument closing on a ચોટ, with the poet's **છાપ inside the verse**:
  `એ તો અખા બહુ ઉત્પાત...`, `અખા મોટાની તો એવી જાણ...` (std-9 ch-1). The era line is a century, not
  a date — `અખો (સમય : સત્તરમું શતક-પૂર્વાર્ધ)` — and the source is `('અખાના છપ્પા'માંથી)`. What is
  printed may be an **excerpt**: ch-1 prints a three-line and a four-line piece, not six-line છપ્પા.
  Teach what is printed.
- **મુક્તક** — a few lines, one turn, a ચોટ at the end; the book's own definition is લાઘવ + ચોટ.
  std-9 ch-22 prints `રાજઘાટ પર` by હસમુખ પાઠક: `આટલાં ફૂલો નીચે ને આટલો લાંબો સમય / ગાંધી કદી સૂતા
  નથી -` — note the ending dash, which is the poem.
- **હાઈકુ** — three lines, one image, no argument and no પ્રાસ. std-9 ch-22 prints રાવજી પટેલનું
  `ગીત સાંભળી / ડુંડું ડોલ્યું, ઉપર / ચકલી બેઠી`, and the chapter's own intro defines the form:
  "મૂળ જાપાની કાવ્યપ્રકાર... ત્રણ પંક્તિમાં પાંચ-સાત-પાંચ અક્ષરોનું ચોક્કસ બંધારણ".
- **The chapter shape.** A લઘુકાવ્યો chapter prints each form under its own section head with its
  own intro paragraph, its own poet and its own source, separated by star rules — `genre: "mixed"`,
  parts dominant-first, every part cut under this same unit rule.
- **The સ્વાધ્યાય** asks about **each piece separately** (std-9 ch-22 block two: three questions, one
  per form), and the વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ asks the child to **collect or memorise** short pieces
  ("દાદા-દાદીના હોઠવગાં બે-ત્રણ દુહા/મુક્તક નોટબુકમાં લખી સૂચના ફલક પર મૂકો"; std-9 ch-1: "છપ્પા
  કંઠસ્થ કરો"). Both are Signal-C evidence for this સ્વરૂપ.

**The decisive tell: the unit is complete in itself and the next unit owes it nothing.** Length,
theme and શિખામણ-content are all secondary; unit length before theme
(`reference/genre_diagnosis.md`).

## Lens

**અનુભવની શિખામણ + દૃષ્ટાંત** — રોજિંદા જીવનનું એક ચિત્ર, જીવનનો એક નિયમ બને ત્યાં સુધી પકડી રાખેલું.

## Guiding question

*કવિ રોજિંદા જીવનના કયા દૃશ્યથી જીવનની વાત કહે છે ?*

(A shape, not a template. std-9 ch-1's own question runs nearer to *અખો કોની મશ્કરી કરે છે, અને કયા
શબ્દથી ?*; std-9 ch-22's હાઈકુ needs a question with no શિખામણ in it at all — *ગીત સંભળાયું ત્યારે
ખેતરમાં શું શું થયું ?* Each chapter derives its own.)

## Explanation unit — **one printed piece, always**

This is the profile's whole reason to exist. A દુહા/છપ્પા/લઘુકાવ્યો page is a set of **independent
poems printed together**; consecutive pieces share a page, sometimes a poet, sometimes not even
that. Merging two because they are short is the single most damaging cut this pack can make.

- **One દુહો = one topic. One છપ્પો = one topic. One મુક્તક = one topic. One હાઈકુ = one topic.**
  No exceptions, no "these two go together", no "લઘુકાવ્યો" overview topic holding two pieces.
- The topic count for the verse part **equals** the number of printed pieces, and the ids run in
  printed order.
- The two lines of a દુહો stay two lines, with the caesura and the comma as printed. The three lines
  of a હાઈકુ stay three lines. A છપ્પો excerpt keeps exactly the number of lines the page prints.
- The **છાપ is part of the verse**, never an attribution label: `અખા` inside the line stays inside
  the line. The printed `— કવિનું નામ` / `('અખાના છપ્પા'માંથી)` attribution is the separate thing and
  belongs inside the LAST topic's `original_chunk` (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- Where a piece prints **no poet** (std-9 ch-22's દુહો), the topic carries no poet. Silence is the
  correct output.
- In a mixed લઘુકાવ્યો chapter this profile is loaded for every part, but **each part keeps its own
  reading protocol** — see the next section. The parts are cut alike and taught unalike.
- The chapter's intro paragraph and its શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા often pre-explain the pieces (std-9 ch-1
  pre-explains both છપ્પા). That prose is evidence about the chapter; it is not the explanation, and
  it is not child-facing text to copy.

## How a દુહો is built, and therefore how it is taught

Almost every દુહો has the same two-beat shape:

| line | does |
|---|---|
| **1** | sets a picture from ordinary life — a neem tree, a lone fighter, sweat, water, a stone |
| **2** | turns it into a rule about people |

The `explanation` follows that shape: **show the picture first, in plain words, then make the turn
explicit.** A child given only the conclusion has the answer without the argument and cannot use it
on the next દુહો. std-9 ch-22's દુહો is built exactly so — three pictures of a man alone (દાન, જંગ,
જગનિંદા) and then the naming: `એ વીરોના રંગ`.

**છપ્પો** runs the same engine longer: a picture or a piece of behaviour observed, held up, and then
cut with a ચોટ — and in અખો's hands the tone is **કટાક્ષ**. `પાણી દેખી કરે સનાન, તુલસી દેખી તોડે
પાન,` is not instruction; it is mimicry of a person the poet is laughing at, and the explanation
must say whom the poem is mocking before it says anything else. Reading a satiric છપ્પો straight —
as advice to bathe when you see water — inverts the poem.

**મુક્તક** compresses the same shape into a few lines, and the whole weight lands on the last one:
`ગાંધી કદી સૂતા નથી -`. The turn is the ending; explain the picture (the flowers, the years, the
memorial), then let the last line do its own work. The book's own ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ names the
લાક્ષણિકતા as "થોડા શબ્દો, મોટી વાત" — that economy is the craft and is worth naming once.

**હાઈકુ has no rule in it at all, and therefore no શિખામણ language may be imported onto it.** Its
shape is different:

| line(s) | does |
|---|---|
| **1** | one image, named plainly |
| **cut** | a turn of attention — the poem looks somewhere else |
| **last** | a second image that lets the first resonate; nothing is concluded |

`ગીત સાંભળી / ડુંડું ડોલ્યું, ઉપર / ચકલી બેઠી` — a song is heard, an ear of grain sways, a sparrow
settles. That is the whole poem. The explanation stays inside the field: what was heard, what moved,
what came to rest, and how the small picture holds the child's attention. It does **not** say that we
should live close to nature, and it does **not** call anything a બોધ or a શિખામણ. A હાઈકુ explained
as a દુહો has been thrown away.

## Form facts — say each once, at module level

`overall_rhyme_scheme` is where the form fact goes, once, per `reference/alankar_chhand.md`:

- **દુહો / દોહરો** — બે છપાયેલી લીટી, ચાર ચરણ; તુક લીટીના છેડે; એક દુહો = આખી એક વાત.
- **સોરઠો** — દુહાની ભાઈબંધ ચાલ, પણ તુક પહેલા/ત્રીજા ચરણને છેડે ખસી જાય છે. *Unwitnessed in this
  corpus — provisional.*
- **છપ્પો** — છ પંક્તિનું કટાક્ષ-કાવ્ય, છાપ પંક્તિની અંદર; std-9 ch-1 prints three- and four-line
  excerpts, and **what is printed is what is true**.
- **હાઈકુ** — ત્રણ પંક્તિ, પાંચ-સાત-પાંચ અક્ષર (std-9 ch-22's own printed intro is the source for
  this count; no other count may be asserted).
- **મુક્તક** — લાઘવ + ચોટ; no fixed line count.

**Never write a માત્રા count this pack has not verified.** The Hindi pack's `13+11` does not apply to
any Gujarati form here and must never appear. Model-recalled Gujarati prosody is inadmissible: the
only two sources are the chapter's own printed intro and a human-verified registry
(`reference/alankar_chhand.md`). Absent both, name the shape the child can see — line count, where
the તુક falls — and stop. છંદ is named at std 10 only.

## Emphasise

- **The દૃષ્ટાંત** — the concrete picture, named plainly, before any moral language: લીમડો, પરસેવો,
  ઘૂડ અને સૂર્ય, હીરો અને પહાણ, એકલો લડનારો.
- **The turn** — the exact word where the picture becomes about people (`એ વીરોના રંગ`).
- **The ચોટ** — where the piece lands, and why the poet kept it to this many words.
- **The કટાક્ષ, where it is there.** std-9 ch-1's ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ names it for us: `મોટા` is
  વક્રોક્તિ, and ઘુવડ↔સૂર્ય, હીરો↔પહાણ are વિરોધાભાસનાં પ્રતીકો. Irony is L2-hard and must be taught,
  not assumed.
- **Compression.** These forms say in two lines what an essay takes a page to say. Name the economy
  once, with the words quoted.
- **પ્રાસ and repetition where the page shows them** — `જંગ`–`રંગ`, and `એકલ` three times over. std
  6–8: describe the effect without naming a device; std 9–10: name it, quote it, say what it does
  (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`).
- **Usability.** A દુહો is meant to be *used*: `real_life_example` puts it in one situation the child
  will actually meet this month, Indian and concrete, ending on a question where that helps.
- **The image, for a હાઈકુ.** Not the lesson — the picture, the cut, and the quiet after it.

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these as a per-topic testable sentence; Agent 13 blocks on `hard`
severity; the structural ones are checked at Agent 4 against the `[[દુહો n]]` / `[[છપ્પો n]]` /
`[[મુક્તક]]` / `[[હાઈકુ]]` marker counts.

- **Merging two pieces. Checked at Agent 4.** `count([[દુહો*]]) + count([[છપ્પો*]]) +
  count([[મુક્તક*]]) + count([[હાઈકુ*]]) == count(topics carrying one of those markers)`; no topic's
  `original_chunk` may contain text from two marker blocks, and no two topics may carry slices of one
  block. A chapter-level "લઘુકાવ્યો" topic holding more than one piece is a blocking fail.
- **The શિખામણ without the દૃષ્ટાંત.** The first sentence of `explanation` must quote or name a
  concrete noun that occurs in **this topic's** `original_chunk`, before any sentence containing a
  rule word (જોઈએ / સૌએ / હંમેશાં / આપણે બધાએ / બોધ / શિખામણ). `"આપણે વીર બનવું જોઈએ"` is not an
  explanation of a દુહો; it is what is left after the poem has been thrown away.
- **A moral lecture.** At most **one** sentence of `explanation` may state the rule, and
  `real_life_example` may not restate it as a directive. No sentence of `explanation`,
  `detailed_summary`, `real_life_example` or `recall_questions[].answer` may end on a
  સૌએ/હંમેશાં/જીવનમાં … જોઈએ clause. The poem already makes its point; repeating it in the teacher's
  voice, at length, is the failure this સ્વરૂપ invites.
- **Importing શિખામણ language onto a હાઈકુ.** For any topic whose marker is `[[હાઈકુ]]`, the strings
  બોધ, શિખામણ, ઉપદેશ, જોઈએ, સૌએ and હંમેશાં may not occur in `explanation`, `summary`,
  `detailed_summary`, `concept_bullets`, `real_life_example` or any `recall_questions[].answer`; the
  last sentence of `explanation` must name something present in the poem's own three lines.
- **Reading a satiric છપ્પો straight.** Where the chapter's intro or ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ names કટાક્ષ /
  વ્યંગ / વક્રોક્તિ (std-9 ch-1 does both), at least one sentence of `explanation` must state whom or
  what the poet is mocking, quoting the poem's own word (`મોટા`, `ઘૂડ`). An explanation that presents
  the mimicked behaviour as the poet's advice is a blocking fail.
- **Modernising the language.** Every quotation in `explanation`, `key_terms`, `concept_bullets`,
  `figures_of_speech[].lines`, `recall_questions[].answer` and `publication_text` must match
  `original_chunk` character for character: `સનાન` stays `સનાન`, `પહાણ` stays `પહાણ`, `ન્હાય` stays
  `ન્હાય`, `ઝૂઝત` stays `ઝૂઝત`, `એકલ` stays `એકલ`. મધ્યકાલીન and તળપદા forms are glossed, never
  corrected, and the કાવ્ય-રૂપ is named as craft so an L2 child does not read it as a misprint.
- **Losing the છાપ.** The છાપ as printed must occur, character for character, in the chapter's
  concatenated `original_chunk`. Trimming `અખા`, moving it out of the verse, or converting it into an
  attribution label is a hard fail.
- **Inventing biography.** No claim about a poet's life beyond the printed કવિ-પરિચય and era line —
  and અખો's era line is a century (`સમય : સત્તરમું શતક-પૂર્વાર્ધ`), not a date. Where the piece names
  no poet (std-9 ch-22's દુહો), no poet may be supplied, inferred from the source title, or guessed
  from style. `[]` and silence beat invention (`reference/no_hallucination_policy.md`).
- **Paraphrasing the chapter's own apparatus.** No sentence of `explanation` may reuse a run of six or
  more consecutive words from the intro paragraph, the કવિ-પરિચય or શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા. Those blocks are
  teacher-addressed evidence; the explanation is built from the verse.
- **Media that shows the moral.** Every media `generation_prompt` must name at least one concrete
  object, creature or action drawn from **this topic's** `original_chunk`, and may not depict a
  virtue being performed, a lesson panel, lettering of the moral, or a classroom-poster tableau.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per piece, showing the **દૃષ્ટાંત**, never the moral. A દુહો about the lone
fighter gets one figure standing alone at the edge of a crowd; a છપ્પો about ઘુવડ and સૂર્ય gets the
owl and the daylight; the હાઈકુ gets the swaying ear of grain and the sparrow settling on it — not a
child being kind, not people learning a lesson. The abstraction is what the child must build; the
picture is what makes it buildable. Prompts are self-contained (setting, figures with fixed
appearance, action, mood, style), soft digital watercolour, 16:9, Gujarat-Indian setting;
`negative_prompt` carries the baseline of `reference/field_shape_rules.md` (including `Devanagari
script labels`) plus `motivational poster layout, text banner with a moral, halo, idol photograph`.
The narrator bar carries one exact line of Gujarati in Gujarati script. At most one `2d_tool` per
chapter, and these chapters rarely need it.

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — measured at std-9 ch-1: `પ્રશ્નની નીચે આપેલા વિકલ્પોમાંથી સાચો વિકલ્પ પસંદ કરી ખરાની
(✓) નિશાની કરો :` (5 MCQ) → `નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના બે-ત્રણ વાક્યોમાં ઉત્તર લખો :` (2) → `નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના
છ-સાત વાક્યોમાં ઉત્તર લખો :` (2). At std-9 ch-22 the ladder shortens to two blocks — 2 MCQ, then
`નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના બે-ત્રણ વાક્યમાં ઉત્તર લખો :` with **one question per form**. Both chapters then
print વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ (કંઠસ્થ કરો; ગમતાં મુક્તકોનો હસ્તલિખિત અંક; દાદા-દાદી પાસેથી દુહા ઉતારો),
ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ (craft commentary — પ્રાસ, પુનરાવર્તન, વક્રોક્તિ) and **શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા**
(teacher-addressed: never an exercise, never a topic, never answered as one). Banner wording
micro-varies across the readers: Agent 1 inventories the exact printed headings per chapter and
Agent 10 answers every block from that inventory, never from this list. Where a topic prepares the
collect-a-દુહો or વિચારવિસ્તાર task, its explanation should name the two-beat shape as the thing to
imitate; where it prepares a કંઠસ્થ task, name the તુક and the repetition that make it stick.

**Language** — મધ્યકાલીન ગુજરાતી with તળપદા forms, glossed generously at point of first use in
`"શબ્દ — અર્થ"` form (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`). The books gloss them on the page themselves
(std-9 ch-1: `પહાણ પથ્થર`, `સનાન સ્નાન`, `મૂરખ મૂર્ખ`, `દેખી જોઈને`, `કૂડેકૂડ છળ, કપટ, પ્રપંચ`,
`ઘૂડ ઘુવડ`; std-9 ch-22: `એકલ એકલા`, `ઝૂઝત ઝઝૂમવું`, `જંગ યુદ્ધ`) — a printed gloss is **evidence
that the word stops a reader, not a source to copy**. For an L2 reader the bar sits lower than a
first-language pack's: inversions like `એકલ ઝૂઝત જંગ` and `મૂકી હીરો ઉપાડે પહાણ` need the plain word
order said once, in Gujarati, without calling the poet's order wrong. Script discipline per
`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`: માત્રા, અનુસ્વાર, ચંદ્રબિંદુ and જોડાક્ષર exactly as printed; ળ/લ
and શ/ષ/સ are load-bearing; full stop `.` as printed — the Devanagari danda is never introduced.

**Sensitivity** — અખો's છપ્પા mock empty ritual and the people who trade on it (`પાણી દેખી કરે સનાન,
તુલસી દેખી તોડે પાન,`), and the class holding the book belongs to many faiths. Teach it as the
chapter does: the poet's attack on ઢોંગ and on borrowed authority, in his own words — never as a
verdict on any community's practice, never as comparative religion, never softened or cut
(`08_sensitivity.json` area label `ધર્મ`; flag-and-guide, never censor). std-9 ch-22's મુક્તક
`રાજઘાટ પર` turns its irony on a national memorial: teach the contrast the poem draws — ફૂલો and
years against a life of કર્મ — and keep present-day politics out of `explanation`,
`real_life_example` and every exercise answer (area label `સંઘર્ષ` where it is raised at all).

## Recall priors

Three per piece, laddered — and the ladder differs by form.

For a **દુહો / છપ્પો / મુક્તક**:

- one **remember** — what is the picture, in the poem's own words (`એકલ` શું શું કરે છે ?);
- one **understand** — what the second line, or the ચોટ, turns that picture into;
- one **apply** — where the child would use this, in one situation from their own week.

At std 9–10 the third may be **analyze** or **evaluate** where the text carries it: why અખો chose
`ઘૂડ` and not another bird, or what the dash at the end of `ગાંધી કદી સૂતા નથી -` leaves unsaid.

For a **હાઈકુ**, the ladder is the same three rungs with no rule in any of them:

- one **remember** — what is heard, what moves, what settles;
- one **understand** — where the poem's attention turns between the lines;
- one **analyze** — what the last line does to the first, or why so few words hold the picture.

Every answer is a real model answer, and every answer ends inside the poem — on the neem tree, the
owl, the lone fighter, the sparrow — not on a rule of conduct.
