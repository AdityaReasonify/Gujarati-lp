## Why one profile

The two forms are merged because **the cutting unit is the same, and the textbook itself treats
them as one skill.**

The unit is one stage of the journey, or one matter the letter turns to — in both cases a stretch of
first-person prose that the writer treats as a single stop, bounded by a change in where the writer
is or in what the writer has turned to talk about. Neither form has a plot, and neither has a
વળાંક: they are description addressed to somebody, laid out in the order the writer moved or the
order the writer thought of things.

The books say so themselves. std-7 ch 15's blue intro box names both forms in one sentence —
"બાળકોમાં **નિબંધનું અને પ્રવાસવર્ણનનું** કૌશલ્ય ખીલે એ માટે માર્ગદર્શક બની શકે એવો આ નિબંધ છે." —
and then its સ્વાધ્યાય block 6 asks for the travel account **as a letter**:
"તમે કરેલા પ્રવાસનું વર્ણન કરતો પત્ર તમારા મિત્રને લખો." A chapter that is a પ્રવાસવર્ણન sets a
પત્ર as its own writing task. Meanwhile std-6 ch 8, a પત્ર chapter, spends most of its body on two
artists' lives — a ચરિત્ર-પ્રસંગ carried **inside** the letter — and its own intro box states the
skill target as letter-writing craft: "પત્રલેખનનું કૌશલ્ય ઘણું જ મહત્ત્વનું છે. બાળકોને પત્ર કેવી રીતે
લખાય, પત્ર લખતી વખતે શી શી કાળજી લેવી જોઈએ તે વિશેની સમજ આપવી એ પણ આ એકમનો આશય છે."

What differs between the two — Media composition, some Language load, and which Sensitivity areas
apply — is handled inside the Priors, not by splitting the profile.

## Diagnosis signals

**First-person prose addressed to somebody, laid out in the order the writer moved or turned.**
Two shapes route here.

**Shape A — પત્ર.** The page prints the letter's own furniture. In std-6 ch 8 that is a sender block
set top-right ("વિવેક ભારતીય / વિઠ્ઠલનગર સોસાયટી, રાંદેર, સુરત / તા. 18-10-22"), a salutation on its
own line ("પ્રિય પ્રાચી,"), a body of personal news, and a closing signature line
("- વિવેકમામાનાં આશિષ"). The addressee is **named**, and the writer's relationship to that addressee
is named or obvious from the closing.

⚠ **Do not make "લિ." the tell.** `reference/genre_diagnosis.md`'s prose branch currently reads
"addressed to a named reader — સ્થળ/તારીખ/સંબોધન, closing લિ."; the one letter chapter actually
measured in this corpus closes on "- વિવેકમામાનાં આશિષ" and prints no લિ. anywhere. The tell is the
**pair** — a salutation to a named reader at the top and a signature-with-relationship at the bottom
— in whatever formula the book prints.

**Shape B — પ્રવાસવર્ણન / પ્રવાસ-નિબંધ.** Between the opening and the departure the text advances by
**place**: the approach, the place itself, the people met there, the leaving. std-7 ch 15 opens on
what Rajasthan is *not* (a mere રણપ્રદેશ), passes through ઉદયપુર, જયપુર, ચિત્તોડગઢ, જોધપુર, ભરતપુર
and નાથદ્વારા, then settles at જેસલમેર — the ઢૂવા, the golden fort in dawn and dusk light, the
કોતરણી, પટવાઓની હવેલીઓ. std-6 P1 item 2 walks ભુજ → ખાવડા → ધોરડો, then કાળો ડુંગર, લુડિયા,
ધોળાવીરા, લખપત, માંડવી-મુન્દ્રા. Description dominates; there is no plot and no turn.

**The tell that separates this profile from `mahitiprad_gadya` — the reorder test.** Ask whether the
sections could be printed in a different order without loss. A માહિતીપ્રદ chapter is organised by
**subject** and could be reordered: the ગરબો/ગરબી ભેદ does not have to come before the list of
લોકનૃત્યો. A પ્રવાસવર્ણન could not, because the order **is** the journey — you cannot reach ધોરડો
before ખાવડા. A પત્ર could not either, because a letter's order is the order the writer thought of
things, and the salutation cannot follow the news. Where the reorder test says "could be reordered",
the chapter is not this profile however much travel vocabulary it carries.

**Signal C — what the સ્વાધ્યાય asks (measured).** Three block types route here:

- a **composition in the same form as the chapter** — "આ પત્ર વિવેકમામાએ પ્રાચીને લખ્યો છે. હવે પ્રાચી
  વિવેકમામાને પત્ર લખતી હોય એ રીતે પત્ર લખો." (std-6 ch 8 blk 10) and
  "તમે કરેલા પ્રવાસનું વર્ણન કરતો પત્ર તમારા મિત્રને લખો." (std-7 ch 15 blk 6). The book asks the
  child to **produce** the form, not merely to answer questions about it. No other prose genre in
  this corpus does that so consistently;
- **form-furniture drill** — "આ સરનામાને યોગ્ય ક્રમમાં ગોઠવીને લખો." (std-6 ch 8 blk 11, two
  scrambled postal addresses, પિન - 364313) and the cloze **reply letter** printed as an exercise
  (std-6 ch 8 blk 7, સિદ્ધિ writing back to પ્રાચી — a second complete letter model inside the
  exercise apparatus);
- **document literacy about travel** — std-7 ch 15 blk 11 prints a real પ્રવાસ જાહેરાત (ધોરણ 6-8,
  નામ નોંધાવવાની છેલ્લી તારીખ 20 ડિસેમ્બર, ST બસ, ફી ₹2500) and asks five questions off it, including
  date arithmetic and eligibility reasoning.

What is **absent** is as telling. Neither measured chapter prints an event-ordering block or a
counterfactual — the two blocks that mark a વાર્તા (std-6 ch 2, std-7 ch 3 and ch 6 all print at
least one). std-6 ch 8 does print a speaker-attribution block ("પાઠના આધારે નક્કી કરો કે નીચેનાં
વાક્યો કોણ બોલી શકે ?"), and that is **not** a story signal here: the utterances belong to people the
letter reports on, not to characters in a plot.

**Where the chapter is a પૂરકવાચન piece, expect no numbered exercises at all.** std-6 P1 and std-8 P3
print none. That is normal for the section and is recorded as such in `01_meta.json` — an empty
`exercise_inventory` is a measured fact, not a gap. std-8 P3 still prints a શબ્દાર્થ box, a
"શબ્દસમૂહ માટે એક શબ્દ આપો." block of eight items and a one-item રૂઢિપ્રયોગ block, so its inventory
is not empty; std-6 P1 item 2's is.

**The mixed case, stated plainly.** std-6 ch 8 genuinely carries ચરિત્ર-પ્રસંગ content inside a પત્ર
frame, and std-8 P3 carries a family's quoted dialogue inside a પ્રવાસનિબંધ frame. Neither is a

## Lens

**પડાવ + દૃષ્ટિ + સંબોધન**

Where the writer has stopped, what the writer notices there, and the person the writer is telling.
The three are one thing: the addressee is *why* this stop and not another got written down, and *why*
it got written down this way.

This lens subsumes both rows currently in `reference/teaching_lens_map.md` — પ્રવાસવર્ણન's
સ્થળ + દૃષ્ટિ + વિગત and પત્ર's સંબોધન + સમાચાર + સ્નેહ. Those two rows are folded into this one
when the slug merge closes.

*લખનાર કોને કહે છે, અને એ અહીં જ કેમ થોભે છે?*

The wording above is a **shape**. Each chapter derives its own from its own text and Agent 1 writes

- std-7 ch 15 — *લેખક જેસલમેરમાં એવું શું જુએ છે જે નકશો કે ફોટો બતાવી શકતો નથી?*

- std-8 P3 — *દાદા આ કાલ્પનિક પ્રવાસમાં પૌત્ર-પૌત્રીને શું બતાવવા માગે છે?*

## Explanation unit — **one પડાવ**

A **પડાવ** is one stop the writer treats as one: one place on the journey, or one matter the letter
turns to. The boundary is **a change of location, or a change in what the writer has turned to talk
about** — never a paragraph break, never a page break, and never a change of speaker inside quoted
dialogue.

This is the single unit that replaces both current rows in `reference/explanation_unit_map.md`
(પ્રવાસવર્ણન's "one place the traveller stops at", પત્ર's "one part of the letter"). The mapping is
exact: a place is a પડાવ, and a પત્રખંડ is a પડાવ.

- **The salutation and the sender block open the first પડાવ — they are not a topic of their own.**
  std-6 ch 8's address, date and "પ્રિય પ્રાચી," are the letter announcing itself and setting the
  voice for everything after. They belong inside the first topic's `original_chunk`, together with
  the કુશળ-સમાચાર sentences that follow them.
- **The sign-off closes the last પડાવ.** "- વિવેકમામાનાં આશિષ" is not formatting; it is where the
  writer's relationship to the reader is finally stated, and it is where the register of the whole
  letter is explained from. Teach it; do not drop it. The `- <name>` attribution convention of
  `reference/gujarati_verbatim.md` — attribution line inside the LAST topic — covers this exactly.
- **A digression stays inside its પડાવ.** These writers wander. std-7 ch 15 leaves the fort to
  mention સત્યજિત રાયનાં ચિત્રપટ ('સોનાર કેલા', 'હિરક રાજર દેશ', 'ગોપી ગાયે બાઘા બાયે') and then
  returns to the કોતરણી; std-6 P1 item 2 leaves ધોરડો to remember the 2001ના ભૂકંપની તારાજી and the
  rebuilding, then returns. The digression happened at that stop and belongs to it. Use `concepts[]`
  for such an internal turn — two concepts in one topic, never a third.
- **Inside a letter, one matter is one topic even when it runs long.** std-6 ch 8's two artists are
  two matters the letter turns to and therefore two પડાવ; the દિવાળીની શુભેચ્છા opening is a third,
  and the closing note about the મંડળ selling cards and calendars (and not taking donations) is a
  fourth. Cutting that letter at the paragraph marks instead would produce topics that mean nothing
  on their own.
- **Quoted dialogue does not create boundaries.** std-8 P3 carries its whole trip in the family's
  quoted speech with a દાદા answering two children. Cut at the stop — સાસણગીર, then સોમનાથ — not at
  the quote marks. Several turns normally sit inside one topic; a turn that carries two stops is
  split at the stop.
- **Do not reorder.** Logical order and printed order are the same for this form, always. The
  emitted plans still ship in the logical sequence per `reference/phase2_contract.md`, and Agent 15
  raises `{human_confirmation_required: true, …}` because the orders match — that flag is the
  correct output here, not a defect.
- Cutting rules 1–5 of `reference/explanation_unit_map.md` apply unchanged: exact verbatim first, a
  pre-topic hook belongs to the NEXT topic, single-theme topics, **સ્વાધ્યાય is never a topic**. A
  પૂરકવાચન unit that prints no સ્વાધ્યાય at all (std-6 P1, std-8 P3) is normal, not a gap.
- Printed **photographs** are page matter and never open a topic: std-6 ch 8's two captioned artist
  portraits, std-8 P3's real Somnath temple photos, std-7 ch 15's desert-sunset camel photograph
  (which is printed inside an exercise, not in the reading matter at all).

## How a પત્ર is built, and therefore how it is taught

The letter's anatomy is not formatting; the book teaches it as the chapter's skill, and it is
therefore TEXT. std-6 ch 8 prints all four parts, and each carries teaching:

| Printed part | As printed (std-6 ch 8) | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|
| મોકલનારનું સરનામું + તારીખ (top right) | વિવેક ભારતીય / વિઠ્ઠલનગર સોસાયટી, રાંદેર, સુરત / તા. 18-10-22 | where a letter comes from and when — the order of a Gujarati address, and the date form the book prints |
| સંબોધન | પ્રિય પ્રાચી, | who is being written to, and at what closeness — પ્રિય to a child of the family |
| પત્રનું કલેવર (the matters) | દિવાળીની શુભેચ્છા → મૃદુલ ઘોષ → મનોજ ભિંગારે → the મંડળ and its cards | the news, in the order the writer thought of it |
| સમાપન / સહી | - વિવેકમામાનાં આશિષ | the relationship, stated at the end, and the register it explains |

Three consequences, and they are what make this form different from every other prose profile:

1. **The furniture is quoted, not described.** A topic that says "પત્રની શરૂઆતમાં સંબોધન હોય છે"
   without the printed સંબોધન inside its `original_chunk` has taught nothing. Quote it, then explain
   it.
2. **The relationship is the register's cause, and it is named in the first topic.** મામા writing to
   his ભાણેજ produces આશિષ at the close, an affectionate imperative voice, and family news as the
   letter's business. A letter to an official would produce none of those. The explanation says which
   relationship this is and what it does to the words — that is the whole of the letter-craft
   teaching this form needs.
3. **The topics prepare a letter the child will actually write.** std-6 ch 8 blk 10 asks for પ્રાચી's
   reply and blk 7 prints a second letter as a cloze; std-7 ch 15 blk 6 asks for a travel letter to a
   friend. A topic that leaves the child able to describe a letter but not to write one has failed,
   however accurate it is (`reference/teaching_block_format.md` — name the સ્વાધ્યાય skill each topic
   prepares).

## Emphasise

- **The eye, not the encyclopaedia.** What the writer noticed is the content. The golden fort
  changing colour as the light changes at દા'ડો ઊગે અને આથમે; the ઢૂવા of જેસલમેર; the painted walls
  of the ભૂંગા at લુડિયા; the ડણક heard from a જીપ at સાસણ. That noticing is the teachable thing,
  never the geography behind it.
- **The addressee shapes the writing, and is named.** std-6 ch 8 is written by a મામા to a ભાણેજ at
  દિવાળી, and that is why it carries શુભેચ્છા, family warmth and આશિષ. std-6 P1 item 2 addresses
  "બાળદોસ્તો" outright, and that is why it is chatty, second-person and full of invitation. std-8 P3
  is a દાદા showing two grandchildren, and that is why it explains as it goes. Name the addressee and
  the relationship in the chapter's own words in the first topic's `explanation`.
- **The writer's own opinion sentences are craft, and are attributed.** std-7 ch 15 is a model essay
  precisely because it weaves opinion into description — its intro box says so. Where the chapter
  states a view, teach it as the writer's view ("લેખક કહે છે કે…"), never as a fact about the place.
- **Comparison as the traveller's tool.** Where the text compares — a place to another place, a
  colour to a metal, a district to a season — name the ઉપમા or રૂપક **only where it is genuinely on
  the page**, quote the exact words, and say what the comparison lets the reader see
  (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`). std-6 P1 item 2's own "ફિનિક્સ પક્ષીની જેમ બેઠું થયું" is the
  clearest instance in the measured corpus.
- **Loan words and local words as objects of study.** સેન્ડ ડ્યૂન, લેન્ડસ્કેપ, ફોરેસ્ટ, આર્ટિસ્ટ sit
  in Gujarati script beside કાઠું, ઠસ્સો, ભોમિયો, હંધાય, ઢોલિયો, તાંસળું. Point at one such word per
  પડાવ and let the child feel where it came from. Never romanise a loan back
  (`reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`).
- **Place, practice and person with respect.** Pilgrimage towns, shrines, craft families and the
  people who work them — સોમનાથ, દત્તાત્રેયનું મંદિર on કાળો ડુંગર, મા રવેચી અને મા જોગણ, પટવાઓની
  હવેલીઓ, the ભરત-work at the હાટમેળા. Describe the practice as the text describes it. This profile
  shares `mahitiprad_gadya`'s standard here, and `reference/global_content_rules.md` §10 governs.
- **Connection to the child's own world** — the letter they last received or wanted to write, the
  school પ્રવાસ their own class took by ST બસ, the fair or the fort in their own district. One
  anchor per topic, Indian and concrete (`reference/teaching_voice_gu.md`).

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these as a per-topic testable sentence; Agent 13 blocks on any hard item
left unaddressed. Each check names the field it is checked against.

1. **Cutting by subject instead of by પડાવ, or reordering the journey.** **Check:** the topic
   sequence in `learning_plan_logical.json` matches, item for item, the printed sequence recorded in
   `05b_textbook_order.json`; and every topic's `explanation` names, in its first sentence, the place
   the writer has reached or the matter the letter has turned to. A topic whose only description is
   "the next part of the chapter" fails. Severity: hard (structural — checked at Agent 4).
2. **Dropping the salutation or the sign-off, or treating them as formatting.** **Check:** for a
   પત્ર chapter, the FIRST topic's `original_chunk` contains the printed sender block, the printed
   date and the printed સંબોધન character for character, and the LAST topic's `original_chunk` ends
   with the printed closing line; neither is cut as a topic of its own; and neither appears **only**
   in `modified_chunk`, `summary`, `brief_summary` or `detailed_summary`. Severity: hard (structural
   — Agent 4 against the marker counts, Agent 5 against the rendered page).
3. **Leaving the addressee, or the relationship, unnamed.** **Check:** the first topic's
   `explanation` names who writes to whom and in what relationship, using the chapter's own words
   (મામા → ભાણેજ for std-6 ch 8; "બાળદોસ્તો" for std-6 P1 item 2; દાદા → પૌત્ર-પૌત્રી for std-8 P3);
   and at least one field of that topic — `explanation`, `concept_bullets` or `key_terms` in
   `"શબ્દ — અર્થ"` form — accounts for a register feature that relationship produces (the closing
   આશિષ, the affectionate imperative, the second-person invitation). Severity: hard.
4. **Inventing a destination detail the text does not give.** **Check:** every place name, distance,
   date, year, price, measurement, film title, building name and person name appearing in
   `explanation`, `summary`, `detailed_summary`, `important_points`, `concept_bullets`,
   `real_life_example` or a recall `answer` also appears in some `original_chunk` of this plan, or in
   the chapter's own printed શબ્દાર્થ / રૂઢિપ્રયોગ / જાહેરાત box. The ~70 km border figure and the
   ₹2500 fee of std-7 ch 15 are usable because they are printed; the district's area, its population
   and the fort's construction date are not. Severity: hard.
5. **Turning it into a travel guide or a geography lesson.** **Check:** no field supplies route
   instructions, fares, opening times, "best season to visit", altitudes, populations, administrative
   statistics or dynastic history that no `original_chunk` carries; and no field corrects or
   supplements the chapter's account from general knowledge about the place. It is a Gujarati
   chapter: teach the text and its language. Severity: hard.
6. **Turning the letter into a biography of the people it reports on.** **Check:** for a letter
   carrying ચરિત્ર-પ્રસંગ content (std-6 ch 8), no field adds a life-fact about the named persons
   beyond what the letter prints; no topic carries `topic_category: "climax"`; and no `explanation`
   or recall `answer` asks or answers why one of them *chose* to act as they did, or names a વળાંક.
   The letter reports; it does not narrate an arc. Severity: hard.
7. **Modernising, normalising or transliterating.** **Check:** `original_chunk` matches the rendered
   page exactly — the Hindi-slogan title printed in Gujarati script ("કચ્છ નહિ દેખા તો કુછ નહિ દેખા !")
   is transcribed as printed and never "corrected" to Gujarati; the printed date form "તા. 18-10-22",
   the pin code, the address line order and the English loans in Gujarati script (સેન્ડ ડ્યૂન,
   લેન્ડસ્કેપ, ફોરેસ્ટ, આર્ટિસ્ટ) stay as printed; spoken elisions stay as printed; base script is
   Gujarati (U+0A80–0AFF) with no Roman and no Devanagari outside bracketed technical terms; the full
   stop is `.` and the Devanagari દંડ (U+0964) never appears anywhere in the file. Severity: hard
   (Agent 5).
8. **A tacked-on બોધ or ઉપદેશ.** **Check:** no `explanation`, `summary`, `detailed_summary`,
   `concept_bullets` or recall `answer` closes on a sentence of the form
   "આપણે પણ પ્રવાસ કરવો જોઈએ" / "આપણે પત્ર લખવાની ટેવ પાડવી જોઈએ" / "આ પાઠ આપણને શીખવે છે…" that is
   not a verbatim quotation from the chapter. Where the chapter states its own closing appeal, quote
   it and teach it as the writer's move. Severity: hard.
9. **Pity, or inspiration-framing, where the chapter reports disability.** **Check:** no field
   contains બિચારા, લાચાર, અપંગ, દયા આવે, "આપણે તો કેટલા નસીબદાર" or "એમની પાસેથી પ્રેરણા લેવી જોઈએ"
   about the artists of std-6 ch 8; the chapter's own words are used (દિવ્યાંગ, as its વાતચીત block
   prints it; કાબેલ; ખાસિયત), and the ability is described as the letter describes it — that they
   paint holding the brush in the mouth, that one holds a diploma from સી. એન. ફાઈન આર્ટ્સ કૉલેજ, that
   the મંડળ sells cards and calendars and does not take donations. The area label in
   `08_sensitivity.json` is **વિકલાંગતા**. Severity: hard.
10. **Exoticising a place, a community or a form of worship — or, equally, explaining it away.**
    **Check:** no `explanation`, `real_life_example`, recall `answer` or exercise `answer` contains
    પછાત, અભણ, જંગલી, અસભ્ય, "આ લોકો" or "એ લોકોની રીતભાત"; where the chapter names a place,

    મા જોગણ, દત્તાત્રેયનું મંદિર, સોમનાથ, સાસણગીર, પટવાઓની હવેલી), the printed name is used and never

11. **Reading the writer's assumptions as the pack's voice.** **Check:** every opinion sentence taken

    silent edit. Severity: hard.
12. **Naming a device that is not there.** **Check:** every `figures_of_speech` entry quotes EXACT

## Priors

**Media** — one image per પડાવ, in the order the text moves, showing the place or the reported scene
as the text describes it and nothing more: the golden fort catching the low light with its કોતરણી
visible; the ઢૂવા with the camel line along the ridge; a painted ભૂંગા wall at લુડિયા; the જીપ track
at સાસણ with the સાવજ in the grass as the text places it. The one-sentence depiction test applies —
an image that cannot be described as a single moment somebody could photograph is a summary and must
be re-authored.

For a **પત્ર chapter the image shows what the letter reports, not the letter itself** — with one
exception: the first topic, which carries the સંબોધન, may show the writing or the receiving of the
letter, because that is the scene that topic actually teaches (a boy at a table finishing a letter by
a દિવાળીના દીવા; a girl reading a letter at a doorstep). One such frame per chapter, no more.

**Real named people are never authored into an image.** std-6 ch 8 prints captioned photographs of
two real, named, living-or-recent artists; std-8 P3 prints real photographs of સોમનાથ. A
`generation_prompt` must not depict a real named person's likeness, and must not invent a "scene
cast" of named characters the text never names. Where the chapter's art is a photograph, the authored
illustration for that scene depicts the activity or the setting generically, or the scene gets no
image at all — `[]` and an empty slot beat an invented portrait
(`reference/no_hallucination_policy.md`).

The one permitted `2d_tool` per chapter genuinely earns its place in this form, in exactly two
shapes: a **route strip** naming the stops the text names, in the text's own order (ભુજ → ખાવડા →
ધોરડો), for a પ્રવાસવર્ણન whose stops are printed; or a **labelled letter-anatomy panel** built from
this chapter's own printed lines, for a પત્ર chapter. Either is **at most one per chapter**, and a
route strip must not add a stop the text does not name.

Standing conventions per `reference/field_shape_rules.md`: setting Indian and Gujarat-flavoured where
the text is, `generation_prompt` self-contained, narrator bar carrying one line of Gujarati in
Gujarati script named exactly, `negative_prompt` at least the baseline including Devanagari script
labels. No Gujarati frame pool exists, so every scene is authored: `image_url: ""`,
`reuse_report.reused: 0`, `rejected: []`.

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — *measured, std 6–8.* A પત્ર chapter (std-6 ch 8, fourteen blocks) prints: વાતચીત
opening on the child's own experience of letters, pictures and દિવ્યાંગ persons; a scrambled-word
sentence-building જૂથકાર્ય; અ/બ half-sentence જોડકાં; a word-fit ✓ block (પુનર્જીવિત, સંકલ્પ,
ખાસિયત, કમાલ); comprehension "નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના ઉત્તર લખો."; a speaker-attribution જૂથકાર્ય; **the
cloze reply letter**; agent-noun formation (દોરવું - દોરનાર); an affix battery (-કાર, અણ-, -વાન,
પ્ર-, -તા, -પૂર્વક); **the reply-letter composition**; **the address-ordering drill**; a suffix game;
શ્રુતલેખન; and the standing L2 block "નીચેનાં વાક્યોનો તમારી પ્રથમ ભાષામાં અનુવાદ કરો."

A પ્રવાસવર્ણન chapter (std-7 ch 15, eleven blocks) prints: વાતચીત; eight comprehension questions;
connective placement (પણ, પરંતુ, કેમ કે, એટલે, અથવા, કે); an imagine-beyond-the-frame picture-writing
task; region ↔ trait matching; **the travel-letter composition**; an unseen paragraph with six
questions; "કારણ આપો :" reasoning items; scrambled-syntax repair; word-pair sentence building; and
**the printed advertisement with document-literacy questions**. The composition this form ultimately
feeds is std-7 ch 12 blk 14 — "નીચે પ્રવાસવર્ણનના મુદ્દા આપ્યા છે, તેને આધારે 'મેં કરેલો પ્રવાસ'
વિષય પર નિબંધ લખો" — and its punctuation-restoration companion at blk 13, which is itself a
પ્રવાસ paragraph.

How topics prepare them: the topic carrying the સંબોધન and the કુશળ-સમાચાર prepares the reply-letter
composition and the address drill, and its `explanation` must make the parts nameable; each પડાવ
topic prepares the comprehension and "કારણ આપો" blocks, because cutting by પડાવ is what makes a
"why here" question answerable; the topic carrying the writer's own opinion sentence prepares the
'મેં કરેલો પ્રવાસ' નિબંધ; the topic that glosses a loan or a local word prepares the vocabulary
blocks. Name the skill each topic prepares (`reference/teaching_block_format.md`). **No સ્વાધ્યાય
block is ever a topic**, and a પૂરકવાચન travel unit printing none at all (std-6 P1, std-8 P3) is
normal.

**Language** — two registers sit under one profile.

*Letter register:* સંબોધન forms and their closeness ladder (પ્રિય / આદરણીય / પૂજ્ય), the closing
formulas (આશિષ, શુભેચ્છા, લિ. where a book prints it), આદરવાચક બહુવચન where the addressee is senior,
and the letter's furniture — address-line order, the printed date form, the pin code. **The furniture
is content, and is glossed like content**, because a std-6 L2 child does not know the Gujarati
address order any more than the vocabulary.

*Travel register:* the longest sentences in the std-7 book, with opinion and description woven
together; place and direction words; measure and shape adjectives; and three vocabulary layers on the
page at once — **tatsama description words** (અભયારણ્ય, સ્થાપત્ય, વ્યૂહાત્મક, પરિવેશ, તળપ્રદેશ,
કર્ણપ્રિય, પરિધાન), **English loans printed in Gujarati script** (સેન્ડ ડ્યૂન, લેન્ડસ્કેપ, ફોરેસ્ટ,
ફિનિક્સ) transcribed exactly and glossed once at first use, and **regional દેશ્ય words the book
itself glosses** (કાઠું, ઠસ્સો, ભોમિયો, ડણક, ઘૂઘવાટ, હંધાય, ઢોલિયો, તાંસળું, વાળુ, બેલડી).

**A word in a local or narrowed sense is worse than an unknown word**, because the child will
confidently read it wrong — સહેલાણી is not "સહેલું કરનાર", કાઠું is not "કાઠી", વાળુ is not "વાળવું".
Gloss those first and concretely. The printed શબ્દાર્થ box is **evidence of difficulty, never the
source of the gloss** (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`); the L2 bar sits lower than a first-language
pack's, so a std-6 child stops on ઉકરડો-class everyday words too, not only on સ્થાપત્ય. Verbatim
discipline throughout per `reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`; the no-numbers rule of
`reference/naming_conventions.md` bans numbered structural references in display prose
("બીજા પડાવમાં", never "પડાવ 2માં"), not the text's own printed figures.

**Sensitivity** — this form needs the paragraph, because its content is real places, real communities
and, in one measured chapter, real named people. Agent 8 **flags and guides, never censors**: it says
how to teach the passage, not whether. Four areas recur, labelled in Gujarati per the pack convention:

- **વિકલાંગતા** — std-6 ch 8 puts two real, named artists with disabilities on the page, with
  photographs. Dignity, the chapter's own words, ability described as the letter describes it, no
  pity and no inspiration-framing (gate 9). No invented biography, no authored likeness.
- **ધર્મ** — સોમનાથ મંદિર, દત્તાત્રેયનું મંદિર on કાળો ડુંગર, નાથદ્વારા, and the local goddesses
  મા રવેચી અને મા જોગણ appear as living devotion inside travel prose. Treat them as the chapter does
  — as places people go to — never as theology and never as comparative religion.
- **સમુદાય / ક્ષેત્ર** — the craft families and communities of કચ્છ and સૌરાષ્ટ્ર (the ભૂંગા-dwelling
  villages of ધોરડો and લુડિયા, the ભરત-workers of the હાટમેળા, the પટવા families of જેસલમેર). Use
  their own names as the chapter prints them; a living craft is present tense.
- **સંઘર્ષ / સુરક્ષા** — std-7 ch 15 prints જેસલમેર's distance from the border as a plain
  geographical fact about why the place matters strategically. It is taught as the chapter frames it
  and is not developed into military or political content. Likewise std-6 P1 item 2's 2001ના ભૂકંપની
  તારાજી: the chapter's own recovery register ("ફિનિક્સ પક્ષીની જેમ બેઠું થયું") is the register to
  keep, and the disaster is not dwelt on beyond what is printed.

## Recall priors

Two to three per topic, Bloom-laddered, each with a real model answer, `bloom_level` lowercase, exact
shape per `reference/field_shape_rules.md`:

- one **remember** — where the writer is and what is there, or what this part of the letter reports:
  what જેસલમેરનો કિલ્લો is made of as the text says, which village the ભૂંગા stand in, whom the
  letter is from and to, what the મંડળ sells.
- one **understand** — why it struck the writer, or why the writer chose to tell this here: why the
  fort is called સોનાનો, why a મામા fills a દિવાળીનો પત્ર with two painters, why the writer says
  Rajasthan is not merely a રણપ્રદેશ. Answerable from **this** topic, not from the whole chapter.
- one **apply** or **analyze** — for a travel stop, what the writer's comparison lets the reader see,
  or a place the child knows described the same way ("તમારા ગામની કઈ જગ્યાને તમે આ રીતે વર્ણવો?");
  for a letter, the line the child would write back at this point in the reply. The model answer
  stays text-grounded and says which part of the chapter it reasons from.

Recall answers are bound by the same hard gates as `explanation`: no invented destination detail, no
supplementing from general knowledge about the place, no appended બોધ, no pity or inspiration
framing, no generic label for a named community, and no summarising away of the salutation or the
sign-off.
