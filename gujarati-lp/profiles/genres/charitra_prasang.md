## Diagnosis signals

**Third-person prose about a real person, organised so that one episode shows one quality.** The
chapter is not remembering a life and not plotting a story: it is putting an act on the page and
letting the act do the arguing. Four tells arrive together, and the negative test comes first.

**Negative test — run it before anything else.** If the "હું" of the *running prose* is the writer,
this is not the form, however admirable the person being described. std-9 ch 15 સો ટચનું સોનું,
std-9 ch 17 છબિ ભીતરની, std-8 ch 3 મારી વ્યાયામસાધના and std-10 ch 6 સામગ્રી તો સમાજની છે ને ! all
put a real person and a real incident on the page in the first person; every one of them routes
elsewhere. A **quoted** "હું" is different and is normal here — std-10 ch 5 opens on a long quoted
memoir block ''દર શનિવારે સવારની શાળા હોય…'', std-7 ch 7 hands the narration to a જવાન at the
રણછોડદાસ ચેકપોસ્ટ, std-8 ch 8 quotes આઈડા લ્યૂસ's own diary-like words — and in each case the
first person sits *inside* quotation marks with a printed speaker.

**Tell 1 — the truth furniture is on the page.** Dates, places, institutions and honours, printed
plainly and checkable: `જન્મ 21મી માર્ચ 1936`, `અવસાન તા. 18-1-2013`, `ઈ.સ. 2007`, `12 ટેન્કો`
(std-7 ch 7); `22-10-1903`, `ઈ.સ. 1930`, `1964`, `1971`, `1994`, 'ખેડા દૂધ ઉત્પાદક સહકારી મંડળી
લિમિટેડ', 'ઓપરેશન ફ્લડ', the રેમન મેગ્સેસે એવોર્ડ (std-10 ch 5); `જન્મ 14-07-1937`, પદ્મશ્રી 1989,
`અવસાન તા. 4-1-2015` (std-9 P2); `23 જિંદગી`, `16 વર્ષ`, ન્યૂપોર્ટ, લાઈમ રોક (std-8 ch 8). A વાર્તા
does not carry a death date. **Dates on the page mean living relatives**, and that governs the hard
gates below.

**Tell 2 — the blue intro box names the form, and usually names the qualities too.** It is the
strongest single signal in the GSEB readers and it is evidence, not a verdict:

- "આ ગુજરાતના એક વીર સપૂતની સાહસકથા છે. રણછોડ પગીનાં સાહસો જાણી દરેક જણ ગર્વ અનુભવશે… આ પાઠ
  વિદ્યાર્થીઓને એક અજાણ્યા ભૂ-પ્રદેશનો પરિચય પણ કરાવશે." (std-7 ch 7) — a સાહસકથા built on a
  documented life, and the box itself adds a second payload, the ભૂ-પ્રદેશ.
- "ત્રિભુવનદાસ અને ડૉ. કુરિયનની પ્રતિભાના અનેક અંશો જેવા કે કાર્યદક્ષતા, સંગઠનક્ષમતા,
  સેવાપરાયણતા… આ ચરિત્ર સૌ કોઈ માટે પ્રેરણારૂપ છે." (std-10 ch 5) — the qualities are **listed**;
  the chapter's job, and therefore the teaching's, is to show where each one was earned.
- "'તોરણ' સંગ્રહમાંથી આ પ્રસંગકથા લેવામાં આવી છે… સાચા શિક્ષકનું રેખાચિત્ર સચોટ અને આસ્વાદ્ય રીતે
  મુકાયું છે" (std-9 ch 13) — the book prints **both** labels, પ્રસંગકથા and રેખાચિત્ર, over one
  four-page prose unit.
- "આ ચરિત્રનિબંધમાં ગુજરાતના રંગભૂમિ સમ્રાટ અને ફિલ્મ અભિનેતા ઉપેન્દ્ર ત્રિવેદીની કારકિર્દી અને
  પ્રદાન વર્ણવવામાં આવ્યું છે" (std-9 P2).
- "ગુજરાતના ભક્તકવિ નરસિંહ મહેતાના જીવન અને કાર્યનો ચિતાર… વિદ્યાર્થીઓને **જીવનચરિત્ર અને
  ચરિત્રલેખ કોને કહેવાય** એનો પરિચય આ એકમ થકી આપી શકાશે." (std-8 ch 6) — the one chapter in the
  corpus that names the સ્વરૂપ as its own teaching objective.

**Tell 3 — the author slot behaves oddly, and the oddity is diagnostic.** A ચરિત્ર is often
`સંકલિત` (std-8 ch 6, std-10 ch 5) because the subject, not the writer, is the unit's identity;
std-10 ch 5 then hides its real writer at the *end* of the text, `(આલેખન : રમેશચંદ્ર ડી. પંડ્યા)`,
and prints no લેખક-પરિચય at all. Where a writer *is* named (ડૉ. રવજી ગાબાણી, નાનાભાઈ જેબલિયા,
કનૈયાલાલ ભટ્ટ, હરીશ નાયક), std 9–10 print the writer's own era line under the title — which is the
**writer's** date, never the subject's. Confusing the two is a first-page error that survives into
`01_meta.json`.

**Tell 4 — the સ્વાધ્યાય asks the child to reconstruct the person (measured).** Three blocks route
here because each only makes sense if a real person's acts are the content:

- an **event-ordering** block over the person's deeds — "નીચેનાં વાક્યોને પાઠના ઘટનાક્રમ મુજબ
  ગોઠવો." (std-7 ch 7 blk 2, six events with checkboxes);
- a **portrait-writing** task — "રણછોડ પગી વિશે પાંચ-છ વાક્યો લખો." (std-7 ch 7 blk 3), and its
  std-9 form, "**પાત્રાલેખન કરો**" inside the સવિસ્તાર tier (std-9 ch 13 blk 3);
- a **go-and-meet-a-real-person** activity — "તમારા વિસ્તારના સમાજસેવક, પોલીસ કે સૈનિકની મુલાકાત
  લઈ તેમના કાર્ય વિશે જાણો." (std-7 ch 7 blk 13), and the skill-imitation activity that grows
  straight out of the subject's own craft: footprints in sand (blk 5) and thumbprints of the class
  (blk 14), because the subject is a **પગી**.

A **counterfactual** ("મદદ ન મળી હોત તો ?", std-7 ch 7 blk 3) is frequent rather than diagnostic —
but it is the block the teaching most often fails to prepare, because it needs the topic to have
stated what was at stake, not only what happened.

## Lens

**પ્રસંગ + સ્વભાવ + મૂલ્ય**

The episode as it happened, the quality it reveals, and what that quality was worth to somebody
else. `reference/teaching_lens_map.md` fixes this formula for the form; read the third term
strictly — **મૂલ્ય is the worth the text itself demonstrates, never a moral the teaching supplies.**
Where the chapter shows what the act cost and who it served (a month's pay handed over to stop a
ભવાઈ; a border crossed at night so a column could move; milk money reaching the farmer who milked
the cow), the મૂલ્ય is already on the page and needs only to be pointed at.

## Guiding question

*કયા પ્રસંગોએ આ વ્યક્તિને યાદ રાખવા જેવી બનાવી?*

The wording above is a **shape**. Each chapter derives its own from its own text and Agent 1 writes
it into `01_meta.json` — for std-7 ch 7 it is about what one man's eyes could read in sand that an
army could not; for std-10 ch 5 about what two very different men had to be, for milk to reach a
city; for std-9 ch 13 about what a village schoolmaster was willing to spend to protect what he was
building. A guiding question that would fit any admirable person in any book has not been derived.

## Explanation unit — **one જીવન-પ્રસંગ**

One episode that shows one quality — or, where the chapter prints person-sections, **one printed
person-section**. `reference/explanation_unit_map.md` fixes both halves of this rule.

- **The printed part-head is the cut.** std-10 ch 5 prints `(1) ત્રિભુવનદાસ પટેલ` and its
  counterpart for ડૉ. વર્ગીસ કુરિયન. Those heads are the topic boundaries for the person layer;
  within a section, the episodes cut further. Never join two person-sections into one topic and
  never begin a topic in the middle of one.
- **An episode is a small arc, and it stays whole**: પરિસ્થિતિ → કૃત્ય → પરિણામ. Cutting between the
  act and what followed leaves a topic with an act nobody can weigh. The ભવાયા episode of std-9
  ch 13 — the troupe arrives, the માસ્તર acts, the ભવાઈ does not happen — is **one** topic.
- **Cut where the quality changes, not at the paragraph break.** A chapter that shows the same
  quality twice (two tracking feats, two rescues) may hold both in one topic when the second is the
  first repeated at higher stakes; it holds them separately when the second shows something new.
- **A dated facts-cluster is a legitimate topic where the chapter is built of facts.** std-10 ch 5
  is measured as "a *facts* chapter cut by તથ્ય, not ઘટના" — its founding dates, the co-operative's
  growth, the awards. Give it one topic, `topic_category: "core"`, not one topic per year.
- **A frame is not a topic unless it does work.** std-7 ch 7's school study-tour reaching the
  રણછોડદાસ ચેકપોસ્ટ — the only BSF post named after a civilian — *is* a topic
  (`topic_category: "introduction"`), because why the telling happens is part of the argument. The
  જવાન's handover sentence is not a topic; it belongs to the episode it introduces.
- **Verse quoted inside the prose stays inside its prose topic.** std-8 ch 6's five પદ lines with
  their છાપ ("ભણે નરસૈયો…", "નરસૈયાના નાથ…") are quoted evidence about a life; they never become a
  પદ topic, and the topic carrying them keeps `rhyme_scheme: null`.
- **A quoted narration block does not create boundaries.** std-10 ch 5's opening ''દર શનિવારે…''
  memoir block and std-7 ch 7's long jawan-narration run across several episodes; cut on the
  episode, not on the quotation mark.
- A topic that genuinely does two separable things — the act, and the honour that followed years
  later — is what `concepts[]` is for: two concepts, never a third.
- Cutting rules 1–5 of `reference/explanation_unit_map.md` apply unchanged: exact verbatim first, a
  pre-topic hook belongs to the NEXT topic, single-theme topics, **સ્વાધ્યાય is never a topic**. A
  પૂરક વાચન unit printing no સ્વાધ્યાય at all (std-9 P2) is normal, not a gap.

## How a ચરિત્ર-પ્રસંગ earns its quality, and therefore how it is taught

A પ્રસંગ in this form is **evidence**, not an anecdote. The difference is what the teaching lives or
dies on:

> An episode with no quality attached is an anecdote. A quality with no episode under it is praise.
> This form is the join, and the join runs in one direction only: **act first, name second.**

So every `explanation` follows the chapter's own shape:

1. **પરિસ્થિતિ** — what actually faced the person, in the chapter's own particulars: a column that
   must cross unmapped રણ at night; a village whose milk money is taken by a દલાલ; a ભવાઈ troupe
   already pitched outside the school; a lighthouse and a boat in a storm.
2. **કૃત્ય** — what the person *did*, as a verb, with the concrete means the chapter names —
   પગેરું, સગડ, કોઠાસૂઝ; a સહકારી મંડળી; a month's pay; oars.
3. **પરિણામ** — what followed, for somebody else. This is where મૂલ્ય becomes visible.
4. **ગુણનું નામ** — and only now the quality, in one clause, in the chapter's own word where it
   prints one (કાર્યદક્ષતા, સંગઠનક્ષમતા, સેવાપરાયણતા, નિષ્ઠાવાન, ખુદ્દારી, કોઠાસૂઝ).

An `explanation` that names the quality in its first sentence has become praise, and the child is
left with an adjective instead of a person. The order is checkable and it is checked (gate 4).

**The truth-claim ladder.** The corpus holds three rungs, and the no-invention gate is identical on
all three, because the gate is about the printed page and not about the archive:

| Rung | Read in | What the page claims |
|---|---|---|
| રેખાચિત્ર of a documented figure | std-10 ch 5, std-9 P2 | dates, institutions, awards — externally checkable, and checkable against **living relatives** |
| ચરિત્ર-પ્રસંગ / સાહસકથા of a real person | std-7 ch 7, std-8 ch 8 | the person and the deeds are real; the frame (a study tour, a narrator) is the writer's arrangement |
| પ્રસંગકથા about a named but unarchived individual | std-9 ch 13 | the writer vouches for the person; the dialogue on the page is the writer's, and is verbatim text |

Agent 1 records which rung the page claims, in `genre_signals.purpose`, and never upgrades a rung to
make a chapter feel more important.

## Emphasise

- **The verb, first sentence.** What did this person *do* here? "રણછોડ પગી રેતીમાં પડેલાં પગલાં
  વાંચીને…" is an opening; "રણછોડ પગી ખૂબ બહાદુર હતા" is not.
- **The concrete means.** This સ્વરૂપ is made of specifics and loses everything when generalised: the
  પગેરું and the સગડ, the કોઠાસૂઝ of a man who knew a ક્રીક from a બાવળું; the સહકારી મંડળી and the
  દલાલ it replaced; the તખ્તો and the રિહર્સલ; the oars and the મશાલ. Name them as the chapter names
  them and gloss them at first use.
- **What the act cost.** The quality is only visible against the price — a month's pay, a night in
  the રણ, an unpopular stand, sixteen years old in a storm. Where the chapter states the cost, the
  explanation states it; where it does not, the explanation does not supply one.
- **The person's own words stay theirs.** Quoted speech is verbatim under
  `reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`: the જવાન's narration, the ''દર શનિવારે…'' memoir block, the
  husband-wife exchange of std-9 ch 13, and the છાપ-bearing પદ lines of std-8 ch 6 are all text, kept
  as printed, never modernised or paraphrased away.
- **The honour is a consequence, not the subject.** General Manekshaw's tribute — the chapter's own
  title comes from it — the Ramon Magsaysay award, પદ્મશ્રી, a BSF post carrying a civilian's name:
  each is taught as *what other people concluded* about an act already shown, and is placed after
  the act, never instead of it.
- **The second payload, where the box names one.** std-7 ch 7's intro promises "એક અજાણ્યા
  ભૂ-પ્રદેશનો પરિચય": the Kutch–Banaskantha border, સૂઈગામ, the ક્રીક, the રણ. That is real content
  of the chapter and belongs in the teaching alongside the life.
- **Connection to the child's own world**, one anchor per topic, Indian and concrete
  (`reference/teaching_voice_gu.md`): the person in their own town whose skill everyone relies on;
  the દૂધમંડળી their milk goes to; a teacher who once did something unusual to protect their class.
  The anchor points at the *kind of act*, never at a comparison of worth.

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these as a per-topic testable sentence; Agent 13 blocks on any hard item
left unaddressed. Each check names the field it is checked against. "Authored fields" below means
`explanation`, `real_life_example`, `brief_summary`, `summary`, `detailed_summary`,
`concept_bullets`, `important_points`, `concepts[].content[].text`, `publication_text`, every recall
`answer`, and every exercise `answer` and `explanation` in the exercise deliverable.

1. **Invented dialogue.** **Check:** every quotation-marked span in an authored field appears
   character-for-character inside some `original_chunk` of this plan, or in the chapter's printed
   શબ્દાર્થ / શબ્દ-સમજૂતી / રૂઢિપ્રયોગ apparatus recorded in `01_meta.json`. No authored field
   contains a quotation mark around words the person is merely *supposed* to have said. Severity:
   hard.
2. **Invented biography.** **Check:** every proper noun, year, date, number, place-name,
   institution, award, work-title and family relationship appearing in an authored field also
   appears in some `original_chunk` of this plan, or in the chapter's printed પરિચય / author line /
   શબ્દાર્થ apparatus. No supplementing from general knowledge about રણછોડ પગી, નરસિંહ મહેતા,
   ડૉ. કુરિયન, ઉપેન્દ્ર ત્રિવેદી, આઈડા લ્યૂસ or anyone else — a birth year that is true but not
   printed is still a hard fail. `[]` and silence beat invention
   (`reference/no_hallucination_policy.md`). Severity: hard.
3. **Dramatised interiority.** **Check:** no authored field attributes an unstated thought, feeling,
   motive, sensation or decision-moment to the real person — વિચાર્યું, લાગ્યું, અનુભવ્યું, મનમાં,
   હૈયે, ડર લાગ્યો, ગર્વ થયો, નક્કી કર્યું and their synonyms — unless that exact interior statement
   is printed in that topic's `original_chunk`. Where the text says what he did, the teaching says
   what he did. Severity: hard.
4. **Hagiography — the unearned quality.** **Check (two parts, both required):** (a) no authored
   field carries a reverential epithet or evaluative superlative — મહાન, અદ્વિતીય, દેવતુલ્ય,
   ભગવાન સમાન, પૂજનીય, સર્વશ્રેષ્ઠ, આદર્શ પુરુષ, યુગપુરુષ — that is not a verbatim quotation from
   the chapter; and (b) every topic whose `explanation` names a quality names, **in the same
   `explanation` and after it**, the printed act that shows it. An `explanation` whose first sentence
   is a quality is a fail even if the act appears later. Severity: hard. *This is the lead gate of
   the profile.*
5. **Biography-by-chronology, or "જીવન અને કાર્ય".** **Check:** no `topic_name` uses a life-stage
   label (બાળપણ, શિક્ષણ, કારકિર્દી, લગ્નજીવન, અવસાન, જીવન અને કાર્ય) unless the chapter prints that
   exact head; and no `explanation` contains three or more year-numbers unless all of them appear in
   that topic's own `original_chunk`. A date-list is not an episode. Severity: hard.
6. **Merging or splitting a printed person-section.** **Check:** the number of topics whose
   `original_chunk` begins at a printed person-section head equals the number of such heads recorded
   in `01_meta.json.structure_inventory`, and no topic's `original_chunk` spans two heads. Severity:
   hard (structural — checked at Agent 4 against the marker counts).
7. **First-person slippage, and the borrowed witness.** **Check:** any authored field containing
   હું / મેં / અમે / મારા encloses it in quotation marks and attributes it to the speaker printed on
   the page; and no authored field claims the writer saw, met, accompanied or spoke with the subject
   unless the chapter prints that. std-10 ch 5's writer is hidden at the end as
   `(આલેખન : રમેશચંદ્ર ડી. પંડ્યા)` and witnesses nothing. Severity: hard.
8. **Confusing the writer's dates with the subject's.** **Check:** every date attributed to the
   subject in an authored field, in `01_meta.json`, or in a recall `answer` appears in the chapter's
   body text, not in the era line under the title, which belongs to the writer (std-9 P2:
   કનૈયાલાલ ભટ્ટ `જન્મ : 16-1-1965` vs ઉપેન્દ્ર ત્રિવેદી `જન્મ 14-07-1937`). Severity: hard.
9. **War content turned into jingoism.** **Check:** for a chapter carrying conflict (std-7 ch 7),
   no authored field contains દુશ્મન, શત્રુ, a nation's name, or a count of enemy losses outside a
   verbatim quotation from an `original_chunk` or from the chapter's printed રૂઢિપ્રયોગ box; no field
   presents killing as the achievement; and every topic teaching a wartime episode names the skill
   or the terrain it turned on. Agent 8 flags and guides, never censors
   (`reference/global_content_rules.md`). Severity: hard.
10. **Disability or hardship used as pathos.** **Check:** no authored field contains અપંગ, બિચારું,
    લાચાર, ખોડખાંપણ, "તેમ છતાં પણ" pity-framing or a synonym; where the chapter names a condition, the
    chapter's own word is used (std-6 ch 8's સ્વાધ્યાય prints દિવ્યાંગ), and every sentence mentioning
    the condition has the person's **work** as its subject — મૃદુલ ઘોષ and મનોજ ભિંગારે are painters
    who paint, not cases who cope. Severity: hard.
11. **A printed ચમત્કાર asserted, or debunked.** **Check:** where an `original_chunk` prints a
    miraculous episode (std-8 ch 6's હૂંડી, નાગદમન), no authored field states it as historical fact
    ("ખરેખર બન્યું હતું") and no authored field corrects it ("એ તો માત્ર દંતકથા છે",
    "વૈજ્ઞાનિક રીતે અશક્ય"). The teaching reports it as the ચરિત્રલેખ reports it, and teaches the
    devotional tradition as a living tradition, not as theology. Severity: hard.
12. **A tacked-on બોધ or ઉપદેશ.** **Check:** no authored field closes on a sentence of the form
    "આપણે પણ … જોઈએ" / "આ પાઠ આપણને શીખવે છે…" / "દરેક વિદ્યાર્થીએ …" that is not a verbatim
    quotation from the chapter. Where the chapter states its own closing appeal, quote it and teach
    it as the writer's move. Severity: hard.
13. **Naming a device that is not there.** **Check:** `figures_of_speech` entries quote EXACT words
    found in that topic's `original_chunk`; `rhyme_scheme` is `null` for every topic of this prose
    form, including a topic that quotes verse inside prose; `[]` is a correct and complete answer.
    std-9 ch 13's ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ box names વ્યતિરેક and સજીવારોપણ — that is teacher-addressed
    apparatus and evidence to verify against the text, never a licence to copy the label into a
    topic where the line does not appear (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`). Severity: hard.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per જીવન-પ્રસંગ, showing the **act being performed**, never a portrait and
never the honour: the tracker crouched over footprints in the sand at first light; the milk cans
arriving at a village collection point; a schoolmaster handing over a cloth-wrapped bundle of notes
outside a pitched ભવાઈ tent; a young woman rowing into a squall toward a lighthouse. The
one-sentence depiction test applies — an image that cannot be described as one moment somebody
could photograph is a summary and must be re-authored. Fix the subject's appearance once
(clothing, age, build, the tools they carry) and repeat that description in every prompt of the
chapter, since the same real person recurs across topics. **Where the chapter prints a photograph or
a portrait of a real person** — std-7 ch 7's garlanded portrait, std-6 ch 8's captioned photographs
of two living artists — the authored image does **not** attempt that person's likeness: it shows the
act (a brush held in the mouth, a canvas taking colour) without a recognisable face, and the printed
photograph stays page matter, never a reading scene. A medal, a statue, a ceremony or a certificate
is not a depiction of a quality and is only imaged where the chapter narrates the ceremony itself.
Setting is Indian and Gujarat-flavoured where the text is; std-8 ch 8 is the corpus's foreign-setting
exception and its prompts follow the chapter, not the default. `generation_prompt` is self-contained
per `reference/field_shape_rules.md`; the narrator bar carries one line of Gujarati in Gujarati
script, named exactly. No Gujarati frame pool exists, so every scene is authored: `image_url: ""`,
`reuse_report.reused: 0`. At most one `2d_tool` per chapter, and this form rarely earns it.

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — *measured*. The std 6–8 readers print a long numbered battery: વાતચીત (7–9 oral
prompts opening on the child's own experience of soldiers, teachers, artists), the ઘટનાક્રમ ordering
block, "પ્રશ્નોના જવાબ લખો" (7–9 Qs, including the counterfactual and the portrait task), error
correction, minimal-pair cloze (ખડ/ખંડ, વાઘ/વાધ), dictionary-order lookup, register-choice synonym
sets (તરણાં/તૃણ/તણખલાં; ચોપડી/કિતાબ/ગ્રંથ/પોથી), reverse-lookup of the રૂઢિપ્રયોગ box, polysemy
sentence-building, a collocation/gender-agreement grid, the interview and observation activities,
અનુલેખન, question-making from a copied paragraph, and the standing L2 block "નીચેના ફકરાનો તમારી
પ્રથમ ભાષામાં અનુવાદ કરો." std-8 ch 6 adds a સુભાષિત gloss, agentive→impersonal passive
transformation, situational-response items, a song-form vocabulary block (પ્રભાતિયાં, મરસિયા, ફાગ,
ફટાણાં, થાળ), a mock bill to read, a સંયોજક table and a ચર્ચા-વિચારણા box; std-8 ch 8 adds conjunct
families, number agreement, question formation and a pronoun cloze. The std 9–10 readers print a
short tiered ladder instead: MCQ with ✓, એક-એક વાક્ય, બે-ત્રણ વાક્ય, સવિસ્તાર — and the tiers are
**not fixed**: std-10 ch 5 prints a measured **3-tier ladder with no બે-ત્રણ વાક્ય tier** and 3 MCQ,
std-9 ch 13 prints 4 MCQ + બે-ત્રણ + સવિસ્તાર with પાત્રાલેખન inside it. Agent 1 inventories what is
printed; nothing here is assumed. **પૂરક વાચન units print no સ્વાધ્યાય at all** (std-9 P2 carries
only a run-on શબ્દાર્થ block; std-10 P1–P5 are measured the same way — શબ્દ-સમજૂતી/સમાનાર્થી gloss
only, no સ્વાધ્યાય of any kind) — normal, not a gap. વિદ્યાર્થી-પ્રવૃત્તિ, ભાષા-અભિવ્યક્તિ, શિક્ષકની ભૂમિકા, the blue intro box, the
ઉખાણું box (std-7 ch 7) and the chapter-final activity frames are **appended or teacher-addressed
matter, not exercises**. How topics prepare them: the topic that holds one whole પ્રસંગ prepares the
ઘટનાક્રમ block; the topic that names a quality *after* its act prepares the portrait task and
પાત્રાલેખન; the topic that states what was at stake prepares the counterfactual; the topic that
glosses the trade vocabulary prepares the register and dictionary blocks. Name the skill each topic
prepares (`reference/teaching_block_format.md`). **No સ્વાધ્યાય block is ever a topic**, and Agent 10
answers every printed block.

**Language** — three layers sit on the page at once. **Trade and skill vocabulary**, which is the
form's signature and usually not in a school dictionary — પગી, પગેરું, સગડ, કોઠાસૂઝ, ભોમિયો, ભાથું,
બાવળું, ક્રીક (= ખાડી); શ્વેતક્રાંતિ, સહકારી મંડળી, દલાલ, સ્વાવલંબી, જુગલબંધી; તખ્તો, રિહર્સલ,
કારકિર્દી, ચિત્રપટ, રંગભૂમિ; ભવાયા, ભવાઈ, ફટાણાં, ઝાલર, વાળુ. **Character-naming તત્સમ compounds**,
which are the lens's own material and the heaviest L2 load — કાર્યદક્ષતા, સંગઠનક્ષમતા,
સેવાપરાયણતા, કર્તવ્યનિષ્ઠા, દૃઢમનોબળ, નિષ્ઠાવાન, ખુદ્દારી, ચરિતાર્થ: gloss each at its point of use,
because the whole teaching turns on the child knowing what the adjective claims. **Names transcribed
into Gujarati script** — institutions ('ખેડા દૂધ ઉત્પાદક સહકારી મંડળી લિમિટેડ', 'ઓપરેશન ફ્લડ',
'પોલસન', વિધાનસભા) and foreign persons and places (આઈડા લ્યૂસ, ન્યૂપોર્ટ, લાઈમ રોક) — kept exactly
as printed and **never romanised back**. Gloss in `"શબ્દ — અર્થ"` form, 3–6 per topic, from the
chapter's own explanation; the printed શબ્દાર્થ box is evidence of difficulty, never the source of
the gloss (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`), and the L2 bar sits lower than a first-language pack's — an
L2 child stops on ભાથું and કદર, not only on ચરિતાર્થ. Register is descriptive past tense for the
acts and present for what stands today; adjectives describe character, per
`reference/teaching_lens_map.md`. Verbatim rules per `reference/gujarati_verbatim.md`: no
transliteration, no nukta, `.` and never `।` — with the one measured exception that a **printed**
Sanskrit દંડ inside a quotation stays as printed (std-9 ch 23). The chapter's own dates and figures
are printed facts and are quoted as printed; the no-numbers rule of
`reference/naming_conventions.md` bans numbered structural references in display prose
("બીજા પ્રસંગમાં", never "પ્રસંગ 2માં"), not the page's own dates.

**Sensitivity** — this form needs the paragraph, because its subjects are real and often recently
alive. Four areas recur in the measured corpus: **સંઘર્ષ** — India–Pakistan war content, a border,
'દુશ્મન' language inside the printed idiom block (std-7 ch 7); **વિકલાંગતા** — two living
mouth-painters, photographed and named (std-6 ch 8); **ધર્મ** — a bhakti tradition with printed
ચમત્કાર (std-8 ch 6), and, next door in the same reader family, Islamic devotional vocabulary beside
a ગીતા line (std-9 ch 23); **સુરક્ષા** — rescue and drowning (std-8 ch 8). A fifth is structural
rather than topical: **the subjects have living families** — રણછોડ પગી died in 2013 and ઉપેન્દ્ર
ત્રિવેદી in 2015 — so an invented private detail is not merely a hallucination, it is a claim about
real people's relatives. Agent 8 **flags and guides, never censors**: it says how to teach the
passage, not whether. Use communities' and persons' own printed names, keep the book's own dignity
register, and do not turn a described belief or a described war into a debate. Area labels in
Gujarati per the pack convention (ધર્મ, સમુદાય, ક્ષેત્ર, વિકલાંગતા, સંઘર્ષ, જાતિ-ભૂમિકા, સુરક્ષા).

## Recall priors

Two to three per topic, Bloom-laddered, each with a real model answer, `bloom_level` lowercase:

- one **remember** — the act as printed: what રણછોડ પગી read in the sand; what the માસ્તર handed
  over; what ત્રિભુવનદાસ organised; what the sixteen-year-old rowed out into. A name, a deed, a
  place — answerable from this topic's `original_chunk` alone.
- one **understand** — what the act shows about the person, or why it mattered to somebody else: why
  an army needed a man who was not a soldier; why replacing the દલાલ changed what a farmer earned;
  why stopping one ભવાઈ was worth a month's pay. Answerable from this topic, not from the whole
  chapter.
- one **analyze** or **evaluate** — which printed detail actually earns the quality the chapter
  claims; whether the honour that followed measures the act or only reports it; and the
  counterfactual the book itself prints, "મદદ ન મળી હોત તો ?" The model answer names the sentence of
  the chapter it reasons from, and is allowed to conclude that the text shows less than the intro
  box promises — that is a correct answer, not a disloyal one.

Recall answers are bound by the same hard gates as `explanation`: no invented fact, no invented
quotation, no interior state, no unearned superlative, no appended બોધ. An answer whose whole
content is praise (`તેઓ ખૂબ મહાન હતા`) is a defect at Agent 13 even when the question invited it.
