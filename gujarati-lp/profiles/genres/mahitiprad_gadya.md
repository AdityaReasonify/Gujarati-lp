## Diagnosis signals

Prose whose payload is **facts** — how electricity reaches a house, what the જલચક્ર does, what a
ગરબો is and how it differs from a ગરબી, what happens at the ગોળ-ગધેડાનો મેળો, how to write a
નિબંધ. The frame that carries the facts varies and is **not** the diagnosis. There may be no frame
at all (std-6 ch 12, expository third person), a grandfather telling a bedtime tale (std-6 ch 5), a
first-person family scene with quoted dialogue (std-6 ch 9), talking raindrops (std-7 ch 4), or a
child's questions pulling the exposition forward (std-8 ch 9).

**The tell — the drop-the-frame test.** Remove the frame and ask what is lost. In a વાર્તા,
removing the frame removes the story: there is nothing left. Here, removing the frame leaves the
whole payload standing — the water still evaporates, rises, condenses and falls; the pole still
carries a ગોળની પોટલી at its top. What the frame supplies is *interest and order*, not content.

**The book usually names the unit's own intent in the blue intro box**, and it is the strongest
single signal, but it is evidence and not a verdict:

- "પાવરહાઉસમાં ઉત્પન્ન થતી વીજળી આપણા ઘર સુધી કેવી રીતે પહોંચે છે તે વાત અહીં રસપ્રદ રીતે મુકાઈ છે…"
  (std-6 ch 5) — note that the intro states a *process*, and only then a manner of telling.
- "…આ એકમમાં ગુજરાત રાજ્યની ઓળખ એવા ગરબા વિશે રસપ્રદ શૈલીમાં રજૂઆત થઈ છે. સાથેસાથ ગરબા અને ગરબી
  વિશેનો ભેદ પણ અહીં રજૂ થયો છે." (std-6 ch 9) — a *ભેદ* to be delivered is a fact payload.
- "…દાહોદ જિલ્લાના અતિપ્રસિદ્ધ 'ગોળ ગધેડાના મેળા'ની વિશેષતાનું વર્ણન કરવામાં આવ્યું છે…
  સ્વાધ્યાયમાં આપેલી પ્રવૃત્તિઓ દ્વારા ભાષાશિક્ષણનું કાર્ય કરવું." (std-6 ch 12)
- "આ વાર્તામાં જલચક્રને હળવી શૈલીમાં રજૂ કર્યું છે." (std-7 ch 4) — the box says **વાર્તા**, and the
  chapter is still cut here: the noun names the manner, the verb names the payload (જલચક્રને…રજૂ
  કર્યું). This is the exact case where the label alone would misroute.
- "ભારત દેશની અનેકરંગી સાંસ્કૃતિક પરંપરાનો પરિચય વાર્તાસ્વરૂપે… આ એકમમાં પ્રશ્નો દ્વારા સંવાદ આગળ
  વધે છે." (std-8 ch 9) — પરિચય is the payload; વાર્તાસ્વરૂપે and પ્રશ્નો દ્વારા are both vehicles.

**Signal C — the સ્વાધ્યાય tells (measured, std 6–8).** Three block types route here, because each
only makes sense if the chapter's content is factual:

- an **identification / fact-matching** block — "કૌંસમાં આપેલા શબ્દો જે વાક્યોને લાગુ પડતા હોય તેની
  સામે લખો." (std-6 ch 9, matching dance names to their descriptions: એકતારો, હીંચ, શ્રમહારી,
  મંજીરાનૃત્ય, ટિપ્પણી), and the noun-to-attribute fill "કૌંસમાં આપેલા શબ્દોમાંથી બંધબેસતો શબ્દ
  પસંદ કરીને લખો." (std-8 ch 9: સપ્તરંગી/નવરંગી/બહુરંગી/ત્રિરંગી/પચરંગી ↔ સાડી, ધ્વજ, ચુંદડી,
  મેઘધનુષ્ય, પાઘડી, ફૂલ);
- a **loanword hunt** — "પાઠમાં અંગ્રેજી શબ્દ 'પાવરહાઉસ' આપવામાં આવ્યો છે. પુસ્તકમાંથી આવા બીજા
  અંગ્રેજી શબ્દો શોધીને લખો." (std-6 ch 5), "આ પાઠમાં કેટલાક અંગ્રેજી ભાષાના શબ્દો વપરાયા છે. એ
  શબ્દોની યાદી કરો અને વાક્યપ્રયોગ કરો." (std-6 ch 9). Informational chapters carry technical loans
  in Gujarati script, and the book turns that into a language block;
- a **find-out / write-about-your-own** composition — "'મેં જોયેલો મેળો' - વિશે દસેક વાક્યો લખો."
  (std-6 ch 12), "ગુજરાતનાં જાણીતાં લોકનૃત્યો વિશે લખો." (std-6 ch 9), "'વિવિધતામાં એકતા' વિશે
  દસ-પંદર વાક્યો લખો." (std-8 ch 9).

A fourth block is frequent rather than diagnostic: **sentence-order repair** ("નીચે આપેલાં વાક્યોમાં
શબ્દો આડાઅવળા થઈ ગયા છે…" — std-6 ch 5 blk 4, ch 9 blk 4, ch 12 blk 3). What is **absent** is as
telling: none of these chapters prints an event-ordering block or a counterfactual, the two blocks
that mark a વાર્તા. std-8 ch 9 is the exception that proves the boundary — it *does* print
"નીચેની ઘટનાઓને તેના યોગ્ય ક્રમમાં ગોઠવીને લખો.", which is why its routing is contested below.

## Lens

**તથ્ય + પરંપરા + જિજ્ઞાસા**

A real practice or a real process, told so a child wants to know more. The frame is the invitation;
the fact is the lesson.

## Guiding question

*આ પરંપરા કે હકીકત આપણને આપણા દેશ વિશે શું કહે છે?*

The wording above is a **shape**. Each chapter derives its own from its own text and Agent 1 writes
it into `01_meta.json` — for std-7 ch 4 it is about where a drop of water goes and comes back from,
for std-6 ch 9 about what separates a ગરબો from a ગરબી, for std-7 P1 about what a child must decide
before writing the first line. A guiding question that would fit any informational chapter in the
book has not been derived.

## Explanation unit — **one માહિતી-ખંડ**

One fact-cluster, or one step of the process, per topic. **Never one page and never one speaker's
turn.**

- **Where the prose walks a sequence, each stage is a topic.** std-7 ch 4 moves દરિયો/નદી/તળાવ →
  વરાળ → વાદળ → વરસાદ → પાછું નીચે; std-6 ch 5 moves પાવરહાઉસ → ગ્રિડ → ફીડર → ઘર. The stage is the
  boundary, not the paragraph and not the speaker.
- **A printed litany is ONE fact-cluster, not many topics.** std-7 ch 4's બાર પ્રકારના વરસાદ
  (ફરફર, ઝરમર, છાંટા, ફોરાં, કરા, પછેડિયો, નેવાધાર, મોલિયો, ઢેફાંભાંગ, અનરાધાર, સાંબેલાધાર, હેલી)
  is one topic about how many names Gujarati has for rain — twelve topics of one word each is a cut
  error. The same holds for std-6 ch 9's list of regional dances.
- **The frame's opening is a real માહિતી-ખંડ when it sets up why the facts are being told** — રામુ
  arriving at his friend's house and a grandfather starting to tell (std-6 ch 5), પૂર્વીદીદી needing
  an article on Gujarat's folk dances (std-6 ch 9). Why the telling happens is itself information.
  Mark it `topic_category: "introduction"`.
- **The frame's closing likewise.** std-6 ch 12 ends on an appeal to go and see a લોકમેળો; that is
  its own small topic, `topic_category: "resolution"`.
- **Quoted dialogue does not create boundaries.** In std-7 ch 4 and std-8 ch 9 the facts arrive in
  quoted speech with no speaker labels at all. Cut on the fact-step; several turns normally sit
  inside one topic, and a turn that carries two stages is split at the stage, not at the quote mark.
- **A definition and its consequence stay together.** std-6 ch 9's ગરબો/ગરબી ભેદ is one topic — the
  distinction is worthless split in half. std-6 ch 12's name-explanation (why "ગોળ ગધેડાનો મેળો")
  belongs with the event it names.
- Where a chapter genuinely does two separable things inside one fact-cluster, that is what
  `concepts[]` is for — two concepts in one topic, never a third.
- Printed **photographs** (std-6 ch 12's two crowd photos, std-8 P3's temple photos) are page matter,
  not reading scenes: they never open a topic of their own.
- Cutting rules 1–5 of `reference/explanation_unit_map.md` apply unchanged: exact verbatim first, a
  pre-topic hook belongs to the NEXT topic, single-theme topics, **સ્વાધ્યાય is never a topic**.
  A પૂરકવાચન unit that prints no સ્વાધ્યાય at all (std-7 P1) is normal, not a gap.

## માર્ગદર્શક લેખ — the how-to sub-form, and therefore how it is taught

std-7 P1 ચાલો, નિબંધ લખીએ... is not a literary reading scene. It defines its object — નિબંધ =
"આપેલા / વિચારેલા વિષયનાં બધાં પાસાં અંગેની મુદ્દાસર લેખિત રજૂઆત" — and then works one example topic
('સ્વચ્છતા') end to end: brainstorm the questions the topic raises → gather the answers → order them
by importance → choose an opening (a newspaper item, a government scheme, the ઉકરડો on the way to
school, a great person's words) → put your own opinion in → then practise, "થોડાક, એટલે કે દસેક
નિબંધ". The chapter that it is paired with says so out loud: std-7 ch 15's intro box orders the
teacher, "બાળકોને માર્ગદર્શન પૂરું પાડતો 'ચાલો, નિબંધ લખીએ' એ પાઠની મદદ જરૂર લેજો."

Three things follow, and they are what make this sub-form different from the rest of the profile:

1. **The unit is one step, and the topic order is the printed order.** A method taught out of order
   is a different method. Reading order equals printed order here, always.
2. **The addressee is the student, in second person.** The chapter says કરો, લખો, વિચારો. The
   teaching keeps that stance: the explanation tells the child what to *do* at this step, and names
   what the step produces (a list of questions; an ordered list; an opening sentence).
3. **The topics prepare a task the child will actually perform.** std-7 ch 12 blk 14 and ch 15 blk 6
   are the exercises this unit exists for. A topic that leaves the child able to describe the step
   but not to take it has failed, however accurate it is.

The chapter carries no શબ્દાર્થ box, so `key_terms` come from the chapter's own defining sentences
(નિબંધ, મુદ્દા, ભૂમિકા) in `"શબ્દ — અર્થ"` form, and from nothing else.

## Emphasise

- **The fact, first sentence.** Each `explanation` opens on what this માહિતી-ખંડ teaches — the
  stage, the practice, the ભેદ, the step. The frame is named once, in passing, and then does its
  job. "દાદાજી કહે છે કે…" is a clause, not a topic's subject.
- **Concrete detail.** Materials, movements, timing, who does what: the વાંસની પટ્ટીઓ in the
  કન્યાઓ's hands, the ગોળની પોટલી tied at the top of a twenty-odd-foot pole, the ગર્ભદીપ inside the
  ગરબો, the ગ્રિડ between the પાવરહાઉસ and the house. This સ્વરૂપ is made of specifics and loses
  everything when generalised.
- **The practice in its own terms.** Name it as the chapter names it — ગરબો, ગરબી, ટિપ્પણી,
  મંજીરાનૃત્ય, નરઘાં, સિંધુડો, પ્રભાતિયું, હાટ, ધજા — and explain what the word means, instead of
  substituting a familiar equivalent. A ગરબી is not "a kind of ગરબા"; the chapter exists to say what
  it is.
- **The frame as craft, in one sentence, where it works.** std-6 ch 5 calls electricity વીજળીરાણી
  and std-7 ch 4 lets the drops introduce themselves; that is સજીવારોપણ, and naming it once at the
  place it happens is the whole of the craft teaching this form needs
  (`reference/alankar_chhand.md` — name it only where it is actually present).
- **Curiosity, not distance.** The stance is "here is something in your own state you may not know
  yet", never "here is how those people live". std-6 ch 12 sets the register itself:
  "આદિવાસી પ્રજાએ હજી પોતાની સંસ્કૃતિની ઓળખ તરીકે આવા મેળાઓની પ્રથા જાળવી રાખી છે એ ગૌરવની વાત છે."
- **Connection to the child's own world** — the મેળો their own town keeps, the શેરીના ગરબા of their
  નવરાત્રી, the meter box in their own house, the first rain of their own ચોમાસું. One anchor per
  topic, Indian and concrete (`reference/teaching_voice_gu.md`).
- **Comparison where the chapter invites it**, with both sides equal: ગરબો beside ગરબી, one region's
  dance beside another's. The unfamiliar practice is never the lesser version of the familiar one.

## Avoid (hard gate)

Agent 7 instantiates each of these as a per-topic testable sentence; Agent 13 blocks on any hard item
left unaddressed. Each check names the field it is checked against.

1. **Re-teaching the frame as a વાર્તા.** **Check:** no topic in the plan carries
   `topic_category: "climax"`; and no `explanation`, `summary`, `detailed_summary`,
   `concept_bullets` or recall `answer` asks or answers why a character *chose* to act as they did,
   or names a વળાંક. std-6 ch 5's grandfather makes no choice; the raindrops of std-7 ch 4 have no
   arc. Severity: hard.
2. **Cutting by speaker turn.** **Check:** in a dialogue-carried chapter, the number of topics is
   strictly less than the number of quoted speaker turns marked in `00_chapter_normalized.md`, and no
   topic boundary falls at a change of speaker unless a fact-step also completes there. Severity:
   hard (structural — checked at Agent 4 against the marker counts).
3. **Cutting by page or paragraph.** **Check:** every topic's `explanation` names, in its first
   sentence, the fact, practice or step that topic teaches. A topic whose only description is "the
   next part of the chapter" fails. Severity: hard (structural — Agent 4).
4. **Adding facts the chapter does not carry.** **Check:** every proper noun, number, measure and
   technical term appearing in `explanation`, `summary`, `detailed_summary`, `important_points`,
   `concept_bullets` or a recall `answer` also appears in some `original_chunk` of this plan, or in
   the chapter's own printed શબ્દાર્થ / રૂઢિપ્રયોગ box. No supplementing from general knowledge
   about the dance, the district or the machinery. Severity: hard.
5. **Turning it into a Science or Social-Science lesson.** **Check:** no field introduces a
   scientific or technical term the chapter does not print (બાષ્પીભવન, ઘનીકરણ, વોલ્ટેજ,
   ટ્રાન્સફોર્મર, ઍમ્પિયર), and no field corrects the chapter's personified account against science
   ("ખરેખર વીજળી રાણી નથી", "વૈજ્ઞાનિક રીતે…"). It is a Gujarati chapter: teach the text and its
   language. Severity: hard.
6. **Substituting a familiar equivalent for the practice's own name.** **Check:** every
   practice-word printed in a topic's `original_chunk` (ગરબી, ટિપ્પણી, નરઘાં, પ્રભાતિયું, ધજા, હાટ,
   ગ્રિડ, પછેડિયો…) appears in that same form wherever the teaching refers to it, and at least one
   such word appears in the topic's `key_terms` in `"શબ્દ — અર્થ"` form. Severity: hard.
7. **Exoticising, or a generic label where the chapter names a community.** **Check:** no
   `explanation`, `real_life_example`, recall `answer` or exercise `answer` contains પછાત, અભણ,
   જંગલી, અસભ્ય, "આ લોકો", "એ લોકોની રીતભાત" or a synonym; and where the chapter names a community,
   region or village (આદિવાસી પ્રજા, જેસાવાડા ગામ, ગરબાડા તાલુકો, દાહોદ જિલ્લો, નળકાંઠો,
   ભાલપ્રદેશ, કાઠિયાવાડ), the teaching uses that printed name and not a generic substitute

8. **Framing a living practice as a thing of the past.** **Check:** no field places a practice the

9. **Ranking cultures, regions or practices.** **Check:** no field contains a comparative that makes

10. **A tacked-on બોધ or ઉપદેશ.** **Check:** no `explanation`, `summary`, `detailed_summary`,

11. **For a માર્ગદર્શક લેખ: losing the order, the addressee, or a step.** **Check:** the topic

## Priors

**Media** — one image per માહિતી-ખંડ, showing the practice **being done** or the stage **happening**:
the real instrument, the real costume, the real setting, the materials as the chapter describes them
(કન્યાઓ circling a pole with વાંસની પટ્ટીઓ while a young man climbs toward the ગોળની પોટલી; a lit
ગર્ભદીપ inside a perforated ગરબો). The one-sentence depiction test applies — a picture that cannot
be described as a single moment someone could photograph is a summary and must be re-authored.
Where the chapter personifies, the image may show the personification because the chapter does
(a crowned વીજળીરાણી travelling the wires, chatting drops above a village), provided the fact stays
legible in the frame. **Where the chapter prints real photographs** (std-6 ch 12, std-8 P3), the
authored image must not invent a "scene cast" of named characters the text never names. A process
chapter is the one place in this pack where the single permitted `2d_tool` genuinely earns its
place — a જલચક્ર or a પાવરહાઉસ-to-ઘર walk-through — and it is still **at most one per chapter**.
Setting is Indian, Gujarat-flavoured where the text is. `generation_prompt` is self-contained per
`reference/field_shape_rules.md`; the narrator bar carries one line of Gujarati in Gujarati script,
named exactly. No Gujarati frame pool exists, so every scene is authored: `image_url: ""`,
`reuse_report.reused: 0`.

**સ્વાધ્યાય** — *measured, std 6–8*: વાતચીત (oral, 5–9 prompts, opening on the child's own
experience of the મેળો/નવરાત્રી/વરસાદ), "નીચેના પ્રશ્નોના ઉત્તર લખો" fact-recall, an identification
or અ/બ half-sentence matching block, a word-bank cloze, sentence-order repair, the loanword hunt,
derivational word-formation batteries (-તા abstract nouns, -વાસી, -નાર agent nouns, -ી diminutives),
સમાનાર્થી/વિરુદ્ધાર્થી grids and word-search puzzles, a punctuation block where the chapter's grammar
box teaches punctuation (std-6 ch 9), a composition on the child's own version of the practice, a
દ્વિરુક્ત/રવાનુકારી hunt (સડસડાટ, ઝરમર, ઢમઢમ), a જૂથકાર્ય/પ્રવૃત્તિ, and the standing L2 block
"નીચેનાં વાક્યોનો તમારી પ્રથમ ભાષામાં અનુવાદ કરો." std-8 adds a closing ચર્ચા-વિચારણા box and an
idiom-paraphrase ✓ block. The chapter-final green grammar box (ક્રિયાપદ અને કાળ, અલ્પવિરામ અને
અવતરણચિહ્ન) and the chapter-final picture boxes (std-6 ch 12's "જોડો :" ornament matching, std-6
ch 5's humour box) are **appended matter, not exercises** — Agent 1 inventories what is printed and
Agent 10 answers every numbered block. How topics prepare them: the topic that names a practice
prepares the identification block, and its `explanation` must name the categories a fact-sheet or
table would need; cutting by માહિતી-ખંડ is itself what prepares the fact-recall block; the topic
carrying an English loan glosses it at first use, which is what the loanword hunt draws on; the
closing-appeal topic prepares the composition. Name the skill each topic prepares
(`reference/teaching_block_format.md`). **No સ્વાધ્યાય block is ever a topic**, and std-7 P1 printing
none at all is normal.

**Language** — domain and regional vocabulary is the main load, and much of it is not in a standard
dictionary; gloss from the chapter's own explanation and its printed શબ્દાર્થ box, which is evidence
of difficulty, never the source of the gloss (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`). Three layers sit on the
page at once: **practice words** (ગરબો, ગરબી, ગર્ભદીપ, નરઘાં, સિંધુડો, પ્રભાતિયું, લોકનૃત્ય, હાટ,
ધજા, સ્વયંવર, ઘરવખરી, આભૂષણ, પ્રથા); **English loans printed in Gujarati script** (પાવરહાઉસ, ગ્રિડ,
ડાન્સ, રેકોર્ડર, આર્ટિકલ, ડ્રેગનફ્રૂટ) — transcribed exactly as printed, glossed once at first use,
and never romanised back; and **દેશ્ય process vocabulary**, of which std-7 ch 4's twelve rain-words
are the richest example in the corpus, every one glossed by the book itself. Register is descriptive
present tense with measure and number words (`reference/teaching_lens_map.md`); the chapter's own
measurements are quoted as the chapter prints them, in Gujarati words — the no-numbers rule of
`reference/naming_conventions.md` bans numbered structural references in display prose
("બીજા ખંડમાં", never "ખંડ 2માં"), not the text's own printed facts. The L2 bar for "everyday word"
sits lower than a first-language pack's: an L2 child stops on ઉકરડો and પહેરવેશ, not only on
સ્વયંવર.

**Sensitivity** — this form needs the paragraph, because its content is real communities' living
practice. std-6 ch 12 puts a named આદિવાસી community's મેળો on the page along with ritual beating
(સોટીઓ, the "ગધેડા જેવો માર" the fair is named for) and a historical bride-choice custom the chapter
itself marks as ended; std-6 ch 9 puts નવરાત્રી and માતાજી on the page as living devotion; std-8
ch 9 puts regional dress, food and dance across India under a civic 'એક ભારત શ્રેષ્ઠ ભારત' frame.
Agent 8 **flags and guides, never censors**: it says how to teach the passage, not whether. Keep the
book's own dignity register (std-6 ch 12's ગૌરવની વાત sentence is the model), use communities' own
names, treat devotional practice as a living tradition rather than as theology or comparative
religion, and do not turn a description of a custom into a debate about it. Area labels in Gujarati
per the pack convention (ધર્મ, સમુદાય, ક્ષેત્ર, વિકલાંગતા, સંઘર્ષ, જાતિ-ભૂમિકા, સુરક્ષા).

## Recall priors

Two to three per topic, Bloom-laddered, each with a real model answer, `bloom_level` lowercase:

- one **remember** — the fact as printed: what is tied to the top of the pole, what a ગર્ભદીપ is,
  what the ગ્રિડ carries, what the drop turns into next.
- one **understand** — why it is done that way, or why the chapter's teller says it here: why a
  perforated pot is called a ગરબો, why the fair earned its name, why the water must rise before it
  can fall. Answerable from this topic, not from the whole chapter.
- one **apply** — a parallel in the child's own place: the મેળો their own town keeps, where the
  electricity in their classroom comes from, which of the twelve rain-words describes today's sky.
  The model answer stays text-grounded and says which part of the chapter it reasons from.

For a **માર્ગદર્શક લેખ**, the third question is not a parallel but a performance: the child carries
out this step on a topic of their own, and the model answer shows one worked result of the step
(a list of questions, an ordered set of મુદ્દા, an opening sentence) rather than describing what the
step is.

Recall answers are bound by the same hard gates as `explanation`: no invented fact, no scientific
correction of the chapter's own account, no appended બોધ, no generic label for a named community.
