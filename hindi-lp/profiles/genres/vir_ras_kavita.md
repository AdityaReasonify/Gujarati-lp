# वीर-रस काव्य

## Diagnosis signals
Verse with **speed** — short beats, hard consonants, a rhythm that gallops. A warrior, a horse, a
battle, a leap. Theme: वीरता, बलिदान, स्वाभिमान. अभ्यास asks about ओज, गति, the animal's or hero's
qualities, and often asks the child to recite with expression.

## Lens
**ओज + गति + चरित्र** — courage carried by rhythm.

## Guiding question
*लय और शब्द मिलकर वीरता का भाव कैसे जगाते हैं?*

## Explanation unit — **one छंद**

Keep the छंद whole. The rhythm is the content here more than in any other विधा — a छंद cut in half
loses the gallop, and the gallop is what is being taught.

## Emphasise
- **Sound as meaning.** Hard consonants, short vowels, repeated beats — the line *moves* like the
  horse. Read a line aloud and the child understands ओज without a definition.
- **The animal or hero as a character**, with named qualities: loyalty, speed, fearlessness. In
  चेतक-type poems the horse is the protagonist, not the setting.
- **The single spectacular action** each छंद holds — the leap, the turn, the charge.
- **अलंकार of sound** — अनुप्रास and पुनरुक्ति do most of the work; उपमा/रूपक carry the scale.
- **Loyalty and sacrifice** as the human centre, which is what survives the battle.

## Avoid (hard gate)
- **Glorifying violence.** The विधा celebrates courage and loyalty, not killing. Keep the
  explanation on the quality, not on the wound.
- **Turning it into a history lesson.** Dates, dynasties and campaign details are not the poem.
  Where the chapter names a historical figure, say what the chapter says and no more
  (`no_hallucination_policy.md`).
- **Communal or regional framing** of a historical conflict.
- **Losing the rhythm** by explaining only the plot.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per छंद: motion, dust, the horse mid-leap, the rider's posture. Indian
historical setting drawn with dignity. Not gore, not a corpse, not a weapon-focused image.

**अभ्यास** — expect ओज/गति questions, अलंकार of sound, शब्दार्थ of ornate तत्सम vocabulary, and a
recitation task. Where a topic prepares recitation, name the beat to carry.

**Language** — ornate तत्सम, compound epithets, unfamiliar battle vocabulary. Gloss generously;
this विधा has the highest शब्दार्थ load of the poetry profiles.

**Sensitivity** — historical conflict involving named communities. Present courage and loyalty;
do not editorialise the conflict.

## Recall priors
One `remember` (what happens in this छंद), one `understand` (which quality is shown), one
`analyze` (how the sound produces the speed).
