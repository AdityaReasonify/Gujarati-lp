# भक्ति-पद

## Diagnosis signals
A sung पद with a **टेक** that returns. A speaker who is a भक्त, or a देवता, or a child. ब्रज /
अवधी forms — `नहिं`, `माखन`, `खायो`, `मैया`, `कहुँ`. A कवि-छाप (`सूरदास`, `मीरा`, `तुलसीदास`) in
the closing line. Theme: भक्ति, वात्सल्य, प्रेम. अभ्यास asks about the speaker's भाव, the टेक, or
asks the child to sing or recite.

## Lens
**भाव + संबंध + लोक-भाषा की मिठास** — a relationship, sung.

## Guiding question
*भक्त और भगवान का यह रिश्ता कैसा है, और कवि उसे किन शब्दों से जीवंत करता है?*

## Explanation unit — **one पद, whole**

A पद is one continuous utterance meant to be sung start to finish. Splitting it by line breaks the
voice — the child-Krishna's whole defence in a माखन-चोरी पद is one unbroken speech, and half of it
is not a teaching unit, it is an interruption.

- One पद = one topic, even when it runs longer than a छंद.
- The **टेक** is quoted inside that topic and named as the returning line, not made a topic of its
  own.
- The छाप line stays.

## Emphasise
- **भाव above all.** वात्सल्य — the mother's love and exasperation, the child's indignation. The
  पद *is* the feeling; everything else serves it.
- **The speaker's voice.** Who is talking, and how? A small child protesting his innocence at
  length is funny and tender at once, and a class-6 reader can hear that immediately.
- **The relationship.** भक्ति in these पद is not distant reverence — it is a mother and a child.
  The theology is a family scene.
- **ब्रज की मिठास.** `मैया`, `माखन`, `नहिं`, `खायो` sound softer than their खड़ी बोली equivalents.
  That softness is chosen; name it once.
- **The टेक** — what returns, and what it does to the song.

## Avoid (hard gate)
- **Flattening the पद into a morality lesson.** A माखन-चोरी पद is **not** a lesson that lying is
  wrong or that stealing is bad. Reading it that way destroys both the humour and the भक्ति, and
  it is the single most common failure with this विधा. If the explanation's conclusion could be
  printed on a classroom poster, it is wrong.
- **Correcting ब्रज to खड़ी बोली**, in the verbatim or in a quotation.
- **Turning it into a theology lesson** or a comparative-religion discussion. Teach the poem.
- **Splitting the पद** by line or by sentence.
- **Explaining the joke to death.** The child-Krishna's defence is funny; say so lightly and move.

## Priors

**Media** — one image for the पद, showing the **scene and the relationship** — the mother, the
child, the pot, the household. Warm, domestic, Indian. Not an iconographic devotional portrait,
and not a moral tableau of a child being scolded.

**अभ्यास** — expect भाव questions, the टेक, ब्रज शब्दार्थ, and often a recitation or singing task.
Where a topic prepares recitation, name the लय and the टेक as the things to carry.

**Language** — heavy ब्रज glossing. Every form the child would stumble on gets a खड़ी बोली
equivalent at point of use, with the ब्रज kept in the text.

**Sensitivity** — a devotional text held by children of many faiths. Present it as the chapter
does: literature with a living tradition behind it, taught for its भाव and its craft.

## Recall priors
One `remember` (who speaks, what is the टेक), one `understand` (what is the speaker feeling),
one `analyze` (why does the poet let the child speak at such length).
