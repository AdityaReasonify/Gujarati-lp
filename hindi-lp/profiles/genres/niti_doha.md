# नीति-काव्य / दोहा

## Diagnosis signals
Two-line units, each complete in itself, each with its own picture and its own turn. A कवि-छाप
(`रहिमन`, `कबीर`, `तुलसी`) inside the line. Theme: नीति, व्यवहार, संबंध. अभ्यास asks the child to
explain each दोहा's भाव separately, or to match a दोहा to a situation.

## Lens
**अनुभव की सीख + दृष्टांत** — a piece of ordinary life held up until it shows a rule for living.

## Guiding question
*कवि किस रोज़मर्रा के दृश्य से जीवन की बात कहता है?*

## Explanation unit — **one दोहा, always**

This is the profile's whole reason to exist. A दोहा collection is a set of **independent poems**
printed together; consecutive दोहे share a poet and a metre and nothing else. Merging two because
they are short is the single most damaging cut this pack can make.

- One दोहा = one topic. No exceptions, no "these two go together".
- The two lines stay two lines, with the caesura as printed.
- The छाप line is part of the verse. Never trim `रहिमन`.

## How a दोहा is built, and therefore how it is taught

Almost every दोहा has the same two-beat shape:

| line | does |
|---|---|
| **1** | sets a picture from ordinary life — water, thread, a tree, a fish, a guest |
| **2** | turns it into a rule about people |

The `explanation` follows that shape: show the picture first in plain words, then make the turn
explicit. A child who gets only the conclusion has been given the answer without the argument,
and cannot use it on a new दोहा.

## Emphasise
- The **दृष्टांत** — the concrete image, named plainly before any moral language.
- The **turn** — the exact word where the picture becomes about people.
- **Compression** — a दोहा says in two lines what an essay takes a page to say; that economy is
  the craft, and worth naming.
- **अलंकार where present** — often उपमा or रूपक carrying the दृष्टांत; sometimes अनुप्रास.
- **Usability** — a नीति दोहा is meant to be *used*. The `real_life_example` should show it in a
  situation the child will actually face this month.

## Avoid (hard gate)
- **Merging दोहे.** Checked at Agent 4.
- **The सीख without the दृष्टांत.** "हमें विनम्र होना चाहिए" is not an explanation of a दोहा; it
  is what is left after the poem has been thrown away.
- **A moral lecture.** The दोहा already makes its point. Repeating it in the teacher's voice, at
  length, is the failure this विधा invites.
- **Modernising the language.** अवधी/ब्रज forms stay.
- **Claiming a biographical fact** about the कवि that the chapter does not state.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per दोहा, showing the **दृष्टांत**, not the moral. A दोहा about water and a
thread gets water and a thread, not a child being kind. The abstraction is what the child must
build; the picture is what makes it buildable.

**अभ्यास** — expect भाव-स्पष्टीकरण per दोहा, शब्दार्थ, matching a दोहा to a situation, and
sometimes writing one's own. Where a topic prepares that writing task, its explanation should name
the two-beat shape as the thing to imitate.

**Language** — तत्सम-heavy with अवधी/ब्रज forms; gloss generously (`reference/shabd_gloss.md`).
Words like `रहिमन`, `पानी`, `मोती`, `मानुष`, `चून` carry more than their dictionary sense — say so.

## Recall priors
One `remember` (what is the picture in line 1), one `understand` (what does line 2 turn it into),
one `apply` (where would you use this) per दोहा.
