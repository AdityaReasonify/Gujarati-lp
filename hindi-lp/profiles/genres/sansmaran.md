# संस्मरण / जीवनी

## Diagnosis signals
Prose in the **'मैं' voice** remembering real events, or a real life told in sequence by another.
Named real people, real places, dates that are lived rather than listed. Theme: जीवन, प्रेरणा,
संबंध. अभ्यास asks what the writer felt, what the life shows, and often asks the child to write
their own संस्मरण.

## Lens
**स्मृति + स्वर + मूल्य** — a remembered life and what it shows.

## Guiding question
*किन क्षणों ने इस जीवन को याद रखने लायक़ बनाया?*

## Explanation unit — **one स्मृति or one जीवन-चरण**

A memoir moves by memory, not by clock. One remembered episode per topic — a match, a morning, a
decision. For a जीवनी told in sequence, one life stage per topic.

Typical arc: जड़ें → कठिनाई → चुनाव → उठान → पहचान. The chapter's own arc governs; do not impose
this one.

## Emphasise
- **The 'मैं' voice.** A संस्मरण is written in first person and that is a teachable fact — the
  अभ्यास usually asks the child to imitate it.
- **The remembered detail.** Memoir persuades through specificity: the bandage, the bench, the
  exact words spoken. Name the detail rather than summarising past it.
- **What the moment shows about the person** — humility, doggedness, generosity — drawn from what
  they did, not asserted.
- **Modesty of the telling.** These writers usually understate. That restraint is craft.
- **The value the chapter itself names**, in the chapter's own terms.

## Avoid (hard gate)
- **A date-list.** Reducing a life to born-in, joined-in, won-in strips out everything the
  chapter was doing.
- **Preaching.** The life makes the point; the explanation should not add a sermon.
- **Turning संस्मरण into जीवनी** — losing the first-person voice by rewriting it as third-person
  facts. The voice is the विधा.
- **Inventing biography.** Only what the chapter states (`no_hallucination_policy.md`).

## Priors

**Media** — one image per remembered episode, showing the person **in the moment being
remembered**. Period-accurate Indian setting; consistent likeness across the chapter.

**अभ्यास** — expect comprehension, "these lines show what", vocabulary, and a
**write-your-own-संस्मरण** task. Where a topic prepares it, its explanation should name the
'मैं' voice and the use of one concrete remembered detail.

**Language** — plain modern Hindi with occasional formal or era-specific vocabulary (सैन्य,
प्रशासनिक, regional). Gloss those at point of use.

## Recall priors
One `remember` (what happened in this memory), one `understand` (what it shows about the person),
one `apply` (a parallel from the child's own life).
