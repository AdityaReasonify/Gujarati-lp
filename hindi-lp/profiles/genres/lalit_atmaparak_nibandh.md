# ललित / आत्मपरक निबंध

> Written for class-9 गंगा ch2 `क्या लिखूँ?` (पदुमलाल पुन्नालाल बख्शी). The chapter's own
> `विधा से संवाद` box names the form — **निबंध** — and its intro box names the sub-form:
> *"इस निबंध की आत्मपरक शैली— जिसमें लेखक स्वयं पाठकों से संवाद करता है— इसे अधिक जीवंत,
> आत्मीय और प्रभावशाली बनाती है।"* The profile is written from that, not from a prior.

## Diagnosis signals

**One voice, and that voice is a person, not a position.** First person throughout; the writer is
a character in his own essay, usually a slightly ridiculous one. There is no plot and no opponent.
The movement is a **sequence of attempts** — he tries something, it does not work, he tries the
next thing — and the essay is honest about the failures.

Four things separate it from the neighbouring prose forms, and each has bitten a generic pipeline:

| looks like | but | tell |
|---|---|---|
| `samvad_nibandh` | that profile wants **two sides** and its avoid-list rejects one-sidedness | here one-sidedness is the **form**, exactly as with `bhashan_uddabodhan` — do not score it as a defect |
| `sansmaran` | a memoir remembers a **life**; this thinks about a **problem in the present tense** | verbs are `मुझे लिखना है`, not `मुझे याद है` |
| `soochnatmak_sanskritik` | the facts here are **borrowed authorities**, quoted so they can be weighed, not taught | गार्डिनर, मानटेन, बाणभट्ट appear as opinions, not as syllabus |
| `bhashan_uddabodhan` | a speech has an **occasion and an audience present**; this has a desk and a deadline | no संबोधन, no अपील, no closing charge |

**Humour at the writer's own expense is a positive signal**, not decoration. So is digression that
turns out not to be digression.

**Tested after `sansmaran` and before `samvad_nibandh`.** If the piece remembers, it is संस्मरण.
If it argues with someone, it is संवाद/निबंध. If it argues with **itself**, it is this.

## Lens

**आत्म-स्वर + पड़ाव + विनोद** — whose voice this is, what step the thinking is on, and where the
writer is laughing at himself.

## Guiding question

*लेखक हर बार क्या आज़माता है, वह क्यों नहीं चलता, और अंत में निबंध लिखा कैसे जाता है?*

## Explanation unit — **one विचार-पड़ाव**

A **पड़ाव** is one complete step in the thinking: an authority consulted, a rule tried, a way out
found, a picture drawn. It ends when the writer **changes what he is trying**, and the text almost
always marks that boundary itself with a question — `पर लिखूँ कैसे?`, `तब क्या किया जाए?`,
`इसलिए मुझसे तो रूपरेखा तैयार न होगी।`

Three rules follow:

- **Cut at the writer's own question, not at the paragraph break.** The rhetorical questions are
  the joints of the essay. A पड़ाव may run across two printed paragraphs and often does.
- **Never separate a borrowed authority from the use the writer makes of it.** गार्डिनर's claim and
  the हैट/खूँटी illustration are one पड़ाव; मानटेन's method and the writer's decision to copy it
  are one पड़ाव. Quoting the authority and dropping the reaction turns the essay into a textbook.
- **The essay's own body counts as reading, not as a demonstration.** When a ललित निबंध about
  writing finally writes the thing, those paragraphs are topics like any other — that turn is the
  whole point of the form and must be visible in the cut.

Do **not** cut by page, by paragraph, or by named authority.

## Emphasise

- **The self-deprecating 'मैं'.** `मैं सोच ही नहीं सकता`, `पर मैं हूँ मास्टर`, `मुझमें खुसरो की
  प्रतिभा नहीं है`. The child should see that a writer is allowed to admit difficulty — this is
  the single most useful thing the form teaches a student who has to write in an exam.
- **Irony, and say where it lands.** When the essayist recommends half-page sentences and wilful
  obscurity "अपनी विद्वता का प्रदर्शन करने के लिए", he means the opposite. Name it; a class-9
  reader can miss it entirely and take it as advice.
- **The turn from talking about writing to writing.** Point at the exact sentence where the essay
  stops preparing and starts being the essay.
- **The frame closing.** These essays end by returning to their first sentence or first image.
  Show the return.
- **Authorities as people with problems**, not as names to memorise — गार्डिनर cannot title his own
  articles; शेक्सपियर gave up and called a play *जैसा तुम चाहो*.
- **The everyday दृष्टांत.** हैट और खूँटी, दूर का ढोल. Let the child re-use the picture before the
  abstraction.

## Avoid (hard gate)

- **Marking one-sidedness as a fault.** It is the form. (`samvad_nibandh`'s avoid-list must not be
  applied here.)
- **Turning the essay into a निबंध-लेखन checklist.** The चरण the essay names — सामग्री, रूपरेखा,
  शैली — are the things it finds *hard*, not a method it endorses. Teaching them as a recipe
  inverts the text. (The अभ्यास's own आरेख invites exactly this mistake; keep it in अभ्यास.)
- **Explaining the joke flat.** Say why it is funny in one clause, then move.
- **Dropping the quoted verse or the anecdote** to save room. खुसरो's दोहा is the hinge of the
  chapter; it stays verbatim, two lines, `।` and `।।` as printed.
- **A tacked-on शिक्षा.** The essay ends on an image, not a lesson.
- **Treating the named authorities as the topic.** A topic is a पड़ाव, not "मानटेन".

## Priors

**Media** — one image per पड़ाव. Two kinds work: the **desk** (the writer stuck, with what is
troubling him made visible around him) and the **दृष्टांत made literal** (a hat on a peg, a drum,
a well). Where the पड़ाव is a borrowed authority, a portrait of that person *doing the thing the
essay says they did* beats a generic portrait.

**अभ्यास** — in गंगा expect `रचना से संवाद` (मेरे उत्तर मेरे तर्क — MCQ with a "why is this the
right answer" tail; मेरी समझ मेरे विचार), `विधा से संवाद` defining निबंध and its शैली with an
आरेख, `भाव-विस्तार`, `मेरा अनुभव`, `विषयों से संवाद`, `सृजन`, `भाषा से संवाद`, `गतिविधियाँ`,
`भाषा संगम`, `झरोखे से`, `खोजबीन`, `शब्द-संपदा`. All of these belong to Agent 10 — **except
झरोखे से**, which is appended reading and gets topics.

**Language** — literary खड़ी बोली of the 1930s–50s with a heavy तत्सम layer (उत्थित, दुर्बोधता,
गांभीर्य, आख्यायिका, उदात्त, समीपस्थ/दूरस्थ, क्षुब्ध). Gloss from the chapter's own
`शब्द-संपदा`, which is built for exactly these words. Do not modernise the prose in
`original_chunk`; modernise in `explanation` and `shabdarth`.

## Recall priors

One `remember` (what did the writer try / who said what), one `understand` or `analyze` (why did
it not work, or what is the दृष्टांत doing), one `evaluate` or `apply` (do you agree, or how would
you do it) — the आत्मपरक form invites the child to answer back, which is why the अभ्यास here asks
`आपको किन विषयों पर लिखना सरल या कठिन लगा और क्यों?`
