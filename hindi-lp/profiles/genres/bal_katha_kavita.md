# बाल कथा / संवाद-कविता

> Written for class-5 वीणा — ch1 `किरन` (निरंकार देव 'सेवक'), ch3 `चाँद का कुरता`
> (रामधारी सिंह 'दिनकर') and ch6 `चतुर चित्रकार` (रामनरेश त्रिपाठी). Three of the book's twelve
> chapters, and all three fall through the whole verse branch of `_genre_index.md` to land on
> `prakriti_deshbhakti_kavita`, which is where that branch bottoms out. Class 10 recorded three
> times what the default landing costs (`CLASS10_PLAN.md` §2.5): *a default landing is not a
> diagnosis.* These poems are not built out of pictures and they have no टेक. **They have a plot
> and they have speakers.** `kahani`'s lens is the right one, on a unit `kahani` does not have.

## Diagnosis signals

**Verse that tells a story or holds a conversation, with no narrator explaining it.**

- **Two named or identifiable speakers, no speaker tags.** ch1 is a बालिका and a किरन talking to
  each other; ch3 is चाँद asking his माँ for a coat and माँ answering; ch6 is a चित्रकार talking his
  way past a शेर. Who is speaking is carried by `कहने लगी किरन`, `कहा माता ने`, `बोला —` inside
  the line — one clause, easy to miss, and if it is missed the whole poem goes flat.
- **Events in sequence, with a मोड़.** ch6: the painter is painting → the lion arrives → he loses
  his wits → he gathers courage and offers a portrait → the lion sits → *"now please face the other
  way"* → he escapes → the lion calls him a coward and he answers from the boat. That is a plot.
  ch3's turn is माँ's closing question; ch1's is किरन revealing she never sleeps either.
- **A rhyme closing every unit, and a caesura marked by a comma inside the line.**
  `चित्र / मित्र`, `होश / जोश`, `बोला / झिंगोला`, `आई / पाई`. The तुक is not decoration — at ten
  years old it is what makes the poem hold together in the ear.
- **The theme is a child's world**, and the अभ्यास asks what a character did and why, not
  `भाव स्पष्ट कीजिए`. वीणा's blocks for these chapters run बातचीत के लिए, पाठ से (multiple choice
  on the events), सोचिए और लिखिए, अनुमान और कल्पना.

The decisive signal is the second one. **Ask whether removing a छंद would break the sequence.**
In a प्रकृति-कविता the छंद could be reordered and little would be lost; here छंद 4 cannot precede
छंद 3, because the lion has to sit down before he can be asked to turn around.

Five विधा would take one of these chapters, and each fails on a different thing:

| looks like | but | tell |
|---|---|---|
| `prakriti_deshbhakti_kavita` | छंद of equal length, rhyme, a sky and a moon in ch3 | that lens is **चित्र + भाव + अलंकार** and its टेक rule says *teach what the टेक changed*. There is no टेक in any of the three, and the moon is a **character**, not an image. Cutting ch6 "छंद-wise by imagery" produces ten topics about a forest |
| `niti_doha` | ch6's couplets have the दोहा's comma caesura and its closing rhyme | the unit is right and the **lens is wrong**, the same split `sakhi.md` exists for. A नीति-दोहा is *complete in two lines and gives advice*; these couplets are **incomplete on purpose** — each hands the story to the next. Its hard gate (one दोहा = one topic) would cut ch6 into twenty two-line topics with no story in any of them |
| `kahani` | plot, characters, a मोड़ — the lens is in fact correct | its unit is **one घटना in prose**, so nothing in it protects the छंद, the तुक or the line break. Applied alone it retells the poem as a story and the verse disappears |
| `chaupai_doha_samvad` | speakers without tags, a कथावाचक reporting between speeches | that विधा's unit is a विनिमय **inside a चौपाई-दोहा structure** in अवधी. These are खड़ी बोली and have no चौपाई run to close |
| `mukt_chhand_kavita` | — | its negative test fails immediately: there **is** a repeating unit, and a strict rhyme |

## Lens

**घटना + संवाद + तुक** — what happens next, who is saying it, and the sound that carries it.

## Guiding question

*इस छंद में क्या हुआ, और यह कौन कह रहा है?*

## Explanation unit — **one छंद-इकाई: one beat, or one speaker's turn**

Not "one couplet" and not "one quatrain" — **cut where the speaker changes or the event moves.**
The three chapters give three different sizes and all three are the same rule:

- **ch6** — one couplet is one beat. The story steps forward with each तुक, so the couplet and the
  beat coincide and the cut is couplet-wise.
- **ch3** — चाँद's request runs three couplets before माँ answers. Those three are **one topic**;
  splitting them leaves a child holding half a request. माँ's reply is the next.
- **ch1** — the poem is two quatrain-groups, one per speaker. Cut on the turn.

A unit may never split. Two units join **only** when the second finishes a turn the first began,
and the merge is recorded in `deviation_from_v1`.

## Emphasise

- **Who is speaking, named at every topic.** These poems have no speaker tags and a child reading
  alone will not find the switch. Point at the clause that carries it (`कहने लगी किरन यह सुनकर`,
  `बच्चे की सुन बात कहा माता ने`) and say plainly: *अब यह कौन बोल रहा है?*
- **What happens next, in the poem's own order.** The `explanation` is a narrative, one beat long.
  It should be readable to a ten-year-old without the छंद in front of them — and then send them
  back to the छंद.
- **The तुक, heard before it is named.** Say the two rhyming words out loud together —
  `चित्र–मित्र`, `होश–जोश`, `बोला–झिंगोला` — before using the word *तुक*. The craft lesson at
  class 5 is **noticing**, not analysis.
- **The joke, where there is one.** ch6 is funny and ch3 is affectionate teasing. A topic that
  explains चतुर चित्रकार without letting the child enjoy the trick has missed the poem. `यम राजा का
  मित्र` for a lion is a joke the child can be shown.
- **The मोड़, named once.** ch6's is *"अब मुँह आप उधर तो करिए"*; ch3's is माँ's final question;
  ch1's is किरन's admission. One topic carries the turn, and it should say so.

## Avoid (hard gate)

- **Cutting mid-turn**, or splitting a couplet.
- **Prose-summarising the poem.** If the `explanation` would read the same with the छंद deleted,
  it is a retelling and not an explanation.
- **A tacked-on शिक्षा.** ch6 ends on a joke, not a moral; ch3 ends on a question the poem
  deliberately leaves open. Where the chapter itself states a lesson, teach that as the poet's
  move — nowhere else.
- **Naming अलंकार the poem does not obviously carry.** `[]` is the correct value for most topics in
  this विधा at this age (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`). मानवीकरण in ch3 and ch1 is real and worth
  one sentence; a hunt for उपमा and रूपक is not.
- **Treating a couplet as self-contained advice** — the `niti_doha` failure above.
- **Turning ch3 or ch1 into a Science lesson** about moons, orbits or sunrise. ch1's chapter has a
  separate `इसे भी जानिए` block for exactly that, and it is a different topic
  (`soochnatmak_sanskritik`'s avoid-list applies there).

## Priors

**Media** — one frame per छंद-इकाई, showing **that beat's action**, not the scene in general. The
phase-1 art for these three chapters is dense (ch3 has 39 frames for a 12-couplet poem, ch6 has 34
for a two-page one), so the finer cut is already illustrated; deal the frames out in
`scene_NN_img_MM` order and confirm against the `generation_prompt`. Do not illustrate the same
scene twice in consecutive topics.

**अभ्यास** — expect बातचीत के लिए (open discussion before reading), पाठ से (multiple choice on the
events, often more than once down the chapter), सोचिए और लिखिए, अनुमान और कल्पना (a "what if"),
भाषा की बात (समानार्थी, विलोम, मुहावरे, संज्ञा/विशेषण). वीणा's blocks are Agent 10's, never topics
— except **इसे भी जानिए**, which is reading matter. Where a topic prepares a पाठ-से question, its
recall questions should reach the same place.

**Language** — plain खड़ी बोली with a few words a class-5 child will not know: `झिंगोला`, `भाड़े का`,
`उकड़ू-मुकड़ू`, `कुढ़ना`, `सलोने`, `जादू-टोने`. Gloss at point of use, in one clause. The difficulty
is vocabulary, never syntax.

## Recall priors

One `remember` (who said this / what happened first), one `understand` (why the character did it),
one `apply` or `analyze` (what you would have done; which two words rhyme and what the rhyme does).
Keep every stem inside the child's own experience — `reference/teaching_voice_hi.md`, class-5 band.
