# प्रकृति / देशभक्ति काव्य

## Diagnosis signals
Verse in छंद of roughly equal length, often with a **टेक** every few छंद. Nature, land, season,
river, mountain — or the country addressed directly. First-person feeling. Theme: प्रकृति,
देशप्रेम, मातृभूमि. अभ्यास asks about तुक, अलंकार, चित्र, and the poet's भाव.

## Lens
**चित्र + भाव + अलंकार** — a feeling built out of pictures.

## Guiding question
*कवि किन चित्रों से अपना भाव जगाता है?* (Derive the chapter's own version — what makes a land
one's own, what a small drop can do, what one light changes.)

## Explanation unit — **one छंद**

Four lines typically. Where a छंद does two clearly different things it may split; where two very
short छंद do one thing they may join. Decide from the content.

**The टेक is a topic at each occurrence when its words change.** This is specific to this विधा and
it is where the teaching lives: when a refrain returns as
पुण्य/स्वर्ण → धर्म/कर्म → युद्ध/बुद्ध, the poem is arguing, and each return is a new claim about
the same land. Teach *what changed*. Where a टेक returns identical, teach it once and reference it
after.

## Emphasise
- **The picture before the feeling.** The child sees the mountain, then feels the pride. An
  explanation that starts at the feeling has skipped the poem.
- **अलंकार that is doing work** — मानवीकरण making a mountain live, अनुप्रास putting the sound of
  water in the line, रूपक turning land into gold. Name it, quote it, say what it does.
- **The टेक's movement** across the poem.
- **Scale and the child's smallness before it** — this is the emotional engine of the विधा and it
  is directly available to an eleven-year-old standing on a railway bridge.
- **तुक and लय** — what the rhyme does to the reading voice.

## Avoid (hard gate)
- **Slogans.** देशभक्ति taught as a chant ("हमें अपने देश से प्रेम करना चाहिए") is the failure this
  profile guards against. The poem earns the feeling through pictures; the explanation must too.
- **Over-scientifying nature.** A poem about the first drop of rain is not a lesson on the water
  cycle. Stay with what the poet sees and feels.
- **Skipping the craft.** A प्रकृति कविता taught only for its content is half-taught.
- **Reading a figurative line literally** — the mountain is not actually kissing anything, and the
  explanation should make that a delight rather than a confusion.
- **Political framing.** The land, not the state.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per छंद, showing **that छंद's picture**. Indian landscape, watercolour,
16:9. A टेक topic's image shows what the टेक's new words name (a golden harvest for स्वर्ण-भूमि,
fields and workers for कर्मभूमि) rather than repeating the previous picture.

**अभ्यास** — expect तुकांत word-finding, अलंकार identification, शब्दार्थ, "कविता की रचना" notes on
poetic licence, and भाव questions. Where a topic prepares the तुकांत exercise, name the rhyme in
its explanation.

**Language** — तत्सम-leaning (`पुण्य`, `निराली`, `छटा`, `पुनीत`). Gloss at point of use. Poetic
licences (`यमुन` for `यमुना`) are explained as लय, never corrected
(`reference/devanagari_verbatim.md`).

## Recall priors
One `remember` (what does the poet name here), one `understand` (what does this image show / what
does the device do), one `apply` or `analyze` on the टेक's movement or the child's own surroundings.
