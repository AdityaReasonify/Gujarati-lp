# कहानी

## Diagnosis signals
Prose with characters, a plot, dialogue, and a turn. Theme carried by events rather than stated.
अभ्यास asks what happened, why a character did something, "क्या होता अगर", and often asks the
child to continue or retell the story.

## Lens
**घटना + पात्र + मोड़** — what happens, who chooses, and where it turns.

## Guiding question
*पात्र का कौन-सा चुनाव कहानी को मोड़ देता है?*

## Explanation unit — **one घटना**

One plot beat per topic: a coherent event with its own small arc. Follow the story's turns, not
paragraph counts. A long descriptive passage that does not move the story may join the beat it
belongs to; a single sentence that turns everything is its own topic.

Typical shape: परिचय → उलझन → टकराव → **मोड़** → परिणाम.

## Emphasise
- **The turn.** Find the sentence where the story changes direction and build the topic around it.
- **Why a character chooses** — not just what they did. A कहानी teaches through motive.
- **Show, don't tell.** Where the author shows a feeling through an action (a hand on a shoulder,
  a bandage tied and a return to the field), name that as craft.
- **Dialogue as character.** How someone speaks is who they are.
- **The theme as something the story demonstrates**, never as a sentence appended at the end.

## Avoid (hard gate)
- **Summary-only teaching.** Retelling the plot is not explaining the story. If the explanation
  could be replaced by the plot summary, it has failed.
- **A tacked-on शिक्षा.** If the story shows its meaning, the explanation does not need to
  announce it. Where the chapter itself states a moral, teach *that* as the author's move.
- **Judging a character** the story treats with sympathy.
- **Spoiling the turn** in an earlier topic's explanation.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per घटना, showing the **moment**, not the summary: the instant of the
choice, the faces at the turn. Indian setting, characters consistent across the chapter's images
(same clothes, same age, same features).

**अभ्यास** — expect comprehension, motive questions, "क्या होता अगर", vocabulary, and a creative
continuation. Where a topic prepares the continuation task, its explanation should name what the
child would have to keep consistent.

**Language** — everyday खड़ी बोली with idioms and मुहावरे. Gloss the idioms — they are exactly
where a child stalls.

## Recall priors
One `remember` (what happened), one `understand` (why the character acted so), one `analyze` or
`apply` (what the turn shows / what you would have done).
