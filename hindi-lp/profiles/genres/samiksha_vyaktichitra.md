# समीक्षा / व्यक्ति-चित्र

> Written for class-10 स्पर्श ch11 `तीसरी कसम के शिल्पकार शैलेंद्र` (प्रहलाद अग्रवाल).
> `CLASS10_PLAN.md` §2.5 left this open — *"a critical essay about a film and its maker — part
> व्यक्ति-चित्र, part समीक्षा. Nearest is `sansmaran`, with the same third-person mismatch class 7
> recorded for सुधा चंद्रन. It is also the richest image set in the class."* This is that decision.

## Diagnosis signals

**A critic writing about someone else's work, in the third person, to persuade you of a verdict.**
Not remembering a life, not narrating events — building a case. The tells arrive together:

- **A claim, then the warrant that earns it.** `‘तीसरी कसम’ फ़िल्म नहीं, सैल्यूलाइड पर लिखी कविता
  थी।` is a verdict; the पुरस्कार, the anecdotes, the songs and the close reading are what pay for
  it.
- **Anecdotes are cited, not told.** The one-rupee advance, the distributors who would not buy —
  each is two sentences long and each is offered as proof of character. A story profile would slow
  down and dramatise them; this one does not.
- **The subject is never interviewed and never speaks in the present.** Quotes come from the work
  and from remembered exchanges, inside the critic's prose.
- **No 'मैं' anywhere except where the critic states his own position** — `मैंने शैलेंद्र को
  गीतकार नहीं, कवि कहा है` — which is an argument move, not a memory.

**The textbook names the विधा itself, and that settles it.** In ch11's योग्यता विस्तार the student
is asked *"इस फ़िल्म की समीक्षा स्वयं लिखने का प्रयास कीजिए"*, and लिखित (ख) asks *"लेखक के इस कथन
से … आप कहाँ तक सहमत हैं?"* — weigh the critic's claim. **Not one अभ्यास question is memoir-shaped.**

| looks like | but | tell |
|---|---|---|
| `sansmaran` | a real person, admired, remembered | its unit is *one स्मृति / जीवन-चरण* and here **no life is told in sequence** — no birth, no childhood, no death. The essay is organised by claim, not by chronology, and the writer never met the subject |
| `samvad_nibandh` | it is an essay making a case | its lens is *दो पक्ष* and its hardest avoid is **"one-sided preaching; flattening the opposing voice."** An appreciation is one-sided **by form** — the same mistake `_genre_index.md` already documents for `bhashan_uddabodhan`. Reaching it marks the form's defining property as a defect |
| `kahani` | the one-rupee scene and the film's failure read like story | they are **evidence, not plot**. No turn, no protagonist's journey. Dramatising them is the failure mode |
| `soochnatmak_sanskritik` | awards, songs, names, dates look like facts | **no fact here is neutral.** Every one is a warrant for a claim. Teaching them "in their own terms" turns an argument into a filmography |
| `sakshatkar` | dialogue is quoted — `निकालो एक रुपया` | it is quoted **inside** the prose. No speaker column, no प्रश्न-उत्तर pair, no interview took place |

## Lens

**दावा + प्रमाण + व्यक्ति** — what the critic asserts, what he offers to earn it, and the person
the verdict is finally about.

## Guiding question

*समीक्षक जो दावा करता है, वह उसे किस-किस चीज़ से कमाता है — और क्या पाठक वह क़ीमत मानता है?*

## Explanation unit — **one समीक्षा-प्रसंग**

One claim staked, plus the warrant that earns it. A warrant is one of four things: an external
verdict (an award, a critic, a public), an anecdote read as character, a counter-fact accounted
for, or a close reading of the work itself.

**Cut where the warrant changes, not at the paragraph break**, and **never split an anecdote from
the judgement it is offered as proof of** — an anecdote alone is a nice story with no argument, and
a judgement alone is an assertion with no ground.

## Emphasise

- **Name the move, every time.** Is this paragraph asserting, or paying? A child who can say
  *"यहाँ दावा है"* and *"यहाँ प्रमाण है"* can read any review afterwards.
- **The counter-facts are the essay's spine, not its interruptions.** The film had no publicity,
  no distributor would buy it, nobody noticed when it came and went. A weaker essay would hide
  these; this one puts them next to the awards. Ask why that makes the verdict stronger.
- **Praise is a claim and may be disagreed with.** The अभ्यास asks the student how far they agree.
  Never present the critic's verdict as a fact to be memorised.
- **Quoted work stays quoted.** Song lines and dialect (`बाँचै`, `हमार`, `कोय`) are text under
  `reference/devanagari_verbatim.md` — never modernised, never paraphrased away. Keep a display-set
  line displayed and an inline quote inline; the page's own layout is the instruction.
- **The person behind the verdict.** The essay is finally about a man who staked everything on one
  film. Let that land — but through the evidence, not through sympathy.

## Avoid (hard gate)

- **Scoring one-sidedness as a fault.** This विधा has no obligation to give the other side equal
  weight; it must only be honest about the facts against it, which this one is.
- **Dramatising an anecdote into a scene** with invented dialogue, feelings, or a turn.
- **Turning the essay into a biography or a filmography.** No date-list. No "life and works".
- **Presenting the verdict as fact.** `सैल्यूलाइड पर लिखी कविता` is the critic's phrase, and it
  should be taught as a phrase someone chose.
- **Reading the film's commercial failure as the artist's failure** — the essay's whole point is
  the opposite, and a careless summary inverts it.

## Priors

**Media** — these chapters carry the largest image sets in the pack (ch11 has 93 frames over 48
scenes, ~3 per topic). Prefer the frame that shows **what the warrant is about** — the award
ceremony for the award, the empty distributor's room for the no-buyer counter-fact, the bullock
cart for the close reading — over generic portraits of the subject.

**अभ्यास** — expect `आशय स्पष्ट कीजिए` on the critic's phrases, at least one `आप कहाँ तक सहमत हैं`,
and a योग्यता विस्तार that asks the student to write a review of their own. Where a topic prepares
one of these, its recall questions should reach the same place.

**Language** — film vocabulary sits beside literary Hindi: `शोमैन`, `एक्टिंग`, `पटकथा`,
`रिलीज़`, alongside `चर्मोत्कर्ष`, `संवेदनशील`, `अभिजात्य`. Gloss both registers; children stall on
the तत्सम, not on the English.

## Recall priors

One `remember` (what claim / whose verdict), one `understand` (what this evidence is doing for the
claim), one `analyze` or `evaluate` (whether the warrant earns it; whether the reader agrees).
