# बाल खेल-गीत / तुकबंदी (नॉनसेंस वर्स सहित)

> Written for class-3 वीणा — ch10, which holds **two** of them: the इकाई-3 opener
> `सुनो भई गप्प` (*– अज्ञात, मालती देवी द्वारा संशोधित एवं परिवर्धित*, book pp. 80–81, printed
> under `(पढ़ने के लिए)`) and `रस्साकशी` (book p. 82), which the page badges **खेल गीत** in so
> many words. Both fall through the whole verse branch of `_genre_index.md` and land on
> `prakriti_deshbhakti_kavita`, which is where that branch bottoms out. Class 10 recorded three
> times what the default landing costs (`CLASS10_PLAN.md` §2.5): *a default landing is not a
> diagnosis.* That profile builds feeling out of **pictures** and teaches "what the टेक changed".
> These poems have a टेक that **never** changes, and their pictures are **impossible on purpose**.
> Read as description, they become nonsense — which they are, and which is exactly the thing an
> explanation must not do straight-faced.

## Diagnosis signals

**Verse whose point is the sound and the game, not the picture and not the story.**

- **An unchanging टेक, repeated after every stanza.** रस्साकशी: *जोर लगाओ, हेई सा! / हेई सा! भई,
  हेई सा!* — twice on the page, identical both times. सुनो भई गप्प: *सुनो भई गप्प, सुनो भई शप्प, /
  नाव में नदिया डूबी जाए।* — five times, identical every time. This is the decisive negative test
  against `prakriti_deshbhakti_kavita`, whose lens is built on **tracking what the टेक changes**.
  There is nothing to track. It does **not** separate this विधा from `niti_upadesh_kavita`, whose
  टेक is equally unchanging — the table below carries the test that does.
- **The stanzas are freely reorderable.** Nothing in सुनो भई गप्प requires the चींटी stanza to
  precede the गधा stanza. This is the negative of `bal_katha_kavita`'s decisive test — there, छंद 4
  cannot precede छंद 3 because the lion must sit before he can be asked to turn round. Here it can.
  **If the order matters, this is the wrong profile.**
- **Either the poem is performed, or its images are impossible — usually one, sometimes both.**
  - *performed*: रस्साकशी is chanted by two teams pulling a rope. `हेई सा` is not a word with a
    meaning; it is the beat on which you pull. The imperatives are instructions —
    *खींचो, खींचो, जोर लगाओ, / पैर गड़ा कर, पीठ अड़ाओ*.
  - *impossible on purpose*: सुनो भई गप्प is उलटबाँसी — *नाव में नदिया डूबी जाए*, *गधा चढ़ा खजूर पर*,
    *ऊँट खाट पर सोए*, *बिल्ली सबकी रोटी सेके*, *चींटी … सिर पर धर ली रेल*. Every line inverts
    something the child knows. The title says so: गप्प.
- **Short lines, heavy end-rhyme, and vocabulary a child already owns.** तेल/रेल, अंगूर/लंगूर,
  बजाए/सोए/सेके/धोए, अकड़ कर/पकड़ कर/जकड़ कर. No word needs a gloss; the difficulty is nowhere.
- **The अभ्यास asks the child to sing, act, clap or add a line** — not to explain a भाव. वीणा
  class 3 prints `खेल गीत` as an अभ्यास banner too (ch7 p62, *"ऐसे ही कोई दो गीत गाइए और लिखिए—"*),
  which is the same instinct: these are things you do, not things you interpret.

Four विधा would take one of these poems, nearest miss first, and each fails on a different thing:

| looks like | but | tell |
|---|---|---|
| **`niti_upadesh_kavita`** — **the nearest miss** | equal छंद closing on the **same unchanged line**, reader addressed in the **imperative**. रस्साकशी satisfies every clause of that entry as written | ask **what the imperative asks for — a change in how you live, or a movement of your body right now.** *विचार लो कि मर्त्य हो* is the first; *पैर गड़ा कर, पीठ अड़ाओ* is the second. नीति-उपदेश also cites named पुराण/इतिहास figures one line each as proof; a खेल-गीत cites nothing, because it is not arguing. Its hard gate — one छंद = one complete piece of counsel — would make *तिरछे पड़ कर, कमर जकड़ कर* into a life lesson |
| `prakriti_deshbhakti_kavita` | equal छंद, strict तुक, a river and a moon and animals | its lens is **चित्र + भाव + अलंकार** and its टेक rule is *teach what the टेक changed*. The टेक changes nothing, and the pictures are not images of anything — they are jokes. Cutting सुनो भई गप्प "by imagery" yields five topics gravely describing a donkey up a date palm |
| `bal_katha_kavita` | rhymed छंद, animals doing things, a child's world | its lens is **घटना + संवाद + तुक** and its decisive test is *can the छंद be reordered*. Here they can, and nobody speaks — there is no plot to hand from one छंद to the next |
| `prarthana_kavita` | a repeating refrain closing every stanza | a प्रार्थना's refrain is an **address** and its repetition is devotional. `हेई सा` addresses nobody and means nothing; it is a work-rhythm |

## Lens

**लय + दोहराव + खेल** — the beat, the thing that comes back, and what the children are doing while
they say it.

## Guiding question

*इस छंद को बोलते समय क्या होता है — और इसमें मज़ा कहाँ है?*

## Explanation unit — **one छंद-इकाई: one stanza with its टेक, or one call-and-response turn**

The टेक belongs to the stanza it closes and is **not** cut into a topic of its own, however many
times it repeats. It is quoted with each stanza in `original_chunk`, exactly as printed — that is
what repetition *is* in this विधा, and a plan that prints it once has removed the poem's method.

रस्साकशी's opening couplet is the टेक standing alone before the first stanza; it is part of the
first topic, not a topic.

A unit may never split. Two units join **only** when the second completes a call the first began,
and the merge is recorded in `deviation_from_v1`.

## Emphasise

- **The beat, out loud, before anything else.** Say the टेक twice and let the child hear that it
  never changes. At eight, *noticing that a thing comes back* is the whole craft lesson —
  `reference/teaching_voice_hi.md`, class-3 band.
- **The joke, named as a joke.** *नाव में नदिया डूबी जाए* — the river drowns in the boat. Ask the
  child which part is impossible and let them answer. That question **is** the explanation for a
  उलटबाँसी stanza; there is no second, deeper meaning underneath, and pretending there is one is
  this profile's characteristic failure.
- **What the body is doing**, wherever the poem is performed. रस्साकशी's *सीना ताने*, *पैर गड़ा कर*,
  *पीठ अड़ाओ* are postures a child can stand up and take. One sentence of the `explanation` should
  put them in the body.
- **The rhyme pair, heard before it is named.** तेल–रेल, अंगूर–लंगूर. Say them together, then — and
  only then — the word *तुक*.
- **Who says which part**, where the poem is call-and-response, so the class can be split in two.

## Avoid (hard gate)

- **Explaining a nonsense image as if it were a description.** If the `explanation` says what the
  donkey was doing on the date palm without saying that donkeys do not climb date palms, the topic
  has failed and must be re-authored.
- **Extracting a शिक्षा from a chant.** रस्साकशी teaches nothing about teamwork; it is a rope and a
  beat. सुनो भई गप्प teaches nothing at all. Where the chapter itself states a lesson, teach that as
  the book's move — nowhere else.
- **Printing the टेक once and referring to it thereafter**, or cutting it into its own topic.
- **Cutting mid-stanza**, or separating a टेक from the stanza it closes.
- **Naming अलंकार.** `[]` is the correct value for every topic in this विधा at this age
  (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`). अनुप्रास may be *heard* — *हेई सा, भई, हेई सा* — but it is not
  named at class 3.
- **Turning the poem into a Science or Sports lesson** — about ants, camels, or how tug-of-war is
  refereed. No fact the page does not carry (`no_hallucination_policy.md`).
- **Attributing an अज्ञात poem to anyone.** सुनो भई गप्प's credit line is *– अज्ञात* followed by
  *(मालती देवी द्वारा संशोधित एवं परिवर्धित)*, and both lines are part of the verbatim.

## Priors

**Media** — one frame per छंद-इकाई, showing **that stanza's impossible thing** or **that moment of
the game**, never the poem in general. Class 3's phase-1 art is dense elsewhere but **thin here**:
ch10 carries 12 frames on a 4-scene clock for two texts, and सुनो भई गप्प is not in phase 1 at all,
so its gap is total. Under the reuse-only rule that means `no_images` with a written reason and an
entry in `output3/_image_gaps.json` carrying `candidates_considered` — expected, not a defect.

**अभ्यास** — expect बातचीत के लिए, सोचिए और लिखिए, भाषा की बात, शब्दों का खेल, and the performance
blocks (`खेल गीत`, `आइए मिलकर गाएँ`, `कुछ नया करें`, `मेरी कलाकारी`). वीणा's blocks are Agent 10's,
never topics — except `कविता से आगे` / `पढ़िए और जानिए` / `इन्हें भी जानिए`, which are reading
matter, and `शिक्षण-संकेत`, which is addressed to the teacher and belongs to neither deliverable
(`CLASS3_PLAN.md` §2.3).

**Language** — plain खड़ी बोली, no gloss needed anywhere. `हेई सा`, `गप्प`, `शप्प` are sound, not
vocabulary; do not define them, and do not claim `शप्प` means anything — it rhymes, and that is its
whole job.

## Recall priors

One `remember` (which line comes back every time / what were the children pulling), one
`understand` (which part of this stanza cannot really happen, and why it is funny), one `apply`
(say the टेक with a clap; make up one more impossible line). Every stem inside the child's own
experience — `reference/teaching_voice_hi.md`, class-3 band.
