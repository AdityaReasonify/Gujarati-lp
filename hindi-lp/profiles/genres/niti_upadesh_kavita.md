# नीति / उपदेश-कविता

> Written for class-10 स्पर्श ch3 `मनुष्यता` (मैथिलीशरण गुप्त). Like `आत्मत्राण` before it, this
> chapter fell through the whole verse branch of `_genre_index.md` and landed on
> `prakriti_deshbhakti_kavita`. मनुष्यता has almost no pictures either — it has **names offered as
> evidence** and **verbs in the imperative**. The borrowed lens (*चित्र + भाव + अलंकार*) sends the
> chapter hunting imagery, and worse, its टेक rule is the exact inverse of what this विधा needs.

## Diagnosis signals

**Verse in equal छंद, every one of them closing on the same unchanged line.** मनुष्यता is eight
छंद of six lines each — three तुकांत जोड़े — and all eight end
`वही मनुष्य है कि जो मनुष्य के लिए मरे।।`, word for word.

Three further signals arrive together and none of them belongs to a nature or a prayer poem:

- **The addressee is the reader, and the mood is imperative** — `विचार लो`, `मरो`, `रहो न`,
  `करो न`, `उठो`, `बढ़ो`, `चढ़ो`, `चलो`. The poem instructs.
- **Named figures carried as proof, not as scenery** — रंतिदेव, दधीचि, उशीनर क्षितीश, कर्ण, बुद्ध.
  Each is one line long and each is there to win an argument.
- **The theme is how a person should live**, and the अभ्यास asks `कवि ने किन पंक्तियों में …`,
  `भाव स्पष्ट कीजिए`, `कवि क्या संदेश देना चाहता है`.

The decisive signal is what the टेक is *doing*. **This poem is an argument, and the टेक is the
claim it keeps re-proving.** Every छंद is one complete step — a new reason, a new example, a new
warning — and it ends by earning the same sentence again.

Four विधा would have taken this chapter, and each fails on a different thing:

| looks like | but | tell |
|---|---|---|
| `niti_doha` | a दोहा also gives a सीख with a दृष्टांत | a दोहा is **complete in two lines**; here the claim does not close until the sixth. Its hard gate (one दोहा = one topic) would cut मनुष्यता into couplets that name a person and stop |
| `prakriti_deshbhakti_kavita` | छंद of equal length, a टेक, and a lofty feeling | that विधा builds feeling out of **pictures**, and its टेक rule says *teach what changed* — here the टेक **never changes**. What changes is the दलील in front of it. Applying that rule produces eight topics about one sentence |
| `vir_ras_kavita` | कर्ण, दधीचि, sacrifice, ओज in the rhythm | the रस is शांत, not वीर. Nothing charges; the warriors appear as donors, and the verbs are addressed to the reader, not to a battlefield |
| `prarthana_kavita` | the same moral seriousness, the same तत्सम register | a प्रार्थना addresses **God** and **asks**. This addresses **the reader** and **instructs**. Nothing is requested of anyone |

## Lens

**सीख + दृष्टांत + टेक** — the instruction, the proof offered for it, and the line that returns to
close the case.

## Guiding question

*कवि हर छंद के अंत में एक ही पंक्ति दोहराता है — तो हर बार उससे पहले नया क्या कहा गया?*

## Explanation unit — **one छंद, closed by its टेक**

Six lines here, but the count is not the rule: **the unit ends where the टेक lands.** A छंद is one
complete step of the argument and its claim only stands once the refrain closes it.

Two छंद may join **only** when the second finishes an example the first began, and the merge must
be justified in `deviation_from_v1`. A छंद may never split — half a छंद is a premise with no
conclusion, and the child is left holding a name.

## Emphasise

- **The टेक is a constant, not a moving refrain.** Teach it once, in full, at its first
  occurrence — what `मर्त्य`, `सुमृत्यु` and `मनुष्य के लिए मरे` mean, and that "मरना" here is
  the whole poem's shorthand for living for others. After that, the question at every छंद is one
  sentence long: *इस बार इसे किस दलील से कमाया गया?* A chapter that re-explains the टेक eight
  times has taught the poem once and repeated itself seven times.
- **दृष्टांत are evidence.** रंतिदेव gave away the food in his own hands while starving; दधीचि
  gave his bones; उशीनर (शिबि) gave his own flesh; कर्ण gave the armour he was born in. Give the
  child exactly enough of each story to see why it is being cited — then return to the argument.
  The छंद spends one line per person for a reason.
- **The imperative mood, named out loud.** Ask whom the poem is speaking to. Once a child hears
  `विचार लो` as an instruction addressed to *them*, the तत्सम vocabulary stops being a wall.
- **The rhyme carrying the argument.** Three तुकांत जोड़े per छंद, and the last जोड़ा must land on
  the टेक's rhyme — `करे / मरे`, `डरे / मरे`, `भरे / मरे`. The poet has to reach that sound every
  time, and watching him do it is the craft lesson.
- **Where the poem turns from claim to command.** मनुष्यता argues for five छंद and then starts
  telling the reader what to do (`उठो तथा बढ़ो सभी`, `चलो अभीष्ट मार्ग में`). Name that turn.

## Avoid (hard gate)

- **Cutting a छंद in half**, or teaching one तुकांत जोड़ा as a unit.
- **Re-teaching the टेक in every topic.** Explain it once; afterwards reference it and spend the
  words on what is new.
- **Reducing a छंद to its moral in one sentence.** "हमें दूसरों की मदद करनी चाहिए" is precisely the
  sentence the poem spends six lines refusing to be. If a topic's explanation would survive
  deleting the छंद, it is not an explanation of the छंद.
- **Turning the दृष्टांत into a mythology lesson.** The story is cited, not told. Four sentences on
  दधीचि's legend and none on why he is standing next to कर्ण is a topic that missed its own छंद.
- **Reading सुमृत्यु as a call to die.** The poem's subject is how to live; `मरे` is its figure for
  living so that others gain. Say this explicitly the first time and never leave a topic in which
  self-destruction reads as the recommendation (`reference/global_content_rules.md`).
- **Supplying pictures or naming अलंकार the poem does not have** to make it look like a
  प्रकृति-कविता.

## Priors

**Media** — one frame per छंद, showing that छंद's **exemplar or its action** (सरस्वती singing the
generous one's story; the four donors; बुद्ध; the orphan under the sheltering hands; people
climbing together; hands joined in a circle; obstacles pushed off a road). Do not illustrate the
टेक — it is the same every time, and eight identical frames teach nothing.

**अभ्यास** — expect `कवि ने कैसी मृत्यु को सुमृत्यु कहा है`, `किन पंक्तियों में यह व्यक्त किया है
कि …`, a शब्दार्थ or two, and one broad `कवि क्या संदेश देना चाहता है`. Where a topic prepares one
of these, its recall questions should reach the same place.

**Language** — तत्सम-heavy and compressed: `मर्त्य`, `क्षुधार्त`, `परार्थ`, `अस्थिजाल`, `मदांध`,
`सनाथ`, `अंतरैक्य`, `अपंक`, `अभीष्ट`, `परस्परावलंब`. Gloss at point of use; the difficulty here is
vocabulary, not syntax. Sanskrit compounds are worth breaking open once (`परस्पर + अवलंब`) — the
child can then read the next one alone.

## Recall priors

One `remember` (which name / which line), one `understand` (why that example proves the claim),
one `analyze` or `evaluate` (what this छंद adds that the previous one did not; whether the reader
accepts the argument).
