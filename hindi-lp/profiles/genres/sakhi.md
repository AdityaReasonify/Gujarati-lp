# साखी

> Written for class-10 स्पर्श ch1 `साखी` (कबीर). `CLASS10_PLAN.md` §2.5 left this open —
> *"the unit is a दोहा, so `niti_doha.md` fits structurally — but a साखी is witness, निर्गुण भक्ति
> and ज्ञान, not नीति… a `sakhi.md` is the cleaner fix. Decide before ch1 is cut."* This is that
> decision, and the chapter's own पाठ-प्रवेश makes it for us — see below.

## Diagnosis signals

**The text tells you what it is, in its own opening prose.** स्पर्श's पाठ-प्रवेश defines the form
before the first couplet, and both halves of the definition matter:

> `'साखी' शब्द 'साक्षी' शब्द का ही तद्भव रूप है। साक्षी शब्द साक्ष्य से बना है जिसका अर्थ होता`
> `है—प्रत्यक्ष ज्ञान। यह प्रत्यक्ष ज्ञान गुरु शिष्य को प्रदान करता है। संत संप्रदाय में अनुभव`
> `ज्ञान की ही महत्ता है, शास्त्रीय ज्ञान की नहीं।`
>
> `'साखी' वस्तुत: दोहा छंद ही है जिसका लक्षण है 13 और 11 के विश्राम से 24 मात्रा।`

So: **the unit is a दोहा and the lens is testimony.** Two-line units, 13+11 = 24 मात्रा, each
complete in itself, in a mixed sant dialect the same page names — `पचमेल खिचड़ी`, `सधुक्कड़ी`.

The decisive signal is **who is testifying and to what**. A नीति दोहा reports how the world
works so the reader can act better in it. A साखी reports what the speaker has *seen* — and the
seeing is inward. Half of a साखी set will have no practical instruction in it at all.

Four विधा are near, and each fails on something specific:

| looks like | but | tell |
|---|---|---|
| `niti_doha` | same unit, same दृष्टांत-then-turn shape, and some साखियाँ genuinely are नीति | its lens is *अनुभव की सीख* and it emphasises "the सीख the child can use". Apply it to `जब मैं था तब हरि नहीं` or `बिरह भुवंगम` and there is no usable सीख to extract — the reading either collapses or invents one. **See the hard gate below: on one साखी that invention is not merely wrong, it is unsafe** |
| `bhakti_pad` | निर्गुण भक्ति, the same devotional world, `दास कबीर` reads like a छाप | a पद is **sung**, has a टेक that returns whole, runs to eight or more lines, and carries ब्रज sweetness. A साखी is two lines, spoken, argued, and dry. `दास कबीर` sits inside the sentence as its subject, not underneath it as a signature |
| `kundaliya` | opens with a दोहा | there is no रोला after it and no returning first word. The couplet IS the whole poem |
| `chaupai_doha_samvad` | a दोहा closing something | nothing is being closed. Each साखी stands alone and the set has no narrative order |

## Lens

**साक्षी + अनुभव + अंतर्दृष्टि** — what the speaker witnessed, that he witnessed it himself rather
than reading it, and the turn inward that the witnessing demands.

## Guiding question

*कबीर किस बात की गवाही दे रहे हैं — और वह गवाही उन्हें कहाँ से मिली, पोथी से या अनुभव से?*

## Explanation unit — **one साखी**

Two lines. A साखी is a complete testimony and it does not combine. **Never merge two साखियाँ into
one topic**, not even when their themes rhyme — v1 for ch1 pairs them (कस्तूरी with जब मैं था,
सुखिया with बिरह, निंदक with पोथी) and every pair hides one of the two.

Never split one either. The picture is in line 1 and the turn is in line 2; a topic holding only
the picture teaches a riddle with no answer.

## Emphasise

- **Teach the definition before the first साखी.** साखी = साक्षी = प्रत्यक्ष ज्ञान. A child who
  knows the word means *eyewitness* reads the whole set differently, and the chapter's own prose
  hands you the sentence.
- **अनुभव over शास्त्र is the spine, not a theme.** `पोथी पढ़ि पढ़ि जग मुवा, पंडित भया न कोइ` is
  the set arguing about its own authority. Connect it back to the definition.
- **The dialect is content.** `बाँणी`, `राँम`, `नाँहिं`, `घटि घटि`, `अषिर`, `नेड़ा`, `आँगणि`,
  `साबण`, `पाँणीं`, `मुराड़ा`, `जाल्या`. This is सधुक्कड़ी — Avadhi, Rajasthani, Bhojpuri and
  Punjabi in one line, because the poet walked to all of them. Gloss every word at point of use
  and **never correct one to खड़ी बोली** (`reference/devanagari_verbatim.md`). Ask why a poet
  whose subject is direct experience would write in a language nobody's grammar owns.
- **The turn in line 2, and what kind of turn it is.** A नीति दोहा turns outward, to conduct. A
  साखी usually turns inward, to the reader's own body or mind — the musk is *in the deer*, the
  lamp is *inside*, the snake is *in this body*.
- **Where the speaker names himself.** `दुखिया दास कबीर है` is testimony, not autobiography and
  not self-pity. He is the witness taking the stand.

## Avoid (hard gate)

- **`हम घर जाल्या आपणाँ` must never be taught as instruction to the reader.** The couplet says
  *I burned my own house and took the brand in my hand; now I will burn the house of whoever
  walks with me.* The घर is one's own मोह and अहंकार, and the second line is the guru's demand
  that a disciple give up the same. Say what the घर stands for **in the same breath as the line
  itself**, and never leave a topic, an image prompt or a recall answer in which burning, or
  following someone who burns, reads literally (`reference/global_content_rules.md`). This single
  couplet is the strongest reason this profile exists: `niti_doha`'s lens asks for "the सीख the
  child can use", and there is no safe way to answer that question about this line.
- **Reading a साखी as worldly advice.** `बिरह भुवंगम` is not about coping with sadness, and
  `जिवै तो बौरा होइ` is not a diagnosis. Do not medicalise it and do not moralise it.
- **Merging or splitting साखियाँ.**
- **Correcting the dialect**, or presenting it as an older, rougher Hindi that has since improved.
- **Flattening `निंदक नेड़ा राखिये`** into "we should accept criticism". The साखी says build your
  critic a hut in your own courtyard — the exaggeration *is* the teaching, and the soap-and-water
  image is what makes it land.
- **Turning the set into a lesson on religion, on sects, or on कबीर's biography.** निर्गुण here is
  a way of seeing, taught as such.

## Priors

**Media** — one frame per साखी, showing **that साखी's picture**: the deer circling the forest for
its own musk, the lamp lit inside, the snake in the body, the hut in the courtyard, the pile of
books. Two or three साखियाँ have no picture to draw (`जब मैं था तब हरि नहीं`); prefer a spare,
symbolic frame — a lamp, an empty doorway — over inventing a scene the couplet does not contain.
Do not depict God, and do not depict the burning house literally.

**अभ्यास** — expect `भाव स्पष्ट कीजिए` per साखी, a शब्दार्थ list drawn from the dialect, and at
least one question on कबीर की भाषा (`पचमेल खिचड़ी` / `सधुक्कड़ी` are the expected answers). Where a
topic prepares one of these, its recall questions should reach the same place.

**Language** — the highest gloss-density chapter in the class. Every second word needs one. Give
the gloss inline in the explanation rather than only in `shabdarth`, or the child stalls at line 1.

## Recall priors

One `remember` (what does this साखी picture), one `understand` (what does the second line turn it
into), one `analyze` or `evaluate` (why this picture for this idea; whether the reader accepts the
witness). Where the साखी is one of the abstract ones, make the middle question do the work.
