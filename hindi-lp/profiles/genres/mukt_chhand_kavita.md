# मुक्त छंद कविता

## Diagnosis signals

Verse — short lines, a lot of white space — with **no metre, no rhyme and no repeating unit**.
Line lengths vary freely; one line may be a single word (`जा,` / `गाएँ`), the next twelve
syllables. There is no टेक returning at fixed intervals, no छंद of equal length, no couplet.

Blank lines may group the text loosely, but those groups are **not छंद**: they are uneven, and
counting them gives a different answer each time you try.

**Tested first in the verse branch, before कुंडलिया / दोहा / पद / छंद.** Every other verse profile's
explanation unit is "one छंद", and a छंद-first test on मुक्त छंद matches nothing and then falls
through to whichever thematic profile is nearest — which silently invents छंद boundaries that are
not on the page. Ask the negative question first: *is there a repeating unit at all?*

## Lens

**भाव + बिंब + गति** — the feeling, the images it arrives in, and the movement the line-breaks
create.

## Guiding question

*कवि ने यहीं पंक्ति क्यों तोड़ी?*

## Explanation unit — **one भाव-खंड**

A **भाव-खंड** is a stretch of the poem held together by one movement of feeling, and it ends where
the feeling turns. Because the form gives no typographic boundary, the cut is a **reading
judgement** and must be defended from the text. Three usable boundaries:

- **a turn in the address** — the speaker stops asking and starts blessing, stops describing and
  starts demanding,
- **a completed image** — a बिंब is introduced, extended, and closed before the next begins,
- **a syntactic close** — a full stop or a `।` after several lines that have run on.

Rules:

- **Record the boundary reasoning in the topic's own terms.** For every cut, the explanation must
  be able to say *what changes here*. If it cannot, the cut is arbitrary and belongs elsewhere.
- **Do not use the blank lines as छंद.** Use them as evidence among others.
- **A repeated opening line that returns at the end is a frame, not a टेक.** एक आशीर्वाद opens and
  closes on `जा, / तेरे स्वप्न बड़े हों` — that return closes the poem, and the closing occurrence
  belongs with the final भाव-खंड, not as a topic of its own.
- **A ladder is one movement, not one topic per rung.** आदमी का अनुपात climbs
  कमरा → घर → मुहल्ला → नगर → प्रदेश → देश → पृथ्वी → नक्षत्र → ब्रह्मांड. Cutting per rung
  destroys the only thing the ladder does, which is accelerate. One भाव-खंड, `concepts[]` for the
  rungs.

## Emphasise

- **The line-break as the craft.** This is the form's one deliberate device and the child has never
  been taught it. Where a short line stands alone, say what standing alone does to it. Where a
  sentence spills across three lines, say what the delay does.
- **बिंब over अलंकार.** मुक्त छंद often carries no named अलंकार at all. `figures_of_speech: []` is
  the correct, honest answer when nothing is there — naming a मानवीकरण that is not present is a
  hard fail (`reference/alankar_chhand.md`). What it does carry is images, and those are teachable
  without a label.
- **Who is speaking to whom.** These poems are usually addressed — a blessing to a child, a
  statement about two people in a room. Identify the speaker and the addressee early; the whole
  reading depends on it.
- **Everyday words doing heavy work.** `उँगली जलाएँ`, `कमरे से छोटे`. The vocabulary is plain; the
  meaning is not. Resist the urge to find difficulty in the words instead of in the thought.
- **The absence of rhyme is a choice.** Say so once, plainly, and say what it buys — speech that
  sounds like a person talking rather than a poem reciting.

## Avoid (hard gate)

- **Inventing छंद.** Numbering "पहला छंद / दूसरा छंद" for a poem that has none.
- **Naming अलंकार that are not there**, or filling `rhyme_scheme` with anything other than the fact
  that there is none.
- **Prose-summarising the poem** and calling the summary an explanation. The lines and their breaks
  are the poem; a paraphrase deletes it.
- **Cutting one line per topic** because the lines are short.
- **Attaching a moral.** A blessing is not advice; a poem about scale is not a lesson in humility.

## Priors

**Media** — one image per भाव-खंड, built from the poem's own बिंब rather than from a scene. Where
the poem is an address (a parent blessing a child), the picture may hold the two figures; where it
is a widening scale, the picture should widen. Avoid literalising a metaphor into an object.

**अभ्यास** — expect `मेरी समझ से` asking which reading of a line is right (multiple-choice on
meaning, because there is no metre to ask about), `पंक्तियों पर चर्चा`, `कविता की रचना` inviting
the child to write मुक्त छंद of their own, and भाषा की बात on पर्यायवाची and क्रिया-रूप. Where a
topic prepares the writing task, its explanation should have already shown one line-break doing
work.

**Language** — plain contemporary खड़ी बोली. Gloss sparingly; the difficulty is conceptual.

## Recall priors

One `remember` (what the खंड says), one `understand` (what the image means / who is speaking),
one `analyze` or `create` (why the break falls there / write two lines the same way).
